### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘ **Crisil BBB+/Stable** ’ rating to the long-term bank facility of Kalpataru Limited (KL; part of the Kalpataru group). 

The rating reflects the company’s strong market position and established track record in the MMR market, improving saleability of its residential projects and improvement in financial risk profile as a result of infusion from promoter and IPO proceeds. These strengths are partially offset by KL’s geographical concentration and its susceptibility to cyclicality in the real estate sector. 

The company has seen significant capital infusion through equity infusion over the past two fiscals and proceeds from IPO in the current fiscal. The capital has been utilized to pare off its debt as well as for infusion into its projects, resulting in an increased pace of construction and improved sales velocity. Resultantly, the saleability of KL’s projects has improved with sales booking increasing by ~40% to Rs. 4,531 crores in fiscal 2025. With a healthy mix of projects in early, mid and late stages of construction, and the company is planning to launch ~ 13-16 million square feet of saleable area over the next three years, the sales booking of the company is expected to increase to ~Rs. 6,500 – 8,000 crores over fiscal 2026 and 2027. 

With healthy saleability and improved pace of construction, collections increased by 36% in fiscal 2025 to Rs. 3,659, and are expected to further strengthen to ~Rs. 6,000 – Rs. 7,500 crores over the current and next fiscal. 

Further, with the promoter infusing Rs. 1,440 crores in fiscal 2024 and 2025 and ~75% of the IPO proceeds (Rs. 1192.5 crores out of Rs. 1,590 crores) being used for repay/prepayment of debt, the company’s financial risk profile is expected to improve significantly. As a result, its Debt to CFO estimated at 2.5-3.4 times over FY2026-FY2028 

Going forward, the company is expected to continue an asset light business model. With no land acquisition envisaged in the medium term, the company is expected to focus on society redevelopment projects, JDA/JVs and launches on already existing land bank. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of all ongoing and planned projects in Kalpataru Limited, its subsidiaries and associate companies. All the entities, collectively referred to as the Kalpataru group, are in the same business, have common promoters and share significant operational, managerial and financial linkages. Unsecured loans from promoters are treated as debt as they are interest bearing.

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Established market position in MMR market:** The group is a prominent developer in MMR, with a healthy track record of over five decades in the real estate business. The group has developed ~19.3 msf in the real estate sector. Strong position is evident from its established brand, and the healthy market share (among the top 5 developers in Mumbai) in the organized residential real estate market in Mumbai, considering the high fragmentation in the real estate industry. 

- **Improving saleability and collections led by increased pace of construction:** The company’s projects, of which a few had seen some delays in the past, saw pickup in construction following the capital infusion by promoters in fiscal 2024 and 2025. Following this, the sales velocity of its project has picked up well. Resultantly, KL is expected to witness sales booking of over Rs. 6,500-7000 crores this fiscal. With launches of ~13-16 million square feet in the next three years, the company is expected to achieve annual sales of Rs 6,500-8,000 crore over the medium term. The sales booking for KL increased to ~Rs. 4,531 crores in fiscal 2025, a ~40% growth from fiscal 2024. 

# **Weakness:** 

- **Moderate financial risk profile:** The financial risk of KL remains moderate as the company with leverage remaining elevated due to historically high debt levels. The debt levels for the company have remained high as a result of previous issues pertaining to delay in few projects. While the promoter’s infusion in FY24 and FY25 used for repayment/prepayment of the loans has led to some improvement in financial risk of the company, the leverage of the company remained high as on fiscal 2025 as well. Further, in fiscal 2026, despite the ~75% of the IPO proceeds (Rs. 1,192.5 crores out of Rs. 1,590 crores) being used for repay/prepayment of debt, the debt to CFO is expected to remain in the range of 3.4-3.5 times. However, the debt to CFO is expected to improve to 2.4- 2.7 times FY 2027 and FY2028 as the company continues deleveraging and collections pick up. 

- **Concentration risk and Susceptibility to cyclicality in the real estate sector:** Exposure to risks and cyclicality inherent in the real estate sector may result in volatility in saleability as well as realisations and hence, cash flow. Factors such as delay in approvals resulting in delayed construction have impacted saleability of its projects in the past. Further, saleability will remain susceptible to economic cycles. In contrast, cash outflow, such as for debt servicing, is relatively fixed. The company’s dependence on the MMR real estate market is elevated (nearly 85% of collections received in fiscal 2025 were from MMR) which exposes the Group’s sales to any region-specific downturn in demand 

# **<u>Liquidity: Adequate</u>** 

KL’s overall liquidity profile remains adequate, aided by cash and cash equivalents of around Rs. 1,976 crores as on June 30, 2025. The refinancing ability for the company also remains strong with the company able to reduce interest rates through refinancing. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes the credit profile of KL will remain healthy over the medium term, driven by its established market position and improving saleability of projects. 

# **<u>Rating Sensitivity Factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors:** 

- Significant deleveraging and strengthening of the financial risk profile with Debt to CFO improving to <2.25 times on a sustained basis. 

- Sustained improvement in business risk profile backed by healthy sales and collections, along with continued wellbalanced distribution of projects in different stages 

# **Downward Factors:** 

- Weakening of the financial risk profile due to debt funded land acquisitions leading to an increase in debt/CFO to > 3.5 times on a sustained basis 

- Sharp decline in operating cash flow, triggered by slackened saleability of existing and proposed projects or substantial delays in project execution 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

KL is the flagship real estate company of the Kalpataru group of companies (the “Kalpataru Group”) which is one of the leading real estate development groups in India. The company focuses on the development of premium residential, commercial, retail, integrated townships, lifestyle gated communities and redevelopment projects primarily in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (the “MMR”) and Pune. The company has also undertaken projects in other key cities such as Hyderabad, Noida & Nagpur.
