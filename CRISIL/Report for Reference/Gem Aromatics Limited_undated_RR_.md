# **Gem Aromatics Private Limited** 

_Rating Suspended_ 

|**Total Bank Loan Facilities Rated**|**Rs.180 Million**|
|---|---|
|**Long Term Rating**|**Suspended**|
||_(Refer to Annexure 1 for Facility-wise details)_|





CRISIL has suspended its rating on the bank facilities of Gem Aromatics Private Limited (GAPL). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by GAPL with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, GAPL is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess GAPL's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key factor in its rating process as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability - a key risk factor in credit ratings' 



### **_About the Company_** 

GAPL was promoted in 1994 by Mr. Vipul Parekh and his family. It refines and blends essential oils and deals primarily in mint, clove, eucalyptus, and basil oils. The company has a fractional distillation capacity of 1200 tonnes per annum (tpa) and chilling capacity of 480 tpa at its manufacturing unit at Silvassa (Dadra and Nagar Haveli).
