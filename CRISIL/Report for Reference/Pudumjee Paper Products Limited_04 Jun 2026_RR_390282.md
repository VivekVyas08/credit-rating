### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities and fixed deposits of Pudumjee Paper Products Limited (PPPL) to ‘ **Stable** ’ from ‘Positive’ while reaffirming the rating at ‘ **Crisil A** ’. The short-term rating has been reaffirmed at **‘** Crisil A1 **’** 

The revision in outlook is driven by flattish performance in fiscal 2026, where operating income remained at Rs 808 crore, as against Rs 809 crore reported in fiscal 2025 and also lower than expectations of a growth of 5-7% earlier. Realisations had dipped by 2.29% on year as the benefit of cost reduction was passed on to customers and performance lagged expectations. For fiscal 2027, the revenues are expected to degrow by 3-5% due to impact of overall current global scenario. Operating margin too was low around 15.32% in fiscal 2026, as against 15.47% in fiscal 2025, due to overall increase in cost structure, except input cost. This fiscal, the margins are expected to be around 15%, largely due to elevated cost structure due to current global scenario. Impact on revenues and margins has led to cash accruals being at Rs.103 crore in fiscal 2026 which will be at around Rs. 93-95 crore in fiscal 2027.The rating takes comfort from strong financial risk profile and liquidity. 

The ratings continue to reflect PPPL’s established market position in the speciality paper segment, supported by extensive experience of its promoters and management team, the wide product portfolio and diverse customer base, and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition and cyclicality in the paper industry, volatility in raw material prices, and risks related to implementation of large capital expenditure (capex) plans. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of PPPL. 

### **-** **<u>Key rating drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of promoters and management team:** The five-decade-long experience of the promoters in the paper industry, their deep understanding of market dynamics, and presence of a strong second-line professional management team will continue to support the business risk profile. 

**Diverse product profile and customer base:** The product portfolio comprises several grades of glassine, opaque laminating base, kraft, tissue, greaseproof, crepe tissue, machine glazed poster and printing paper. Hence, the company caters to various end-user industries, and thus, benefits from diversified revenue streams. The company also provides customised paper, which fetches a higher margin, and acts as a differentiator in the competitive paper industry.  This has led to increase in revenue to Rs 808 crore in fiscal 2026, from Rs 437 crore in fiscal 2021, and the top five customers have contributed to around 45-50% of revenue. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth was healthy at Rs 604.91 crore as on March 31, 2026. Gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratios were low at 0.04 time and 0.41 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2026, as against 

0.02 time and 0.35 time, respectively, a year before. Debt protection metrics were robust, with interest coverage ratio of around 40 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio of 4.5 times for fiscal 2026, as against 42 times and 11 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025.  Despite the debt-funded capex plan, to be undertaken over the next couple of years towards the solar battery project, the financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable. **<u>Key rating drivers - Weaknesses</u> Exposure to intense competition:** Imports cater 50–60% of domestic demand for speciality paper. PPPL competes with other large domestic players in the writing and printing paper, and hygiene and tissue paper segments. Many organised players have also enhanced their capacities. Intense competition, along with the cyclical nature of the paper industry, may continue to constrain scalability, pricing power and profitability. 

**Susceptibility to volatile raw material prices:** Raw material prices account for around 60% of the cost of sales. The company manufactures paper from imported wood pulp as well as wastepaper, whose prices tend to fluctuate sharply. This risk is mitigated by moderate inventory and pass-through mechanisms with certain time lag. Operating margin has been steady at 15.32–15.47% in the two fiscals ended March 31, 2026. Sustenance of the operating margin at healthy levels will be key monitorable. 

**Exposure to risk related to timely implementation of large capex plans:** The company plans to increase its speciality paper manufacturing capacity at Mahad over the next five years. Capex would entail tentative investment of Rs 1,350 crore, and the mode of financing is yet to be decided. Though it would be mainly funded through internal accrual and available liquidity, and will be spread over 5 years, any increase in debt-funded capex will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity remains adequate, driven by healthy cash accrual, minimal bank limit utilisation and surplus funds maintained in the form of bank balances and mutual fund investments. Expected net cash accrual of over Rs 93 crore per fiscal should more than suffice to cover the yearly debt obligation of Rs 3–6 crore over the medium term. Cash credit limit of Rs 40 crore was utilised negligibly, at 3% on an average during the 12 months through March 2026. Unencumbered cash and bank balance and liquid investments were around Rs 130 crore as on March 31, 2026, and shall continue to support financial flexibility. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

PPPL’s business risk profile will continue to benefit from its established market position in the speciality paper segment and strong relationship with customers. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained revenue growth, driven by higher volume, and operating margin of over 16%, leading to higher net cash accrual on a sustained basis 

Better working capital management and sustenance of healthy financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors:** 

Steep decline in revenue and/or operating margin, resulting in net cash accrual below Rs 75 crore 

Increase in working capital requirement, larger-than-expected, debt-funded capex or acquisition, or huge dividend payout, weakening financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the company</u>** 

PPPL was set up in January 2015, to take over the paper manufacturing division of Pudumjee Pulp & Paper Mills Ltd, Pudumjee Industries Ltd and Pudumjee Hygiene Products Ltd. The company manufactures a variety of speciality, printing and tissue paper at its plant in Pune, which has capacity of 72,000 tonne per annum. Mr Arun Kumar Jatia is the promoter of the company. The company is listed on both Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange of India.
