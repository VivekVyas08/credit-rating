### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable’ rating on the long-term bank facilities and non-convertible debentures of R Systems International Limited (R Systems). 

The rating continues to reflect the company’s comfortable business risk profile, as reflected in its healthy operating performance and steady improvement in the operating margin, and its healthy financial risk profile backed by low debt profile and healthy cash surplus. The ratings also factor in strong articulation of support from Blackstone Inc. (Blackstone; rated A+ by S&P Global Ratings), which through its fund, BCP Asia II Topco II Pte. Ltd., Singapore, holds majority stake of 51.85% in R Systems as on May 31, 2026. Blackstone has also supported the operations of R Systems with new customer additions from its portfolio, and this is expected to continue over the medium term, diversifying and enhancing R System’s customer base. Based on its track record, Blackstone is expected to provide need based timely financial support to R Systems, as it pursues organic and inorganic expansion plans. 

Revenues stood at Rs. 1,958 crore in 2025 registering around 12% growth over previous year, in line with Crisil Ratings’ expectations, as a result of addition of Novigo and favourable currency movements. Revenues in the first quarter of 2026 stood at Rs 575 crore which grew at 29% on year and by 4% on a quarter basis supported by revenues from Novigo and continued currency tailwinds. Crisil Ratings expects the revenue growth in 2026 is expected to remain at healthy double digits led by full year benefits of the Novigo acquisition acquired in November 2025. Ex-off inorganic growth, the core (constant currency) revenue growth is expected in low single digits due to the prolonged geopolitical uncertainties besides the pressures of AI-led disruption. Strategically, the company continues to advance its AI-first approach through business partnerships and prudent inorganic acquisitions, which is expected to support its evolving business risk profile. Operating margin improved to 15.8% in 2025 as against 14.8% in the corresponding period of the previous year. Further, margins in the first quarter of 2026 improved to 17.5% driven primarily by currency tailwinds. Crisil Ratings believes, operating margins in current year shall remain healthy at similar levels amid continued currency tailwinds besides prudent cost management and better utilisation rates. 

As on December 31, 2025, adjusted debt remained moderate at Rs 410 crore comprising mainly of Rs 275 crore non-convertible debentures (NCD) for the Novigo acquisition, Rs 95 crore lease liabilities and remaining short term borrowings. Against this, the adjusted networth stood healthy at Rs 1,196 crore leading to leverage metrices and debt protection metrices to remain at healthy levels. The company generates steady cash accruals in the range of ~ Rs 180-200 crore (net of dividend). R Systems has specialised offerings in the product engineering, analytics and design services with healthy growth across the verticals supported by a healthy operating margin profile. To sustain this trend going forward, the company remains on the lookout for inorganic opportunities; the size of the remain is expected to remain modest. 

The strengths are partially offset by exposure to its relatively moderate scale of operations and geographical concentration risk amid the exposure to industry specific challenges like macroeconomic development and policies in key operating markets and AI led disruptions. Any change in the expectation of support from Blackstone will be a key rating sensitivity factor. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>**

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Comfortable business risk profile, supported by industry and customer diversity:** 

The company has over 250 customers across verticals such as telecom, technology, healthcare and life science, finance and insurance, and retail and e-commerce. Also, the top 10 clients contributed ~24% of revenues in the first three months of 2026, compared with 25% in 2025. Since Blackstone’s investment, R Systems have added 15 new customers which are portfolio companies of Blackstone demonstrating the customer synergies accessible. Revenue growth in 2026 is expected to remain above the industry average supported by full year addition of Novigo acquisition, and favourable currency movement. Operating margins for 2026 are expected to remain healthy at similar levels driven by higher utilization rates and prudent cost management. The margins are lower as compared to their counterparts in the IT Industry primarily on account of the company being present in smaller ticket sizes catering to start-ups. 

# **Healthy financial risk profile, bolstered by support from Blackstone:** 

The financial risk profile remains strong with cash accruals expected to be steady at Rs. 180-200 crore per annum (net of dividend) and efficient working capital management. Adjusted net worth was Rs 1,196 crore as on December 31, 2025, although accretion to reserves is limited due to high dividend payouts (over 50-80% of PAT). The company had an adequate liquid surplus of Rs 314 crore as of December 31, 2025, and moderate annual maintenance capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 30-40 crore. Debt protection metrics have been strong as indicated by interest coverage of ~25 times and net cash accrual to total debt ratio of 2 times for 2025, and debt of around Rs 410 crore as of December 31, 2025. 

Blackstone is a global investment firm and an alternative asset management company with assets under management (AUM) of around $1 trillion. It was founded in 1985 as a mergers and acquisitions firm and has since expanded into various investment strategies, including private equity, real estate, credit, and hedge funds. It has experience in investing in a diversified set of businesses across the globe, and in India including Embassy group, Sona Comstar, Mindspace, VFS Global, Mphasis Ltd among others. Crisil Ratings expects Blackstone to provide necessary need based financial support to ensure R Systems maintains financial prudence, as it pursues its organic and inorganic growth plans, and also necessary and timely liquidity support in the event of financial distress. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **High geographical concentration in revenue:** 

Over 65% of the total turnover accrued from North America and 10% from Europe in first three months of 2026. These markets (combined) have accounted for more than 70% of the revenue since 2006. R Systems will remain susceptible to economic downturns or policy changes in these regions. 

# **Exposure to industry specific challenges:** 

The sector remains highly competitive among domestic players and continued expansion of multinational corporations’ offshore operations. The sector is also facing structural challenges, including rapid adoption of AI and automation, pricing pressures, and a shift towards outcome-based, cost-efficient client engagements. AI-led changes are reshaping the workforce, necessitating continuous reskilling and potentially displacing low-value roles. Revenue and profitability also remains exposed to forex fluctuations due to high dependence on international revenues, while global macroeconomic uncertainties, weak technology spending, and protectionist measures continue to weigh on growth. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash surplus was adequate at Rs 314 crore as of December 31, 2025, and is expected to remain healthy barring any cashfunded acquisitions. The fund-based limit of Rs 145 crore was modestly utilised during the twelve months ended May 2026. Expected cash accrual of Rs 180-200 crore should comfortably cover debt obligations in 2026. R systems is also expected to receive the required need based liquidity and funding support, from Blackstone’s healthy credit profile, in the event of exigencies. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

R Systems will continue to benefit from its healthy business and financial risk profiles, supported by diversity in clientele and product offerings, healthy cash accrual and modest debt. Any sizeable acquisition is also expected to be prudently funded, ensuring currently healthy financial risk profile is not materially impacted. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained healthy revenue growth, and improving operating margins above 17% levels, benefitting cash generation Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile, while pursuing organic and inorganic expansion opportunities 

# **Downward factors** 

Any change in support philosophy from Blackstone 

Weakening operating performance due to factors such as AI-led disruption, leading to continued decline in revenue or operating margins 

Large, debt-funded capex or continued acquisitions, leading to deterioration in debt metrics; for instance, long term net debt (excluding lease liabilities) to Ebitda of over 0.6-0.7 times on sustained basis 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

R Systems was incorporated as a private limited company in 1993 by Mr Satinder Singh Rekhi and was reconstituted as a public limited company in April 2000 and came up with its initial public offering in 2006. As on May 31, 2026, Blackstone Capital Partners through its fund BCP Asia II Topco II Pte. Ltd., Singapore, held 51.85% of the company. R Systems is a leading provider of technology, artificial intelligence, analytics and knowledge services. It partners with customers to enable or elevate their digital transformation with diversified digital offerings, including product engineering, cloud enablement, quality assurance testing, and digital platforms and solutions. The company offers services and solutions across telecom, technology, healthcare and life science, finance and insurance, and retail and e-commerce. It has 22 development and service centers to serve customers in North America, Europe and the Far East. Around 80% of revenue comes from North America and Europe. 

During the first three months of 2026, the company reported revenue of Rs 575 crore (Rs 442 crore in previous corresponding period) and net profit of Rs 65 crore (Rs 39 crore in previous corresponding period).
