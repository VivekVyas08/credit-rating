### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil B+/Stable/Crisil A4’** ratings to the bank facilities of Kirloskar Electric Company Limited (KECL). 

The ratings reflect susceptibility of the operating margin to volatility in raw material prices and weak financial risk profile. These weaknesses are partially offset by the group's long track record of operations, supported by extensive experience of the promoters in the electrical equipment industry, and diversified product profile and end-user industry base. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of KECL along with its wholly owned subsidiaries, i.e., Luxquisite Parkland Private Limited, SLPKG Estate Holdings Private Limited, SKG Terra Promenade Private Limited and KELBUZZ Trading Private Limited; step-down subsidiary, Kirsons B V, and an associate, Kirloskar (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd. This is because all these entities, collectively referred to as the Kirloskar Electric group, operate in the same industry and have operational and financial linkages. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u> Weaknesses:** 

**Susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in raw material prices:** Cost of production and profit margin are heavily dependent on the cost of raw materials (iron, steel, copper, etc.), which are highly volatile and driven by global price fluctuations. Thus, operating performance will remain susceptible to volatility in input cost. Presence of escalation clauses in contracts of over three months and backward integration partially mitigate the risk. 

**Weak financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile has been constrained, marked by negative networth (adjusted for revaluation reserves) during fiscals 2022-2024), negative gearing and weak debt protection metrics due to high interest rates, though it is showing an improvement in the current fiscal. Thus, ramp-up in operations would be key for improvement in the financial risk profile and remain monitorable over the medium term. Proceeds from the sale of property will aid the financial risk profile in the near term and remain a key rating sensitivity factor. 

### **Strengths:** 

**Long track record of operations supported by extensive industry experience of the promoters:** KECL has been involved in the business of electrical equipment, motors and transformers since 1946. It was promoted by Mr Ravindra L Kirloskar, youngest son of Mr Laxmanrao K Kirloskar, the founder of the Kirloskar Group of Companies. The Kirloskar Electric group is headed by Mr Vijay R Kirloskar (chairman and whole-time director), having an experience of more than three decades in the electrical equipment industry. Expertise of the promoters, their strong understanding of market dynamics and healthy relations with customers and suppliers should continue to support the business. 

**Diversified product profile and end user industry base:** The Kirloskar Electric group is an established player in the electrical equipment industry and has longstanding relationships with customers and suppliers. Its product basket includes alternating current (AC) motors, AC generators, direct current (DC) motors, transformers, switchgear, and 

electronics equipment. The group has different product verticals such as transformers and distribution, large machines, low voltage machines and power generation. 

Furthermore, its products cater to a diversified end-user industry base, which includes power generation, transmission and distribution, transportation, renewable energy, sugar, steel, cement and allied industries. Well-diversified product offering and end-user industry base insulate the business from risk of slowdown in any particular industry and aid steady growth. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Stretched</u>** 

Bank limit utilisation was 95.84% on average for the past 12 months through May 2025. In the absence of any yearly maturing debt over the medium term, cash accrual -- expected at Rs 17-22 crore per annum -- will act as a cushion to liquidity. The current ratio stood low at 0.42 time on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believe the Kirloskar Electric group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and their established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained improvement in scale of operation and sustenance of operating margin over 4%, leading to higher cash accruals 

- Improvement in the financial risk profile, with improved networth and interest coverage ratio, backed by the sale of nonoperational assets or equity infusion 

Improvement in liquidity profile, with higher cushion in bank lines 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue or operating profitability dropping below 2%, leading to lower net cash accrual 

- Large debt-funded capital expenditure or substantial increase in working capital requirements, thus weakening the liquidity and financial profile

Engaged in the manufacturing of various electrical equipments such as AC motors, AC generators, DC motors, alternators, diesel generators sets, transformers, switchgear and other electronics equipments.
