### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Gulshan Polyols Ltd (GPL) to **‘Negative’** from ‘Stable’ and reaffirmed the rating at **‘Crisil A’** . Crisil Ratings has also reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A1’ rating on the short-term bank facilities of the company. 

The revision in outlook reflects Crisil Rating’s belief that the company’s financial risk profile, particularly its liquidity, shall weaken over the medium term in case of lower-than-expected operating profitability, and thus, steady improvement in same will remain a key monitorable. During fiscal 2025, the company witnessed moderation in operating profitability at ~5% as against Crisil Rating’s earlier expectation of 7-7.5%, thus leading to lower cash accruals. Resultantly, the net cash accrual to repayment ratio stood moderate at an estimated 1.1 times for the fiscal. While operating profitability is likely to rise to 7-8% in the ongoing fiscal, amidst stabilization of operations in Assam plant and moderation in raw material prices, any volatility in the same is likely to impact the net cash accruals and thus the liquidity, as maturing repayment continues to remain sizeable at Rs 55-60 crore per annum. The overall business risk profile, however, was partly aided by the company’s improved scalability during fiscal 2025 which is likely to witness incremental revenue growth of 6-8% during fiscal 2026 as well. Revenue for fiscal 2025 was estimated at approximately Rs 2000 crore. 

The ratings also factor in moderation in debt protection metrics, particularly interest coverage ratio, which stood at 2.75 times for fiscal 2025 (5.81 times in fiscal 2024). While with the expected improvement in profitability, the interest coverage shall improve to 4.0-4.2 times over the medium term, the same shall remain lower than Crisil Rating’s earlier expectations of over 7-8 times. Though net worth and hence the capital structure remain comfortable and aids the financial risk profile, sustained improvement in debt protection metrics, which thereby strengthens the overall financial risk profile, will remain a key monitorable. 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position of GPL and the extensive experience of its promoters in the agricultural products industry. The ratings also factor in the company’s reputed clientele and diversified product mix, and healthy capital structure. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility of the operating margin to volatility in raw material prices and modest debt protection metrics. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach:</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of GPL. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Established market position, supported by the extensive experience of the promoter** : The four-decade-long experience of the promoter in the establishing products industry, his strong understanding of market dynamics and healthy business relationships helped the company build a diversified customer base across geographies as well as establish its presence in the domestic market. The diverse product portfolio, comprising starch, sugar, sorbitol, ethanol and calcium carbide among others, mitigates the risk of downturn in any specific segment. Revenue reported a compound annual growth rate of 23% for the three fiscals through 2025, driven by ramp up of operations in the ethanol segment, which contributed Rs 1,187 crore in fiscal 2025 (an on-year growth of 141%). With increase in capacity 

utilization in the ethanol segment and higher revenue from other segments, backed by volumetric growth, operating income is projected at Rs 2,100-2,200 crore in fiscal 2026. 

**Healthy capital structure:** The capital structure has been comfortable, as reflected by gearing ratio of 0.49 times as on March 31, 2025. The same is backed by healthy accretion to reserves, with a net worth of Rs.614 crores in fiscal 2025. In the ongoing fiscal, with no major debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) and accretion to reserves, the capital structure is expected to remain comfortable with gearing expected at 0.40-0.45 times as on March 31, 2026, providing headroom to take additional debt for business requirement. 

# **Weaknesses:** 

**Susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in raw material prices:** The operating margin was lower than expected for fiscal 2025, at 5%. The operating margin fell largely on account of increased raw material prices, both maize and rice, amid market volatility and restricted supply from Food Corporation of India (FCI). Moreover, Assam plant started commercial production in fiscal 2025, wherein, with low-capacity utilization during the initial period, the fixed cost absorption remained low, which further impacted the operating profitability of the company. Going forward, benefits of stabilization of operations in Assam plant along with moderation in raw material prices seen recently, the operating margin is expected to improve to ~7-7.5% in fiscal 2026. However, its sustained improvement will remain a key monitorable. 

**Modest debt protection metrics:** Low operating profitability and increased dependence on working capital limits have resulted in moderate debt protection indicators especially interest coverage, which stood at 2.76 times in fiscal 2025 (5.81 times a year ago). Crisil believes that the company’s reliance on external debt is expected to increase, amidst business growth, however, an expected improvement in operating profitability to 7-7.5%, shall improve the interest coverage to 4-4.2% in fiscal 2026. Sustained improvement in debt protection metrics, thereby strengthening the overall financial risk profile, will remain a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Bank limit utilization is moderate around 78.05 percent for the past twelve months ended May 2025. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 100-110 crore which shall remain sufficient against repayment of Rs. 55-60 crores and the surplus will cushion the liquidity of the group. The current ratio was moderate at 1.16 times as on March 31, 2025. The promoters are likely to extend support in the form of equity and unsecured loans to meet the working capital requirement and debt obligation. 

# **<u>Outlook: Negative</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes that the business risk profile and liquidity profile of the company may weaken, in case of any further decline in the operating margins of GPL. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

- Sustained improvement in operating income supported by volumetric growth along with sustenance of operating margin in range of 9-10%, leading to higher-than-expected net cash accruals of the company. 

- Improvement in financial risk profile amid efficient working capital management, thus leading to improvement in debt protection metrics. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in operating income or operating margins falling below 7% leading to lower-than-expected net cash accruals. Stretch in working capital cycle or any large debt funded capex adversely affecting the financial risk profile, particularly liquidity profile. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1981, GPL is a multi-location, multi-product manufacturing company, with presence in more than 35 countries across three continents. It operates in three key segments: grain processing, ethanol (bio-fuel)/distillery, and mineral processing. GPL is among the market leaders in manufacturing sorbitol, precipitated calcium carbonate and wet ground calcium carbonate. The company plans to expand its presence in the distillery segment and has undertaken significant capex to set up capacity to manufacture ethanol. GPL caters to a wide range of industries and niche markets in core sectors such as pharmaceuticals, personal care products, footwear, tyres, rubber and plastics, paints, alcohol, valueadded paper, agrochemicals and food and agricultural products. Its large clients include Hindustan Unilever Ltd, Dabur, Asian Paints and ITC.
