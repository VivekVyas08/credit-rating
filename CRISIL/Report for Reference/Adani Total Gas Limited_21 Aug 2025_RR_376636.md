### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **'Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+** ' ratings to the bank facilities of Adani Total Gas Ltd (ATGL). 

The ratings reflect the large scale of operations, strong financial risk profile, and established market position of the company in the city gas distribution (CGD) industry in India. 

ATGL is one of the largest CGD players in the country with total gas sales volume of around 1,000 million metric standard cubic meter (mmscm) in fiscal 2025. The company is one of the players to report the highest operating margin, led by scale, operational efficiency and strong track record of supply risk management. Crisil Ratings expects continued growth in ATGL's total gas volume over the medium term, with increasing penetration in newer geographic areas (GAs) and continued competitiveness of CGD against alternatives, especially in the compressed natural gas (CNG) segment. Further, margin per scm are expected to remain range bound around levels reported in fiscal 2025, due to pricing power in existing GAs, low gas supply risk and continued operational efficiency. 

The financial risk profile is strong, marked by healthy operating cash accrual, comfortable debt protection metrics and adequate liquidity. Interest coverage ratio was around 11 times, and the debt-to-earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and tax (Ebitda) ratio was 1.54 times as on March 25. The ratios could moderate due to planned yearly capex of Rs 1,0001,500 crore, to be undertaken over fiscals 2026 and 2027, mainly towards new GAs. Nevertheless, credit metrics are expected to remain strong over the medium term, as the capex will be funded by a mix of healthy internal accruals (expected to be Rs 700–900 crore per fiscal) and debt. Also, the company has entered into ECB program of USD 375 Mn in September 2024 for future capex, of which USD 315 Mn has already been sanctioned (~ USD 255 Mn being unutilized as on March 2025) , which mitigates the funding risk. This, along with proven execution track record of ATGL, should ensure timely completion of capex. Healthy volume growth and sustenance of margin and capital structure remain key rating monitorables. 

Crisil Ratings expects that ATGL will maintain adequate liquidity in the form of unencumbered cash and bank balances, with cash to debt servicing requirement ratio of over one time throughout. Further, in case of any additional requirement - though unlikely - presence of strong sponsors and their strong commitment towards the business will provide comfort over the medium term. 

However, operations will remain susceptible to volatility in natural gas prices, and any regulatory changes in the allocation of domestic gas for CGD entities will be a key rating sensitivity factor. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has fully combined the business and financial risk profiles of ATGL and its subsidiaries, along with moderate consolidation of its joint venture (JV) to arrive at the ratings.

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u> Strengths:** 

**Established market position as one of the largest CGD players in India:** ATGL has a strong market presence in 34 districts across thirteen states including Gujarat, Rajasthan, Haryana and Maharashtra. It has 4 matured GAs, which fetched ~70% of total sales volume in fiscal 2025. Sales volume from the 9<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> round of GAs could increase in the near to medium term, on account of enhanced infrastructure in those GAs. It has around 15 GAs in 9<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> rounds and rest from the 11<sup>th</sup> round. 

New GAs should help the company achieve geographical diversification and expand its scale of operations. The user base comprised around 9.9 Lakhs domestic households and 650 CNG stations as on June 30, 2025, providing strong revenue diversity. Total annual gas sale was ~1000 mmscm in fiscal 2025. Overall gas volume is expected to grow 1015% during fiscal 2026 with major portion of incremental volume coming from newer GAs. 

Further, ATGL is largely in line with its achievement of CNG and steel InKm targets for the 9th and 10th rounds, on prorata basis. For 11<sup>th</sup> round GAs, the MWP period for many of them is expected to shift in the absence of a trunk pipeline. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile is driven by healthy cash accrual and debt protection metrics  and sufficient liquidity. The company has reported cash accrual of over Rs 800 crore in fiscal 2025, as against Rs 500 crore in fiscal 2021. Interest coverage ratio has remained above 9 times over the past few fiscals, while gearing was less than one time. Debt protection metrics could witness moderation with higher reliance on debt to fund capex in near to medium term. However, the leverage ratio is expected to remain healthy below 3 times and liquidity position at 1 time. 

- **Favourable long-term demand prospects, backed by competitive advantage of CGD segments:** ATGL has a healthy mix of CNG and piped natural gas (PNG) sales, in the ratio of 67:33 in fiscal 2025. Gas sales volume grew 15% year-on-year to around 1,000 mmscm in fiscal 2025, from 662 mmscm in fiscal 2024, across both segments, due to the addition of CNG stations and customers in new GAs. The Ahmedabad GA was the major contributor, forming ~38% of the sales volume in fiscal 2025. 

- **Presence of strong sponsors:** ATGL is promoted by the Adani group and TotalEnergies SE (rated ‘A+’ by S&P). While the former has strong experience in executing large-scale projects, the latter is one of the leading liquefied natural gas (LNG) players in the world. ATGL will benefit from efficient gas sourcing tie-ups. Any change in sponsor linkages with ATGL will be a key monitorable. 

# **Weakness:** 

- **Project risk associated with pending MWP capex in new GAs, albeit mitigated by proven track record and healthy cash accruals of the company to fund the capex requirements** : The Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB) granted ATGL 29 new GAs in the 9<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> CGD bid rounds concluded in August 2018, March 2019 and December 2021, respectively. The company plans to incur huge capex of around Rs 8,500 crore over the next five years, starting fiscal 2026, towards these GAs. Cumulative capex over fiscals 2026-2028 be in the range of Rs 4,000-4,500 crore. Significant scale of capex exposes the company to project execution risk, although its track record and progress achieved in several GAs in the 9<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> rounds mitigate the impact to some extent. Any significant delay or under-achievement of the minimum work programme (MWP) target in GAs, could attract major penalties and/or encashment of the performance guarantees submitted by ATGL towards the new GAs and this will be a key rating sensitivity factor. Crisil Ratings also factors in the low financing risk, as debt requirement has already been tied up. Nevertheless, the company has debt funding and undrawn limits available to fund capex plans over the near term to medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

ATGL has adequate liquidity as indicated by cash flow from operations of around Rs 800 crore in fiscal 2025 and free cash of more than Rs 450 crore as on March 31, 2025 (including current investments). Cash flow from operations is expected to be in the range of Rs 800-900 crore in fiscals 2026 and 2027, as against debt obligation of Rs 166 crore in Fiscal 2026 and Rs.562 crore in fiscal 2027. Further the company also has unutilised fund-based limit of Rs 1,460 crore. Capex of around Rs 1,000 crore is planned for fiscal 2026 and around Rs 1400-1500 crore in fiscal 2027. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings expects steady improvmeent in the operating performance of ATGL, backed by healthy volume growth, stable realisations and strong financial risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Substantial rise in scale of operations and higher-than-expected operating profitability (current Ebitda of around Rs 10 per scm) supporting healthy free cash flow (post capex) and material improvement in capital structure against Crisil Ratings’ expected levels 

Timely achievement of MWP target with no penalty or cost overrun 

# **Downward factors:** 

- Materially lower-than-expected operating cash accrual on a sustained basis, on account of weaker volume growth or reduced operating margin 

- Higher-than-expected debt funded capex, leading to material weakening of credit metrics and liquidity, with net leverage sustaining over 2.5-3 times 

Delay in achievement of MWP target leading to penalty implications or lower-than-expected returns 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

ATGL, incorporated in 2005, is engaged in the CGD business, which involves marketing and distribution of natural gas (piped and compressed).  Adani Group and TotalEnergies each hold 37.40% stake in the company and the rest is with the public. At present, ATGL is one of the largest CGD players in India, present in 34 GAs, including 14 GAs won under the 11<sup>th</sup> round. ATGL has also entered into a 50:50 JV with Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. IOAGPL is engaged in the implementation of CGD network in several other GAs across India and is present in 19 GAs across the country
