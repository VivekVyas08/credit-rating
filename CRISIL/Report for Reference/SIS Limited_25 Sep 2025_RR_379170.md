### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the existing non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bank facilities of SIS Ltd (a part of SIS group) at ‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+’. Crisil Ratings has also **withdrawn** its rating on Rs.190 crore term loan facility as same has been repaid and we have received no dues certificate from lenders and withdrawal request from the company. This is in line with Crisil Ratings’ withdrawal policy. 

The ratings continue to reflect the healthy and sustained business risk profile of SIS and its subsidiaries, supported by its leadership position in the Indian and certain overseas security services markets, diversified service offerings across different end-user industries and a large and sticky customer base. The ratings are further supported by sustenance of healthy operating efficiencies marked by stable operating margins, strong manpower base, training infrastructure, and longstanding experience of the promoters in the security services industry. 

The ratings also factor in the group's comfortable financial risk profile, backed by healthy debt protection metrics and capital structure despite debt-funded acquisitions undertaken in the past. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition in a fragmented industry and working capital intensive operations. 

Consolidated revenues (including cash logistics business) grew 8% YoY in FY25 to Rs. 13,903 crore, driven by healthy growth in the India security and facility management businesses with new orders bagged across sectors and escalation clauses built into the contracts and steady performance in cash logistics. Going forward, SIS Cash will no longer be consolidated as company is planning to go for an IPO and post listing SIS will cease to remain a majority shareholder . Operating margin remained rangebound between at 5.2% in fiscal 2025 as against 5.3% in fiscal 24. Overall operating margin should sustain at 4.5-5% over the medium term. 

Company has further announced acquisition of APS Group, wherein SIS will acquire 100% stake in the company in two tranches (51% upfront in FY26 at Rs 73.4 cr and funded from internal accruals and remaining 49% by FY29). This acquisition is expected to expand SIS’s customer base across banking, logistics, education, and outsourcing, add ~37,000 employees, and enhance diversification across end-user industries in India. 

The financial risk profile is aided by healthy operating performance and steady debt despite the announced acquisition. Gross debt to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) improved to 2.31 times in fiscal 2025 (fiscal 2024: 2.49 times) while interest coverage stood stable at 4.36 times (fiscal 2024: 4.35 times). Debt protection metrices are likely to improve further with sustained growth in absolute profitability and no major debt addition. The company plans to incur moderate capital expenditure (capex) of around Rs. 100-120 Crore over the medium term majorly funded through internal accruals. However, any major debt-funded capex/acquisition will remain a key rating sensitivity factor.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position and strong promoter experience:** SIS is India’s largest security services provider (40% of revenue) and second-largest cash management player, besides being the largest in Australia (~21% share). Consolidated revenue (including SIS Cash) rose to Rs. 13,903 crore in FY25 (Rs. 12,895 crore in FY24) majorly driven by new order wins across sectors. Going forward, revenues (excluding SIS cash) are expected to grow at  8-9% per annum. Further,  the proposed acquisition of APS Group (51% in FY26 and 49% by FY29) will enhance domestic presence, adding ~37,000 employees and 1,000+ customers. Promoters bring over three decades of experience and strong client relationships. Revenue mix is diversified with security services (~79%), facility management (~16%), and cash logistics (~5%). 

Indian security operations grew 8% in FY25 to Rs. 5,531 crore, with margins at 5.5% (5.7% in FY24), supported by wins in BFSI, retail, mining, and education. International revenues rose 7% to Rs. 3,310 crore, driven by new contracts in Australia worth AUD 180 million across defense, airports, and transport. Margins for international security business remain modest (~3.7%) due to legacy low-margin contracts, though renegotiations are underway; improvement is expected from FY27. 

The group’s facility management business revenues rose 7.4% in FY25 to Rs. 2,229 crore (Rs. 2,075 crore in FY24), with margins improving to 4.5% from 4.1%, aided by contract rationalization, integrated solutions, and ESG-led demand. 

Cash logistics revenues grew 13% to Rs. 714 crore in FY25, with margins at 17.1% (16.7% in FY24). The segment is expected to see steady growth, supported by healthy operating margins. 

The group’s offerings, spanning security, facility management, and cash logistics , are essential in nature, with mediumterm demand supported by rising focus on security, hygiene, and bundled solutions 

**Steady operating efficiency:** Operating margin has consistently remained in line with the industry average, at 5-5.3% over fiscals 2015 to 2025, driven by strong operating leverage and branch performance, better productivity through deployment of technology for effective real-time monitoring, improved client mix and sale of solutions. The SIS group can recruit, train and deploy around 50% of its manpower requirement, through its 22 in-house training academies. Return on capital employed (RoCE) ratio has also been over 10% in the five fiscals through March 2025. Further, despite a high client base, receivables have been 50-55 days over the five fiscals through March 2025 and are likely to be rangebound going forward too. 

Overall margin is expected to remain steady at 4.5-5% in the medium term. In the first quarter of fiscal 2026 the company recorded operating profit before depreciation, interest and taxes (OPBDIT) of ~Rs 152 crore at a margin of 4.3%. 

- **Healthy financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile remains strong, supported by healthy net worth of ~Rs 1,996 crore as on March 31, 2025 and it is likely to improve further in fiscal 2026, aided by steady profitability and cash accruals. However, during fiscal 2025, the company recognized a goodwill impairment of Rs. 305.8 crore in Q4 FY25, which weighted on profitability.  Stable working capital management have helped the group maintain a healthy financial risk profile over the last few years. Gross debt/EBITDA stood at ~2.3x in FY25, improving from ~2.5x in FY24, with net debt/EBITDA at ~0.3x, aided by better margin and no significant increase in debt. With improvement in scale and operating margin, gross debt to ebdita ratio is expected to improve further going forward. Despite cumulative acquisitions of ~Rs. 800 crore in the last decade, SIS has maintained prudent capital structure, with gearing under control and comfortable coverage metrics (interest coverage >4x). Further, the group has strong liquidity with unencumbered cash balance of Rs 1,231 cr as on 31 March 2025. The expected healthy cash accruals, undrawn working capital limits and cash balances will be sufficient to meet the debt obligations, incremental working capital requirements, capex and acquisition. Any large debt funded capex/acquisition will remain key monitorable. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

- **Moderate working capital requirement:** The security services industry is inherently working capital intensive. SIS had receivable days of ~55 as on March 31, 2025, broadly in line with historical levels. While receivables have been contained through stronger collection practices, the business continues to require high deployment of manpower and upfront funding of wages. Hence, incremental increases in receivables remain a key monitorable. 

**Exposure to intense competition:** The Indian security services sector is highly fragmented, with presence of a large number of small and unorganised players offering services at low cost and hold around 65% of the domestic market. This exerts pricing pressure and constrains scalability. Further, dependence on the low margin manpower-intensive 

guarding segment (~80–84% of costs) continues to weigh on margins. Regulatory developments such as the Private Security Agencies Regulation Act, minimum wage revisions, and labour reforms remain monitorable as same can have an impact on profitability 

**Weak but improving performance of group companies:** Many group entities such as SIS Alarms and Monitoring and Tech SIS are in early stage of operations and may need funding support via equity, loans or guarantees, till they scale up and become independent. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

The group has strong liquidity with unencumbered cash and bank balance of Rs 1,231 crore as on March 31, 2025.  SIS (Standalone) has bank limits sanctioned of Rs. 869 which remain utilized at an average of 83% over the past 12months ending July 2025. Annual cash accrual of ~ over Rs 500 crore will be sufficient to meet the debt obligations and capital expenditure requirements of Rs 150-200 crore per annum, incremental working capital requirements and capex over the medium term. The recently announced acquisition funding will also be met majorly from the internal accruals **.** 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes SIS will continue to benefit from its established market position, efficient operating efficiency and healthy financial risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

- Sustained increase in scale of operations supported by increase in market share while maintaining operating margins at over 4.5-5% 

- Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile supported by strong cash generation; leading to Debt to Ebitda ratio below 2.0 times on a sustained basis. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Substantial decline in operating performance, including due to intense competition, or loss of large customers Deterioration in the credit metrics or liquidity surplus led by large capex/acquisitions/dividends or elongation of working capital cycle; for instance, gross debt/EBITDA weakening to more than 2.75 times on a sustained basis 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Established in 1974, by Mr Ravindra Kishore Sinha, SIS has been providing security services since inception, mainly manned guarding. It has grown over the years through organic as well as inorganic routes and operates in Australia (through MSS after acquisition of Chubbs Security in 2008), New Zealand (through P4G) and Singapore (through Henderson), apart from India. The SIS group has a pan-India presence through 293 branch offices, 50 regional offices and 29 training academies. It has a trained workforce of 2,90,736 employees. The company got listed on the National Stock Exchange and Bombay Stock Exchange effective August 10, 2017. 

Besides security solutions, the group also provides facility management and cash logistics services. SMC Integrated Facility Management Solutions Ltd (previously known as Service Master Clean Ltd) ('Crisil A/Stable/Crisil A1'), wholly owned subsidiary of SIS India provides cleaning services. Some of the other companies in the segment rated by Crisil Ratings are Dusters Total Solutions Services Pvt Ltd ('Crisil A/Stable/Crisil A1') and Tech SIS ('Crisil BBB+/Stable'). The SIS group also offers cash management services through its joint venture, SIS Cash Services Pvt Ltd ('Crisil A+/Stable/Crisil A1') and its subsidiary SIS Prosegur Holdings Pvt Ltd ('Crisil A/Stable/Crisil A1').  SIS Cash Services Limited has also filed for IPO in March 2025. 

As on June 30, 2025, the promoters and promoter group had a shareholding of 72.13%, while balance 27.87% was held by public.
