### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Clean Science and Technology Ltd (CSTL). 

The ratings continue to reflect the strong market position of CSTL in the speciality chemicals industry, its large product portfolio and customer base, and experienced management. The ratings also factor in the healthy operating margin and strong financial risk profile of the company, marked by low leverage and adequate debt protection metrics and strong liquidity. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and the working capital intensive operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of CSTL and its four wholly owned subsidiaries, which are strategically important to, and have a significant degree of operational integration, with CSTL.

### **<u>Key rating drivers & detailed description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Strong market position in niche product segments:** The two-and-half-decade-long experience of the promoters, their technical knowledge and focus on inventing cost-efficient processes and clean technology have helped the company establish a strong market position in the specialty chemicals industry. Over the years, the company has undertaken capacity expansion and put in place efficient processes, to cater to reputed clients. Addition of new product categories have further helped diversify the product basket and tap new customers and markets. This has ensured sustained growth, as reflected in operating income of Rs 966 crore in fiscal 2025 (up from Rs 686 crore in fiscal 2022). 

**Large product portfolio and customer base:** CSTL’s products find application in diverse industries such as stabiliser (acrylic acids), pharmaceuticals, agricultural chemicals, polymer, plastic, food and animal feed, fragrances, water treatment etc. It has also developed new HALS series products, which are import substitutes and have application in master batches, automobiles and other industries. The company derives 60-65% of its operating income from exports and is a preferred supplier to customers in China, Europe and America. The balance is derived from domestic sales. The top 10 customers accounted for around 35% of the operating income in fiscal 2025. Strong market position, diversified product basket and established relationships with a reputed clientele bolsters the business risk profile and should ensure a steady ramp up in scale of operations. 

**Healthy operating margin, supported by integration:** Operations are integrated, with facilities to manufacture products from base chemicals. wherein the company uses phenol to produce anisole to MEHQ and to BHA. Operating margin was healthy at over 40% over the three fiscals through March 2025. The margin is likely to moderate in the near to medium term due to ramp up of  newly launched products and volatility in input cost. However, it shall remain healthy owing to the integrated operations and adequate profitability in core products. 

**Robust financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile should remain strong, marked by nil debt, steady accretion to reserve and ample liquidity. Networth and debt protection metrics are likely to remain robust due to healthy operating 

profitability and negligible debt and cost of finance. Marked by networth of Rs 1,413 crore, and no reliance on external debt, capital structure has been robust with nil gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOL/ANW) ratio of 0.13 time as on March 31, 2025, as against 0.16 time a year ago.  Interest coverage ratio stood at 493 times for fiscal 2025 as against 282 times in the previous fiscal. 

The company plans to incur capital expenditure (capex) of around Rs 300 crore in fiscal 2026 towards the performance chemicals segment. Capex will be met via internal cash accrual and surplus liquidity. With steady accretion, backed by healthy operating profitability, the financial risk profile is expected to remain robust over the medium term. 

# **Weaknesses:** 

**Exposure to volatility in raw material prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates:** Exports account for 60-65% of revenue. While raw material imports offer a partial natural hedge, the balance is covered through forward contracts. However, the operating margin remains vulnerable to any sharp and sudden fluctuations in forex rates and volatility in raw material prices and realisations. Prices of key raw materials, which are crude oil derivatives, tend to fluctuate. Though there is a pass-through mechanism, it happens with a time lag and hence, any sharp fluctuations in raw material prices can adversely impact the margin. Operating margin has remained in the range of 40.8-44% over the three fiscals ended March 31, 2025. Sustenance of healthy operating margin, despite input price volatility and forex fluctuation, will remain monitorable **.** Also, impact of the recent hike in US tariffs on profitability will bear watching. 

**Working capital-intensive operations:** Gross current assets (GCAs) stood at 172 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by inventory of 95 days and receivables of about 78 days. As the company deals with large global players, it has to extend credit as per business requirements. Inventory mainly comprises stock of key raw material. GCA days are expected to remain at similar levels over the medium term **.** Improvement towards  same will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Liquidity is likely to remain robust, with cash accrual of over Rs 320 crore expected against nil debt obligation. Working capital limit of Rs 45 crore was utilised sparingly in the 12 months through June 2025. The company also had unencumbered liquid investments of around Rs 430 crore as on June 30, 2025. It is expected to maintain surplus liquidity over the medium term, despite the planned capex, supported by healthy operating profitability. A healthy capital structure leads to strong financial flexibility. Current ratio was healthy at 5 times as on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

CSTL will continue to benefit from its strong market position in niche product segments, healthy operating efficiency, diversified product basket, robust financial risk profile and strong liquidity. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained revenue growth of over 25% year-on-year, primarily driven by ramp-up in sales from new products and steady operating margin 

Sustenance of strong financial  risk profile and liquidity 

# **Downward factors** 

Steep decline in revenue or operating margin (below 35%), resulting in lower-than-expected cash accrual 

Stretch in the working capital cycle or sizeable, debt-funded capex or acquisition, weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

CSTL was established in 2006 by Mr Ashok R Boob and Mr Siddharth Sikchi. The company has plants in Kurkumbh, Maharashtra, and manufactures specialty chemicals, which find application in diverse industries such as stabiliser (acrylic acids), pharmaceuticals, agricultural chemicals, polymer, plastic, food and animal feed, fragrances, water treatment etc. The company is listed on the National Stock Exchange and the Bombay Stock Exchange.  The promoters have divested 24% of their shareholding recently in the company and currently holds 51%.
