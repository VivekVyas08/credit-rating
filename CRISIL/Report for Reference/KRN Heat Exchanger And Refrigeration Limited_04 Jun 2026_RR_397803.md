### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Rating has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A-/Stable’ rating on the long term bank facilities of KRN Heat Exchanger And Refrigeration Limited (KRN). 

The rating reflects the extensive industry experience of its promoters, strong financial profile, and diversified end user industry base. These strengths are partially offset by its large working capital cycle and susceptibility of operating margins to volatility in raw material cost and foreign exchange (forex) rates along with variation in product mix. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of KRN along -with its wholly owned subsidiaries i.e. KRN HVAC Products Private Limited (KRNH) and Thermotech Research Laboratory Private Limited (TRLPL).

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive industry experience of the promoters:** The promoters have experience of over two decades in heat exchangers and the refrigeration industry. This has given them a deep understanding of the dynamics of the market and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. The promoters are further supported by a team of professional management for the day-to-day operations of the group. This has led to a healthy scale of operations with continuous increase in the scale of operations with a CAGR of around 40% for the past 2 years ended fiscal 2026. The scale increased to Rs revenue of Rs 600-610 crore in fiscal 2026 as compared to Rs 310 crore in fiscal 2024. The growth in fiscal 2026 was driven by healthy ramp up of the new manufacturing facility set up by the group along with addition of new customers and product diversification done by the group. Additionally, the group benefits from strong relationship with its longstanding relations with reputed customers leading to continuous repeat orders from them along with the group’s ability to produce supplies in accordance with the customer specifications, standards, and customization. Further the group has been continuously increasing their export reach. The scale is further expected to increase, driven by further ramp up of operations in the new manufacturing facility. 

**Strong financial profile** : The financial risk profile of the group is marked by healthy networth of around Rs 570-575 crore as on March 31, 2026, marked by healthy operations and steady accretion to reserves. The group further has raised Rs 350 crore through Qualified institutions placement (QIP) in May 2026 to cater to increased working capital requirements of the group. This will further improve the networth of the group. The capital structure of the group is strong as reflected in gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio of around 0.3-0.4 times and 0.5-0.7 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (as compared to 0.07 times and 0.2 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025). Despite increasing working capital requirement, the capital structure is expected to remain strong driven by equity infusion incurred in May 2026, leading to expected reduction in reliance on external debt and creditors. Debt protection metrics remains robust with interest coverage of 20-21 times for the year ended March 31, 2026, driven by healthy profitability and low leverage. It is expected to remain stable over the medium term. 

**Diversified end-user industry base:** The group’s products are widely utilized by Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) across diverse HVAC&R applications, including air conditioning, refrigeration, and process cooling systems.  It caters to a diversified end-user industry base comprising sectors such as air conditioning, refrigeration, process cooling, 

data centers etc. Furthermore, the group has added Bus Air conditioning segment and started catering to data centers which are expected to ramp up in fiscal 2027 and provide revenue visibility. A diversified end-user industry base allows the group to mitigate risks associated with a slowdown in any single industry while enabling it to achieve more stable and sustained growth 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Large working capital cycle:** The operations of the group are working capital intensive as seen in the Gross current assets (GCA) of around 340-350 days as on March 31<sup>st</sup> , 2026. The working capital requirement of the group increased in fiscal 2026 primarily due to stretch in debtors and inventory marked by onboarding of new customers with extended credit terms, along with higher export sales requiring longer credit periods. Inventory levels also increased owing to the increase in the minimum inventory requirements of the group due to addition of new products in fiscal 2026. This led to increase in the debtor and inventory days to around 105-110 days and 200-210 days, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (79 and 98 days in the previous year). This is supported by increase in credit period received by the suppliers with creditor days of 125130 days (62 days in previous year). Working capital cycle is expected to improve but will continue to remain large over the medium term. Management of the same will remain a key monitorable. 

**Susceptibility of operating margins to volatility in raw material cost and foreign exchange (forex) rates along with variation in product mix** : Prices of the key raw materials copper and aluminum are volatile in nature and the same accounts for 65-75% of the total operating income. While the group incurs periodic price revisions partially mitigating the risk, the same is with a time lag. Further the group has majority of their procurement through imports while exports are only 15-20%, hence the operating margin remains vulnerable to any sharp and sudden fluctuations in forex rates and volatility in raw material prices and realizations. This can be reflected in the volatile operating margins in the range of 12.520.5% over the past five years through fiscal 2026. The margins have also been volatile due to varied product mix. The group has incurred margins of around 19% in fiscal 2026 and stability of the same will remain a key in the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Bank limit utilisation is low at around 56% for the past twelve months ended  April 2026. Annual net cash accrual are expected to be over Rs 90 crore against which there are no term debt obligation over the medium term. In addition, it will be act as cushion to the liquidity of the group. Rs 46 crores of cash and cash equivalent as on March 31, 2026, out of which Rs 17-18 crores is unencumbered. Current ratio at 1.6-1.7 times as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believe that KRN along with its subsidiaries will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, established relationships with clients and healthy financial profile of the group.

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

KRN, erstwhile known as KRN Heat Exchanger And Refrigeration Private Limited was incorporated in 2017. The company along -with its subsidiaries is engaged in the activity of manufacturing and sale of heating ventilation & air conditioning (HVAC) parts & accessories i.e. mainly precision-engineered aluminum and copper fin heat exchangers, water coils, condensers, and evaporator coils. Its two manufacturing facilities are located at Neemrana, Rajasthan. Its listed in BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange Of India Limited. Mr. Santosh Kumar Yadav is Chairman and Managing Director, wh has been in the industry for more than for more than 20 years. 

KRNH was incorporated in April 2023, to set up a new manufacturing facility in Neemrana, Alwar, Rajasthan. This facility has enabled  KRN to manufacture heat exchanger types that were not in its product line earlier, specifically, roll bond evaporators, oil cooling units, and bar and plate heat exchangers addressing the evolving needs of sectors like railways, data centers, compressor manufacturing, and refrigeration TRLPL was incorporated on February 2025, for HVAC&R Lab in India to equipped with the latest testing, validation, and certification technologies.
