### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘ **Crisil BB+/Stable** ’ rating to the long-term bank facilities of Balaji Phosphates Limited (BPL). 

The rating reflects the BPL’s established market position backed by extensive industry experience of the promoters, strategically location of its facility and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by its susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and regulatory changes, working capital intensive operations and high bank limit utilization. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of BPL along with its wholly owned subsidiary, i.e., Jyoti Weighing Systems Private Limited (JWSPL), together referred to as BPL group This is because these entities are strategically important to, and have significant operational integration with, BPL. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive industry experience of the promoters** : The group is promoted by Mr Mohit Airen and Mr Alok  Gupta who have been in the industry for over two decades. This has given them an understanding of the dynamics of the market, and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. The group is engaged in manufacturing phosphatebased fertilizers and micronutrients and have built a strong reputation for delivering high-quality Single Super Phosphate (SSP), NPK fertilizers, and Zinc Sulphate under its trusted brands “RATNAM” and “BPPL.” Additionally the group is also involved in manufacturing weighbridges through its subsidiary. The long standing relationship with customers have led to continuous repeat orders from them as can be reflected in healthy business performance of BPL over the years. The revenue in fiscal 2026 has grown to around Rs 170 crores from Rs 126 crores a year ago. Going ahead, the growth is expected to be driven by healthy demand for the products backed by nutrient based subsidy (NBS). 

**Strategic location of its facility** : The operations of the group are strategically located at Dewas, Madhya Pradesh, enabling proximity to both raw material sources and key agricultural markets. This provides them with cost efficiency in terms of logistics and marketing costs. This provides the group with a competitive edge over the other players. 

**Comfortable financial risk profile** : The financial profile of the group is comfortable marked by networth of around Rs 90.65 crore as on March 31<sup>st</sup> 2026 as compared to Rs 80.04 crores a year ago. The capital structure has also been healthy due to lower reliance on external funds yielding estimated gearing of 0.5 and low total outside liabilities to adj tangible networth (TOL/ANW) of 0.8 for year ending on 31st March 2026 (0.3 time and 0.6 time a year ago). The group’s debt protection measures have also been at healthy level due to leverage and healthy profitability. The adjusted interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratio are at 4.5 times and 0.3 times for fiscal 2026. The group’s financial profile is expected to remain comfortable driven by steady accretion to reserves, and no major debt funded capex over medium term. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and regulatory changes** : The cost of raw materials accounts for 6570% of total expenses. The prices of key raw materials like sulfuric acid, rock phosphates, furnace oil are volatile in nature. Any volatility in raw material prices can have an impact on the profitability as reflected in the volatile operating margins in the range of 8-12% over the past four fiscals through fiscal 2026. The significant increase in prices of sulfuric acid and 

furnace oil in the second half of fiscal 2026 have further pressured the margins to around 7% for second half of fiscal 2026. Further, the fertilizer industry continues to remain extensively regulated by the government. Their intervention in deciding the minimum retail prices and the NBS subsidies will continue to impact the profitability, as there is limited scope of passing on the increased prices to the end customers. Sustained improvement of margins at healthy levels remains a key monitorable. 

**Working capital intensive operations** Its intensive working capital management is reflected in its gross current assets (GCA) of 280-290 days as on March 31, 2026. Its' large working capital requirements arise from its high debtor and inventory levels. Debtor days were high due to high credit period extended to the customers along with subsidy receivable from the government leading to debtors of around 145 days as on March 2026, increased from 130 days in fiscal 2025. Furthermore, it holds large raw material and finished goods inventory of around 3 months to maintain the production and the customer requirement. The inventory levels are higher as on March, as fertilizers need to be distributed before the start of monsoon season. Working capital cycle is supported by creditors of around 50-60 days. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Stretched 

Bank limit utilisation is high at around 97 percent for the past twelve months ended Mar-26. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 8-11 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 2-3 crore over the medium term. Current ratio is expected to be healthy at around 2.5 times and cash balance is low around Rs 1.7 crores on March 31, 2026. 

Low gearing and moderate net worth support it’s financial flexibility, and provides the financial cushion available in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believe the company will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factor** 

Sustained improvement in scale of operation and operating margin, leading to higher cash accruals Improvement in working capital cycle leading to bank limit utilisation below 90% on a sustained basis 

# **Downward factor** 

Decline in net cash accruals below Rs 6 crore on account of decline in revenue or operating profits. Any large debt-funded capital expenditure or substantial increase in its working capital requirements thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

BPL was incorporated in 1996 by Mr. Manoj Kanodia & family and was acquired by Mr Mohit Airen, Mr. Alok Gupta & family in 2014. BPL is engaged in manufacturing of phosphate-based fertilizers and micronutrients such as single super phosphate (SSP), granule single super phosphate (GSSP) along with nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium (NPK) fertilizers. BPL market it’s products under its brand names “Ratnam” and ""BPPL.” BPL’s manufacturing facility is located at Dewas, Madhya Pradesh with an installed capacity of 120,000 metric tonnes per annum (MTPA) of SSP and 3,300 MTPA of Zinc Sulphate. BPL is listed on National Stock Exchange of India (SME platform). JWSPL was incorporated in June 1979. 

BPL acquired 99.98% stake in JWSPL in 2020, with this JWSPL became a subsidiary of BPL. JWSPL is engaged in manufacturing weighbridge both PIT less (surface mounted) & PIT type (ground level), rail static weigh bridge, moduler weigh bridge, mobile weigh bridge, alpha numeric weight indicator, etc. and their annual service maintenance. It’s manufacturing facility is located at Dewas (Madhya Pradesh) with a total installed capacity of more than 600 weighbridges p.a.
