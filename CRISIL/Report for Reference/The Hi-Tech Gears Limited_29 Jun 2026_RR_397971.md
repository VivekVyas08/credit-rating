### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its rating on the long-term bank facilities of The Hi-Tech Gears Limited (THGL, part of the HiTech group) at ‘Crisil A-/Stable’. 

The rating is supported by the sustained scale of operations and profitability of the Hi-Tech group. The Group has achieved revenues of Rs 916.7 crores in fiscal 2026 and is expected to clock in revenues of Rs 900-1000 crores in FY2027. Although the margins have reduced to 12.35%, the consistent measures undertaken by the management, including productivity improvement and high growth from new customer additions like Royal Enfield in the medium term, are expected to lead to sustained growth in profitability by 150-200 bps. Going forward, improved offtake from customers, leading to sustained and significant improvement in volume, along with a steady operating margin, remains a key monitorable. 

The rating factors in the group's financial risk profile, which remains comfortable, as indicated by a total outside liabilities to tangible net worth (TOL/TNW) ratio estimated at less than one time over the medium term. Further, Crisil Ratings has noted that a demand notice of Rs 63 lakhs was received pursuant to searches by the GST department, which concluded on January 21, 2026. The management has provided necessary clarifications to the department and has deposited a sum of Rs 40 lakhs. The management is currently seeking a legal remedy to recover the deposit of Rs 40 lakhs made to the department. Crisil Ratings believes that this will not have a major impact on the business or financial risk profile of the company. In the event that this liability crystallizes, the company has adequate liquid funds, as of May 2026, amounting to Rs 70-80 crores, to meet the obligation. 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters, which resulted in established market position and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by working capital-intensive operations and Exposure to the policy risk.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoters resulted in established market position:** The three-decade-long experience of the promoter in the auto component industry, his sound understanding of market dynamics and healthy relationships with customers and suppliers, will continue to support the business risk profile. The company is taking multiple measures to increase its market share by adding more original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) such as CNH, Dana, Stackpole, Mahindra, Harley etc. The group recorded an operating income of around Rs 916.7 crore for the fiscal 2026 and is expected to grow at 10-15% annually in medium term. This is because of the additions of the new customers like Royal Enfield driving the growth over the medium term. Focus on addition of new products and new customers in each segment should strengthen the overall business risk profile. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Continued accretion to reserves has improved the overall financial flexibility of the company. As a result,  net worth is projected to improve to Rs 460-480 crore as of March 31, 2027, from around Rs 443 crore as of March 31, 2026. The total outside liabilities to adjusted net worth ratio is estimated to be less than one time as of March 31, 2027, at 0.71 times in fiscal 2026, thus providing the company with sufficient headroom to raise additional debt. With continuous debt reduction over the past few fiscals and improvement in operating profitability, debt protection metrics remain comfortable, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios expected to range from 7-8 times and 0.50.75 times, respectively, in fiscal 2027, compared to 7.63 times and 0.54 times in fiscal 2026. Crisil Ratings believes that the GST demand of Rs 63 lakhs will not have a major impact on the business or financial risk profile of the company. In the event that this liability crystallizes, the company has adequate liquid funds, as of May 2026, amounting to Rs 70-80 crores, to meet the obligation. Going forward, expansion in the scale of operations and the absence of any sizeable, debt-funded capital expenditure plans should enhance accretion to reserves. Improvement in the financial risk profile remains a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Working capital-intensive operations:** Large working capital requirement arises because of the commoditised nature of business. Gross current assets (GCAs) were high at 150-160 days over the three fiscals through 2026 and are likely to be rangebound at 150-170 days. GCAs are driven by extended credit provided to customers to combat competition, and huge inventory, comprising raw material and finished products, given the diverse product mix. However, with increase in scale of operations, reliance on short-term debt may increase further. The ability of the company to efficiently manage its working capital and limit reliance on debt will be monitored. 

**Exposure to the policy risk:** Company earns around 46% of the revenues from exports with majorly from USA. Hence, in fiscal 2026, due to US Tariffs, there was muted activity in the commercial automobile industry, coupled with geopolitical tensions, which led to lower revenue over fiscals 2026 at Rs 916.7 crores. Going forward, onboarding of new clients should help expand the overall scale of operations. Given the uncertainty around imposition of tariffs by the US, the overall growth in revenue, along with sustenance of healthy operating margin, remains a key rating sensitivity factor. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Bank limit utilisation was moderate, averaging 41.8% for the 12 months ending May 31, 2026. Expected net cash accrual of Rs 90-110 crore per fiscal should suffice to meet yearly debt obligation of Rs 7-9 crore over the medium term, resulting in healthy cushion. Current ratio is expected to remain healthy at 2-2.3 times as on March 31, 2027 (1.95 times in fiscal 2026). Healthy cash balance of Rs 70-80 crore also supports liquidity. Crisil Ratings believe that in the event of this liability crystallizing, the company has adequate liquid funds as of May 2026 at Rs 70-80 crores to meet the obligation and there will be no major impact on the business or financial risk profile of the company. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believe the financial risk profile of the company shall continue to improve with reduced term debt obligation and sufficient net cash accrual to cover the working capital expenses. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

- Sustained and significant growth in operating income, along with steady operating margin of above 13.5%, leading to higher-than-expected net cash accrual 

- Efficient working capital management, reducing reliance on bank debt and thus, strengthening the overall financial risk profile of the group 

# **Downward Factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin (to 9-10%), leading to lower-than-expected net cash accrual 

- Stretch in the working capital cycle or any large, debt-funded capex, weakening the financial risk profile, particularly liquidity. 

# **<u>About the group</u>** 

THGL, incorporated in 1986, is a public limited group, listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange. The group manufactures auto components, particularly transmission gears. It has three manufacturing plants in India: two in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, and one in Manesar, Haryana. Mr Deep Kapuria is the founder and promoter of the group.
