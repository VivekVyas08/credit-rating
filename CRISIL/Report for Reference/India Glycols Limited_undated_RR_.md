

##### **September 28, 2012 Mumbai** 

# **India Glycols Limited** 

## **Rating suspended** 

|**Instrument**|**Rating**|
|---|---|
|**Fixed Deposit Programme**|**Suspended**|



CRISIL has suspended its ratings on the fixed deposit programme of India Glycols Limited (IGL).The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by IGL with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, IGL is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess IGL’s ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria ‘Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings’. 

### **_About the Company_** 

IGL manufactures MEG, EOD, glycol ethers, and potable alcohol. The company has manufacturing facilities at Kashipur (Uttaranchal) and Gorakhpur (Uttar Pradesh). It has a total installed capacity to manufacture up to 150,000 tonnes per annum of MEG and EOD. The company also has a licensed and installed capacity to manufacture up to 60 kilolitres per day of potable alcohol. IGL’s businesses can broadly be classified into chemicals, alcohol, and other products (guar gum and industrial gases).
