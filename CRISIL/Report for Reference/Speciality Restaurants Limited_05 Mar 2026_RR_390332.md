### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Speciality Restaurants Limited (SRL) at ‘Crisil A-/Stable’. 

The rating continues to reflect SRL’s established market position by the presence of strong brands, and its robust financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition, leading to volatility in operating profitability and concentration of revenue from Mainland China and Asia Kitchen by Mainland China brand. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SRL. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Established market position:** 

SRL has an established market position with around 20 brands under its kitty. These include well-known brands liked Mainland China, Oh! Calcutta, Sweet Bengal and Sigree Global which offer different cuisines with Asian cuisine being prominent. 

Company had around 126 outlets as on December 31,2025 and plans to open new outlets in last quarter of fiscal 2026 and Q1 of fiscal 2027. This addition to outlets coupled with scaling of relatively newer brands will continue to support the revenue growth. SRL has clocked in revenue of Rs 341.5 Cr for the first nine months of fiscal 2026 as against Rs 413 Cr clocked in fiscal 2025. 

### **Robust financial risk profile:** 

SRL has a robust financial risk profile with strong capital structure with healthy networth of Rs 335 Cr  as on September 30, 2025, as against nil debt. Company has remained debt free since fiscal 2019. 

Company has a project pertaining to Durgapur region in development however it is under joint development agreement, and the company will not be incurring any capex towards these projects. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

### **Exposure to intense competition:** 

Intense competition in the domestic fine dining space (with marked by presence of organised players and several unorganized players) may result in stagnation of revenue and lower profitability. Fixed cost (mainly lease rentals for store premises, employee cost and electricity charges) forms a significant portion of operating cost for a restaurant and may lead to potential compression of margin in the event of slowdown in business. 

Thus, growth in same-store sales and ramp up in newer brands launched by the company will remain critical to sustain and improve profitability. 

### **Concentration of revenue from Mainland China and Asia Kitchen:** 

Mainland China and Asia Kitchen are prominent brands which have generated 24% and 18% of the total revenues of the company. While there has been some ramp up in other brands, these two brands continue to contribute to nearly 50% of the revenue of the company. 

While the company has been focused on diversifying its revenue streams through launch of new brands and focus on other cuisines over the past few years, contribution from these brands to the overall revenue will be a key rating sensitivity factor. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity is strong marked by estimated net cash accruals of Rs 72-75 Cr for fiscal year 2026 as against nil repayment obligations. Company is debt free and has not utilized its funds based limits for the past twelve months ended January 2026. Company also has a cash surplus of Rs 160-165 Cr as on September 30,2025 which remains sufficient to fund its organic expansion plans. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes SRL will continue to benefit from its established brands and healthy financial risk profile. Sustenance of operating margin will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained revenue growth by over 30% while maintaining operating margins resulting in higher accruals. Sustained financial risk profile with low leverage levels and ample liquidity 

# **Downward factors** 

- Decline in revenue and subdued operating margins, leading to net cash accrual of less than Rs 45 crore over the medium term. 

Higher than expected losses, debt funded capex or further reduction in networth or significant reduction in liquid assets weakens the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

SRL was promoted by Mr Anjan Chatterjee (Founder promoter) in 1999. The promoter entered the restaurant business by setting up Only Fish in 1992, followed by Mainland China, in Mumbai. SRL is now one of the largest fine-dining restaurant chains in India. Its brands, Mainland China, Asia Kitchen by Mainland China, Riyasat, Barishh, Episode One, Oh! Calcutta, Sigree/Sigree Global Grill, Haka, Flame & Grill, Café Mezzuna, and Hoppipola offer a variety of cuisines and dining formats. Sweet Bengal and Dariole are company’s confectionary brand.
