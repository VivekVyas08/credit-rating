CRISIL has suspended its ratings on the bank facilities of Dollar Industries Ltd (Dollar Industries). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by Dollar Industries with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, Dollar Industries is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess Dollar Industries's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings'. 



### **_About the Company_** 

Dollar Industries (formerly, Bhawani Textiles Pvt Ltd) was set up in 1972 by Mr. Dindayal Gupta. The company has been manufacturing and marketing men's innerwear, under the Dollar brand name, since 1973. In 2010-11 (refers to financial year, April 1 to March 31), Dollar Industries commissioned an elastic manufacturing unit and acquired a spinning unit at Tirupur (Tamil Nadu).
