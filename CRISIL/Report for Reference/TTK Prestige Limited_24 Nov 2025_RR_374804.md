### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its 'Crisil AA/Stable/Crisil A1+' ratings on the bank facilities of TTK Prestige Ltd (TTKPL). 

The ratings continue to reflect the healthy business risk profile of the company, backed by its robust market position in pressure cookers and cookware segment, strong brand, established distribution network and longstanding relationships with dealers and distributors. The ratings also factor in the strong financial risk profile, supported by healthy networth and negligible debt, and sufficient liquidity. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition, and susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates. 

Revenues of TTKPL grew 8% during the first half of fiscal 2026, led by strong double-digit growth in the e-commerce segment and bounce back of the military canteen sales department (CSD) segment. Revenue is expected to grow moderately at 4-5% over the medium term, supported by festival season, reduced Goods and Service Tax (GST) rates, declining interest rates and introduction of new products in the portfolio. Earlier in fiscal 2025, revenue growth was sluggish, due to weak consumer demand on account of high inflation, low wage growth and reduced lending by micro finance institution (MFI) due to higher delinquencies, along with rationalisation of procurement from the CSD segment, which have collectively reduced demand for discretionary and kitchen products. 

Operating margin remained flat at ~9.5% during the first half of fiscal 2026, in line with the corresponding period previous fiscal, on account of expenses towards process refinement and cost optimisation measures. However, this was partly offset by improved volumes, leading to better operating leverage. The margin has continued to moderate in the last few fiscals on account of sluggishness in revenue growth, resulting in lower absorption of fixed cost, along with elevated sales/promotional expenses due to intense competition. In fiscal 2025, the margin moderated to 9.6% from 10.7% in the previous fiscal. 

Since the last fiscal, the company has been engaging external consultants to drive the overall organisational transformation and devising growth strategies for building new product designs, exploring go to market strategy, process improvements and operational efficiency. As part of this exercise, it incurred Rs 30 crore in fiscal 2025 and is also expected to incur Rs 70-80 crore annually for the next 2-3 fiscals. Excluding these expenses, the operating margin of TTKPL was around 11.95% in the first half of fiscal 2026 and is expected to moderate to ~11% over the medium term. Nevertheless, the margin is expected to improve gradually from fiscal 2028 onwards, driven by strategic initiatives towards cost saving, innovative design and increased operating leverage with growing size of the operations of subsidiaries. More-than-anticipated spend towards this exercise may impact the profitability and shall remain monitorable. 

The financial risk profile was robust, with healthy networth of over Rs 1,771 crore as on March 31, 2025, and comfortable debt protection metrics in the fiscal owing to almost debt-free balance sheet. However, tangible networth was impacted in fiscal 2025 due to the buyback of equity shares totalling Rs 250 crore, which represented 1.20% of the total paid-up equity shares. Additionally, the company recorded a goodwill impairment of Rs 71 crore in its UK subsidiary, Horwood Homewares Ltd (Horwood), due to decline in performance caused by sluggish demand, geopolitical tensions, and uncertainty in the UK and European economies. Nevertheless, TTKPL’s debt metrics will likely remain strong with no debt addition and moderate capital expenditure (capex), to be funded by own cash accrual. 

Liquidity remains strong, supported by cash and equivalent of ~Rs 760 crore as on September 30, 2025. Fund-based limit of Rs 56.5 crore remained unutilised, while non-fund-based limit of Rs 53.75 crore was sparsely utilised on average at 4% in the 12 months through October 2025. This, along with expected cash accrual of Rs 150-170 crore per annum, will comfortably cover annual capex of Rs 180 crore and nil debt obligation over the medium term. The term debt of Rs 44 crore in the subsidiary is to be repaid only in fiscal 2028. With estimated gearing of 0.03 time as on March 31, 2026, the company has sufficient headroom to raise additional debt. 

# **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of TTKPL, TTK British Holdings Ltd, Horwood and Ultrafresh Modular Solutions Ltd (Ultrafresh), collectively referred to as TTK. Horwood and Ultrafresh are subsidiaries of TTKPL and operate in the same business and have business and financial linkages.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Robust market position in the kitchen equipment industry and sound operating capabilities:** The company will likely maintain its strong market position, driven by its wide distribution network and healthy opportunities in the global market. Prestige is one of the strongest kitchen equipment brands, with leading market share in the domestic pressure cooker segment. The company offers cookware, appliances, gas stoves, mixer grinders and cleaning solutions, among others. To compete with global brands, it has ventured into the IoT (Internet of Things) -based luxury segment in categories such as built-in ovens, dishwashers, rice cookers and island chimneys, through Prestige Lifestyle stores. An in-house research and product development team helps expand the product base with innovation and improve efficiency. 

The company has changed the brand, Judge from UK (entry-level cookers at lower price points) to Judge by Prestige and included the same in Prestige’s distribution channel, which will result in increased visibility for the Judge products and bring in revenue of ~Rs 500 crore in the next 5 fiscals. 

TTKPL has entered into a binding agreement with GramyaHaat Rural Tech Pvt Ltd (GramyaHaat), which is a start-up company engaged in rural distribution of consumer durable products, including kitchen appliances and utensils. The total commitment is of Rs 15 crore. GramyaHaat has strong distribution network through physical stores and village level entrepreneurs. This investment will enable the company to leverage the network of GramyaHaat and enhance the reach of Prestige products in many households. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile is expected to remain stable over the medium term, backed by high cash accrual, comfortable capital structure, no debt (on standalone basis) and robust debt protection metrics. Networth was sizeable at over Rs 1,771 crore as on March 31, 2025, despite reducing from Rs 1,919 crore as on March 31, 2024, on account of buyback of equity shares totalling Rs 250 crore, and goodwill impairment of Rs 71 crore in its UK subsidiary, Horwood. However, with nil addition of debt and steady cash generation, the debt protection metrics were comfortable, as reflected in adjusted interest coverage ratio of over 20 times for fiscal 2025. With no addition of debt over the medium term, the rearing is estimated to be negligible at 0.03 time as on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to intense competition:** TTKPL has healthy market share in the domestic pressure cooker segment. In the inner-lid category, the company competes with established players such as Hawkins Cookers Ltd, which holds significant market share. Multichain, large-format stores have launched their own brands to target customers looking for all products under one roof. Though the company continues to face competition from regional and national players, the company has been engaging with external consultants to drive the overall organisational transformation and devising growth strategies for building new product designs, exploring go to market strategy, process improvements and operational efficiency, which is set to benefit the company in the long run. 

**Susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and forex rates:** The operating margin remains exposed to volatility in the prices of key raw materials–steel and aluminum–and adverse movements in forex rates. The company has successfully reduced its reliance on imports from China and has been passing on price hikes to customers but may still be partly vulnerable to adverse movement in input cost. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash and equivalent stood at ~Rs 760 crore as on September 30, 2025. Fund-based limit of Rs 56.5 crore remained unutilised in the 12 months through October 2025. This along with expected cash accrual of Rs 150-170 crore per fiscal will comfortably cover yearly capex of Rs 180 crore and debt obligation of Rs 44 crore in fiscal 2028. With estimated gearing of 0.03 time as on March 31, 2026, the company has sufficient headroom to raise additional debt. The minimal working capital requirement will likely be met through cushion in the bank limit. 

# **ESG profile of TTKPL** 

Crisil Ratings believes the environment, social, and governance (ESG) profile of TTKPL supports its already strong credit risk profile. TTKPL is developing a detailed ESG framework, which will mitigate environmental and social risks. 

# **Key ESG highlights:** 

- The share of renewable energy in total energy consumption increased to 6.5% in fiscal 2025 with the commissioning of a 500-kilowatt capacity solar rooftop. 

- The company reported a zero lost time injury frequency rate for both employees and 0.64 for workers during fiscal 2025. Its governance structure is characterised by ~50% of its board being independent directors, presence of two women independent directors, dedicated investor grievance redressal committee, and extensive financial disclosures. 

There is growing importance of ESG among investors and lenders. The commitment of TTKPL to ESG principles will play a key role in enhancing stakeholder confidence. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the business risk profile of TTKPL will continue to be supported by healthy market position in the kitchen equipment segment. Furthermore, strong balance sheet and adequate liquidity should offset any impact of stressed business conditions. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Steady diversification in revenue streams and improvement in the market share of key products Healthy double digit revenue growth, and operating margin in excess of 15%, benefitting cash generation Sustenance of strong financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **Downward factors:** 

Sluggish revenue growth and increase in costs leading to dip in operating profitability below 10-11% Large, debt-funded capex or acquisition, or significant stretch in working capital requirement, moderating debt metrics Reduction in liquid surpluses due to buy back, sizeable dividend payout or capital reduction structure 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Set up as a private limited company in 1955, TTKPL was reconstituted as a public limited company in 1994. The company is among the leading brands in the kitchen equipment space, especially in the pressure cooker segment. Its product profile is diversified, with 29% of revenue coming from pressure cookers, 15% from cookware and 44% from appliances. TTKPL was focused on south-India earlier. It entered into kitchen appliances by leveraging its brand and expansive distribution network to penetrate the west, north and east markets. It derives over 50% revenue from non-south markets. 

Prestige is a premium brand primarily targeting the upper middle class. TTKPL is the flagship company of the TT Krishnamachari group of companies, which has interests in healthcare, consumer products and services. On the recommendations of nomination and remuneration committee, the company’s board decided to split the roles of managing director (MD) and CEO and appointed Venkatesh Vijayaraghavan as CEO from January 2024, who will report to Mr Chandru Kalro, MD. However, Post, retirement of Mr Chandru Kalro on September 30, 2024, Mr Venkatesh took over as MD. 

In April 2016, TTKPL acquired Horwood through its 100% subsidiary, TTK British Holdings Ltd, UK, as part of its global expansion strategy. Horwood is one of UK’s largest table and cookware sellers under the brands Horwood, Stellar and Judge. In fiscal 2022, the company made strategic investment of around 41% in Ultrafresh, which is engaged in modular kitchen solutions and kitchen appliances. The shareholding was increased to 51% in fiscal 2023 pursuant to which it became a subsidiary.
