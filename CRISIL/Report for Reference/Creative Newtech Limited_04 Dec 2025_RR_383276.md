### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’ ratings on the bank facilities of Creative Newtech Ltd (CNL). 

The ratings reflect the extensive experience of the promoter in the distribution of computer peripherals and consumer electronics, the company’s diversified product portfolio, established relationships with principal suppliers, efficient working capital management and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition, low operating margin and customer concentration in revenue. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

While arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has evaluated standalone business and financial risk profile of CNL. 

Unsecured loan of Rs 16 lakhs as on March 31, 2025 from the promoter has been treated as debt. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoter and a diversified product offering:** The promoter has more than two decades of experience in the consumer electronics business which has helped establish a clientele across multiple channels including channel partners, large format stores, sub-distributors and e-commerce players. Extensive experience helps with stocking decisions and addition of products to increase sales, which is also reflected in the company having around 25 principals and variety of products in its portfolio.  Steady order flow from the customers has led to steady growth in revenue from Rs 1348 crores in fiscal 2023 to Rs 1653 crores in fiscal 2025. The momentum in revenue growth should continue over the medium term, with the company having clocked revenues of around Rs 970 crores in the first half of fiscal 2026. <u>Crisil Ratings believes that the extensive experience of the promoters backed by</u> an experienced professional team should continue to support the business risk profile of the company. 

Further, the company is an exclusive distributor across India for various products for reputed brands such as Honeywell, Cooler Master, Lexar, Fujifilm, Olympus, i-ball, AOC among others across information technology (IT), imaging, lifestyle and security segments. The company also has a distribution agreement with Fujifilm. The company has established relations with suppliers and is an exclusive distributor for many of its suppliers, which has helped scale up operations. They receive compensation for any price revision or obsolescence of products, helping it manage inventory. The diversified product offerings reduce the risk of slowdown in demand for a single product 

**Efficient working capital requirement:** The working capital cycle is efficiently managed, as reflected in gross current assets (GCAs) of 83 days as on March 31, 2025 (62 days as on March 31,2024). This was driven by receivables and inventory of 46 days and 19 days respectively as on March 31,2025 (26 days and 15 days respectively a year earlier). While the debtor days have increased due to larger share of B2B customers which entail higher credit period, inventory remains modest ensuring limited inventory risk. The working capital cycle is partly supported by payables of 25 days. The working capital cycle should remain efficiently managed over the medium term, however higher than expected increase in debtor days going forward remains monitorable. 

- **Comfortable financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile is marked with improved net worth of Rs 265 crores as on March 31, 2025 (Rs 210 crores a year ago) and has steadily increased over the years driven by the healthy accretion to 

the reserves along with the equity infusions. Capital structure is comfortable as reflected in gearing and total outside liability to adjusted net worth ratio of 0.76 times and 0.27 times as on March 31,2025 (0.59 times and 0.35 times a year earlier). While the capital structure is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term, any higher-than-expected debt availed to fund the working capital requirements remain monitorable. Debt protection metrics were adequate, reflected in interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios of 4.5 times and 0.36 times for fiscal 2025 (5.52 times and 0.43 times for fiscal 2024). The financial risk profile will remain healthy over the medium term owing to steady accretion to reserves with increasing scale of operations and healthy profitability. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Customer concentration in revenues:** The revenues of the company remained rangebound at around Rs 1650 crores over last two fiscal years ended FY 2025, with ~60-65% being derived from one overseas customer leading to a risk of concentration in revenues. The company has been adding on new customers and the same has led to reduction in contribution of the top customer to~23% of the total revenues as of H1 of fiscal 2026. Though the customer concentration has improved, sustenance of the improved customer mix remains monitorable amidst the growing scale of operations as any loss of business or change in commercial terms with principal customer can adversely impact the credit profile of the company. 

- **Exposure to intense competition and low operating margins:** Intense competition in the consumer electronics distribution business because of low entry barriers, as indicated by negligible capital requirement, leads to stiff pricing competition among players, impacting scalability. The operating margin is typically low in the distribution business because of limited value addition. CNL had an operating margin of 2.5-2.8% in the three fiscals through 2025. The low operating margin leaves cash accrual highly vulnerable to changes in operating cost. Revisions in terms with vendors or pressure to enhance margin sharing with the distribution network amid intense competition will remain key monitorable 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilization is moderate at around 67% percent for the past twelve months ended October 2025. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 26-27 crores which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 2.3 crores over the medium term. Current ratio are healthy at 2.1 times on March 31, 2025. Cash and cash equivalents are estimated at about Rs 12-13 crores as on date. The promoters are likely to extend support in the form of equity and unsecured loans to meet its working capital requirements and repayment obligations. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes that CNL will continue to benefit over the medium term from the extensive industry experience of its promoters and its established relationships with principals. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

- Reduction in customer concentration to less than 45% from each customer on a sustained basis 

- Sustained double digit revenue growth and stable operating margin leading to higher than anticipated net cash accrual of above Rs 35-40 crores 

Continued efficient working capital cycle and comfortable financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors:** 

Any decline in the scale of operations or a decline in the operating margins leading to cash accruals below Rs 15 crores Stretched working capital cycle, weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Set up in 1992 by Mr Ketan Patel, CNL is a distributor of consumer electronic goods in the information technology, imaging, lifestyle and security segments for brands such as Honeywell, Cooler Master, Olympus, Samsung, Lexar, Fujifilm, and Viewsonic. The company is based in Mumbai and is listed on the National Stock Exchange.
