### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank loan facilities of Roto Pumps Ltd (RPL; part of the RPL group) at ‘Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+'. 

The ratings continue to factor in the group’s established market position backed by the promoters’ extensive experience, healthy operating profitability and strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the modest scale of operations and large working capital requirement. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of RPL, its wholly owned subsidiaries, Roto Pumps America Inc; Roto Pumpen GMBH, Germany; Roto Energy Systems Ltd; Roto Overseas Pte Ltd and Roto Pumps Mena, FZE; and step-down subsidiaries, Roto Pumps North America, Inc; Roto Pumps (Malaysia) SDN BHD; Roto Pumps (Africa) Pty Ltd. This is because all these entities, collectively referred to as the RPL group, are in the same business and have strong business and financial linkages. RPL holds 100% equity in Roto Pumps America Inc, Roto Pumpen GMBH, Roto Energy Systems Ltd., Roto Overseas Pte Ltd and Roto Pumps Mena, FZE.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position and extensive experience of the promoters:** Revenue increased at compound annual growth rate of 30.9% over the three fiscals through 2025, driven by the five-decade-long presence of the promoters in the high value-added industrial pumps industry, geographically diversified revenue profile and established relationships with customers and suppliers. The group reported operating income of Rs 299 crore in fiscal 2025 as against Rs 276 crore in fiscal 2024. Group is expected to clock operating income of nearly Rs 315 crore in fiscal 2026 (achieved around Rs 131 crore during H1-FY 2026). The operating income is lower than Crisil Ratings’ previous expectations owing to delay in commencement of operations of solar pump and downhole pump segments. Nonetheless, the growth in operating income was supported by increased sales from existing product lines. Improvement in operating income driven by ramp-up in revenue from solar pumping systems and mud motors will remain a key monitorable. Crisil Ratings believes that the ability to develop products to cater to upcoming sectors will enhance growth over the medium term. The group will continue to benefit from the expertise of its management team and healthy customer relationships. 

**Healthy operating profitability:** The operating margin of the group has ranged between 22% and 25% over the three fiscals through 2025, backed by its ability to pass on hikes in steel prices to customers without a major lag. The group follows different pricing mechanisms for its domestic and overseas markets. The operating margin stood at 22.2% in fiscal 2025, against 25.0% in fiscal 2024 with the group supplying low-margin pumps during the fiscal year. In-house research and development facilities and niche product offerings should help the operating margin sustain at 21-23% over the medium term. Return on capital employed was healthy at 20.8% in fiscal 2025 and is expected to range between 18-22% over the medium term. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** Networth and gearing are expected to be Rs 240-245 crore and around 0.1 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 216.7 crore and 0.1 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025). The capital structure will be aided by healthy profitability and steady accretion to reserves over the medium term. Debt protection metrics are expected to 

remain healthy supported by interest coverage ratio of around 13 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio of around 1.5 times for fiscal 2026 (13.5 times and 1.5 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025). 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Modest scale of operations amid intense competition:** Intense competition from several small and mid-sized players and global entities restricts scalability, as reflected in revenue of Rs 299 crore in fiscal 2025. Though the scale is likely to improve through multiple product offerings by venturing into new end-user industries, the ability of the group to ramp-up operations through new products and geographical expansion will remain key rating sensitivity factors. 

**Large working capital requirement:** Gross current assets (GCAs) were sizeable at 208 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by large inventory and receivables. The group maintains sizeable inventory considering its vast product range, high lead time for manufacturing and significant revenue contribution from exports (around 70% of revenue). Receivables and inventory were 80-90 days and 80-110 days, respectively, over the five fiscals ended 2025. Commensurate with the increase in scale of operations, GCAs are expected to remain at 210- 240 days over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Expected cash accrual of Rs 45- 65 crore per annum will comfortably cover yearly debt obligation of Rs 1- 2 crore and working capital requirement over the medium term. The fund-based limit was utilised at 38.12% on average during the 12 months through October 2025. Unencumbered cash and equivalent stood at Rs 24 crore as on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The RPL group will continue to benefit from its established track record, increasing scale of operations and extensive experience of its promoters. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Steady growth in revenue to over Rs 450 crore and stable operating margin leading to higher cash accruals Efficient working capital management and sustenance of healthy financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue and operating margin falling below 18%, leading to lower cash accrual 

Large, debt-funded capex or further stretch in the working capital cycle weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

RPL manufactures different types of industrial pumps, such as progressive cavity and single- and twin-screw variants. These pumps are specialised and custom-made and used in industries where the fluid viscosity is high. They are also used in wastewater treatment, oil and gas, marine and sugar industries. RPL is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange and is based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh.
