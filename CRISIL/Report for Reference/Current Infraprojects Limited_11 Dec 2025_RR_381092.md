### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’** ratings to the bank facilities of Current Infraprojects Ltd (CIPL). 

The ratings reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) sector and healthy financial risk profile of the company. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to tender-based operations, presence in a highly fragmented industry and geographical concentration in the revenue profile. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has moderately consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of CIPL and its special purpose vehicles (SPVs)--Current Infra Bolnada Solar Pvt Ltd, Current Infra Talabera Solar Pvt Ltd, Current Infra Ompura Solar Pvt Ltd and Current Infra Dhanbad Solar Pvt Ltd. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key rating drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Extensive experience of the promoters** 

The promoters have more than three decades of experience in the EPC sector, with expertise in engineering, business development, operations and strategic planning. Their strong understanding of the market dynamics and healthy relationships with suppliers and customers should continue to support the business. The company booked revenue of Rs 99 crore in fiscal 2025 (Rs 78 crore in fiscal 2024), from segments such as electrical EPC, solar EPC and water EPC. While diversification in the revenue stream strengthens the market position of the company, sustainability of growth in the three segments will remain a key monitorable. CIPL booked revenue of Rs 45 crore till September 2025 and had order book of Rs 219.59 crore as on September 30, 2025, including private, state and central counter parties, which is to be executed over next 12-18 months. With a healthy order book to revenue ratio of 2.2 times and orders under bidding worth Rs 175 crore, revenue visibility should be healthy over the medium term. 

### **Healthy financial risk profile** 

The capital structure has been healthy due to lower reliance on external funds, yielding low gearing of 0.85 time and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio of 1.95 times as on March 31, 2025. Debt protection metrics were also strong due to leverage and healthy profitability. The interest coverage ratio is estimated at 9.54 times and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratio at 0.52 time for fiscal 2025. With the initial public offering in September 2025, the financial risk profile should further strengthen; thus, interest coverage ratio is projected at 12-13 times and NCATD ratio at 1.4-1.5 times for fiscal 2026. 

### **<u>Key rating drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

### **Susceptibility to tender-based operations** 

Revenue and profitability entirely depend on the ability to win tenders. Also, entities in this segment face intense competition, thus requiring to bid aggressively to get contracts, which restricts the operating margin to a moderate level. Also, given the cyclicality inherent in the construction industry, the ability to maintain profitability margin through operating efficiency becomes critical. 

# **Presence in a highly fragmented industry** 

The solar EPC industry is highly fragmented and the consequent intense competition may continue to constrain scalability, pricing power and profitability. Competitive pressure also restrains any pass-through mechanism, leading to a volatile operating margin. 

# **Geographical concentration in revenue profile** 

CIPL derived about 81% of its revenue in fiscal 2025 from three states--Rajasthan, Kerala and Uttar Pradesh. Thus, any slowdown in these economies may adversely affect the financial and business performances of the company. CIPL has been actively bidding for orders in other states such as Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Union Territory of Lakshadweep; however, conversion of these bids into orders and thereby reduction in geographical concentration will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Liquidity should remain supported by the ample surplus available in cash accrual and bank lines. Bank limit utilisation was around 82% for the 12 months through November 2025. Cash accrual is projected at more than Rs 19 crore per annum, against yearly debt obligation of just Rs 0.06-0.08 lakh over the medium term. The company has liquid funds of Rs 2-3 crore in case of any liquidity exigency. Current ratio was moderate at 1.35 times on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

CIPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and their established relationship with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained improvement in the scale of operations by 20% while sustaining the operating margins leading to higher net cash accruals. 

Improvement in the working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors** 

Revenue declining by 20% and operating margin dropping below 8%, resulting in lower-than-expected net cash accrual Stretch in the working capital cycle 

# **<u>About the group</u>** 

CIPL, formerly known as Current Infraprojects Pvt Ltd, was incorporated in 2013. The company has taken over the running business of M/s Current Services Consultants (a proprietorship concern of promoter, Ms Sujata Gangwar) established in 2000. The company was converted into a public-limited company under the current name in 2025. CIPL is engaged in providing EPC services, offering comprehensive solutions in solar EPC, electrical EPC, water EPC and civil EPC contracts. It is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. 

The SPVs Current Infra Bolnada Solar Pvt Ltd, Current Infra Talabera Solar Pvt Ltd, Current Infra Ompura Solar Pvt Ltd and Current Infra Dhanbad Solar Pvt Ltd are undertaking the Solar Independent power production plants for solar electricity generation. 

Mr Sunil Singh Gangwar, Ms Sujata Gangwar, Mr Satyavrath Singh and Mr Devvrath Singh manage the business. 

CIL’s shares are listed on the NSE EMERGE platform.
