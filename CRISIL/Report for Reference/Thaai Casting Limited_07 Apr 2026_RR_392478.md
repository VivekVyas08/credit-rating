### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable’ rating on the long term bank facilities of Thaai Casting Limited (TCL). 

The rating continues to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the automotive (auto) components and ancillary industry and TCL's healthy order book, diversified end-user industry base and moderate financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by its susceptibility to cyclicality in the auto industry and to government regulations and vulnerability of the operating margin to volatility in raw material prices 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of TCL and Thaai Induction and Nitriding Pvt Ltd, as the companies have significant business and operating linkages and are managed by common promoters. 

Crisil Ratings has revised its analytical approach and has no longer combined the business and financial risk profiles of Simtech CNC as the promoters of TCL exited the partnership on February 2, 2026, and the entities now have no common promoters. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive industry experience of the promoters and healthy order book:** The promoters have experience of over three decades in the auto components and ancillary industry. This has given them a strong understanding of the market dynamics and enabled them to establish healthy relationship with suppliers and customers. Over the years, the company has been certified by major original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) such as Hyundai, Amphenol, Tata and Mahindra, as their supplier for auto components. Revenue rose to Rs 122.21 crore in fiscal 2025 from Rs 70.96 crore in fiscal 2024, and the company has already booked Rs 62 crore in the first half of fiscal 2026, supported by the addition of new customers and order book of Rs 520 crore as of March 2025. 

**Diversified end-user industry base:** TCL has longstanding relationship with customers and suppliers. It caters to a diversified end-user industry base which includes auto, non-auto, agriculture, power and textiles. This allows the company to overcome the risk of slowdown in a particular industry and achieve higher growth. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility to cyclicality in the auto industry and to volatility in raw material prices:** The business risk profile is susceptible to inherent cyclicality in the auto industry, linked to the performance of the economy. The key raw materials are different grades of aluminum ingots, the prices of which are volatile. As raw material cost comprises 44-50% of the operating income, the operating margin is susceptible to sharp movements in input prices, as reflected in the margin ranging from 24% to 28% in the past three fiscals. 

**Risks associated with ongoing project:** The group is undertaking debt-funded capex of Rs 97 crore, funded through debt of Rs 68 crore, to introduce a new product, which is expected to commence production from April 2026. Timely commencement of production, leading to increase in revenue and net cash accrual, sufficient to meet debt obligation, remains monitorable. 

**<u>Liquidity</u>** Stretched 

Bank limit utilisation was high at 90% on average during the 12 months through December 2025. Annual cash accrual is expected over Rs 22 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 19 crore over the medium term. 

The current ratio was moderate at 1.3 times as on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes TCL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and established relationship with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Healthy revenue growth of over 25% with sustenance of operating margin over 23%, leading to higher net cash accrual Improvement in the financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue and operating margin falling below 20%, resulting in lower cash accrual Any delay in project commencement, weakening the financial risk profile Stretch in liquidity, particularly higher bank limit utilisation 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

Tamil Nadu-based TCL was established as a partnership firm called Thaai Casting in 2010 and was reconstituted as a public limited company with the present name in June 2023. TCL is an auto ancillary company, specialising in high-pressure die casting, as well as the precision machining of ferrous and non-ferrous materials and induction heating and quenching. The company caters to auto as well as non-auto sectors. The manufacturing unit is in Tiruvallur, Tamil Nadu, with installed capacity of 2,500 tonne of high-pressure die casting. 

TCL got listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) Emerge platform in February 2024. 

TCL is promoted by Anandan Sriramulu, Shevaani Anandan and Chinraj Venkatesan.
