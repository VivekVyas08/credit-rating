|**Total Bank Loan Facilities Rated**|**Rs.480 Million**|
|---|---|
|**Long Term Rating**|**Suspended**|



_(Refer to Annexure 1 for Facility-wise details)_ 



CRISIL has suspended its rating on the bank facilities of A B Cotspin India Ltd (ABCIL). The suspension of rating is on account of non-cooperation by ABCIL with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, ABCIL is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess ABCIL's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings'. 



### **_About the Company_** 

ABCIL, promoted by Mr. Piyush Kansal, Mr. Suresh Kumar, and Mr. Rahul Goyal, was set up as a cotton ginning and spinning unit with 18,000 spindles in Bhatinda (Punjab). The company began commercial operations in December 2011.
