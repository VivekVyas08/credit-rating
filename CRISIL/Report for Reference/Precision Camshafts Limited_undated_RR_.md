CRISIL has suspended its ratings on the bank facilities of Precision Camshafts Ltd (Precision). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by Precision with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, Precision is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess Precision's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings' 



### **_About the Company_** 

Promoted by Mr. Yatin Subhash Shah, Precision manufactures camshaft castings and machined camshafts at its units in Solapur (Maharashtra). Camshafts are key components for internal combustion engines of automobiles and locomotives to operate valves. In the domestic division, the company has one foundry and one machining unit, with capacities of 1.5 million and 0.54 million camshafts per annum, respectively. In its export-oriented unit (EOU), it has three foundries and two machining units, with capacities of 9.6 million and 0.06 million camshafts per annum, respectively. Precision is in the process of enhancing its capacity in the EOU division by about 1.5 times; the increased capacity is expected to become operational by December 2012.
