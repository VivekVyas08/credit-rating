### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Foods and Inns Limited (FIL). 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of FIL’s promoter in the food processing business, the company’s established market position, geographical diversification in revenue and healthy capital structure . These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility of revenue and profitability to volatility in fruit prices, large working capital requirement and moderate working capital requirements. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of FIL. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoter:** The promoters’ group, has been in the food processing industry for more than four decades, which has helped him develop deep understanding of the market dynamics and establish healthy relationships with customers and suppliers. This will continue to support the business. The daily operations are managed by an experienced professional management team. 

**Established market position in the food processing industry:** FIL caters to several reputed customers, including top beverage companies such as Coca-Cola and Pepsico, both in India and overseas. The company generates 35-40% of revenue from exports, primarily to Europe. Coupled with capacity expansion, this has led to healthy revenue over the two fiscals through 2025, with Rs 972 crore achieved in fiscal 2025 and Rs 565 crore in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. Revenue is expected to remain healthy over the medium term, backed by healthy demand and product additions leading to sustained volume growth. Diversity in geographical reach and clientele along with addition of new products should continue to support the business **.** 

**Healthy capital structure** : Overall capital structure remains healthy as indicated by networth and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio which stood at Rs 530 crore and 1.4 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. The capital structure is estimated to improve further in fiscal 2026 and over the medium term backed by steady accretion to reserves and absence of large debt funded capex plans. 

### **<u>Key rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility of revenue and profitability to volatility in fruit prices:** Tropical fruit processing units can operate only during the harvest season (May to July for mangoes). Furthermore, the yield varies each year, rendering the company's revenue susceptible to availability of mangoes. Also, the mango harvest is affected by weather conditions which impact prices. As a result, FIL’s operating margin has been volatile, ranging from 8% to 11% over the four fiscals through 2023. The operating margin improved since fiscal 2024 due to income from the production-linked incentive (PLI) scheme, net off which the margin would be 9.5-11.0%. The company has limited bargaining power due to intense industry competition. Currently, mango pulp is the major revenue contributor (70-80%). However, the company is adding products and expects revenue to increase from other products over the medium term. 

**Large working capital requirement:** Operations are working capital intensive, as reflected in gross current assets (GCAs) of 333 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by moderate receivables and large inventory of 83 days and 203 days, respectively. The company provides credit of around two months to customers while the inventory is high due to the seasonal nature of the raw material. However, delays in payment from customers can affect liquidity. The GCAs were high, as on March 31, 2025, due to significant increase in advances paid to suppliers for procurement of raw materials, which is expected to ease as on March 31, 2026. Hence, management of the working capital cycle amid increasing scale of operations remains monitorable. 

**Moderate debt protection measures** : Debt protection metrics were moderate, as reflected in interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios of 2.2 times and 0.15 time, respectively, in fiscal 2025. While the same stood at to 1.9 times during first nine months of fiscal 2026, sustained improvement in the debt protection metrics with reduced reliance on external debt will remain monitorable over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation was high at 91% on average for the 12 months through April 2026 and has exceeded 98% some months primarily during the procurement season. While bank limit enhancement received in November 2025 provides liquidity cushion, moderate utilisation of the limit will be a key rating sensitivity factor going forward. Annual cash accrual is expected to be Rs 40-50 crore (without accounting for subsidies of around Rs 70 crore expected over the next two fiscals), against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 20-21 crore over the medium term. The surplus will cushion liquidity. The current ratio was moderate at 1.37 times, and the company had cash and bank balance of around Rs 15 crore as on March 31, 2025 which is unencumbered. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes FIL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Improvement in the working capital cycle and financial risk profile with interest coverage ratio more than 2.8 times along with reduced bank limit utilisation 

Significant increase in revenue with stable operating margin leading to higher cash accrual 

# **Downward factors** 

Increasing working capital requirement leading to stretched liquidity with bank limit utilisation sustaining above 90% Decline in revenue or operating margin leading to net cash accrual less than Rs 30 crore 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

FIL, a public limited company incorporated in 1967, processes and markets fruit pulp, purees, concentrates and spray dried fruit and vegetable powders in the domestic and international markets. The company processes mango, guava, banana and tomato pulp. It has manufacturing units in Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat and Maharashtra. The company is promoted by Bhupendra Dalal and is professionally managed. 

It is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Mr Dalal is the chairman.
