### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Centum Electronics Limited (CEL; part of Centum group) to ‘ **Positive** ’ from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at ' **Crisil BBB** '. The rating on the short-term bank facilities has been reaffirmed at ‘Crisil A3+ 

The revision in outlook reflects anticipated improvement in the operating profitability of the Centum group, following the discontinuation of its Canadian subsidiaries, Centum E&S (Centum Equipment’s ET Systems) and Centum T&S (Centum Technologies ET Solutions) and initiation of a judicial reorganisation (redressement judiciaire) for Centum T&S Group Société Anonyme (S.A.) in France. 

These subsidiaries had previously weighed down the group's consolidated operating margin, which stood at 8.88% for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 (as against 8.40% and 8.31% in fiscals 2025 and 2024, respectively). In contrast, the standalone operating margin was significantly higher at 12.07% for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 (as against 13.64%, and 12.65% for fiscals 2025 and 2024). The subsidiaries incurred losses mainly due to the built-to-specification nature of projects undertaken. The group's obligation to accommodate ad-hoc client requests and delays in obtaining approvals for modified projects resulted in idle, non-productive resources and material, ultimately leading to losses. This issue was prevalent in multiple contracts, contributing to losses in the subsidiaries over the five fiscals ending March 31, 2026. The group's operating margin is expected to gradually improve to 11–12% in the medium term, post discontinuation of operations in subsidiaries. Revenue from existing operations is likely to grow by over 20% on year, even as consolidated revenue could moderate in fiscal 2027, owing to discontinuation of operations in subsidiaries. 

The standalone order book (Rs 1,424 crore as of December 2025) provides healthy revenue visibility in the near term. As per the applicable accounting standards, the group has recognized impairment of goodwill and intangible assets in its consolidated financials, and impairment of investments in subsidiaries under standalone financials. However, most of these items are non-cash and hence unlikely to affect overall cash flow. While the management believes adequate provisions have been made in Q3FY2026 with further smaller provisions to be made in Q4FY2026, Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor any potential write-offs in the financials in the near term. 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters and the professional management, the company’s established track record in the strategic electronics business unit (SEBU) and electronic manufacturing solutions (EMS) segments, and its comfortable financial risk profile. These rating strengths are partially offset by working capital-intensive operations and susceptibility to risks posed by any change in technology or sourcing policies of customers. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings continues with the analytical approach of consolidating CEL with its subsidiaries on account of high degree of operational fungibilities along with common management. The analytical approach will be appropriately revised once the subsidiaries are wound up or delinked from CEL.

**<u>Key rating drivers - Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position, aided by the extensive experience of the promoters:** The promoters and the professional management team at CEL have diverse industry experience spanning around three decades. CEL caters to various enduser industries, including defence, space, aerospace, transportation, automotive, energy and healthcare. The company has maintained longstanding relationships with most of its customers, which include the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and Indian Space Research Organization (ISRO), with whom it has been associated for 20-25 years. Over the years, these associations have ensured a steady flow of repeat orders and a strong trust factor between the entities. Given the high technical complexity and know-how, the customers preferred to stay with their suppliers. Products used in critical areas, such as space and defence, bear testimony to the technical capability of the group. CEL is engaged in two broad segments, namely EMS and SEBU, ensuring diversity in revenue profile. 

Strong understanding of market dynamics would continue to support the business risk profile. Over the decades, CEL has developed strong engineering and design capabilities, which enable it to fulfil the changing demands of its customers. CEL had orders in hand worth Rs 1,424 crore as on December 31, 2025, vis-à-vis Rs 1,118 crore as on March 31, 2024, spanning across business segments and aided by its established track record of timely and satisfactory completion of projects. Further, established relationships with its key customer, continued innovation, and improving prospects of clean energy will support sustenance of healthy growth over the medium term. 

**Comfortable financial risk profile:** Debt protection metrics are comfortable marked by estimated interest coverage and NCATD (net cash accruals to total debt) of over 3.50 times and 35% respectively in fiscal 2026 as against 3.07 times and 20% respectively for fiscal 2025. Net worth and gearing continue to remain healthy, estimated at around Rs. 300 crore and 0.35 times respectively as on March 31, 2026. Total outside liabilities to tangible net worth (TOL/TNW) is expected to be at 2.60 times as on March 31, 2026. However, with the expectation of healthy accretion to reserves and no major debt funded capex plans over the medium term, the capital structure is expected to improve over the medium term and would also remain a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Key rating drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility to changes in technology and sourcing policies of key customers:** Any change in technology will require realignment of products in line with the end-user technology. Delays in such adjustments could weaken the group's competitive position. Also, significant portion of revenue is derived from various public sector undertakings and defencerelated organisations such as DRDO, ISRO, and the Ordinance factories of the Government of India. 

**Working capital-intensive operations:** Gross current assets were sizeable at 290 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by inventory and receivables of 139 days and 99 days, respectively, as on the same date. The company needs to hold inventory of various components and receivables too tend to stretch. However, the tenders generally cater to projects which are funded or have an allocated budget. Any significant stretch in working capital cycle, constraining liquidity, would remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation is moderate at around 60 percent for the past twelve months ended February 2026. Cash accrual is expected to be over Rs 60-80 crore which is sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 5-10 crore over the medium term. Current ratio is moderate at 1.20 times on March 31, 2025. Group had maintained healthy cash and bank balances to the tune of Rs. 85 Crore on average for the past 12 months ending December 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

Crisil Ratings believes CEL shall continue to benefit from its established market position and witness improvement in its operating margin. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained revenue growth rate while maintaining operating margin of more than 9% resulting in higher-than-expected accruals against repayments. 

Sustenance of working capital management along with improvement in financial risk profile specifically TOL/TNW. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Sustenance of TOLTNW to more than 3 times due to higher working capital debt or debt funded capital expenditure. Stretch in liquidity due to stretch in working capital or material moderation in accruals

CEL manufactures products for the rail transportation market, focusing on improving energy efficiency, security, and real-time information access. CMD is ably supported by his professional team consisting of Joint Managing Director Mr Nikhil Mallavarapu, five independent directors and Group CFO Mr Sundararajan Parthasarathy. It is listed on BSE and NSE.
