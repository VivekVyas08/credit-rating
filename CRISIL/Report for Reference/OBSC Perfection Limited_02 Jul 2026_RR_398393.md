### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil BBB+/Stable’** rating to the long-term bank facilities of OBSC Perfection Limited (OBSCPL). 

The rating reflects the extensive experience of the promoters of OBSCPL, diversified product profile & healthy orderbook and the comfortable financial risk profile of the company. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicality in the end user industries, working capital intensive operations and moderate scale & customer concentration. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of OBSCPL. 

Unsecured loans from group company and directors of Rs 15.45 crore as on 31<sup>st</sup> March 2026 has been treated as debt since same is expected to be repaid over long term. 

### **<u>Key rating drivers - Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoters:** The promoters experience of nearly a decade in the auto components and ancillary industry has helped establish strong relationships with customers leading to repeat orders. The company has an established customer base and has added clients across various industries in both domestic and overseas markets. This has led to revenue growth to Rs 220 crore in fiscal 2026 from Rs.115 crore in fiscal 2024. Revenue is further expected to increase over the medium term driven by healthy demand, continuous addition of new SKUs, customers and increase in manufacturing capacity. 

**Diversified product profile and healthy order book:** The company offers a diverse range products such as spline shafts, piston rods, drive shafts, steering shafts and ignition primers comprising of an extensive portfolio of around 100+ SKUs catering to varied automotive, defense, marine, and telecommunication industries. Currently the company has outstanding orders of Rs 1200 crore as on March 31, 2026, to be executed over the next 5-6 fiscals, which provide revenue visibility. 

**Comfortable financial risk profile:** The company’s capital structure remained healthy, as reflected in gearing of 0.4 time and low total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio of 0.72 time as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics have been comfortable due to low leverage and healthy profitability. The interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCADD) ratios were 9.72 times and 0.47 time, respectively, in fiscal 2026. Debt protection metrics are expected to remain at a similar level over the medium term. Financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term in the absence of any major debt-funded capital expenditure (capex). 

### **<u>Key rating drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to cyclicality in end-user industries:** The demand for OBSCPL products is correlated with the end-user industries i.e automotive, industrial development, defense, marine, and telecommunication industries. The performance of these industries are driven by factors such as economic policies, demand and supply situation, prices of raw materials. Any downturn in any of the application industries or macro-economic conditions impacting the application industries will impact the company topline and its profitability. However, over the years the company has been continuously diversifying its products and improving its application industries. 

**Working capital intensive operations:** Gross current assets (GCAs) remained elevated at 133-223 days over the three fiscals ended March 31, 2026, reflecting the company’s working capital intensive operations. GCAs stood at 223 days as on March 31, 2026, driven by high receivables and inventory levels. The company is required to extend relatively long credit periods to its export customers, leading to elevated debtor levels. Additionally, the nature of operations necessitates maintaining sizeable work-in-process and inventory levels. With export sales expected to be higher over the medium term the working capital requirement is expected to increase. The ability to manage working capital efficiently over the medium term with limited reliance on external borrowings and maintenance of adequate liquidity will be monitorable. 

**Moderate scale and customer concentration:** The company generates 50–55% of its revenue from its top five customers, with the largest customer contributing 15–20%. While OBSCPL remains a key supplier to these customers, any adverse change in their demand patterns or vendor selection policies could impact the company’s business risk profile and will remain a key monitorable over the medium term. Additionally, despite improvement in the last few fiscals, the company’s scale of operations remains modest. Sustained growth in scale will be critical and will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit fund-based utilisation averaged at 64% for the 11 months through April 2026 with peak utilisation touching 95% in February 2026, recent enhancements is expected to aid liquidity. Expected cash accrual of more than Rs 37 crore per fiscal, should suffice to cover the debt obligation of Rs 8–12 crore over the medium term. Current ratio stood healthy at 1.60 times as on March 31, 2026 and cash and cash equivalents were moderate at Rs. 0.07 crores as on March 31, 2026. Crisil Ratings expects internal accruals, cash & cash equivalents and unutilized bank lines to be sufficient to meet its repayment obligations and incremental working capital requirements over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes OBSCPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and their established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained growth in revenue by more than 40% and sustenance of operating margin over 18%, leading to higher net cash accruals. 

Improvement in working capital management with GCAs below 210 days along with sustenance of financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or profitability, leading to net cash accrual of less than Rs 25 crore 

Large debt-funded capex or a substantial increase in working capital requirement, weakening the capital structure and liquidity. 

# **<u>About the company</u>** 

OBSCPL, incorporated in 2017, manufactures precision steel and metal components for automotive, defense, marine, and telecommunication industries. The manufacturing facilities are in Pune, Chennai and Faridabad. Operations are managed by Saksham Leekha. 

The company is listed on the National Stock Exchange’s small and medium enterprises platform.
