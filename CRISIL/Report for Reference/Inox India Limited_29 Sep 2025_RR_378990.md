### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+ **’** ratings on the bank facilities of Inox India Ltd (IIL). 

The ratings continue to reflect IIL’s leading market position in the domestic cryogenic storage industry, improving global footprint backed by a sizeable order book with a healthy mix of overseas orders, healthy financial risk profile and strong parentage as part of the reorganized Inox group (Pavan Jain and Siddharth Jain faction). These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition in international markets and susceptibility of the revenue to the investment climate in its various end-user industries including iron and steel, and oil and gas. 

IIL’s business risk profile continues to benefit from its strong market position in the cryogenic equipment segment in India and healthy share of exports. The business risk profile also benefits from the increasing demand for small-scale liquefied natural gas (LNG) due to its varied applications such as industrial heating, captive power generation as well as for high horse-power applications such as heavy-duty trucks and buses, mining trucks and marine engines. IIL is also strengthening its presence in the cryo-bio and life sciences segment with new products developed for vaccines, stem cells, blood and bio specimens. IIL has also been steadily improving its global footprint and has secured high value supply contracts from South Korea, Japan and Europe. The company had set up a plant at Savli in Gujarat in fiscal 2024 to ramp up its capacity for manufacturing stainless steel beverage kegs, which are used widely in the food & beverage industry. 

IIL achieved healthy revenue growth of 15% in fiscal 2025 to Rs 1,306 crore (Rs 1,132 crore in fiscal 2024) driven by steady increase in demand and realizations for major products. It had a healthy order book of ~Rs 1,457 crore as of June 2025, which is expected to be completed in the next 9-15 months. This will ensure healthy growth momentum over the medium term. The operating margin was strong at 22.4% in fiscal 2025 against 22.7% in fiscal 2024, with contraction of ~70 basis points in gross margins partially offset by increasing scale of operations translating into operating leverage gains. The operating margin is expected to remain stable over the medium term. 

The healthy financial risk profile is supported by negligible debt and strong net worth at Rs 864 crore as on March 31, 2025. The company plans capital expenditure (capex) of ~Rs 80 crore per annum towards regular maintenance and some expansion, which is expected to be funded entirely through internal accruals and liquid surplus. 

The strong liquidity position of IIL is reflected in unencumbered cash surplus of ~Rs 294 crore as on March 31, 2025. The working capital is managed efficiently, supported by increased share of localized raw material procurement and healthy cash accrual. In the absence of any debt-funded capex plan, the interest coverage is expected to remain strong over the medium term. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of IIL, INOXCVA Europe BV and INOXCVA Comercio E Industria De Equipamentos Criogenicos Ltd (Inox Brazil), collectively referred to as the IIL group. This is because the companies have a common management and business interests.

**-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Strong market position in the cryogenic storage industry in India:** The IIL group is the largest manufacturer in the cryogenic tank segment in India, with a market share of 70-75% in fiscal 2025. It is a market leader in related products such as cryo containers (~50% share), vaporizers and disposable cryogenic cylinders. Its customer base consists of large engineering companies and companies engaged in the manufacture and distribution of cryogenic gases. The clients include Air Liquide (France) for its worldwide operations, Praxair, Linde, ITER, Inox Air Products. 

Improvement in IIL's performance is expected to be driven by the revival of capex in the industrial gas and LNG segment, along with increased traction in new segments such as cryo-bio and life sciences and beverage kegs. Of the order book of ~Rs 1,457 crore as on June 30, 2025, the industrial gas segment accounted for ~58%, LNG for ~28%, and the remaining comprised others. The large order book provides healthy revenue visibility over the medium term. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** The company maintained low debt of Rs 43 crore as of March 2025, down from the peak of Rs 419 crore as on March 31, 2018. The balance sheet is expected to remain debt free due to modest capex plan of ~Rs 80 crore from fiscal 2026 onwards, which is expected to be funded entirely through internal accrual and liquid surplus. The interest coverage is expected to remain comfortable over ~40 times over the medium term. 

**Benefits of being part of the Inox group:** IIL benefits from being a part of the diverse Inox group, which has presence in air gases and cryogenic tanks. Leading companies in the group are among the top two in their respective sectors in the country and are financially strong. Besides, the promoters have supported IIL in the past through infusion of funds to offset losses in the overseas subsidiaries and to retire debt. Promoter support is expected to be forthcoming in the event of any exigencies. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to intense competition in international markets:** The IIL group faces stiff competition in the international cryogenic products market. Although the group has a strong market position in India, where it commands a dominant market share, its scale of operations is modest in the international market (accounted for 53% of the consolidated revenue in fiscal 2025). The group has been gradually increasing its footprint in the global market, and the revenue share of exports is expected to remain at 50-60% over the medium term. 

**Susceptibility of revenue to slowdown in end-user segments:** The IIL group primarily operates in the capital goods sector, which is cyclical in nature, and susceptible to international policies governing end-user industries, such as oil and gas and industrial gases. The group's revenue is closely linked to the investment climate in its end-user segments, and its performance will remain susceptible to any slowdown in the end-user segments. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

IIL has a strong liquidity profile with cash surplus of over Rs 294 crore as on March 31, 2024. Modest proposed capex of ~Rs 80 crore per annum from fiscal 2026 onwards is expected to be funded entirely through internal accrual, projected at Rs 200-250 crore per annum. IIL has cushion in the form of fund-based bank limit of Rs 205 crore which was utilized just ~18% on average over the 12 months through July 2025. Crisil Ratings expects the liquidity profile to remain strong over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes IIL will continue to benefit from its improving business risk profile led by strong operating performance and healthy order book, which should further improve the capital structure backed by steady cash flow. Crisil Ratings believes the promoters will continue to provide support during any exigencies. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Significant increase in revenue driven by increasing product and geographical diversification, and operating margin maintained over 22-24% 

Sustenance of the financial risk profile, including through prudent management of working capital and capex Maintenance of healthy liquidity in the normal course of business. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Sluggish business performance resulting in operating margin below 17% on a sustained basis 

Significant rise in debt due to sizeable expansion or acquisition or stretch in the working capital cycle, impacting key debt metrics 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

IIL started commercial operations in 1993 as a manufacturer of cryogenic tanks. The company later diversified into manufacturing atmospheric vaporizers, cryoseal containers, and disposable gas cylinders. It mainly operates in three product segments: industrial gas, LNG and cryo-scientific. It manufactures cryogenic tanks for liquefied gases, cold convertor systems, disposable gas cylinders, cryoseal containers, atmospheric vaporisers, liquid cylinders, beverage kegs and cryogenic containers. The IIL group also manufactures cryogenic transportation vessels and pumpers used in oil and gas exploration. 

IIL has four plant locations in India. It has two subsidiaries: (i) INOXCVA Comercio E Industria De Equipamentos Criogenicos Ltda, a service unit started in 2012 at Sao Paolo in Brazil. (ii) INOXCVA Europe B.V, a trading set up started in 2014 in Netherlands. 

IIL (on a consolidated basis) registered net revenue of Rs 340 crore and profit after tax (PAT) of Rs 61 crore in the first quarter of fiscal 2026 (Rs 296 and Rs 53 crore, respectively, in the corresponding period of fiscal 2025).
