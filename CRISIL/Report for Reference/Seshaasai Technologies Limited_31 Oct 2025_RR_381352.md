### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Seshaasai Technologies Limited (STL; Formerly known as Seshaasai Business Forms Private Limited (SBFPL) to **‘Crisil A+/Stable’** from ‘Crisil A/Stable’ while the shortterm rating has been reaffirmed at ‘Crisil A1’. 

The upgrade factors in the overall improvement in the credit risk profile of the group. The business risk profile has improved, driven by significant expansion in operating margins to 25% in fiscal 2025 from 19.3% a year ago, driven by better product mix, better operating efficiencies opted by the company including better logistics operations as well as availing cash discounts leading to improved gross margins. While operating margins are expected to sustain above 21%, the scale of operations have also remained healthy in the range of Rs 1450-1600 crore over the past two fiscals through fiscal 2025 and is expected to sustain its growth over the medium term. The gradual increase is driven by strong market position, new product developments customized to the evolving technologies, continued capex and healthy ramp up in the same with high repeat orders and established customer relationships. 

The rating action also incorporates the improvement in financial risk profile, driven by strong networth of Rs 621 crores as on March 31, 2025, which is further improved backed by equity funds raised through initial public offering (IPO) of around Rs 450 crores. Proceeds from the IPO have been used towards debt reduction in third quarter of fiscal 2026 leading to significant improvement in the capital structure, with gearing estimated to reduce to below 0.5 time for fiscal 2026 year end, compared with 0.8 times as on March 31, 2025. Debt protection metrics have remained healthy and are further expected to improve. Liquidity continues to remain strong. 

The rating continues to reflect established market position backed by long track record and extensive experience of the promoters, diversified and reputed clientele with increasing product portfolio, strong operating efficiencies, competitive advantage with pan-India presence and strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by moderate working capital requirement and revenue concentration in the BFSI segment. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of STL and its two subsidiaries, Rite Infotech Private Limited and Atol Solutions Private Limited referred to as the Seshaasai group. This is because these entities are strategically important to, and have significant operational integration with, STL.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position backed by long track record and extensive experience of the promoters:** The group is promoted by Mr. Pragnyat Lalwani and Mr. Gautam Jain, who have more than three decades’ experience in the security data printing business. It has held its market position in its key sector, BFSI, for more than two decades, leading to 75% of revenues from long-standing customers. Over the years, the group has developed new products and has been customizing solutions for its customers while keeping up with evolving technologies, leading to healthy CAGR of 28% over the last four years ending fiscal 2025. The company has undertaken multiple initiatives for fostering innovation in the Indian financial 

ecosystem. The credentials built by the company in the BFSI sector have enabled them to expand their product portfolio as well as increase their market share with large customers leading to strong market position in the sector. 

**Diversified and reputed clientele with increasing product portfolio:** Over the last couple of years, the group has been diversifying its product offerings in the payment solutions, communication and fulfillment solutions and IoT solutions. The company has developed multiple products including payment wearables, key fobs, wristbands and stickers, customized RFID products and expanded into other sectors such as telecom, information technology (IT), logistics and e-commerce. The group has a strong and diversified clientele in the BFSI, telecom, IT, retail and e-commerce sectors, though BFSI remains one of the key end market. Top customers include State bank of India, Bank of Baroda, LIC of India, Punjab National Bank among others. The group also caters to brands such as Amazon, Flipkart and other e-commerce players for loyalty, gift cards, wallet, co-branded cards and merchant onboarding kits. The top 5 customers contributed 49-50% revenue over the past two fiscals through fiscal 2025. 

**Competitive advantage with pan-India presence and strong operating efficiencies:** The group offers the entire range of security data printing services and has operations in 24 locations across the country. Sufficient in-house capacity and diversity in geographical reach helps reach a broader customer base. Its technological advantages along with better operating efficiencies opted by the group, including better logistics operations as well as availing cash discounts have led to improved gross margins. This along with better product mix have lead to improvement in the margins to 25% in fiscal 2025 as compared to 19% a year ago. The group can cater to large requirements leading to benefits of economies of scale, and hence, remained competitive in the unorganized and fragmented market. Also, it faces limited competition because of stringent security approvals and high compliance cost for key security printing products. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile is supported by strong net worth of Rs 621 crores as on March 31<sup>st</sup> , 2025, which is further improved backed by equity funds raised through initial public offering (IPO) of around Rs 450 crores. Proceeds from the IPO have been used towards debt reduction in third quarter of fiscal 2026 leading to significant improvement in the capital structure, with gearing estimated to reduce to below 0.5 time for fiscal 2026 year end, compared with 0.8 times as on March 31, 2025. Debt protection metrics are adequate, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 10.8 times and 0.7 time, respectively, for fiscal 2025 and is further expected to improve driven by reduced reliance on external debt. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate working capital requirement:** Working capital cycle is moderate marked by gross current assets (GCA) days of 153 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by debtors and inventory of 73 days and 51 days respectively. The group has to maintain inventory to cater to regular as well as ad hoc orders and offers credit of 2-3 months to its customers. However, with increasing cash accruals and cash inflow from the proceeds of IPO, the reliance on creditors has been continuously reducing and was at 38 days as on March 31<sup>st</sup> , 2025 (as compared to 100 days as on March 2022). The working capital cycle will continue to remain moderate over the medium term. 

**Revenue concentration in the BFSI segment:** Although the group is focusing on diversifying its clientele into telecom, retail and IT, more than 80% of its revenue comes from BFSI, including private and public banks, mutual funds and insurance companies. This exposes the operations to changes in overall industry dynamics and has to keep its services and facilities abreast with technological changes in the banking sector. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash accruals, expected around Rs 200-250 crore per annum, are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 20 crore and Rs 7 crore in fiscals 2026 and 2027, respectively; leaving majority of the accrual to support liquidity. Bank limit utilization was around 51% on average for the 12 months through August 2025. Unencumbered cash balance was about Rs 124 crore as on March 31, 2025, of which Rs. 20 crore is lien marked. The cash balance have further increased driven by cash proceeds from IPO though partly earmarked for future capex. Current ratio is estimated at 1.9 times as on March 31, 2025. The company has sufficient gearing headroom to raise additional debt to meet its capex and working capital requirement. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the business risk profile will continue to benefit from the strong market position and established customer relationships, while sustaining its financial risk profile 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Better-than-anticipated revenue growth by around 40-50% along with sustenance of operating profitability leading to stronger operating cash flows 

Sustenance of strong financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors:** 

Lower than expected  revenue or operating profitability below 20 % on a sustained basis leading to lower cash accruals Sizeable, debt-funded capex, or further stretch in the working capital cycle impacting financial and liquidity profile 

**<u>About the Company</u>** 

STL was incorporated in 1993 and is promoted by Mr. Pragnyat Lalwani and Mr. Gautam Jain. The company is a technology driven multi-location solutions provider focused on offering payments solutions, and communications and fulfilment solutions catering primarily to the banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) industry, while adhering to data security, as well as compliance. 

The company has three business verticals including payment solutions, communication and fulfillment solutions and IoT Solutions. The company is based in Mumbai and has 24 manufacturing facilities across India. The company got listed on BSE and NSE on 30<sup>th</sup> September 2025.
