### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of DCM Nouvelle Ltd (DCMNL; part of DCM Group) to ‘ **Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2** ’ from ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’. 

The rating upgrade factors in the sustained improvement in the overall credit profile of the group. Group reported revenue of around Rs. 1076 crores in fiscal 2025 (Rs. 1082 crores in fiscal 2024; on account of slight moderation in volumes of yarn sales as the demand for China declined). However, the impact of decline in demand from China did not have any substantial impact on the revenue as it was mitigated through higher exports to Bangladesh and increase in domestic sales. The group is expected to clock over Rs 1100 crore in fiscal 2026. Operating margins improved to around 6% at a group level in fiscal 2025 as against 4.1% in fiscal 2024 due to increased gap between cotton prices and cotton yarn prices resulting in better operating margins. Operating margins are expected to further improve (range between 6.0-6.5%) supported by savings in power cost (group is setting up a solar power plant which is expected to commence operations from November 2025) and automation and upgradation of manufacturing facilities resulting is reduction of labour costs. 

Financial risk profile has continued to remain healthy with networth of around Rs 306 crore as on March 31, 2025 and is expected to improve to around Rs 325 crore for the fiscal ending March 31, 2026. Total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) was 1.35 time as on March 31, 2025 (1.4 times as on March 31, 2024) and is expected to remain at a similar level for the fiscal ending March 31, 2026, in absence of any major debt funded capital expenditure plan. Interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratio were 2.3 times and 0.13 time in fiscal 2025 and are expected to be around 2.8-3 times and 0.15-0.17 respectively over the medium term. 

The ratings reflect the established position of the group in cotton industry supported by strong relationships with customers and extensive experience of the promoters, and group’s healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuations in raw material prices and exposure to demand risks in key operating countries.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established position in the cotton industry and strong relationships with customers:** The promoter family has been operating the textiles unit since 1989, which has enabled them to understand market dynamics and establish healthy relationships with customers and suppliers. The group exports to China, Bangladesh, Portugal, Mauritius and Singapore. Group reported revenue of around Rs. 1076 crores in fiscal 2025 (Rs. 1082 crores in fiscal 2024; on account of slight moderation in volumes of yarn sales as the demand for China declined). However, the impact of decline in demand from China did not have any substantial impact on the revenue as it was mitigated through higher exports to Bangladesh and increase in domestic sales. The group is expected to clock over Rs 1100 crore in fiscal 2026. The 

established market position and healthy clientele will continue to aid the business risk profile over the medium term as well. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile has continued to remain healthy with networth of around Rs 306 crore as on March 31, 2025 and is expected to improve to around Rs 325 crore for the fiscal ending March 31, 2026. Total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) was 1.35 time as on March 31, 2025 (1.4 times as on March 31, 2024) and is expected to remain at a similar level for the fiscal ending March 31, 2026, in absence of any major debt funded capital expenditure plan. Interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratio were 2.3 times and 0.13 time in fiscal 2025 and are expected to be around 2.8-3 times and 0.15-0.17 respectively over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuations in raw material prices:** As cotton is an agricultural commodity, its availability depends on the monsoon. Furthermore, government interventions and fluctuations in global cotton output may result in sharp movement in cotton prices, thereby impacting the profitability of spinning mills. Adverse market conditions will likely result in moderation in the cotton-yarn spread. Operating margins improved to around 6% at a group level in fiscal 2025 as against 4.1% in fiscal 2024 due to increased gap between cotton prices and cotton yarn prices resulting in better operating margins. Operating margins are expected to further improve (range between 6.06.5%) supported by savings in power cost (group is setting up a solar power plant which is expected to commence operations from November 2025) and automation and upgradation of manufacturing facilities resulting is reduction of labour costs. The subsidiary is currently reporting operating losses as it is initial stage of operations and ramp-up in the scale of operations and improved profitability will remain key monitorables. 

**Exposure to demand risks in key operating countries:** Exports account for around 40% of total revenue, majority of which are to Europe, China and Bangladesh. Bangladesh is one of the largest exporters of textiles in the world and any economic disturbance there could adversely affect the business risk profile of the group. There was a slight moderation in volumes of yarn sales as the demand for China declined in fiscal 2025. However, the impact of decline in demand from China was mitigated through higher exports to Bangladesh and increase in domestic sales. Sustenance of demand from the overseas market will remain monitorable over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Adequate</u>** 

Bank limit utilization is moderate and averaged at around 71 percent for the past twelve months ended April 2025. The net cash accruals are expected to be Rs 45-50 crore annually against repayment obligation of Rs. 9 crores in fiscal 2026 and around Rs. 15-16 crores annually over the medium term leaving sufficient cushion to meet the incremental working capital requirements. The current ratio was moderate at 1.3 times on March 31, 2025. Cash and bank balance stood at Rs 1.4 crore as on March 31, 2025. A healthy capital structure and strong networth support the financial flexibility and provide the financial cushion available in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

The group will continue to benefit from its established market position in the cotton industry, strong relationships with customers and strategic location of the plant. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** : 

Significant and steady increase in revenue supported by growth in volume sales along with operating margin sustaining at around 7% 

Improvement in debt protection metrics 

# **Downward factors** : 

Decline in revenue and operating margin falling below 5%, leading to net cash accrual below Rs 22 crore. Stretched working capital cycle or larger-than-expected, debt-funded  capex,  weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

DCMNL is a Hissar-based cotton yarn manufacturer with capacity of 158,000 spindles. Its textile unit at Hisar has been operational since 1991. Till March 31, 2019, the unit was part of DCM Ltd, post which it was demerged into DNL through a National Company Law Tribunal order. DNL is listed on the National Stock Exchange and the Bombay Stock Exchange. Mr Hemant Bharat Ram manages daily operations. 

DCM Nouvelle Speciality Chemicals Ltd. was incorporated in February 2022 and enagaged in manufacturing specialty chemicals with fiscal 2025 being first year of operations.
