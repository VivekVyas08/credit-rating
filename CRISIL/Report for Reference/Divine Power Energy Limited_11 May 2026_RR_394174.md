### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’** ratings to the bank facilities of Divine Power Energy Limited (DPEL). 

The rating reflects a strong business risk profile, supported by well-entrenched relationships with key customers, a diversified procurement and supply base, and established manufacturing capabilities. DPEL’s product portfolio comprises wires and strips, which find applications in critical industries, including the power and transformer industries. The scale of operations is estimated to improve to around Rs 770 crores in FY26 from Rs 426 crore in FY25, mainly due to the acquisition of Vimlesh Industries Private Limited. This acquisition has helped the company diversify into the automotive segment and benefit from the technical know-how of the company and technologically advanced production processes. It is expected that the group will achieve revenue of more than Rs 950-1000 crore in FY27. The business risk profile is further supported by the estimated operating margins of around 9%, mainly due to the absorption of lower-cost inventory and better absorption of fixed costs. These margins are expected to stabilize at around 7%-8% during the medium term. 

The group's net worth is estimated to remain healthy at Rs 136 crores as of March 31, 2026, which has increased due to funds infused through the IPO in FY25; the funds are utilized for working capital requirements and general corporate purposes. The capital structure of the group is estimated to remain moderate, with a TOL/TNW ratio of 1.78-1.85 times in FY26 and an interest coverage ratio of 4.5 times. Any major debt-funded capex impacting the financial risk profile will remain a key monitorable. 

The ratings reflect the extensive experience of the promoters, along with an established market position and comfortable operating margins. These strengths are partially offset by a moderate financial risk profile and risks associated with changes in government policies related to tightened environmental norms. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of DPEL, Vimlesh Industries Private Limited (VIPL), and Viraj Upkram Private Limited (VUPL). This is because all these entities, together referred to as the group, are under common management, operating in the same industry, and have operational and financial linkages.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Long track record of operations and extensive industry experience of the promoters:** DPEL has been manufacturing insulated and bare copper and aluminum wires and strips in India for over 20 years. The company has a strong presence in North India, with key markets in Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Uttarakhand, Haryana, Punjab, and Bihar. It has also recently expanded into markets in Karnataka and Gujarat. The promoters have experience of over two decades in the electrical components and equipment industry. This has given them an understanding of the dynamics of the market and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. DPEL has established a strong customer base over the years through its technical expertise, robust manufacturing facilities, and long-term relationships with leading industry players. The 

group is estimated to achieve revenue of around Rs 770 crores, which is expected to improve to around Rs 950-1000 crore in FY27. 

**Moderate Financial Risk Profile:** The financial risk profile is moderate, with an expected gearing ratio of around 1.5-1.6 times as of FY2026. The debt has increased as the group is undergoing capex for the new CTC machinery. Debt protection metrics are expected to be adequate in fiscal 2026, reflected in an interest cover of over 4.5 times and a total outside liabilities to tangible net worth ratio of close to 1.78-1.85 times. With no major debt-funded capex planned, the financial risk profile is expected to improve further. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Working capital intensive operations:** The company's operations are working capital-intensive, with gross current asset (GCA) days standing at around 130 days as of March 31, 2026, as against 122 days as of March 31, 2025. The company's inventory levels have increased, driven by the rise in prices of inventory, which can be seen in the estimated inventory remaining at 67 days as of March 31, 2026, as against 60 days as of March 31, 2025. Prudent working capital management amidst volatile raw material prices would remain a key monitorable. The working capital-intensive nature of the company is reflected in high limit utilization of over 90%; improvement in utilization will remain a key monitorable. 

**Susceptibility of the operating margin to volatility in raw material prices:** Operating margins are exposed to volatility in raw material prices, and raw material costs account for 80-90% of the operating revenue. Aluminium (main raw material) is mainly purchased from Hindalco Industries Ltd. The operating margins of the group are estimated to improve to around 9%, mainly due to an increase in raw material prices. These margins are expected to stabilize at around 7%-8% during the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

The expected cash accruals of the group are around Rs 42-45 crores in FY26, which should comfortably cover repayment obligations of Rs 10-12 crores over the medium term. The company's bank limit utilization (BLU) remains at around 91.7% over the past 12 months ended February 2026. Debtors have remained stable, with no instances of stretch in receivables seen in the month-on-month trend of working capital parameters. The current ratio is estimated at a healthy 1.45 times as of March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

- Sustained improvement in scale of operation and sustenance of operating margin of over 8-9% leading to higher cash accruals. 

- Improvement in the financial risk profile of the company along with utilization of working capital limits below 85% on sustained basis. 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in scale of operations or  profitability margin below 6%, hence leading to lower than expected cash accruals. Further stretch in the working capital cycle impacting the financial profile and liquidity of the group.

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

DPEL, formerly known as PDRV Enterprises Private Limited, was incorporated in 2001. DPEL is engaged in the manufacturing of electrical conductors, wires, and strips (insulated and bare copper and aluminum wires and strips) used in the power and transformer industry. DPEL has its manufacturing facilities in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad (U.P.). DPEL’s equity shares are listed on NSE EMERGE Platform. 

- Incorporated in 1986, VIPL is a wholly owned subsidiary of DPEL. It is engaged in manufacturing of Bare, Enamelled, Paper covered, Fibreglass covered, Cotton covered, Kapton covered, Nomex covered, Mica covered round & rectangular wires of copper & aluminium for use in electrical power equipments & appliances industries. Its manufacturing unit is located in Sonepat, Haryana. 

Incorporated in 2012, VUPL is engaged in manufacturing of copper & aluminium products.
