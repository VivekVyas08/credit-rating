### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of TD Power Systems Limited (TDPS; a part of the TDPS group) to **‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+’** from ‘Crisil A+/Positive/Crisil A1’. 

The rating upgrade takes into account sustained strengthening expected in the business risk profile of the TDPS group, driven by a 45% on-year growth in revenue to Rs 1,856 crore in fiscal 2026. Strong double-digit growth is expected over the medium term, supported by increasing demand for data centres and renewable energy (in the export market), steady demand for industrial applications (in the domestic market) along with healthy power-related demand. Export growth has outpaced domestic sales in recent fiscals, and a similar trend is expected to continue over the medium term. 

Revenue is expected to show healthy, high double-digit growth in fiscal 2027. This will be driven by healthy demand from end-user industries, increasing export orders and a strong order book of Rs 1,973 crore as on March 31, 2026, with average order execution period of 3–6 months. Operating profitability is likely to remain stable—with earnings before interest taxes depreciation and amortisation margin sustaining at 17–18% over the medium term—due to economies of scale, costoptimisation measures, healthy product mix, ability to pass on higher costs to customers and increasing focus on exports. 

The group’s operating performance improved significantly in fiscal 2026, with revenue increasing by 45% year-on-year to Rs. 1,856 crore, driven by robust growth in the gas engine and turbine generator segment, particularly in export markets, which supported strong order inflows and execution. Demand remained stable in the other key segments, including steam and hydro generators. The company’s operating margin has also remained healthy over the past few fiscals, at 18.1% in fiscal 2026, as against 18.5% in fiscal 2025 and 17.2% in fiscal 2024. 

Financial risk profile has been strong, backed by healthy tangible networth of Rs 1,062 crore (as on March 31, 2026), nil term debt and strong debt protection metrics (interest cover of 47 times in fiscal 2026). Robust liquidity (with cash and liquid investments of Rs 199 crore as on March 31, 2026) and annual cash accrual (projected at Rs 240–300 crore) will be sufficient to fund capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 60–80 crore over the medium term. The financial risk profile is expected to remain strong as the company has no plans of availing any significant debt. 

The ratings continue to reflect strong market position of TDPS in alternating current (AC) generator business, backed by a healthy order book, improving business risk profile and robust financial risk profile with negligible debt. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility of the operating performance to cyclical demand in end-user industries and customer concentration risks.

**-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u> Strong market position** 

The group is a leading manufacturer of AC generators in the 1–50-megawatt (MW) segment in India. It has supplied 8,055 generators till March 31, 2026, compared to 5,280 till March 31, 2022. The group has the capability to manufacture generators across steam, hydro, diesel and gas segments, and has a diverse end-user industry base that includes cement, sugar, data centre, oil and gas, metals and mining, and power generation (both conventional and renewable). Over the years, the group has been able to partially offset the slowdown in domestic demand by expanding into the overseas market (which accounted for ~70% of sales in fiscal 2026, compared with 40–50% around five years back) and building relationships with key multinational original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). 

# **Improving business risk profile supported by increasing scale and healthy profitability** 

The TDPS group had a healthy order book of Rs 1,973 crore as on March 31, 2026, diversified across the domestic and overseas markets. Order inflow increased to Rs 2,239 crore in fiscal 2026, registering a 51% on-year growth, from Rs 674 crore in fiscal 2022. This growth is driven by strong demand for gas generators and motors used in key high growth industries such as data centre and grid stabilisation for renewable power. Revenue recorded a compound annual growth rate of 24% during the four fiscals through 2026, supported by healthy end-user demand. Healthy double-digit growth is expected over the medium term due to strong order book, order visibility provided by customers and steady demand from key industries. 

Over the past decade, the group has gradually increased its presence in the export market and now supplies generators to 111 countries across the globe, with sizeable presence in Europe and North America through its own sales offices. To further diversify revenue streams, the group is scaling up its motor manufacturing segment. With expected improvement in scale of operations through higher execution, the continued economies of scale benefit coupled with cost-optimisation measures and the ability to pass on raw material price increases should help sustain the operating margin at 17–18% over the medium term. 

# **Robust financial risk profile** 

The financial risk profile has been supported by healthy tangible networth of Rs 1,062 crore and nil term debt as on March 31, 2026. The TDPS group reported minimal working capital utilisation in recent months to fund its expanding scale. Debt protection metrics are expected to remain strong, with interest cover above 40 times over the medium term. In December 2025, the group commissioned a new facility for manufacturing generators, motors and their sub-assemblies and parts; this ~Rs 140 crore project is to be fully funded through internal cash accrual. The group plans to incur capex of Rs 60–80 crore annually, which would be funded entirely through internal cash accrual. The expenses will be towards debottlenecking of capacities, incremental capacities in certain areas, maintenance and automation along with capex towards large generators. The financial risk profile should remain healthy over the medium term, in the absence of any large, debt-funded capex, strong cash accrual and adequate liquidity to meet the incremental working capital requirement. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Susceptibility of operating performance to cyclical demand in end-user industries as well as volatility in raw material costs** 

Demand for generators relies heavily on the capex of end-user industries, thereby exposing the group to the investment plans of customers, especially during an economic slowdown when many companies may defer capex. Profitability and return on capital employed had weakened significantly in the past (fiscals 2015–2018) on account of low-capacity utilisation following slowdown in end-user industries. Though operating performance has improved in the recent fiscals, with increasing global presence and applications across varied industries, it remains susceptible to volatility in raw material prices. While strategic pricing of contracts and the ability to pass on with a lag partially help combat price fluctuations to an extent, sudden sharp hikes in commodity prices continue to pose a threat to profitability. 

# **Customer concentration in revenue** 

The TDPS group’s core generator manufacturing revenue remains highly concentrated, with 60–70% sales being generated from top 10 customers that include Siemens, INNIO Jenbacher GmbH and Triveni Turbine Ltd, etc. This exposes the group to counterparty-specific risks, including fluctuations in ordering patterns from key customers, changes in sourcing strategies, or loss/reduction of business from any large OEM due to competitive pressures, technological shifts or internal vendor rationalisation. However, these risks are partially offset by longstanding relationships with customers and a stringent prequalification process for onboarding new suppliers. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash and liquid surplus stood at Rs 199 crore as on March 31, 2026, of which Rs 120–130 crore remains unencumbered. Liquidity is also supported by the unutilised bank limit of Rs 75 crore as of April 30, 2026 (total limit of Rs 75 crore), which was utilised at just ~5% during the 12 months ended April 30, 2026. In the absence of any yearly debt obligation, the available liquidity and annual cash accrual (projected at Rs 240–300 crore) will be more than sufficient to fund the ongoing capex and working capital requirement. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The credit profile of the TDPS group will remain healthy over the medium term, with the business risk profile benefiting from a healthy order book, improving scale and sustained operating performance. Healthy cash-generation ability and negligible reliance on debt will ensure strong financial risk profile. 

**<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Sustained double-digit revenue growth, driven by strong order inflows, alongside improving customer and product segment diversification 

- Sustained healthy operating efficiency with operating margins maintained at 17-18%, supported by prudent working capital management 

Sustenance of healthy capital structure and debt protection metrics 

# **Downward factors** 

- Weaker-than-expected performance with muted revenue growth and operating margin below 12–13% on a sustained basis 

Large, debt-funded capex or inorganic expansions deteriorating the leverage and coverage indicators Weakening liquidity profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Based in Bengaluru, TDPS commenced operations in 2001 and manufactures AC generators of up to 200 MW. It has three units, one of which is a dedicated large generator manufacturing plant. The company also has a facility in Turkey. 

TDPS is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange. As on March 31, 2026, 26.87% stake was held by the promoters, 19.08% by mutual funds, 24.58% by foreign portfolio investors, ~19% by individuals. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

TDPS group comprises of TDPS Ltd and its subsidiaries including DF Power Systems Pvt Ltd, TD Power Systems (USA) Inc, TD Power Systems Europe GmbH and TD Power Systems Jenerator Sanayi Anonim Sirketi.
