### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil AA+/Stable’** rating to the Rs 750 crore non convertible debentures of Balkrishna Industries Limited (BIL). Crisil Ratings has also reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities and commercial paper programme. 

The ratings continue to factor in BIL’s strong business risk profile, led by its established market position in the off-highway tyres (OHT) segment, geographic and segmental diversification of revenue and strong operating efficiency. The company has developed its product portfolio over the years, by venturing into new sub-segments in the OHT space/new tyre specs — its stock keeping units (SKUs) have grown from 2,700 in fiscal 2019 to more than 3,600 currently. 

Revenues in 9MFY26 were flat YoY owing to the volume remaining flat with US and Europe volumes declining by double digits owing to the tariffs and the weakened demand in EU. However, this was offset by the strong growth in India by ~23%. Q3 saw a recovery in overall volumes led by continued strong growth in India as well as a QoQ recovery in volumes in Europe and US volumes by 16% and 11% respectively. This recovery is expected to continue into Q4 with the company expecting further improvement in the EU market as well as the US tariffs now reducing to ~15%. The volumes and revenues are therefore expected to show a slight growth in FY26 over FY25. Revenues in FY27 are expected to grow by 7-9% driven by the continued OHT volumes in India, higher revenues from the carbon black segment and recovery in EU and US markets. Thereafter, the revenues are expected to grow at upwards of 10%, driven by the revenues from the new tire segment (CVR&PCR), scale-up of the carbon black revenues and the continued momentum in the OHT tire segment 

BIL, being a low-cost manufacturer of high-value specialised OHT, commands industry-leading margins. The earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (Ebitda) margin improved to 26.2% in fiscal 2025 from 25% in fiscal 2024 with higher operating leverage and price hikes taken during the year. However, margins weakened to 21.0% in 9MFY26 from 23.6% in 9MFY25 owing to reduction in high margin export sales, partial absorption of tariffs, and lower absorption for fixed cost. With the expected recovery in the US and EU market, and continued growth in India over the medium term, margins are expected to improve, although offset by the increasing share of new lower-margin businesses. Although margins are expected to moderate, they are still expected to remain healthy around 20-23% 

BIL’s financial risk profile remains strong. The company had announced a capital expenditure plan (capex) of Rs 3,500 crore over the span of 3 years towards the development and operationalization of Carbon Black production, Premium Passenger Car Radials (PCR) and Commercial Vehicle Radials (CVR) projects during May 2025. Including the earlier announced OHT expansion capex of ~Rs. 1300 crores and other maintenance capex, the total capex planned over the next 3 years is ~Rs. 5300-5500 crores part of which is already spent post the announcements. This will be funded through a mix of internal accruals and external debt. The company is planning to raise Rs. 750 crores of NCD in March 2026 for reimbursing the capex and is expected to raise the debt to which would result in a net debt of ~Rs. 1000 crores in FY26. However, the debt metrics are expected to remain strong over the medium term. 

The strengths are partially offset by the vulnerability of the company’s operating performance to fluctuations in raw material prices, susceptibility to regulatory actions in importing countries, and to volatility in foreign exchange (forex) rates.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position:** BIL’s market share in the international OHT segment has increased steadily over the years to ~6%, backed by association with major global original equipment manufacturers, wide distribution network in more than 160 countries, and a varied product portfolio. Capacity expansion as well as the new carbon black plant should help BIL leverage the benefits of low-cost manufacturing. The company is also entering the Premium Passenger Car Radials (PCR) and Commercial Vehicle Radials (CVR) segment to fuel future growth and increase its presence in India. Supported by its widening distribution network and ability to cater to niche, small order lots, the company has diversified its geographical presence over time, from being a majorly Europe-focussed player (from 54% of revenue in fiscal 2022 to 45% in fiscal 2025), to other geographies such as America (from 17% of revenue in fiscal 2022 to ~15% in fiscal 2025) as well as the Indian market (from 18% of revenue in fiscal 2022 to ~28% of revenue in fiscal 2025). 

**Healthy operating efficiency:** Manufacturing OHT is a labour-intensive process, BIL’s presence in low-cost locations results in strong operating efficiency. As the company’s employee cost is lower vis-à-vis most global peers, BIL’s products are competitively priced. Also, the company has maintained operating profitability of over 24% for the past eight years, barring fiscal 2023, when it dipped to 20%, which is still healthy and better compared with peers. Backward integration into carbon black and access to more captive power augur well for the company’s operating efficiency. Besides, BIL is able to quickly cater to small niche order lots from clients, which permits for healthy profitability as well. Owing to the US tariffs as well as the new lower margin CVR and PCR tire segments, the margins are expected to moderate. However, they are still expected to remain healthy above 21-22% driven by the continued growth in India over the medium term. 

**Robust financial risk profile:** The company’s strong cash generating ability and prudent funding of capex over the years have helped solidify its balance sheet and shore up liquidity as well. Considering the announced Capex and sustaining capex, the total capex planned over the next 3 years is ~Rs. 5300-5500 crores part of which is already spent. This will be funded through a mix of internal accruals and external debt. The company is planning to raise Rs. 750 crores of NCD in March 2026 for reimbursing the capex and is expected to raise the debt to which would result in a net debt of ~Rs. 1000 crores in FY26. However, the debt metrics are expected to remain strong over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Vulnerability to fluctuations in input prices:** Raw materials account for ~70% of BIL’s production cost and the prices of key raw materials, natural and synthetic rubber, are volatile as they depend on global demand, area under cultivation and crude oil prices. BIL’s operating profitability declined to 20% in fiscal 2023 from 27% in fiscal 2022, because of a lag in passing on rising input and ocean freight cost to customers. Thus, operating performance is highly correlated to crude oil prices and the company’s ability to pass on price increases to customers will remain monitorable. 

**Exposure to regulatory risks:** In 2023, the EU announced the European Union Deforestation Regulations (EUDR) to combat global deforestation led by consumption. Adherence to this regulation requires that the natural rubber used in supplies to the EU are sustainably sourced and free from deforestation or environmental harm, tracking of which would increase costs. Additionally, the External Producer Responsibility (EPR) regulations set by the Central Pollution Control Board, part of the Ministry of Environment Forest and Climate Change, require tyre companies to offset their producer obligation through purchase of EPR credits from recyclers that use domestic end-of-life waste tyres. Compliance with these regulations could increase operating costs for BIL. However, the implementation of EUDR has been delayed by a year. While the cost is passed on to customers, in line with industry practice, BIL’s ability to manage its operating profit amid these regulations will be monitorable. 

**Volatility in forex rates:** Around 50% of the raw material is imported. Also, the entire borrowings are in foreign currency, exposing BIL to the risk of sharp fluctuations in forex rates. However, with the bulk of revenue coming from exports, the exposure to forex risk is largely naturally hedged. Furthermore, the company’s net receivables are covered by staggered forward contracts. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

BIL will maintain strong liquidity, supported by expected adequate annual cash accrual of over Rs 1,600 crore over the medium term against long-term debt of only about Rs 722 crore (as of March 2025), raised at low cost. Furthermore, the company has strong liquidity with cash, investments in liquid funds and unencumbered cash and bank balance of Rs 2976 crore as on December 31, 2025. Bank limits were utilised moderately at 67% on average in the 12 months through January 2026. The capex plans of ~Rs. 5300-5500 crores will be funded through a mix of internal accruals and external debt. The company is planning to raise Rs. 750 crores of NCD in March 2026 for reimbursing the capex. Net Cash Accruals of over Rs. 1600 crores will be sufficient to cater to the annual repayments of ~Rs 300-400 crores along with the capex requirements 

# **<u>ESG Profile</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes the ESG profile of BIL supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

The tyre sector has a significant impact on the environment because of high greenhouse gas emission in core operations as well as waste generation. The sector has a social impact because of its large workforce. BIL has focused continuously on mitigating its environmental and social impact. 

# **<u>ESG highlights</u>** 

All the company’s plants are implementing various projects to reduce greenhouse gas emissions. The company constantly monitors green energy production processes and outcomes as well as the implementation of emission reduction programmes. During fiscal 2025, off-gas accounted for ~60% of total energy consumption. 

The company remains committed to embedding resource efficiency and circular economy practices in its operations. In fiscal 2025, ~98% of total waste generated was diverted from traditional disposal methods, with ~60% recycled and the remaining ~38% reused. 

- All the plants have safety committees which identify work-related hazards and assess risks on periodic basis. Any identification of work-related hazard or risk is appropriately addressed as part of the process. The company continues to maintain its record of zero fatalities at the workplace for the third consecutive fiscal year, underscoring its focus on a safe and secure work environment 

The company’s governance structure is characterized by a Board comprising 50% independent directors and ~25% women directors, along with a dedicated investor grievance redressal mechanism and robust financial disclosures 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

BIL is likely to continue consolidating its strong market position globally and sustain its industry-leading operating margin, supported by backward integration initiatives, notwithstanding the temporary tepid business environment, especially in key overseas markets. The company is also likely to sustain its strong balance sheet and solid liquidity, due to its steady cash generating ability and moderate capex plans 

# **<u>Rating Sensitivity Factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

Robust double-digit growth in volume on a sustained basis supported by geographic and segmental diversification, resulting in reasonable growth in the global market share, with operating margin sustaining above 26-27% Improvement in the return on capital employed backed by healthy profitability and completion of capacity expansion plans. 

Sustained robust financial risk profile, with healthy capital structure and liquid surplus 

# **Downward Factors** 

Sustained decline in operating revenues caused by increased competition or impacted by significant strain on exports also resulting in sustained decline in operating profitability below 18-20% 

Weakening in the financial risk profile and liquidity, because of large capex or acquisition. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Based in Mumbai, BIL manufactures OHTs used in vehicles meant for agricultural, mining, industrial, construction, and earth-moving purposes. Achievable capacity across its plants in Bhuj (Gujarat), Waluj (Maharashtra), Bhiwadi and Chopanki (both in Rajasthan) is 360,000 tonne per annum. The company has a wide product profile and sells in more than 160 countries. It also has over 3,600 SKUs to cater to varied requirements of customers. In fiscal 2024, ~73% of revenue came from exports. The carbon black facility at Bhuj with total achievable capacity of around 265,000 has commenced operations and is running at full achievable capacity. 

The promoter group, Mr Poddar and his family continues to hold 58.29% of the company. 

In the nine months of fiscal 2026, BIL recorded revenue of Rs 7,890 crore and profit after tax of Rs 944 crore as against Rs 7,694 crore and Rs 1,286 crore, respectively, in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal.
