### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Agarwal Industrial Corporation Limited (AICL) to **'Negative'** from 'Stable' while reaffirming the rating at **'Crisil A'.** 

The revision in outlook reflects moderation in the group’s operating performance, marked by decline in revenues and profitability margin in 9-month ended December 2025. The revenues declined by around 20% and operating profitability by around 260 basis points (bps), on year, for period April-Dec 2025. This was driven by moderate demand for bitumen as onset of the early and extended monsoons slowed construction activity, as well as decline in realizations. Further, shipping segment was also impacted due to geopolitical disruptions, impacting shipping movements. With on-going situation in the Middle East, which is major supplier base for AICL, revenue and profitability may be further impacted and hence remain monitorable. The strong financial risk profile and adequate liquidity, supports the credit profile. 

The rating continues to reflect extensive experience of AICL’s promoters and established market position in the bitumen products business, diversified revenue profile with established clientele, prudent risk management strategies and strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicality in end user industry, susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in raw material prices and forex rates and exposure to supplier concentration risk. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at its ratings, Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of AICL, and its subsidiaries, which are strategically important to, and have a significant degree of operational integration with AICL. These companies are - Bituminex Cochin Private Limited, Agarwal Translink Private Limited, AICL Overseas FZ LLC, AICL Finance Private Ltd and Konkan Storage Systems (Karwar) Private Limited. Crisil Ratings considers these entities as being strategic to AICL in view of their strong integration with AICL’s operations.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position in the bitumen industry:** AICL group benefits from the extensive business experience of the promoters of over four decades, their understanding of the dynamics of the local and global bitumen market, and established relationships with suppliers and customers. The group’s strategically located manufacturing facilities and storage units has helped in reduction of transportation cost. A combination of steady demand and strong realizations has led to a significant increase in scale with a CAGR of 15% for the past three years ending fiscal 2025. Crisil Ratings believes that the AICL group would maintain its established presence aided by extensive experience of promoters in the industry. Increasing demand and customer base has helped increase revenues steadily to Rs. 2401 crores in fiscal 2025 from Rs.904 crores in fiscal 2021. However in current fiscal the revenues have moderated due to the moderation in bitumen demand due to onset of the early and extended monsoons slowed construction activity and furthermore the revenues and margins from shipping business have moderated due to geopolitical situations. The group has reported revenues of Rs. 1247 crores for first 9 months of fiscal 2026 (degrowth of around 21% from 9 months last fiscal) and is expected to achieve revenues of around Rs.2000-2100 crores for fiscal 2026. 

**Diversified revenue profile with established clientele:** Apart from being a manufacturer and trader of bituminous products, AICL group provides integrated solution to its customers in terms of transportation. AICL group is also engaged in 

transportation of LPG cylinders, power generation through windmill and ship chartering business. AICL transports around 60-70% of bitumen through its own vessels leading to lower freight expenses for the parent company AICL. The profitability from ship chartering business offsets for the moderate margins in the Bitumen trading segment. In addition, the group benefits from the established customer and supplier network in domestic and international markets which supports repeat business across segments. 

**Comfortable financial risk profile:** Continuous accretion to reserve has strengthened the networth, which is expected to be Rs 660-680 crore as on March 31, 2026. In the absence of any major dividend outgo, the networth will increase over the medium term. Despite increase in working capital requirement, the gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio remain healthy and are expected to be 0.5-0.65 time and 0.9-1 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2026, from 0.69 time and 1.15 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. The debt protection metrics are comfortable, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios expected at 4.4-4.6 times and 0.25–0.35 time, respectively, for fiscal 2026. Financial risk profile is expected to remain strong with accretion to reserve and moderate profitability over the medium term. 

**Prudent risk management strategies:** AICL group has prudent risk management strategies marked by moderate credit extended to customers and low inventory holding. The average inventory maintained over the past 3 years ended fiscal 2025 is low at around 23-33 days. Majority of the purchases are order backed while certain inventories are maintained in order to cater to urgent demand from its customers. Counterparty risk is mitigated to some extent by a large and diversified clientele and established long relationship of 3-4 decades with major clients. It is also able to pass on its foreign exchange exposure to customers. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility of the operating margin to volatility in raw material prices:** The price of the main raw material, bitumen has been extremely volatile in the past as it is a derivative product of crude oil. Thus, any sudden sharp increase or decrease in the price of bitumen, can impact the operating margins. Also, in the shipping business, any fluctuation in the prices of fuel is covered in the freight rates charged to the customer. Thus, although, company is able to pass on this increase to customers, inability to do so fully or lag in passing on, has led to some volatility in operating margins that have ranged from 6.3% to 8.6% in the past six fiscals, through fiscal 2025. With high volatility in crude prices and geopolitical situations In Current fiscal the margins are expected to moderate to around 6-7%. 

**Exposure to cyclicality in end user industry:** The entity caters to the infrastructure, industrial gases, and power industries. The end-user industries are cyclical and are strongly correlated to economic cycles. In the past, because of economic recession, the construction sector faced a slowdown, impacting credit profiles of players. This also leads to a stretch in debtors from small clients, reflected in sizeable debtors more than 6 months of Rs.68 crores and Rs.56.94 crores as on March 31 and September 30, 2025 respectively. Furthermore, in this current fiscal due to onset of the early and extended monsoons slowed construction activity leading to softer demand for bitumen in end user industry leading to moderation of revenues and profitability. 

**Exposure to supplier concentration risk:** The company majorly procures its bitumen from suppliers based in Middle East, with 80-85% from top 3 suppliers. High supplier concentration limits price negotiation capability. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Annual cash accrual is expected to be Rs 100-150 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 65-79 crore over the medium term. Bank limit utilisation was moderate at around 81% on average for the last 6 months through December 2025. The current ratio was moderate at 1.14 times as on March 31, 2025 and Cash and cash equivalents were high at Rs 44.5 crores coupled with liquid mutual funds of Rs.37.52 crores as on September 30, 2025. Crisil Ratings expects internal accruals, cash & cash equivalents and unutilized bank lines to be sufficient to meet its repayment obligations and incremental working capital requirements over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Negative 

Crisil Ratings believes the business risk profile of AICL may remain under pressure over the medium term  due to geopolitical situations impacting revenues and profitability. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

- Significant improvement in scale of operations or sustained improvement in operating margins above 8%, leading to higher cash accruals. 

- Sustenance of Working capital management along with improvement in greater than 6 months debtors, financial risk profile. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in net cash accruals below Rs.85 crore on account of decline in revenue or operating margin. 

Substantial increase in its working capital requirements or large debt funded capex, weakening its liquidity and financial profile. 

**<u>About the Company</u>** 

AICL, formerly known as Bombay Baroda Roadways India Limited, was incorporated in 1995. AICPL is primarily engaged in manufacturing of bitumen and bitumen products. It also transports Liquified Petroleum Gas (LPG) and bituminous product. AICPL also forayed into ship operating and chartering business through its subsidiary in UAE. 

The company is headquartered in Mumbai (Maharashtra) and is promoted by Mr. Jaiprakash Agarwal, Mr. Lalit Agarwal, Mr. Ramchandra Agarwal and Mr. Mahendra Agarwal. The manufacturing units are located at Belgaum, Hyderabad, Taloja and Baroda. The company is listed on Bombay and National Stock Exchanges.
