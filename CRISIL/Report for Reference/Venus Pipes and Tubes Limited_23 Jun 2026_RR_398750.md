### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Venus Pipes and Tubes Limited (VPTL) to **‘Positive’** from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at **‘Crisil A’** . Crisil Ratings has also reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A1’ rating on the short-term bank facilities of the company. 

The revision in outlook reflects improvement in the business and financial risk profiles of VPTL. Revenue grew to Rs 1,167 crore in fiscal 2026, from Rs 958 crore in fiscal 2025, owing to healthy demand from domestic and export markets. The operating margin stood at 17% in fiscal 2026. The financial risk profile was comfortable, with gearing estimated at 0.43 time as on March 31, 2026, and adequate debt protection metrics. Further sustenance of revenue growth and profitability will remain a key monitorable. 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the stainless steel pipes business along with increasing scale of operations, healthy operating margin and moderate financial risk profile of the company. These strengths are partly offset by large working capital requirement and exposure to risks related to completion of capital expenditure (capex) and ramp up in scale. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of VPTL. 

### **-** **<u>Key rating drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Experienced management and longstanding relationship with clients** 

The top management, which includes the promoters—Megharam Chaudhary, Jayantiram Chaudhary, Arun Kothari and Dhruv Patel—has been engaged in steel pipes and tubes manufacturing for more than a decade. Their strong understanding of the market dynamics and healthy relations with customers and suppliers should continue to support the business. Operating revenue recorded a compound annual growth rate of more than 30% for the past five fiscals, reaching Rs 1,167 crore in fiscal 2026. 

### **Increasing scale of operations and healthy operating margin** 

Revenue increased to Rs 1,166 crore in fiscal 2026, up from a modest Rs 552 crore in fiscal 2023, supported by higher capacity, successful ramp-up in operations and strong demand. The operating margin also rose to 17–18%, from 12.6%, aided by higher-margin orders. While doubling capacity aids revenue growth, set up of the hollow pipe manufacturing unit (operational since May 2023), expansion of capacity in the seamless division and commencement of the fitting division (in May 2026) will further boost revenue and operating margin. 

### **Moderate financial risk profile** 

The capital structure remains comfortable, as reflected in healthy networth of around Rs 668 crore and low gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio of 0.43 time as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics were adequate, as indicated by interest coverage of 4.95 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio of 0.44 time for fiscal 2026. 

The company is also undertaking capex of Rs 75 crore in fiscal 2027, which will be funded through internal cash accrual and external debt. The financial risk profile will likely remain healthy over the medium term, aided by steady accretion to reserve 

and modest debt. 

# **<u>Key rating drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Large working capital requirement** 

Gross current assets (GCAs) were estimated at 215 days as on March 31, 2026, driven by receivables of 145 days and inventory of 81 days. Because supplier credit remains moderate, the company relies heavily on the working capital limit. However, healthy accretion to reserve, enhancement in the bank limit along with increase in scale of operations will support the working capital cycle, which will remain a key monitorable. 

# **Exposure to capex risks** 

The company plans to undertake capex of Rs 75 crore during fiscal 2027 to set up a spooling division. The expenses will be funded through a mix of internal cash accrual and debt, with marginal impact on gearing. Timely completion of the project with no major cost overrun and subsequent ramp up of operations will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity should remain supported by the ample surplus available in cash accrual and bank lines. Cash accrual is projected at more than Rs 150 crore per annum, against yearly debt obligation of Rs 26 crore over the medium term. Bank limit utilisation was 74% for the 12 months through April 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

The business risk profile of VPTL will further strengthen over the medium term, driven by steady growth in operating revenue, extensive experience of the promoters and their established relationship with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Significant revenue growth and operating margin sustaining above 17%, leading to higher-than-expected cash accrual Improvement in the working capital cycle, with reduction in GCAs 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or profitability, resulting in cash accrual less than Rs 110 crore 

Additional large, debt-funded capex/acquisition or a further stretch in the working capital cycle, weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **<u>About the company</u>** 

VPTL, incorporated in February 2015, manufactures stainless steel pipes and tubes used in multiple industries. Strategically located on the Bhuj-Bhachau highway near Dhaneti in Kutch (Gujarat), its facility is in close proximity to the Kandla and Mundra ports. The company has manufacturing capacity of 48,000 tonne per annum (TPA) of welded and seamless pipes and tubes and 20,400 TPA of mother hollow pipes. Megharam Chaudhary, Jayantiram Chaudhary, Arun Kothari and Dhruv Patel are the promoters.
