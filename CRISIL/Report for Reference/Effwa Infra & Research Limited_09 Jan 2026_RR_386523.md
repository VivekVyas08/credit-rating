### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’** ratings on the bank facilities of Effwa Infra & Research Limited (Effwa) and subsequently **withdrawn** the rating of Corporate Credit Rating at the company’s request.  This is in line with the Crisil Ratings withdrawal policy. 

The rating reflects extensive experience of the promoters in industrial wastewater treatment industry, healthy unexecuted order book along with improving operational efficiencies and its comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by working capital intensive operations, moderate yet improving scale of operations and their susceptibility to tender based operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

While arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has evaluated standalone business and financial risk profile of Effwa. 

Unsecured loans of Rs 2.33 Crores as on March 31, 2025, extended by the promoters are treated as debt. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive industry experience of the promoters in the wastewater management industry:** Company benefits from the extensive experience of its promoters, Dr. Varsha Kamal and Mr. Subhash Kamal, who possess over three decades of domain expertise in wastewater management industry. This technical understanding has been instrumental in the company’s ability to meet pre-qualification criteria for complex tenders, particularly in niche segments like Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems. The promoters’ established track record has helped them develop strong relationships with key clientele, evidenced by repeated orders from marquee customers such as Tata Steel (29+ projects completed by Effwa since inception), 4<sup>th</sup> project under execution for Afcons Infrastructure Limited, 7<sup>th</sup> Contract under execution for Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL) and other major PSUs like IOCL and BPCL. This has helped them build strong technical credentials which directly support revenue growth, as seen in the revenue improving to Rs 185 crores in fiscal 2025 from Rs 55 Crores in fiscal 2021. Crisil Ratings believes that the extensive promoter experience will continue to support the business risk profile of the company. 

**Healthy unexecuted order book and improving operational efficiencies:** Company’s healthy order book, standing at over 4x of FY 2025 revenues as of December 2025, provides adequate revenue visibility for the near to medium term given the project execution timelines of 12 to 18 months. The order book to revenue ratio has improved significantly in current fiscal year supported by improved credentials and timely execution of orders in hand in the past. Additionally, company has healthy orders bid pipeline with a bid to win ratio of ~25-30% which shall help sustain the healthy order book quantum over medium term, in turn providing revenue visibility. Moreover, company has demonstrated its competence through its ability to execute complex, technically demanding projects such as Zero Liquid Discharge systems, which is also margin remunerative. This is further supported by in-house design and engineering capabilities that minimize subcontracting risks and cost overruns leading to sustained improvement in operating leverage. As a result, operating margins have improved and sustained above 16% range for last two fiscal year ended FY 2025. This has also resulted in healthy RoCE of 35% in 

fiscal 2025 (44% in fiscal 2024). With steady growth in the scale of operations and healthy profitability the same is expected to remain above 25% over the medium term. 

**Comfortable financial risk profile:** Net worth of the company improved to Rs 95 crores as on March 31, 2025, and has further increased to Rs 105 crores as on September 2025 supported by recent equity infusion on account of fund raise done by company via Initial Public Offering (IPO) during FY 2025 and healthy accretion to reserves. With healthy networth and limited dependence on external borrowings, capital structure has improved as reflected in gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted net worth ratio of 0.31 times and 0.58 times as on March 31, 2025 (0.40 times and 1.17 times a year ago). The debt protection metrics are also healthy backed by steady profitability with interest coverage and net cash accruals to adjusted debt ratio estimated above 10 times and above 1 time respectively for fiscal 2025.  In the absence of any major debt funded capital expenditure the overall financial risk profile of the company is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Working capital intensive operations:** The operations of the company are working capital intensive as highlighted by the gross current assets (GCA) of 243 days for as on March 31, 2025, (188 days a year ago). The moderation was largely on account of increase in the debtor days to 232 days as of March 2025 as against 168 days as of March 2024. This is driven by cyclicality in business where in the year end billings are high. Moreover, the debtors also include the retention money (as reflected in debtors above 6 months) as well which are cleared after defect liability period. Additionally, the company is required to maintain additional deposits as per business requirements. Inventory levels are low at around 4 days as of March 2025 (5 days a year ago) and is expected to remain in similar range which majorly includes the Work in progress or Unbilled portion of the work. Overall working capital cycle is supported by credit received from suppliers as evident in creditors of 40-90 days historically. Working capital operations are expected to remain large and a key rating sensitivity factor. 

**Moderate scale of operations and susceptibility to tender-based operations:** Despite a strong growth trajectory, company’s current scale of operations remains moderate as reflected in revenues of Rs 185 crores in fiscal 2025, which can constrain its financial flexibility and bargaining power compared to larger and more diversified infrastructure players. While the scale is visibly improving as evident in company achieving revenues of Rs 90 crores in H1 of fiscal 2026 (48% growth year on year over the same period last fiscal year), supported by its expanding order book, the company’s business model remains inherently susceptible to the risks associated with tender-based operations. Future revenue growth relies heavily on the company’s ability to successfully bid for and win contracts in a competitive environment, where the uncertainty of tender invitations and award timelines can lead to uneven order inflows and potential volatility in operational performance. Furthermore, the necessity to bid competitively to secure new projects may occasionally exert pressure on operating margins, making the company vulnerable to aggressive pricing strategies from competitors within the waste management and EPC sectors. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Cash accruals are expected of Rs 27-30 crores over medium term, which will be sufficient against the negligible repayment obligation. The utilization of the fund-based bank limits averaged at 72% for the last 12 months ended Oct 2025. Current ratio stood at 2.84 times with cash and bank balance of Rs 26 crores as on March 31, 2025 (majority of which remains encumbered against Bank Guarantees and Letter of Credits). Liquidity is further supported by the need-based support from the promoters in the form of unsecured loans. Crisil Ratings expects internal accruals, cash & cash equivalents and unutilized bank lines will be sufficient to meet its repayment obligations and working capital requirements. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believe Effwa will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, its healthy order book and its comfortable financial risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained improvement in scale of operations by 30% and sustenance of operating margins leading to higher than anticipated net cash accruals 

Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors** 

Witnesses a substantial increase in its working capital requirements thus weakening its liquidity and financial risk profile Decline in scale of operations or operating profitability leading to net cash accrual below Rs 8-10 crore per fiscal

Located in Thane (Maharashtra), the company is promoted by Mr. Subhash Kamal and Mrs. Varsha Kamal, who also look after its day-to-day operations. 

Effwa is listed on NSE SME platform.
