Please note that the rating(s) are based on best available information with the credit rating agency: the entity whose debt is being published via this rating rationale did not provide the requisite information needed to conduct the rating exercise or did not provide the No Default Statements (NDS) for the last three months and is therefore classified as 'non cooperative'. 

### **<u>Non cooperation by Issuer</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has been consistently following up with Deccan Cements Limited (DCL) for obtaining information through letter and email dated May 05, 2026 among others, apart from telephonic communication. However, the issuer has remained non cooperative. 

‘The investors, lenders and all other market participants should exercise due caution with reference to the rating assigned/reviewed with the suffix 'ISSUER NOT COOPERATING' as the rating is arrived at without any management interaction and is based on best available or limited or dated information on the company. Such non co-operation by a rated entity may be a result of deterioration in its credit risk profile. These ratings with 'ISSUER NOT COOPERATING' suffix lack a forward looking component.’ 

### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Despite repeated attempts to engage with the management, Crisil Ratings failed to receive any information on either the financial performance or strategic intent of DCL, which restricts Crisil <mark>Ratings’ ability to take a forward looking view on the entity's credit quality. Crisil Ratings believes that rating action on DCL is consistent with ‘Assessing Information Adequacy Risk’. Therefore, on account of inadequate information and lack of management cooperation, Crisil Ratings has migrated the ratings on bank facilities of DCL to ‘</mark> **<mark>Crisil BB/Stable/Crisil A4+ Issuer not cooperating’</mark>** <mark>from ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’.</mark> 

### **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Set up in 1979 as a public-limited company by the late Mr M B Raju, DCL manufactures cement at its plant in Bhavanipuram that began commercial production in 1982. It has a cement manufacturing capacity of 2.2 million tonnes per annum and currently the company is promoted by Ms. Parvathi.
