### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Rajratan Global Wire Limited (RGWL, part of the Rajratan group) to **‘Stable’** from ‘Negative’ while reaffirming the rating at ' **Crisil A+** '. The short-term rating has been reaffirmed at ‘Crisil A1’. 

The outlook revision reflects the improvement in the company’s business risk profile while maintaining a healthy financial risk profile and strong liquidity. The group reported an operating income of Rs 1,156 crore for fiscal 2026, reflecting an onyear growth of around 24%, supported by volume growth of 18.4% and improved realisations by 4%. Volume growth is likely to remain healthy at over 15% in fiscal 2027 on the back of improved capacity utilisation and increase in the share of revenue from existing customers and new clients. 

Despite operating margin dropping to 12.4% in fiscal 2026 from 13.59% in fiscal 2025 due to higher input costs in the fourth quarter the accruals have improved to Rs 88 crore against Rs 71 crore in fiscal 2025 with healthy volume and revenue growth. 

The financial risk profile continues to remain healthy with comfortable capital structure despite the ongoing debt-funded capital expenditure (capex), and with adequate liquidity backed by unutilised bank limits and healthy cash and bank balance of around Rs 22.51 crore as of March 31, 2026. 

The ratings continue to reflect the Rajratan group's strong market position in the tyre bead wire (TBW) industry, backed by its established presence in India and Thailand; extensive experience of the promoter; reputed and diverse clientele; and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicality in demand from the end-user industry, susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in raw material prices, and exposure to project risk. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of RGWL and its subsidiaries, which are strategically important to, and have a significant degree of operational integration with RGWL.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Strong market position in the TBW business, backed by an experienced promoter:** The group is a leading manufacturer of TBW in India with a sizeable market share, and the sole TBW manufacturer in Thailand. Aided by its large capacity and long track record, the group has been increasing its wallet share with key customers. Furthermore, high entry barrier (such as long product approval phase given the criticality of TBW in safety, and  high cost of setting up fresh capacity) implies that established market players such as the Rajratan group shall benefit from the rising demand. 

The group also benefits from the extensive experience of its promoter and managing director, Mr Sunil Chordia, who has over two decades of experience in this industry. His expertise has been instrumental in ramping up operations in Thailand and increasing capacities. 

**Established and diversified customer base** : The group has strong relationships with key customers such as MRF Ltd, Apollo Tyres Ltd (‘Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’), CEAT Ltd and Bridgestone Tyres in India, and caters to reputed clients such as Sumitomo Rubber (Thailand) Co Ltd and Yokohama Tire in Thailand. The capacity in Thailand meets local demand there as well as exports to other countries; the group derives 15-20% of its revenue from exports to the United States, Europe, Sri Lanka, Vietnam, Finland, Sweden, and Indonesia. Diversity in geographical reach and clientele, along with enhanced capacities, should continue to support the business. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Despite capital expenditure (capex), the financial risk profile remains strong, supported by healthy accretion to reserve. Networth was large at Rs 644 crore while gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio were comfortable at 0.50 time and 0.79 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (against 0.43 time and 0.72 times, respectively, a year before). Debt protection metrics improved in fiscal 2026, as reflected in interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 5.13 times and 0.27 time, respectively, against 4.56 times and 0.3 time, respectively, for fiscal 2025. The group may continue to incur debt-funded capex over the next two fiscals, and benefits from this will be closely monitored. 

**Efficient working capital management:** Gross current assets (GCAs) stood at 131 days as on March 31, 2026, driven by receivables of 81 days and inventory of 38 days. The group provides its customers a moderate credit period and payments are received on time. Inventory is generally order-backed and should remain moderate over the medium term. The GCAs were relatively higher in fiscals 2025 and 2026 as operations in the Chennai unit are at an initial phase, but should improve going forward over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to cyclicality in demand from end-user industries:** Performance is linked to demand for tyres. Generally, 70% of demand comes from the replacement segment, and the balance from new vehicles. Demand for tyres is linked to the performance of the auto industry and the overall economy. Cyclicality in the auto industry will continue to constrain the business risk profile. Sustenance of strong revenue growth and steady operating margin are critical during various business cycles. Furthermore, the business is dependent on tariffs and trade restrictions. Any unfavourable change in tariff or trade restrictions may impact the group's income and earnings. 

**Susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices:** Prices of key raw materials such as steel, copper and zinc tend to fluctuate sharply. As raw material cost comprises 60% of the operating income or cost of manufacturing, profitability remains susceptible to sharp movement in input prices. However, the group can pass on any such hike to its customers with a lag of about three months. Operating margin has been 12.40-13.59% over fiscals 2025-2026 amidst intense competition and higher input cost in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, and is expected to improve with ramp up in operations. Sustenance of the margin at healthy levels will remains monitorable over the medium term. 

**Exposure to project risk** : The group is currently executing Phase II of its Chennai plant expansion and simultaneously adding steel cord capacity for conveyor belts at its Indore facility, both of which involve sizeable capital expenditure. Completion and stabilization risk relating to the project persist, and completion of the same with no time or cost overruns remains monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Annual cash accrual is expected to be Rs 115-130 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 42-51 crore over the medium term. Bank limit utilisation was moderate averaged at around 68% on average for the last 12 months through March 2026. The current ratio was adequate at 1.21 times as on March 31, 2026 and unencumbered cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 22.51 crores as on March 31, 2026. Crisil Ratings expects internal accruals, cash & cash equivalents and unutilized bank lines to be sufficient to meet its repayment obligations and incremental working capital requirements over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the Rajratan group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter in the TBW industry and their established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Significant improvement in scale while sustaining healthy operating margin, leading to cash accrual of more than Rs 130 crores. 

Sustenance of Working capital management, financial risk profile and liquidity. 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in operating income or margin leading to cash accrual below Rs 80 crore 

Larger-than-anticipated capex or stretch in the working capital cycle weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity. 

# **<u>About the group</u>** 

RGWL was incorporated as Rajratan Wires Pvt Ltd by Mr Sunil Chordia in 1988 and commenced commercial production in 1991. The company got its current name in 2004. It manufactures TBWs and high-carbon steel wires and supplies to tyre 

manufacturers in India. Manufacturing units are in Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh; and Chennai, Tamil Nadu. It also has a unit through a subsidiary in Thailand. 

The company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange.
