### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil A1+’** rating to the short-term bank facilities of Brigade Enterprises Limited (BEL; a part of the Brigade group) and has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Positive’ rating on the long-term bank facilities. 

The continuation of ‘Positive’ outlook reflects expected improvement in the business risk profile, driven by on-year increase in pre-sales and collections envisaged for the current fiscal and over the medium term driven by healthy unsold inventory and strong launch pipeline. Reaffirmation of rating and continuation of positive outlook also factors sustained strong financial risk profile, marked by low leverage in the residential and hospitality segments, strong overall liquidity, and healthy financial flexibility. 

Operating performance in fiscal 2026 was moderate, with a marginal decline in residential sales to Rs 7,424 crore (Rs 7,847 crore in fiscal 2025) and real estate collections at Rs 5,480 crore (Rs 5,412 crore in fiscal 2025), primarily due to delays in project launches in Bengaluru, the company’s key micro market, owing to approval-related challenges. Crisil Ratings understands these challenges were largely industry-wide and have seen some resolution recently, as reflected in the launch of four residential projects in Bengaluru in March 2026, aggregating ~2.26 million square feet (msf) against total residential launches of 4.31 msf in fiscal 2026 and 6.18 msf in fiscal 2025 in Bengaluru. It is to be noted that BEL’s total residential launches for fiscal 2026 were at 8.31 msf as compared to 9.5 msf in fiscal 2025. 

Business visibility remains healthy, supported by a healthy pipeline and inventory of ~7.5 msf as on March 31, 2026, with pre-sales expected at Rs 8,500–9,000 crore and collections in the vicinity of Rs 6,500–7,000 crore in fiscal 2027. That said, timely regulatory approvals in Bengaluru and achievement of planned launches, translating into improvement in pre-sales and collections in the current fiscal, will remain key rating sensitivity factors. 

Furthermore, the company's low reliance on external debt for its real estate segment has led to healthy adequacy of committed receivables (committed receivables as percentage of debt obligation and construction costs) for ongoing projects at 100–110%. 

The commercial and retail segments have also shown improvement, with rental income exceeding Rs 1,300 crore in fiscal **-** 2026 (Rs 1,165 crore in fiscal 2025) due to hike in rentals under long term contracts and incremental new leasing. The business profile under the commercial portfolio is underpinned by robust occupancy levels, with leased commercial properties maintaining an occupancy rate of 85–90% and retail assets over 88%. Furthermore, projects equivalent to 3 msf are under construction in its commercial portfolio, which will further increase the rental income. 

The financial risk profile is expected to remain healthy with gross debt at Rs 5,231 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 4,444 crore as on March 31, 2025). The debt profile is expected to remain stable, with 88% of the total debt secured by leasegenerating assets, consistent with the level as on March 31, 2025. The proportion of lease rental discounting (LRD) debt is expected to remain above 80% of the total debt over the medium term. The company aims to maintain LRD loans at less than 4-5 times the annual lease rentals, with a healthy debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) of over 1.5 times. That said, BEL's debt protection metrics are expected to remain robust over the medium term, with key indicators such as the gross 

debt to operating cash flow ratio (excluding LRD) and operating cash flow to interest coverage ratio (for the residential business) expected to remain below 1 time and above 10 times for the residential segment, respectively. Any deviation from this understanding and any material debt-funded acquisition will be monitorable. 

Moreover, the company’s liquidity is robust with cash and equivalent of around Rs 2,900 crore (comprising Rs 1,400 crore in Real Estate Regulatory Authority [RERA]) as on March 31, 2026. The company's liquidity is further augmented by steady cash flow from its leasing business and the ability to raise additional LRD loans, if needed. Additionally, the company has a land bank with development potential of approximately 57 msf. 

The ratings continue to reflect the strong track record of the group in the real estate market in Bengaluru, diverse revenue profile and strong financial profile with low leverage in residential and hospitality segment. Further, financial flexibility is supported by the demonstrated refinancing ability and steady progress in construction of ongoing projects. These strengths are partially offset by geographical concentration in revenue and exposure to risks inherent in the real estate segment. 

# **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has fully combined the business and financial risk profiles of all ongoing and planned projects of BEL and those in its subsidiaries and associate companies. All these entities, collectively referred to as the Brigade group, are in the same business, have common promoters and share significant operational, managerial and financial linkages. 

The fully convertible debentures of Rs 257 crore (as on March 31, 2026) from GIC, have been treated as neither debt nor equity. These are long-tenured instruments, and the coupon and principal payment have no scheduled due date. GIC and BEL have jointly invested in three land parcels till date. 

Also, unsecured loan of Rs 38 crore, as on March 31, 2026, from the JV partner has been treated as neither debt nor equity as it is interest-free, with no fixed repayment schedule.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Strong track record and established position in the Bengaluru market** 

The Brigade group is a prominent developer in Bengaluru, with a healthy track record of over three decades in the real estate business. It has developed more than 90 msf, mostly in the residential segment. Its strong position is evident from its established brand, and strong market share of 12–13% in Bengaluru, considering the high fragmentation in the real estate industry. 

The group has ongoing projects of around 28 msf of saleable area in real estate development. With regulatory bottlenecks easing, sales volume and collections are expected to sustain above 8- 9 msf and above Rs 6,500–7,000 crore, respectively, over the medium term, supported by new launches, healthy sales of ongoing projects and collections from completed projects. 

# **Moderately diversified revenue profile** 

The Brigade group derives its income mainly from three segments— real estate development, leasing assets and hospitality. Real estate development contributed to 73% of the total cash inflow of Rs 7,476 crore in fiscal 2026. The group also has a leased asset portfolio of 9.36 msf and nine operational hotels (four in Bengaluru, two in Mysuru and one each in Chennai, Kochi and Gujarat International Finance Tec-City). Diversification, both in terms of geography and revenue, is expected to improve with share of new development increasing in Hyderabad and Chennai and increase in lease rental income, respectively. 

# **Strong financial risk profile marked by limited debt in residential and hospitality segments** 

The financial risk profile remains strong, supported by limited debt in the residential and hospitality segments, with majority of the group’s borrowings concentrated in the commercial leasing portfolio, which is backed by lease-generating assets. The residential segment is expected to sustain a conservative leverage profile, with debt-to-CFO below 1.0 time and CFO-tointerest coverage exceeding 10 times, supported by a reduced reliance on construction finance over the medium term. The leasing business is also expected to sustain a healthy leverage profile, with an average DSCR of over 1.5 times and a debtto-lease rental ratio of below 4-5 times. 

The group had also raised Rs 126 crore through pre-initial public offering (IPO) and Rs 759 crore through IPO in fiscal 2026 under its hospitality segment, which was utilized to repay the entire debt, making the hospitality segment net debt free as on March 31, 2026. 

However, any sharp increase in debt due to aggressive land acquisition or construction debt due to subdued sales or substantial vacancy in commercial and retail portfolios will remain key rating sensitivity factors. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Geographical concentration in revenue:** BEL’s reliance on the real estate market of Bengaluru has been high, with around 70% of collections received from this region in fiscal 2026. However, the company is focusing on diversifying its 

geographic reach to Hyderabad and Chennai as well. However, the extent of geographical diversification in the revenue profile will remain a key monitorable. 

# **Exposure to risks inherent in the real estate sector** 

Cyclicality in the domestic real estate sector leads to volatility in saleability and realisations and, thus, fluctuations in cash inflow. However, outflows such as construction cost and debt repayment are relatively fixed. Muted demand could also reduce collections and cash flow. Nevertheless, the strong track record of the Brigade group in the real estate space in Bengaluru mitigates the implementation and demand risk. 

The rental collection from the commercial segment is also susceptible to economic downturns, which constrains the tenant’s business risk profile and, therefore, occupancy and rental rates. While majority of the tenants are established corporates and may continue to occupy the property, any industry shock leading to vacancies may make it difficult to find alternate lessees within the stipulated time. Emergence of competing facilities in the vicinity could also have the potential to cannibalise tenants or rental rates. These could adversely impact the cash flow and, hence, will be key rating sensitivity factors. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity is supported by healthy saleability and collections in ongoing projects and upcoming launches. Financial flexibility is supported by strong refinancing ability. Furthermore, cash and equivalent of Rs 2,900 crore as on March 31, 2026, supports liquidity. Liquidity is further supplemented by steady cash flow from the lease business and the ability to raise additional LRD loans, if required. 

# **<u>ESG Profile</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes that the ESG profile of BEL supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

The real estate sector has a significant impact on the environment owing to high emissions, waste generation and impact on land and biodiversity. The impact on social factors consists of labour-intensive operations and safety issues on account of construction-related activities. 

BEL has an ongoing focus on strengthening the various aspects of its ESG profile. 

# **Key ESG highlights:** 

- BEL aims to achieve net-zero emissions by 2045, in line with Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi), with a current Scope 1 & 2 emissions intensity of 0.001 tCO2e/sqft and Scope 3 intensity of 0.013 tCO2e/sqft. 

- BEL targets 100% renewable energy usage across operations by 2040 and has made progress by sourcing approximately 15% of its total energy consumption from renewable sources in fiscal 2025. 

- The company has a strong focus on green building certifications, with 78% of its operational portfolio currently certified, and aims to adopt green building norms across all prospective projects by 2030. 

- BEL aims to achieve water positivity by 2030 and has made progress by recycling approximately 52% of its total water consumption in fiscal 2025, Furthermore it reported a waste recycling/reuse rate of approximately 86% with plans to improve it further. 

- The company prioritises diversity and safety, with a gender diversity rate of approximately 19%, and a strong safety culture with no lost time injury frequency rate (LTIFR) or fatalities reported. 

- The governance structure includes a board with approximately 50% independent directors and 33% women directors, a dedicated sustainability committee, and comprehensive financial and non-financial disclosures. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

Crisil Ratings believes the credit risk profile of the Brigade group is expected to improve over the medium term, driven by its established market position and likely increase in scale of operations. The financial risk profile is also likely to remain strong, aided by low leverage of the residential business and cap on leveraging the leasing business. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Successful launches of new projects, resulting in sustained increase in the cash flow from residential portfolio or scale up of lease rentals, along with improvement in geographical diversity 

- Sustenance of the leverage indicated by Debt to Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) below 1 time for real estate development or a steady DSCR of over 2 times for the leasing business 

# **Downward factors** 

Sharp decline in operating cash flow for residential segment triggered by slackened collections or delays in launches or significant increase in vacancy in commercial office segment 

- Weaker debt protection metrics, with DSCR of less than 1.5 times for the leasing segment or debt-to-CFO ratio of above 1.5 times for the residential segment, on a sustained basis 

- Increase in debt for land acquisition or substantial cash outflows towards land purchase 

**<u>About the Company</u>** 

BEL is the flagship company of the Brigade group, which was established by MR Jaishankar in 1986, and is one of the largest real estate players in South India. Till date, it has developed more than 90 msf, 80% of which has been in the residential segment. Though it mainly focuses on the Bengaluru market, Chennai is emerging as the second largest market for the entity followed by Hyderabad. The group has developed projects in Mysuru, Cochin, Chennai, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad.
