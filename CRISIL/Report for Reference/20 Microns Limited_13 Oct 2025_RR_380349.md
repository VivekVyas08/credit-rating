### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A/Stable/Crisil A1’ ratings on the bank facilities of 20 Microns Ltd (ML, part of the 20 ML group). 

The ratings reflect ML’s established market position and the extensive experience of its promoters in the micronized mineral industry, sound operating efficiency and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the moderate working capital cycle **,** susceptibility to adverse changes in government regulations, and concentration in revenue profile .

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position and extensive experience of the promoters:** ML (hereafter referred to as the 20 ML group), has an established presence in the micronised minerals segment catering to marque customers such as Berger Paints India Ltd (Crisil AAA/Stable/Crisil A1+), Akzo Nobel, Asian paints Ltd ('Crisil AAA/Stable/Crisil A1+'), Grasim Industries Ltd ('Crisil AAA/Stable/Crisil A1+'), among others. With relationships developed over the years, the group is among the dominant suppliers in its product segments to these players. The 20 ML group is among the leading producers of ultrafine industrial minerals and specialty chemicals, which find application as functional fillers, additives and extenders. The company also generates around a sixth of its turnover through exports. 

Furthermore, the promoters have experience of more than 25 years in the micronized mineral industry. This has given them an understanding of the market dynamics and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** The capital structure of the 20 ML group has been healthy due to low reliance on external funds yielding gearing of 0.34 time and low total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio of 0.64 time as on March 31, 2025. The group’s debt protection metrics have also been moderate due to leverage and strong profitability. The interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratios were 6.5 times and 0.5 time, respectively, in fiscal 2025. The group’s debt protection metrics are expected to remain at similar levels over the medium term. 

**Sound operating efficiency:** The operating margin of the group has remained at 12.5–14.0% over the last 5-6 years, while the return on capital employed ratio is expected to be healthy at 18–20% over the medium term. The group benefits from a wide geographical reach (nine plants and 13 warehouses across India), which helps save on time and logistics costs. The group will continue to benefit from better scalability, translating into sustained revenue growth. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate working capital cycle:** The group’s moderate working capital cycle is reflected in its gross current assets (GCAs) of 145 days as on March 31, 2025. GCAs are driven by inventory of ~75 days and receivables of 55–60 days. Furthermore, to meet its business requirements, the group holds large inventory. However, credit period of 90–120 days, provided by suppliers, partially eases pressure on the working capital cycle. Continued support from payables is critical for 

the group to meet its working capital requirement. 

**Susceptibility to adverse changes in government regulations, and concentration in revenue profile:** The industry is highly susceptible to government regulations, and any unfavourable changes in policies (such as ban on mining, stringent environment norms, changes in royalties, among others) may adversely impact the performance. Furthermore, the company is exposed to high concentration in its revenue profile, with paint and plastic industries contributing significantly to the topline. This exposes the company to economic cycles, with demand taking a beating in a slowing economy. Also, the company faces high customer concentration risk, with the top five customers contributing to almost half of its turnover. 

# **<u>Liquidity:</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation was high at 70% on average for the 12 months through June 2025 for 20 ML group, Expected net cash accrual of over Rs 80 crore should comfortably cover yearly debt obligation of ~Rs 5 crore. Unencumbered cash and equivalent of ~ Rs 35.0 crore as on March 31, 2025, acts as cushion in case of any requirement. 

The current ratio was healthy at 1.46 times as on March 31, 2025. Low gearing and moderate networth support financial flexibility and provide financial cushion in case of any adverse condition or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes ML will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Improvement in revenue by 20% and sustenance of operating margin, leading to higher-than-expected cash accrual Diversification in the end-user industries and customer profiles 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in net cash accrual to below Rs 50 crore on account of fall in revenue or operating profit Large, debt-funded capital expenditure weakening the capital structure 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1987, ML was promoted by the late Mr Chandresh Parikh and is being managed by his sons, Mr Rajesh Parikh and Mr Atil Parikh. It is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange Ltd and National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. The company manufactures micronised minerals such as calcium carbonate, calcined clay and other specialty minerals.
