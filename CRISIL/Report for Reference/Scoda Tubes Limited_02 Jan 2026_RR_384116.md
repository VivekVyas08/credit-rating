### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Scoda Tubes Ltd (STL) and has subsequently **withdrawn** the ratings at the request of the company and on receipt of a ‘no-objection certificate’ from its lenders. This is in line with the Crisil Ratings policy on withdrawal of bank loan ratings. 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the pipe and pipe fittings industry, sound operating efficiencies and healthy debt protection metrics of the company. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility of profitability to volatility in raw material prices, and working capital-intensive operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of STL. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive industry experience of the promoters:** Experience of over a decade in the pipe and pipe fittings industry has given the promoters an understanding of the market dynamics and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. 

**Sound operating efficiencies:** The company has healthy operating efficiencies as indicated by healthy return on capital employed (RoCE) of 15–16% in the three fiscals through 2025. Furthermore, the company has booked sales of Rs 242.69 crore, with operating margin of 15.05% in the first half of fiscal 2026. The company has been able to register higher operating margin with continuous improvement in technologies and improved scale of operations. The company is expected to maintain healthy RoCE over the medium term, backed by healthy profitability. However, volatility in raw material prices (majorly steel) with the company’s large inventory of 120–135 days in the past three years will remain monitorable over the medium term. 

**Healthy debt protection metrics:** STL’s debt protection metrics have been comfortable despite leverage due to moderately healthy profitability. The interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratios were 3.62 times and 0.24 time, respectively, for fiscal 2025. The debt protection metrics are expected to remain at similar levels over the medium term. Moreover, the funds raised through initial public offering (IPO), of Rs 220 crore (June 2025), will further improve the debt protection metrics in this fiscal. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility of profitability to volatility in raw material prices:** Raw materials such as stainless steel hollow pipes constitute 70–75% of the total cost of sales. Its prices have remained volatile in the past. The company mainly imports its raw materials and, hence, keeps large inventory. However, STL mitigates the risk by ordering raw material against orders, which leads to higher inventory and large working capital requirement on account of high lead time of around six months in production. This safeguards the company from any significant changes in raw material prices. Crisil Ratings believes backward integration for the manufacture of hollow pipes will help STL with the risk of raw material volatility and will improve the operating margin over the medium term. 

**Working capital-intensive operations:** Gross current assets (GCAs) were at 185–207 days over the three fiscals ended March 31, 2025. Its intensive working capital management is reflected in its GCAs of 207 days as on March 31, 2025. Its 

large working capital requirement arises from the stretched receivables of 76 days and large inventory of 135 days. It is required to extend long credit period. Furthermore, due to its business need, the company holds large inventory. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation was moderate at 70% on average for the 17 months through September 2025. Annual cash accrual is expected to be Rs 42–55 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 11.50 crore over the medium term, and will cushion liquidity. 

The current ratio was moderate at 1.27 times as on March 31, 2025. 

The promoters are likely to extend equity and unsecured loans to meet the working capital requirement and debt obligations. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes STL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained improvement in margin to 10% and scale, leading to high cash accrual Improvement in the working capital cycle, with gross current assets improving to 160 days Improvement in the interest coverage ratio 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in the net cash accrual below Rs 35.00 crore on account of fall in revenue or operating profit Large, debt-funded capital expenditure weakening the capital structure 

Substantial increase in the working capital requirement thus weakening the liquidity and the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 2008, STL commenced commercial production in September 2010. It manufactures stainless steel seamless welded tubes and pipes, and U tubes. The company is currently managed by Mr Jagrut Patel, Mr Ravi Patel, Mr Vipul Patel and Mr Samarth Patel. Its manufacturing facility is in Mehsana, Gujarat.
