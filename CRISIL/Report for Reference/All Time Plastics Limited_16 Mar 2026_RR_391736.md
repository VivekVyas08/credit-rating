### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its rating on the long-term bank facilities of All Time Plastics Limited (ATPL, part of the All Time Group) to ‘ **Crisil A/Stable** ’ from ‘Crisil A-/Positive’. 

The upgrade in ratings reflects the improvement in ATPL's overall credit profile. The business risk profile has seen sustained improvement over the years, supported by the ramping up of production following capacity additions, improving volume sales driven by the continued addition of products and customers, and a healthy order flow from existing customers. This has resulted in steady improvement in overall revenues, despite moderation in realizations due to raw material price fluctuations. Revenues improved to Rs. 558 crores in fiscal 2025, reflecting a CAGR of ~12% over the last three fiscal years ending FY 2025. Revenues are expected to improve by over 20% year-on-year in fiscal 2026, supported by healthy growth in volume sales. Operating margins are expected to remain healthy, around 15%–18% over the medium term, despite temporary moderation due to certain one-off events and high fixed costs associated with continued capacity additions. This is supported by improving operating leverage, led by optimum utilization of manufacturing capacities and the ability to pass on any sharp fluctuations in raw material prices. 

While the financial risk profile remained at comfortable levels, it has strengthened further on the back of equity infusion via pre-IPO and IPO fund raises. This has helped the company's net worth reach around Rs. 594 crores as of September 2025, with an expected net worth of Rs. 615–625 crores as of March 2026. This has helped improve the capital structure, reflected in gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted net worth ratios of 0.21 times and 0.38 times, respectively, as of September 2025, expected to be around 0.1–0.2 times and 0.2–0.3 times, respectively, as of March 2026 (0.88 times and 1.27 times a year ago). The debt protection metrics also remain strong, as reflected in an interest coverage of over 6 times for H1 FY 2026, expected to be over 7 times for fiscal 2026 (6.9 times for fiscal 2025). With debt repayment as part of the usage of funds raised, the debt-to-EBITDA has improved meaningfully. Nevertheless, a strong financial risk profile continues to support the company's overall credit profile. 

The rating continues to reflect ATPL's established market position in houseware plastic product industry supported by its extensive experience of the promoters, growing scale of operations, efficient working capital management, and a strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and customer concentration in revenues. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at its ratings, Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of ATPL with its subsidiaries All Time Plastic Pte Limited and All Time Bamboo Private Limited, which are strategically important to, and have a significant degree of operational integration with All Time Plastics Limited. Crisil Ratings considers these entities as being strategic to All Time Plastic Limited in view of their strong integration with Parent’s operations.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established position in houseware plastic product industry:** ATPL has a strong competitive position in the domestic and export plastic consumer ware segment, supported by a track record of over five decades in the industry and a wide product portfolio spanning houseware, kitchenware, bath ware, tableware, kids’ products, and storage solutions. The company is recognized as one of India’s leading manufacturers and exporters of plastic houseware, supplying to marquee global 

retailers such as IKEA (with a relationship spanning over 27 years), Tesco (over 17 years), Asda (over 14 years), and Michaels, as well as established domestic modern trade players like Spencer’s Retail, which demonstrates its healthy position in a quality and compliance-intensive segment. ATPL’s market position is further supported by its extensive promoter experience. The promoters bring around four decades of domain experience, which has enabled the company to build strong customer relationships and consistently scale up capacities in line with global demand trends. Their longstanding engagement with the business, combined with an in-house product and mould design team established over 20 years ago, has supported continuous product innovation, allowing the company to secure repeat orders from key customers, thereby providing steady visibility to revenues and sustained growth to ATPL’s business risk profile. 

**Improving scale of operations and its operating efficiencies:** The scale of operations of ATPL has been steadily improving on the back of capacity additions, healthy capacity utilization levels, and improving volume sales. This is supported by a steady order flow from key customers, driven by strong relationships. Revenues have improved at a CAGR of 12% over the last three fiscal years ending FY 2025, while volumes have improved at a CAGR of over 15% during the same period. With capacity additions and healthy ramp-up, revenues are expected to grow by over 20% in fiscal 2026 compared to fiscal 2025, as indicated by the first nine-month revenues of Rs. 464 crores. Operating efficiencies also remain healthy, as evidenced by the healthy utilization of existing capacities and strong ramp-up in newly added capacities. As a result, return on capital employed (RoCE) has remained at healthy levels (high teens), which is expected to moderate in the current fiscal year due to fund raises; however, it will improve gradually as incremental capacities’ utilization increases. Crisil Ratings believes that the steady utilization of capacities, improving volume sales supported by a steady order flow from clients, will continue to help the company improve its scale of operations over the medium term. 

**Efficient working capital management and positive cash flows:** The working capital operations of ATPL are efficient, as highlighted by gross current assets of 120 days as of March 31, 2025. This is primarily driven by debtors of 57 days and inventory of around 58 days, which has moderated from 35 days and 46 days, respectively, a year ago. The moderation is largely attributed to the transition phase with additions in production capacities and new customers. The company offers a moderate credit period to its customers of around 60 days, and payments are realized within the stipulated timelines. The company needs to maintain inventory of 60 to 80 days, in line with business requirements. The working capital cycle is further supported by credit received from suppliers of around 45 days. Moreover, with efficient working capital management and healthy absolute EBITDA, cash flow from operations has consistently remained healthy, enabling the company to support its capital expenditure (capex) requirements, which are partially met from internal accruals. The overall working capital cycle is expected to remain in a similar range over the medium term, with gross current assets ranging between 100 and 120 days. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile of ATPL is strong, as reflected in a net worth of Rs. 594 crores as of September 2025 and is expected to be around Rs. 615–625 crores as of March 2026 (Rs. 247 crores a year ago). The improvement in net worth is supported by fund raises in fiscal 2026 via pre-IPO and IPO rounds. This has also helped further improve the capital structure of the company, which was already at healthy levels, as reflected in gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted net worth ratios of 0.21 times and 0.38 times, respectively, as of September 2025, expected to be around 0.1–0.2 times and 0.2–0.3 times, respectively, as of March 2026 (0.88 times and 1.27 times a year ago). The debt protection metrics also remain strong, as reflected in an interest coverage of over 6 times for H1 FY 2026, expected to be over 7 times for fiscal 2026 (6.9 times for fiscal 2025). With debt repayment as part of the usage of funds raised, the debt-to-EBITDA has improved meaningfully. With no large debt-funded capital expenditures in the pipeline, healthy growth in the scale of operations leading to steady accretion to reserves and sustained healthy levels of profitability amidst moderate interest costs, the financial risk profile of the company is expected to remain healthy over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Customer concentration in revenues:** ATPL faces significant customer concentration risks, as its major customer, IKEA, accounts for about 60% of its total sales in fiscal 2025. This makes the company's revenue growth and profitability dependent on its key customers' future growth plans. However, the risk is partially mitigated by the long-standing relationship of the company with IKEA, along with multiple product offerings, as no single product contributes to more than 15% of IKEA exposure in terms of revenues. That said, any event, such as the cancellation of orders or business pressures faced by key customers, will impact ATPL's performance and therefore remains monitorable 

**Susceptibility to volatility in prices of raw materials:** Operating margins are susceptible to fluctuations in polymer prices, which is the primary raw material. The prices of raw materials are impacted by global demand and are linked to global crude oil prices, making them highly volatile. Any sharp increase in price may impact profitability. The fluctuation in prices is generally passed on to customers, albeit with a lag. Hence, the operating margin has been in the range of 16%–18.6% over the past three fiscals. Although the operating margins remain healthy, they have moderated in the current fiscal, especially in Q2, where EBITDA margins moderated to ~11% due to front-loaded expenses for capacity expansion, a one-time inventory-related adjustment, and an adverse sales mix of low-margin products. Nevertheless, it has improved in Q3 due to the benefit from operating leverage and absorption of capex-related costs, owing to the steady ramp-up in capacities, which is expected to continue over the coming quarters as well. However, any sharp fluctuations in material costs or delayed ramp-up in capacities can affect operating profitability and will remain a key monitorable. 

**<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Bank limit utilization is moderate, around 45%, for the past twelve months ended December 2025. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs. 70 crores, which are sufficient against term debt obligations of Rs. 16–20 crores over the medium term. Cash and cash equivalents stood at over ~Rs. 200 crores as of September 2025, of which Rs. 137 crores was earmarked towards utilization of its IPO proceeds for debt repayments and capex requirements; the balance remains free, supporting liquidity. The current ratio is moderate at 1.03 times as of March 31, 2025. ATPL has sufficient gearing headroom to raise additional debt to meet its capex requirements. Its bank lines are expected to meet its incremental working capital requirements, which are assessed to be moderate. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes ATPL will continue to benefit from its strong market position, heathy operating margins and robust financial risk profile

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

All Time Plastics Limited (Previously known as All Time Plastics Private Limited) was incorporated in 2001 by Mr. Kailesh Shah and his brothers, Mr. Bhupesh Shah and Mr. Nilesh Shah. The company manufactures moulded plastic household products, including kitchen and home utility products. The manufacturing facility is located in Silvassa, Daman and Khatalwada, primarily offering houseware, kitchenware, bath-ware, tableware, children products such as plates, bowls and spoons, as well as storage containers. The company recently got listed on BSE and NSE platform
