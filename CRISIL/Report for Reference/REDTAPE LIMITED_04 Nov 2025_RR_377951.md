### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of REDTAPE LTD (REDTAPE) to **‘Stable’** from ‘Positive’ and reaffirmed the rating at **‘Crisil A’** . 

The revision in outlook reflects the increasing working capital requirement of REDTAPE, driven by higher inventory levels resulting from showroom expansion. REDTAPE had also increased inventory levels in fiscal 2025 due to political instability in Bangladesh, a major manufacturing base for the company. This led to an increase in short-term debt. The liquidation of the built-up inventory and its impact on profitability will be closely monitored. To mitigate any future geo-political risk, the company is focusing on diversifying its supplier base and reducing reliance on any specific country or supplier. Based on discussion with the management of REDTAPE, Crisil Ratings understands the inventory is compliant with the Bureau of Indian standards (BIS) norms for imports that came into effect in August 2024. 

The business risk profile of REDTAPE remains healthy with revenue growth of 9.7% in fiscal 2025 to Rs 2,022 crore from Rs 1,843 crore in fiscal 2024, driven by growth in both footwear and garment sales. The revenue growth is expected to remain healthy over the medium term, backed by strong domestic demand from metro cities and increasing penetration in Tier 2 and 3 cities. The company has been focusing on expanding its online and offline presence while maintaining its profitability. The operating margin remained healthy at 17.1% in fiscal 2025 (17.6% in fiscal 2024) and is expected at a similar level over the medium term. Revenue stood at Rs 464 crore in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, up 5.1% on-year, with operating margin of 16.6%. 

The financial risk profile remains comfortable, supported by moderate capital structure and adequate debt protection metrics over the medium term. Gearing was 0.9 time as on March 31, 2025 (0.7 time a year earlier) while total outside liabilities to tangible networth (TOLTNW) ratio was 1.8 times as on March 31, 2025 (1.5 times a year ago). The debt, including lease liability, increased to Rs 724 crore (Fiscal 2024: Rs.480 crore, mainly due to increase in working capital debt. Interest coverage moderated to 6.4 times in fiscal 2025 from 8.6 times in fiscal 2024. The debt protection metrics are expected to remain healthy over the medium term, despite working capital-intensive operations, because of limited debt-funded capital expenditure (capex). 

The ratings continue to factor in the company’s established brand and ability to adapt to changing market trends and consumer preferences, the extensive experience of the promoters in the footwear and garments industries, and comfortable financial risk profile, reflected in healthy capital structure and adequate debt protection metrics. These strengths are partially offset by large working capital requirement and exposure to intense competition in the footwear and garments industry.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Established brand and ability to adapt to changing market trends and consumer preferences** 

REDTAPE has positioned itself as a provider of affordable footwear and garments without compromising on quality. This value proposition appeals to a broad customer base, ensuring steady demand and repeat business. The company has invested in effective marketing strategies and strong brand positioning. This has enhanced brand recognition and loyalty, contributing to sustained revenue growth. REDTAPE has shown agility in staying ahead of trends by continuously innovating its product line in the Indian market, adapting to the changing preferences of the country’s burgeoning youth population. 

# **Extensive experience of the promoters and integrated operations** 

The promoters’ experience of more than two decades has helped REDTAPE maintain moderately strong market position in the domestic footwear and garment segments. The company has an integrated manufacturing facility in Unnao, Uttar Pradesh, which accounts for ~25% of products manufactured and includes contract manufacturing by the domestic supplier. The remaining ~75% is imported from Bangladesh, Myanmar and Nepal. REDTAPE held reasonable inventory shielding it from the impact of the political unrest in Bangladesh. Inventory rose from Rs 764 crore as on March 31, 2024, to Rs 1,221 crore as on March 31, 2025, and 18-20% is aged more than a year. However, the inventory is compliant with the BIS import norms which became effective in August 2024. Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor the movements in inventory and the impact on the business risk profile of REDTAPE. 

# **Comfortable financial risk profile:** 

Tangible networth stood at Rs 779 crore as on March 31, 2025. Of the total debt of Rs 724 crore as on March 31, 2025, Rs 48 crore was long term, Rs 318 crore was working capital debt and Rs 358 crore comprised lease liabilities<sup>[1]</sup> . Gearing stood at 0.9 time and TOLTNW ratio remained at 1.8 times as on March 31, fiscal 2025 (0.7 time and 1.5 times, respectively, a year ago). Despite the increase in short-term debt, the financial risk profile is healthy, supported by limited long-term debt, healthy networth, comfortable gearing and adequate liquidity. The capital structure and debt protection metrics are expected to remain strong over the medium term due to healthy cash accrual, low debt-funded capex and gradual repayment of longterm debt. 

_[1]Lease liabilities are contractual in nature and are included in debt under IndAS._ 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Working capital-intensive operations:** 

Owing to the retail business and planned expansion through addition of offline retail stores, REDTAPE is required to keep substantial inventory leading to large working capital requirement. As a result, gross current assets (GCAs) remained high over 250 days as on March 31, 2025. Inventory has increased over the past three years and its timely liquidation and the impact on profitability will be monitorable. 

# **Exposure to risks related to intense competition in the footwear and garments industry:** 

The footwear and garment industries are highly fragmented, with the unorganised sector accounting for a large share of the market. The emergence of e-commerce and increasing presence of international brands have further intensified competition. However, after the BIS certification regulation, REDTAPE is expected to have an edge, as most of its suppliers are already BIS certified. Also, the company has a strong presence in e-commerce with more than 30% of its total sales coming from the online channel. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Cash and equivalents were at Rs 10.6 crore as on March 31, 2025. Utilisation of the Rs 615 crore working capital limit was moderate at ~73% on average for the six months through June 2025. Expected cash accrual of above Rs 250 crore (prior to lease payments) per annum over the next three years should comfortably meet the moderate capex, incremental working capital requirement and debt obligation. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

REDTAPE will continue to benefit from its strong brand recall across product categories, wide variety of product offerings, strong presence in metros and Tier 1 cities and improving penetration in Tier 2 and 3 cities. The financial risk profile should remain strong, driven by low long-term debt, high financial flexibility and moderate capex. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Increased revenue while sustaining operating margin at 16-18% leading to improved cash generation Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and improvement in the working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin below 12% leading to lower cash accrual 

Large working capital requirement or significant debt-funded capex, weakening the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

REDTAPE is a prominent player in the retail footwear and garments segments for men, women and children. It sells products under three brands – REDTAPE, MODE (REDTAPE London) and BOND STREET (REDTAPE London). Most of the revenue comes from the domestic market with negligible exports. The company has presence of 26 years in the footwear segment and around 15 years in the garments segment. 

The company has a pan-India presence with around 600 retail stores, of which ~70% are exclusive brand outlets and ~30% are shop-in-shop/multibrand outlets. It has one manufacturing unit in Unnao, Uttar Pradesh, where it manufactures ~25% of its products (mainly footwear) including contract manufacturing by domestic suppliers. The remaining 75% is imported from Bangladesh, Myanmar and Nepal through contract manufacturing.
