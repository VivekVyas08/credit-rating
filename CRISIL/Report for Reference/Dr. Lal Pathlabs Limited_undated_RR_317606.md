### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘CRISIL AA-/Positive/CRISIL A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Dr Lal Pathlabs Ltd (DRLPL) and subsequently **withdrawn** the ratings at the request of the company and on receipt of no-objection certificate from the bankers. The rating action is in line with the withdrawal policy of CRISIL Ratings. 

The ratings continue to reflect healthy business risk profile of DRLPL, with sustained revenue growth over the medium term, driven by strong market share in the organised diagnostic sector, healthy brand equity, established presence in the business-to-consumer (B2C) segment, robust franchise model, highest pick-up points on Pan India basis and improving test portfolio. Revenue stood healthy at Rs 1,525 crore during the first nine months of fiscal 2023, against Rs 2,087 crore in fiscal 2022, aided by growth in the non-Covid portfolio. Non-Covid revenue continued its growth momentum with increasing patient inflow, driven by a strong brand recall, quality results offering and customer-centric services. Growth will also be supported by improving geographical diversity in the western region. DRLPL has strong presence across India; acquisition of the SDIPL group should further improve geographical diversity in the western region. 

Operating margin remained healthy at ~24% during the first nine months of fiscal 2023, against 26.9% in fiscal 2022. The margin marginally declined in fiscal 2023 due to consolidation with Suburban Diagnostics (India) Pvt Ltd (SDIPL), which has a low margin (16-17%). The margin should remain comfortable over the medium term due to strong presence in the B2C segment, sustenance of pricing despite intense competition from new players in the diagnostics market and increase in share of specialised tests (radiology, non-covid-related diabetes, blood pressure) having regular and better realisations. 

Financial risk profile has been robust, with networth more than Rs 1,600 crore as on March 31, 2023, supported by healthy accretion to reserve. Gearing was below 0.2 time as on March 31, 2023. Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 489 crore as on December 31, 2022. Strong balance sheet and healthy liquidity provide the flexibility to absorb modest-sized acquisitions without significantly impacting the key credit metrics. However, any large, debt-funded acquisition will be a key monitorable. 

The ratings reflect the leading position of the group in the pathology laboratory (path lab) industry in India, supported by established brand equity, strong infrastructure, healthy operating efficiency and return on capital employed (RoCE) and robust financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition owing to low entry barriers to the industry. 



### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

CRISIL Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of DRLPL and its subsidiaries: Paliwal Diagnostic Pvt Ltd, Paliwal Medicare Pvt Ltd, APL Institute of Clinical Laboratory & Research Pvt Ltd, Dr Lal Pathlabs Nepal Pvt Ltd, Dr Lal Pathlabs International B V, Dr Lal Pathlabs Bangladesh Pvt Ltd, Dr Lal Ventures Pvt Ltd, PathLabs Unifiers Pvt Ltd, Dr Lal Pathlabs Kenya Pvt Ltd, Centrapath Labs Pvt Ltd and APRL Pathlabs Pvt Ltd and SDIPL. All these entities are collectively referred to as the DRLPL group.

### **Key rating drivers and detailed description Strengths:** 

**Leading market position, backed by established brand equity and strong infrastructure network:** The group has maintained its dominant position in the Indian path lab services industry, reflected in operating income of Rs 1,525 crore in the first nine months of fiscal 2023; and improved pan-India network of 277 clinical laboratories (including national reference lab in Delhi and regional reference lab in Kolkata), 4,731 patient service centres and 10,599 pick-up points. The group is regularly enhancing its laboratory footprint to meet extant and emergent sampling. The market is highly fragmented, with almost half of it being catered by unorganised standalone centres, while hospitalbased diagnostics centres account for 37%. Organised diagnostic chains such as DRLPL account for 17% of the total market. 

Further, post-acquisition of SDIPL that holds strong presence in the Western markets, performance of the group should improve over the medium term. With 38 labs and 177 collection points in Maharashtra and strong presence in Mumbai and Pune, SDIPL provides DRLPL a robust platform to strengthen and penetrate its geographical presence in western region. 

**Robust financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile is supported by steady cash accrual, absence of any significant debt-funded capital expenditure (capex), and minimal reliance on the working capital limit. Capital structure was healthy, reflected in networth of more than Rs 1,600 crore and low total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio of 0.50 time as on March 31, 2023. Debt protection metrics were comfortable, indicated by interest coverage ratio more than 20 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio at 0.99 time in fiscal 2023. 

**Healthy operating efficiency and RoCE levels:** The DRLPL group has healthy operating efficiency, with operating margin of 24-27% during the three fiscals through 2023, supported by the strong quality controls, improving revenue per patient, cost-optimisation measures and continuous process improvement through an in-house research and development set up. Further, low capital intensity (focus on asset-light model) and strong margin led to robust RoCE levels; the ratio stood at 35% for fiscal 2022 and is projected at 20-25% over the medium term. Increasing share of the B2C segment sales with aggressive network expansion should sustain the earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation (Ebitda) margin at 26-27%. Though the portfolio of SDIPL has lower margin, the EBITDA margin of the group should remain healthy owing to higher contribution coming from DRLPL and with the shift of customer towards quality health diagnostic along with better test mix, cost-rationalisation measures undertaken, and significant increase in specialised testing. 

# **Weakness:** 

**Exposure to intense competition:** Intense competition in the diagnostic services market, which has several players offering similar services, results in pricing pressure. The diagnostic sector is highly fragmented, with almost half of it being catered by unorganised standalone centers, while hospital-based diagnostics centers account for 37%. Organised diagnostic chains such as DRLPL account for 17% of the total market. However, DRLPL provides attractive test packages to customers in the B2C segment, reflecting in its strong customer base. 



# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Cash accrual, expected over Rs 300-320 crore per annum, will be sufficient to meet incremental working capital requirement and the repayment obligation of Rs 80-100 crore over the medium term. Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 489 crore as on December 31, 2022. Bank limit utilisation was negligible during the 12 months through March 2022. Low gearing and moderate networth aid financial flexibility. However, any further large, debt-funded acquisition and deployment of liquid funds will remain key rating sensitivity factors. 



# **<u>Outlook: Positive</u>** 

The business risk profile of DRLPL will continue to improve over the medium term, driven by increase in demand post the acquisition of SDIPL. 



# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Improved geographical presence, leading to revenue growth of 10-12% and sustained operating profitability of 25%, resulting in higher-thanexpected cash accrual 

Improvement in the working capital cycle and maintenance of a healthy capital structure 

# **Downward factors:** 

Revenue declining by more than 20% and operating margin dropping by 400 basis points, leading to lower-than-expected cash accrual Large, debt-funded capex or acquisition, weakening the financial risk profile, with gearing increasing above 0.5 time Stretch in working capital cycle or considerable fall in cash and liquid investments 



# **<u>About the group</u>** 

DRLPL is the flagship company of the DRLPL group. Set up as a partnership firm in 1949 by the late Dr S K Lal, it was reconstituted into a private-limited company in 1992. The entity was further converted into a public-limited company before being listed on the National Stock Exchange and the Bombay Stock Exchange in December 2015. DRLPL operates its own path labs, while collection centres are run on franchisee model. Though operations are spread across the country, north India accounts for over 70% of the total revenue.
