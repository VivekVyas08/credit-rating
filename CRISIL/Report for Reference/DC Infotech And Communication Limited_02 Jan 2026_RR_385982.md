### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ ratings to the bank facilities of DC Infotech And Communication Limited (DCICL). 

The ratings reflect DCICL's established presence in the distribution and supplies of IT products as well as services, established relationship with its supplier & clientele and its healthy financial profile. These strengths are partially offset by their moderate scale of operation, susceptibility to competition and moderate working capital cycle. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of DCICL. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market presence:** DCICL is an established player in the market, and its promoters have experience of around three decades in technology distribution industry. This has given them an understanding of the dynamics of the market and led to diversifying its offerings. Revenues hence increased to Rs. 555.75 crores in fiscal 2025 from Rs 232.51 crores in fiscal 2022. 

**Established relationship with its supplier and clientele:** DCICL has a pan-India distribution network with over 1,600 channel partners and sales representatives in major cities such as Delhi, Kolkata, Bangalore, Chandigarh, and Indore etc. The top 10 customers contributed less than 30% revenue in fiscal 2025. It also has established supplier base with brands such as D-Link, Netgear, Samsung, NetScout, Arbour, Zscaler, Aten, SonicWall, Kramer, Riverbed, and others. DCICL has exclusive distributorship with few of these brands, providing the competitive edge. 

**Healthy financial profile** : DCICL’s capital structure is comfortable with gearing of 0.7 times and moderate total outside liabilities to adjusted net worth (TOL/ANW) of 2.4 times as on March 31, 2025. DCICL’s debt protection measures is also adequate with low leverage with interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratio are 3.96 times and 0.29 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025. DCICL’s financial profile will remain at similar levels with controlled debt levels. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate working capital cycle:** Gross current assets were about 150 days over the three fiscals, with 149 days as on March 31, 2025. This is driven by debtors of 85-100 days as it is required to extend long credit period due to reputed customers. Furthermore, to meet its business requirements, it holds moderate inventory of 45-50 days due to lead time in procuring them. With increasing revenues, the working capital requirements shall increase and management of same remains monitorable. 

**Moderate scale and exposure to intense competition:** Revenues are moderate despite increase, reflected in revenue of Rs. 555 crores in fiscal 2025. Due to presence of large number of organized & unorganized players in the segment driven by low capital requirement, the industry is exposed to intense competition. Therefore, scale of operations determines the negotiating power with suppliers and customers, and ability to withstand business downturns. This also impacts operating margin, which is modest at 4.5-5% in past three fiscals. 

**<u>Liquidity:</u>** Adequate 

Bank utilization is moderate at around 80-85% for the past twelve months ended October 2025.  Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs.20-25 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 0.1 crore over the medium term. Current ratio is healthy at 1.4 times and cash and cash equivalents of Rs. 24.2 crores, as on March 31, 2025. Low gearing and moderate net worth support its financial flexibility and provides the financial cushion available in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook:</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believe DCICL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter and established relationships with suppliers. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained improvement in scale of operation by sustenance of operating margin, leading to cash accruals of above Rs.25 crores. 

Improvement in working capital cycle and TOLANW 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in scale of operations leading to fall in revenues and profit margin leading to net cash accrual being lower than Rs 12 crore. 

Substantial increase in its working capital requirements, thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Initially set as partnership in April 1998 by Mr. Devendra Sayani and Mr. Chetankumar Timbadia, DCICL was constituted as public limited company in 2019. 

It is engaged in distribution and supplies of IT products such as networking, security, firewall's, desktop virtualization, broadband, surveillance, wireless, digital signage, power solutions and others like cloud security, digital performance, network performance, and enterprise security. It also provides installation and maintenance services. The company is listed on the Bombay and National Stock Exchanges
