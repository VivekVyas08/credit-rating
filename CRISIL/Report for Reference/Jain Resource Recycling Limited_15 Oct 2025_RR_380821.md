### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its rating on the long term bank facilities of Jain Resource Recycling Limited (JRRL; part of the Jain Metal group) to **‘Crisil A+/Stable’** from ‘Crisil A/Stable’ while reaffirming the short term bank facilities at ‘Crisil A1’ 

The upgrade factors in strengthening of the group’s overall credit risk profile with improvement in business profile along with financial profile. In the initial public offer (IPO) during October 2025, company had raised a fresh issue of around Rs.500 crores, out of which around Rs.375 crores planned to repay or prepay debt, due to this the capital structure is expected to improve going forward, with gearing coming down from 1.3 times as on March 2025 to below 0.7 times expected as on March 2026. Debt protection metrics is also expected to improve, with expected reduction of debt levels going forward, and interest cover is expected to stay at comfortable levels. Business risk profile also has remained strong, with company’s revenue growing nearly around 60% in FY25 to around Rs.7126 crores, and the revenue is expected to grow further over the medium term. Operating margins have remained stable over the years, and are expected to remain stable aided by company efficient working capital policy and hedging policy, which enables price pass on in times of fluctuations. With improved revenue and stable profitability, company’s net cash accruals have increased to Rs.238 crores in FY25 compared to Rs.179 crores in FY24, and is expected to improve further going forward. 

The rating continues to reflect the established market position of the Jain Metal group in the non-ferrous metal recycling industry, strong risk management policies and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the low operating margin and its vulnerability to intense competition, and risks associated with change in government policies related to tightened environmental norms.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market presence in the non-ferrous metal recycling industry:** The group has maintained its strong market position in the copper and lead recycling business segments. The promoters have maintained healthy relationships with customers, which include reputed players in the domestic and overseas markets. Import of lead scrap in India is subject to licensing from the Ministry of Environment and Forest, while setting up of lead recycling plants requires permissions from central and state pollution boards, which in turn act as barriers for new entrants. The group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience and expertise of its promoters and management and has established relationships with large players. The end-user industry base comprises manufacturers of batteries, electrical panels, industrial furnaces, automobiles and power cables. The company has a vast base of over 250 suppliers and procures goods from over 120 countries, including the US, South Korea, China, Malaysia, and Hong Kong. Group has registered its brand on the London Metal Exchange and on MCX. This has enabled the company to improve its scale of operations, and company has recorded a revenue of Rs.7126 crores for fiscal 2025, and the scale is expected to improve further going forward. 

**Strong risk management policies:** The group follows a strong risk management policy and hedges risk arising from fluctuations in commodity prices through the LME. Forex risk is mitigated through a natural hedge as 80% of the raw 

material requirement is imported and exports form around 60% of revenue. The balance risk is hedged via forward contracts. The group also maintains a fixed deposit (FD) of over Rs 200 crore and avails an overdraft (OD) against the same, so as to ensure sufficient liquidity. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Capital structure was above average marked by networth of Rs.724 crores, with gearing at 1.3 times for fiscal 2025. In IPO during October 2025, company had a fresh issue of Rs.500 crores, due to which capital structure is expected to improve, with gearing to stay down below 0.7 times over the medium term. Debt protection metrics is comfortable marked by expected improvement in interest cover to around 6-7 times in the upcoming years compared to 4.1 time for fiscal 2025, aided by reduced dependence on working capital debt. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Low operating margin and vulnerability to intense competition:** EBIDTA has ranged between 4.8% and 5.3% for the three fiscals ended March 31, 2025, amidst intense competition and low value addition in the copper and lead recycling industry. However, with efficient risk management (hedging) policies, the EBITDA margins have remained stable without major fluctuations. Hence, the improvement in margins, due to improved contribution from higher margin segments remains key monitorable. 

**Exposure to risks associated with change in government policies related to environmental norms:** Companies in the lead metal industry have to adhere to stringent pollution control norms. With norms getting tightened and environmental activism taking centre stage, players face risks posed by environmental concerns. Any change in government policies, impacting operations of the group, will also be monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash accrual are expected to be around Rs 230-270 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of less than Rs 1 crore  over the medium term. Average BLU is around 75% for the last 6 months ending January 2025. Current ratio are moderate at 1.5 times on March 31, 2025. The promoters are likely to extend support in the form of equity and unsecured loans for any business needs. Free cash and bank balance of around Rs.23 crores as on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the Jain Metal group will continue to benefit from its established market position and strong risk management policies. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained growth in revenue, and stable operating margin of around 5-6%, leading to higher cash accrual. Improvement in financial risk profile. 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin less than 3-4% leading to lower net cash accruals. 

Large debt-funded capital expenditure or substantial increase in working capital requirement, weakening the financial risk profile, especially liquidity. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

JRRL is engaged in recycling and manufacturing of non-ferrous metals. Its product line includes lead and lead alloys, copper ingots, and aluminium alloys, which are critical inputs across multiple industries. It recently got listed on BSE and NSE. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

The Jain Metal Group has been engaged in non-ferrous metal recycling for since 2013. The Tamil Nadu-based group has three advanced technology plants at Gummidipoondi in Tiruvallur district, which recycle scrap arising from cables, lead acid batteries, lead, copper and plastic. 

**JRRL** (Jain Resource Recycling Limited) was formed as a partnership called Jain Metal Rolling Mills in 1953 and was reconstituted as a private limited company in 2022 and public limited in 2025. It manufactures refined lead antimony alloys, lead ingots, copper ingots, copper billets, etc, at its three units at Tiruvallur, with a lead recycling capacity of 141,000 MTPTA, and copper recycling capacity of 83,042 MPTA. JRRL got listed on BSE and NSE on 1<sup>st</sup> October 2025. 

**JRPL** (Jain Recycling Private Limited) was formed as a partnership firm called Jain FGL Metal Industries in 2018 by Mr Kamlesh Jain and Mr Mayank Pareek. The firm was reconstituted into a private limited company in January 2020. JRPL recycled copper billets, ingots and barley, polyvinyl chloride granules, plastic peels, etc. On 29 January 2025, JRPL has merged with JRRL under the order of NCLT. 

**JGT** (Jain Green Technologies Private Limited) has set up as an aluminum recycling plant with a capacity of 6000 MT. 

**JIGVF** (Jain Ikon Global Venture FZC) is 70% subsidiary of JRRL and involved in gold refining business in SAIF Zone Sharjah. 

**JIPL** (Jain Investments Private Limited) is incorporated as private limited company having a registered office in Sri Lanka. It is engaged in the business of investment holding activities without engaging in any retail trading business activities.
