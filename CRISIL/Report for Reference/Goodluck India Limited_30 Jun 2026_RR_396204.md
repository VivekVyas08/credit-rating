### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of Goodluck India Limited (GIL; a part of the Goodluck group) to ‘ **Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+** ’ from ‘Crisil A+/Positive/Crisil A1’. 

The upgrade in ratings reflects the improvement in the company's business risk profile, driven by its strong market presence in value-added products such as tube structures and other engineering products. This improvement is further supported by the continuous diversification of its product range, including the successful operationalization of its artillery shell manufacturing facility, with a total capacity of 150,000 shells per annum. This expansion supports an increase in the scale of operations to Rs. 4,100 crores in fiscal 2026 from Rs. 3,941 crores in fiscal 2025, largely driven by growth in volume sales as raw material prices moderated, leading to lower realizations.Going forward, the business risk profile is expected to be supported by the defense business, which is highly regulated in nature with stringent approval processes and large upfront capital expenditure requirements to manufacture quality-sensitive products. As a result, negligible competition exists in the industry for GIL. 

Despite moderation in raw material prices throughout most of fiscal 2026, leading to a fall in realizations for GIL, the scale of operations improved due to higher sales from higher value-added products. This led to an expansion of operating margins to 9.7% in fiscal 2026, compared to 8.48% in fiscal 2025. 

The company plans to enhance its production capacity under its legacy steel processing segment to 600,000 MTPA from 500,000 MTPA, along with increasing its artillery shell manufacturing capacity to 400,000 shells per annum from 150,000 shells per annum over the next 12–24 months. This is expected to result in stable growth in the scale of operations to around Rs. 4,500–4,700 crores in fiscal 2027. Additionally, the increased contribution of high-margin products to the company's overall revenue is expected to result in further expansion of operating margins to around 9.75–10% over the medium term. 

The rating action is further supported by the company's strong financial risk profile, which is supported by a healthy adjusted net worth of around Rs. 1,528 crores. The management has maintained a prudent funding mix in the past while undertaking capital expenditure (capex), resulting in a gearing ratio of 0.73 times and a Total Outside Liabilities to Tangible Net Worth (TOL/TNW) ratio of 0.99 times as of March 31, 2026. Although GIL is expected to undertake capex of around Rs. 300–350 crores annually, partially funded by debt, over the next 2–3 fiscals, its capital structure is expected to remain comfortable. This is indicated by expected gearing and TOL/TNW ratios of 0.7–0.8 times and 0.95–1.00 times, respectively. The company's healthy accretion of reserves is expected to result in an adjusted net worth of around Rs. 1,730 crores by the end of fiscal 2027. 

The ratings continue to further reflect the established presence in the pipe and engineering product industry, timely completion of the defence equipment manufacturing facility leading to further diversification of product profile, healthy scale of operations and strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by large working capital requirement and susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and intense competition.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Established market position in steel processing with presence in the pipe and engineering product industry along with diversified product profile resulting in healthy scale of operations:** 

The group has leveraged its promoters' nearly four-decade-long experience in the steel processing industry to expand its capacity and product portfolio. The group offers a diverse range of products with a high level of customization. GIL has a diversified product profile across five key segments: defense & aerospace, engineering structures & precision fabrication, forgings, precision pipes & auto tubes, and cold-rolled coil pipes and hollow sections. The product portfolio and end-user industry base are well-diversified and include players from various segments, such as automotive and trucks, heavy commercial vehicles, agricultural machinery, equipment, valves, fittings, petrochemical applications, hardware, off-road, railway equipment, general industrial equipment, renewable energy, aerospace, and defense. This diversification shields the company from downturns in any specific industry. 

In fiscal 2026, the group operationalized a facility to manufacture defense equipment and received a government license for the commercial production of 155mm missile shells, contributing Rs. 46 crores to the group’s total operating income of Rs. 4,100 crores in fiscal 2026 (up from Rs. 3,941 crores in fiscal 2025). The growth in scale of operations in fiscal 2026 was supported by a 7.8% growth in volume, despite moderation in realization due to softening of raw material prices. The growth in sales volume was driven by healthy demand for the company’s legacy products and entry into niche segments like defense and aerospace with high demand. As of date, the company has received orders worth Rs. 307 crores under the defense segment to manufacture missile shells. Limited competition in the segment due to stringent regulations, coupled with healthy order flows, will support healthy offtake from the defense segment, resulting in an expected revenue of around Rs. 4,500 crores in fiscal 2027. With the increasing scale and established market position, along with a diversified product profile and end-user industry base across domestic and overseas markets, and a healthy order inflow, the company should achieve revenue growth of 10–15% over the medium term. 

# **Strong financial risk profile:** 

The financial risk profile has strengthened, with the net worth improving to Rs. 1,527 crores as of March 31, 2026, from Rs. 1,346 crores a year ago. This improvement was driven by the continuous accretion of reserves, which led to a healthy capital structure despite undertaking debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) in the past 2–3 fiscals. The capital structure has been healthy, indicated by a gearing ratio of 0.73 times and a total outside liabilities to tangible net worth (TOL/TNW) ratio of 0.99 times as of March 31, 2026 (compared to 0.77 times and 0.88 times, respectively, as of March 31, 2025). A sizable capex of Rs. 346 crores was incurred in fiscal 2026 to operationalize its defense equipment manufacturing facility and for debottlenecking the production process. The capex undertaken was funded by additional debt of around Rs. 62 crores in fiscal 2026 and the balance through internal cash accrual. GIL has plans to undertake an additional capex of around Rs. 300 crores annually over the next 2–3 fiscals to enhance its artillery shell manufacturing capacity to 400,000 shells per annum from 150,000 shells per annum. This capex also aims to enhance the steel processing capacity to 600,000 MTPA from 500,000 MTPA, focused on increasing ERW precision tubes and GI Conduit tubes capacity. The capex will be partially funded by additional debt and the balance through internal cash accrual. Despite the additional debt-funded capex planned, the capital structure is expected to remain comfortable, indicated by gearing and TOL/TNW ratios of 0.7–0.75 times and 0.95–1.0 times, respectively, by the end of fiscal 2027. Debt protection metrics remained comfortable, supported by the expansion of operating profitability and the sustenance of capital structure. This is indicated by an interest coverage ratio of 3.76 times and a net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratio of 0.22 times as of March 31, 2026. Through timely repayment of debt and healthy operating profitability, the interest coverage ratio and NCAAD ratio are expected to remain stable at 4.6–4.7 times and 0.2–0.25 times, respectively, in fiscal 2027. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Working capital intensive operations:** 

Operations are working capital intensive indicated by Gross current assets (GCAs) of 152 days as on March 31, 2026, driven by receivables of 43 days and inventory of 84 days. The group has a diversified customer profile with domestic payment terms of 60-90 days and export orders are secured through payment confirmation after which material dispatched. With the increase in export sales the payment realisation is better, however, increased offtake from the defense segment, primarily by government entities resulting in further stretch in realization from customers will remain monitorable. Raw material inventory of 60-80 days is kept which is majorly order-backed. GCAs are expected at 140-150 days going forward. Working capital management and extent of bank limit utilisation remain key monitorable. 

# **Susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices due to intense competition:** 

The steel industry is inherently cyclical and remains vulnerable to volatility in raw material prices and price realizations. The key raw material, hot-rolled coils, accounts for around 70% of the total raw material cost. Moreover, the company has limited room to pass on the impact of increases in raw material prices to customers in short-term contracts, although they can pass on such costs for some products. Even in the case of long-term contracts, price changes can be passed on only after a lag of 1–2 months. Furthermore, intense competition and limited product differentiation can exert pressure on margins. The 

management has taken strong initiatives to mitigate the risk of limited product differentiation by venturing into the defense equipment manufacturing industry. This includes operationalizing an artillery shell manufacturing facility with a capacity of 150,000 shells per annum in January 2026, which has high entry barriers and limited competition due to stringent regulations and large capital expenditure requirements. Further, the improvement in the offtake of high-margin products, resulting in their increasing contribution to the company's overall revenue, led to an expansion of operating margins to 9.7% in fiscal 2026 from 8.5% in fiscal 2025. Increased offtake from the defense segment, coupled with the introduction of highvalue-added products and healthy offtake post-enhancement of existing capacity, is expected to result in further improvement in operating profit margins to around 9.75–10% over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Bank limit utilisation was around 84% for the 12 months ended April 2026. Cash accrual is expected at Rs 240-320 crore per annum, against yearly debt obligation of Rs 46-62 crore over the medium term; the surplus cash will act as cushion to liquidity. 

Current ratio stood at 1.32 times on March 31, 2026. 

The company also maintains moderate cash and bank balance of around Rs 25-30 crores. 

Low gearing and moderate networth aid financial flexibility. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes that healthy and sustained growth in revenue along with sustenance of operating margin, driven by increased contribution of high margin products to the overall revenue of the group will support the credit risk profile over the medium term. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Significant increase in revenue and sustenance of operating margin of more than 10% due to contribution from high margin product segment. 

- Better working capital management and no significant increase in debt levels, keeping gearing below one time and improvement in debt coverage indicators on a sustained basis 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in scale of operation and operating margins below 7% resulting in lower net cash accrual. 

Large debt-funded capital expenditure or substantial increase in working capital requirements thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1986, GIL (formerly, Goodluck Steel Tubes Ltd) manufactures sheets, pipes, engineering structures, fabricated structures, forgings, and automobile tubes. The company has five manufacturing facilities in Sikanderabad (Uttar Pradesh) and one recently operational in Kutch (Gujarat), with a total installed capacity of more than 500,000 tonne per annum. The company is listed on Bombay Stock Exchange Ltd and National Stock Exchange Ltd. GIL reported a revenue of Rs.1088 crore with net profit of Rs. 56 crore during Q4-FY25. GDAL was incorporated on August 31, 2023, with objective to specialize in the forging, machining, treatment, and coating of various metals for defense applications. It is the subsidiary of GIL.
