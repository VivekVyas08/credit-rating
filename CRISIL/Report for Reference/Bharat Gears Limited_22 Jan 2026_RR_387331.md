### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank facilities of Bharat Gears Ltd (BGL) and has simultaneously **withdrawn** the ratings on receipt of a withdrawal request from the company and on receipt of No-objection certificates (NOCs) from the bankers. The ratings withdrawal is in line with Crisil Ratings policy on the withdrawal of its ratings. 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the auto components industry and their established relationships with key customers, and the partially integrated operations of the company. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicity in the auto industry and leveraged capital structure. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of BGL 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position:** BGL is a leading player in the tractor gear market and enjoys strong relationships with original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). Its products have high brand recall. Though a major part of the revenue comes from the John Deere group (35% of the turnover), BGL has a long association of over two decades with the client, which mitigates the risk. 

**Strong manufacturing set-up backed by wide distribution network:** The company’s state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in Mumbra (Maharashtra) and Faridabad (Haryana) are fitted with modern equipment and certified as per International Organization for Standardization. Over the years, BGL has developed strong capabilities in gear manufacturing, enabling it to be a quality and reliable supplier to reputed OEMs in both the domestic and international markets. It has a dedicated research and development (R&D) wing to come up with new product variants and help evolve existing products to meet customer needs. A wide dealer network spread across states helps further consolidate the strong market position. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to cyclicality in end-user industries and volatile operating margin:** Pricing power against key customers such as John Deere is limited. Though the price variation clause in client contracts protects the company from any significant movement in input cost, there is limited flexibility to pass on an increase in overheads and other costs to end users. Furthermore, revenue depends on demand from the tractor, construction equipment, and commercial vehicle segments, which are inherently cyclical. Nevertheless, the impact of these factors on operating profitability is likely to be mitigated by increasing volumes and greater contribution from the higher margin export business or diversification of customer base. 

**Leveraged capital structure:** Adjusted debt to adjusted networth ratio remained weak at 0.70 time due to higher debt on books as on September 30, 2025. Also, debt protection metrics were muted, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 1.93 times and 0.27 time, respectively, in September 2025. The metrics are expected to remain under pressure over the medium term. 

### **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Expected annual cash accrual of over Rs 25 crore should cover yearly repayment obligations of Rs 18-20 crore and likely capex requirements of Rs 2-3 crore per annum over the medium term. Cushion between net cash accrual and repayment obligations is sensitive to improvement in operating margin, which will remain key monitorable over the medium term. Bank limit utilisation was 68% (fund-based limit of Rs 45 crore) as of November 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Negative 

BGL’s credit risk profile will remain under pressure on account of subdued operating performance with fall in operating margin. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

- Sustained revenue profile and operating margin resulting in net cash accrual to repayment obligation remaining above 1.5 times 

- Improvement in the financial risk profile, particularly interest coverage ratio, efficiency in working capital management and strengthening of overall financial flexibility 

# **Downward factors:** 

- Subdued revenue growth or operating margin below 3.5% resulting in lower net cash accrual against significant repayment obligations 

- Stretch in working capital limits or weakening of debt protection metrics further weakens financial risk profile, especially liquidity 

# **<u>About the company</u>** 

Established in 1971, BGL manufactures a range of gears for tractors, commercial vehicles, buses, and utility vehicles. Products include hypoid and spiral gears, differential gears and shafts, complete automotive transmissions, gearbox subassemblies, and differential assemblies.
