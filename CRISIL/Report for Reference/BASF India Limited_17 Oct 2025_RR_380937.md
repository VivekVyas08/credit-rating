# **Update on BASF India Limited** 

_The common independent director on Crisil Ratings Limited and BASF India Limited boards did not participate in the rating process or in the meeting of the rating committee, when the rating for securities of BASF India Limited was discussed. This rating was also not discussed in the meeting of Crisil Ratings’ Board of Directors. Note: None of the Directors on Crisil Ratings Limited’s Board are members of rating committee and thus do not participate in discussion or assignment of any ratings. The Board of Directors also does not discuss any ratings at its meetings._ 

Crisil Ratings has taken note of the latest announcement by BASF India Limited (BASF India), dated October 10, 2025, wherein, its parent BASF SE, Germany (BASF SE; rated ‘A-/Stable/A2’ by S&P Global Ratings) had announced that BASF SE and funds managed by global investment firm Carlyle, in partnership with Qatar Investment Authority (QIA), have entered into a binding agreement relating to BASF’s automotive OEM coatings, automotive refinish coatings and surface treatment businesses. The global enterprise value of the transaction is valued at ~EUR 7.7 billion and the transaction is expected to close in second quarter of CY2026, subject to customary regulatory approvals. Further, BASF SE will reinvest in coatings business holding a 40% equity stake and will receive pre-tax cash proceeds of ~EUR 5.8 billion. 

It may be noted that, in BASF India, the automotive OEM coatings and automotive refinish coatings (Coatings business), was transferred to BASF India Coatings Private Limited, (BASF ICPL) a wholly owned subsidiary of BASF India, effective January 1, 2025. During fiscal 2025, the coatings business generated a revenue of Rs 479.6 crore representing 3.1% of the consolidated BASF India revenues. BASF India will evaluate and assess the effect of this global announcement on BASF India and BASF ICPL. 

Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor developments related to the said announcement and expects the divestment of majority stake in BASF ICPL will not materially impact BASF India’s credit profile, given the subsidiary’s modest scale of operations. 

The ratings on credit facilities of BASF India continue to reflect the stable business risk profile supported by diverse revenue segments, strong financial risk profile and Crisil Ratings expectation regarding the strong operational and financial support from BASF SE. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility of profitability to inherent risks in the crop protection sector and volatility in crude prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates.
