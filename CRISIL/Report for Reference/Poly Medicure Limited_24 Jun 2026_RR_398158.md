### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Poly Medicure Limited (PolyMed; part of the PolyMed group) to **'Positive'** from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at ' **Crisil AA-** ’. The rating on the short-term bank loan facilities has been reaffirmed at ‘Crisil A1+'. 

The outlook revision reflects the Crisil Ratings’ belief that the business risk profile of the group will remain benefited from the inorganic expansions in newer geographies by improving the proximity to the customer, and product diversification across segments such as cardiology, orthopedics, and implants hence leading to improved volumes and better efficiencies. Amidst improved demand from existing customers, new product introductions, and customer acquisitions, revenue has witnessed a compound annual growth (CAGR) rate of ~19% over the three fiscals through 2026, reaching Rs 1875 crore and a further 20-22% on-year growth is expected in fiscal 2027. Operating profitability has also improved consistently over the past few fiscals and was ~26% in fiscal 2026 (28.8% in fiscal 2025). Despite an expected increase in raw material prices, profitability is likely to remain robust at 23-25% over the medium term. Moderate profitability, along with efficient working capital management, has resulted in a healthy return on capital employed, estimated at ~12-14% in fiscal 2026. 

The ratings also factor in the PolyMedi group's robust financial risk profile, supported by a sizeable tangible networth of Rs 3106 crore as on March 31, 2026 (~Rs 2765.6 crore a year earlier), backed by steady accretion to reserve. Despite sizeable capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 200-300 crore planned annually over the medium term—which is funded through internal cash accruals—the capital structure is expected to remain robust. Healthy accretion to reserve is likely to support this, with gearing estimated below 0.10 time as on March 31, 2026. Liquidity also stands robust, with healthy net cash accrual against maturing debt, a cushion in bank lines, and positive cash flow from operations. 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position of the PolyMed group in the IV cannula product segment, its strong operating profitability and robust financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the high, though reducing, dependence on core products, and exposure to risks posed by intense competition, fluctuations in raw material prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates, and regulatory changes. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of PolyMed and its subsidiaries, namely Poly Medicure (Laiyang) Co Ltd (PMLCL; based in China), Poly Medicure BV (Netherlands; PMBV), Plan1 Health India Pvt Ltd, Plan1Health SRL (a 100% subsidiary of PMBV), Ultra Medical Products Company (a wholly owned subsidiary of PolyMed), Poly Health Medical Inc. (USA), a step-down subsidiary, RISOR Holding BV, Medistream SA (Switzerland), Medyneo Comércio de Produtos para Saúde Ltda (Brazil), and Himalayan Mineral Water Pvt Ltd. These entities are collectively referred to herein as the PolyMed group.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Strong market position in the medical devices industry:** Revenue has grown steadily, recording a 19% CAGR over the three fiscals through March 2026; this trend should continue over the medium term, supported by capacity addition and product innovation and development. Revenue is estimated at Rs 1,875 crore for fiscal 2026, a ~12% improvement over the 

previous fiscal. The group has over 390 registered patents across countries. Continuous capacity addition and product innovation and development will support revenue growth over the medium term as well. Further diversification in the product portfolio, lowering dependence on core products, will be a key monitorable. 

**Strong operating profitability:** Operating efficiency, reflected in the healthy operating margin of 26-28% during the three fiscals through 2026, is driven by the labour-cost advantage over global competitors and in-house tool design and research and development (R&D) facilities. However, the operating margin is expected to moderate to 23-25% in the medium term due to increased raw material prices. Nevertheless, the margin is likely to be supported by better economies of scale, which in turn will be driven by steady capacity utilisation, modernisation of facilities, and cost-cutting initiatives. 

**Robust financial risk profile:** Networth is projected to be Rs 2,900-3,100 crore as on March 31, 2027 (Rs 2,754 crore as on March 31, 2026). Gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth (TOL/TNW) ratios are expected to remain below 0.2 time over the medium term. Networth is likely to improve further, aided by better accretion to reserve. Net debt remains negligible due to the group's strong liquidity. Debt protection metrics are also robust, as indicated by interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios projected at 17-20 times and 1.0-1.3 times, respectively, for fiscal 2027 (compared to 28.2 times and 1.0 time, respectively, in fiscal 2026). Capex of Rs 250-300 crore, towards regular maintenance and R&D in the coming fiscals, is expected to be largely met through internal accruals. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**High, though reducing, dependence on core products:** The group is highly dependent on core products; infusion therapy products formed ~53% of sales in fiscal 2026 (reduced from 69% in fiscal 2023). Though new products have been added to the portfolio, they still have a lower revenue contribution and will take time to penetrate the markets. Benefits, likely to accrue over time, should gradually reduce dependence on core categories. Thus, the group is likely to remain exposed to revenue concentration in core product categories over the medium term. These risks are however, mitigated by the strong market position of the group in these segments. 

**Exposure to fluctuations in raw material prices and forex rates:** The price of the key material (plastic) is directly linked to crude oil prices, which are highly volatile. As the cost of procuring plastic forms bulk of production expenses, even a slight variation in rates could have an adverse impact on profitability. Further, as exports accounted for nearly two-thirds of sales, operating margin will remain exposed to any unfavourable fluctuation in forex rates. 

**Susceptibility to intense competition and changes in regulations:** The group exports its products to highly qualityconscious markets such as Europe, reflecting its adherence to stringent quality standards. Its Unit-II at Faridabad, Haryana, was audited by the US Food and Drug Authority; all plants have received CE certifications, permitting exports to Europe. Any change in policies in these markets can impact profitability. Intense competition, both locally (from unorganised players) and globally (from reputed players such as Baxter, Becton Dickinson, B Braun and Boston Scientific), may continue to constrain scalability, pricing power and profitability. Further, lower expenditure on R&D activities, than international players, limits the capability to develop new products for global markets. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Superior 

Bank limit utilisation averaged 56.8% for the 12 months through March 2026. Expected cash accrual of Rs 350-400 crore should comfortably cover term debt obligation of Rs 18-20 crore over the medium term. Current ratio is estimated at a healthy 3.4 times as on March 31, 2026. The company held cash and bank balance and liquid investments in shares, debentures, and mutual funds of Rs 700-800 crore as on March 31, 2026, raised majorly from qualified institutional placement. Low gearing and moderate networth offer financial flexibility to withstand adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

Crisil Ratings believes that the business risk profile will further improve amidst global expansion and expected improvement due to diversified product profiles though timely ramp up of new subsidiaries will remain a key monitorable.

# **Upward factors** 

Growth in revenue above Rs 2200 crore, with increasing contribution from newer products, and steady operating margin of 22-24% leading to healthy net cash accrual 

Sustenance of superior financial risk profile, especially liquidity 

# **Downward factors** 

Moderation in revenue and profitability due to intense competition or impact of any adverse action; for instance, fall in operating margin below 20% on a sustained basis 

Higher-than-expected capex or acquisition, leading to moderation in credit metrics 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

The PolyMed group is promoted by Mr Himanshu Baid and Mr Rishi Baid. The group's flagship company, PolyMed was incorporated in 1995; it manufactures disposable medical items, such as IV cannula, blood bags, blood collection tubes, and infusion and transfusion sets. The company is currently listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange. PMLCL started commercial operations in April 2009. PolyMed also has a joint venture, Ultra for Medical Products 

Co, Egypt, with the El-Agar group, which directly caters to Africa and other markets. In fiscal 2019, PolyMed also acquired Plan1Health SRL (100% subsidiary of Poly Medicure B.V., Netherlands), an Italy-based company that manufactures mainly cancer-related devices and other critical devices. The group has 12 manufacturing facilities across 4 countries. 9 manufacturing facilities in India (6 in Faridabad, 2 in Jaipur and 1 in Haridwar), 3 overseas facilities (one in China through a wholly owned subsidiary, one joint venture in Egypt, and one in Italy).
