### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank loan facilities and commercial paper programme of Rupa & Company Limited (Rupa; part of the Rupa group). 

The ratings continue to reflect a strong market presence and extensive experience of the promoter in the hosiery industry, along with healthy financial risk profile. These rating strengths are partially offset by large working capital requirement and exposure to intensifying competition in the innerwear industry. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of Rupa and its wholly owned subsidiaries, Euro Fashion Inners International Pvt Ltd (Euro), Imoogi Fashions Pvt Ltd (Imoogi), Rupa Bangladesh Private Limited, Rupa Fashion Private Limited and Oban Fashions Pvt Ltd (Oban). This is because all these entities, together referred to as the Rupa group, have significant operational and financial linkages. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Strong market presence and extensive industry experience of the promoter:** The group has established its position in the intensely competitive domestic hosiery industry. The promoter have extensive industry experience of more than five decades. Rupa benefits from strong brand recall in the premium and economy segments and has over 18 sub-brands that cater to the economy, medium, premium and super-premium segments. RCL is well-positioned to capitalize on its operational efficiencies and deepen its penetration in the market. Backed by the company’s well-established presence in PAN India, revenue rose to Rs 1239 crore in fiscal 2025, exhibiting a growth rate of 2% as against the previous fiscal. Robust demand scenario led to volumetric growth of about 3% in the fiscal resulted in healthy overall growth in the scale of operations. 

The brand is expected to grow further with expansion into newer segments like athleisure and female range along with healthy marketing spends and consumer recall value. It has also developed premium brands and after having consolidated its position in the mid premium segment, RCL is now focusing on increasing its brand presence of its premium segment in newer states to enhance its margins. 

RCL’s robust supply chain management with extensive distributor network has helped in greater market reach supported by PAN India. It currently has a diversified geographical presence states such as North East, West Bengal, Orissa, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, with major brand presence in 

Eastern and Northern India through a distribution network comprising 1500+ distributors and 1,50,000+ retailers which has ensured strong growth in scale of operations for the company over the years. The company is also present in the export markets. 

RCL also markets its products through Modern Retail Trade (i.e., Large Format Stores) and aspires to grow its sales through these channels going forward. RCL’s extensive distribution network shall continue to support the business risk profile over the medium term. 

Backed by steady volumetric growth through diversified geographical presence and greater market penetration, on the back of wide product portfolio, wide geographical presence, better brand recall, increased distribution leading to capturing of market demand via expansion of dealer distribution network from accrued benefits of increased advertisement, penetration into newer product segments such as women-wear and athleisure and robust demand scenario, RCL is expecting a turnover growth of 10-12% over the medium term backed by volumetric growth and steady realisations. 

**Above-average financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile should remain supported by steady accretion to reserves. Capital structure continues to remain healthy marked by net worth of Rs 1016.60 crore and gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth (TOL/TNW) of 0.22 and 0.49 time respectively as on March 31<sup>st</sup> , 2025. In the absence of any major debt funded capex plans along with steady accretion to reserves, capital structure is expected to remain healthy over the medium term. Debt protection metrics continues to remain comfortable marked by interest coverage and net cash accruals to total debt (NCATD) of  6.28 times and 33% respectively as on 31<sup>st</sup> March, 2025. 

# **Weaknesses:** 

**Large working capital requirement:** Intense competition in the hosiery industry necessitates offering substantial credit to distributors and maintaining large inventory to minimise delays in delivery. Inventory management has improved considerably with focus on optimising production in line with sales patterns. As such, inventory days remained moderate at around 147 days as on 31<sup>st</sup> March, 2025 and is expected to remain along similar lines over the medium term. Amid intense competition, credit period extended to distributors shot up significantly during fiscal 2025 coupled with greater skewedness towards the year end yielding debtor days of around 151 days as on March 31st, 2025. Going forward, improvement in working capital management while sustaining the healthy business risk profile will remain a key monitorable. 

**Exposure to intensifying competition in the innerwear industry and profitability exposed to volatile raw material prices:** The innerwear market in India is dominated by the unorganised sector, despite the robust market potential for branded innerwear, leading to intense competition. Competition could also increase with the advent of other established foreign brands through the franchisee route, large domestic readymade garment manufacturers venturing into innerwear segments, and other players spending heavily on brand-building and product positioning. 

In FY2023, cotton yarn prices had risen sharply which had restricted EBITDA Margins of players in the hosiery industry owing to intense competition and moderate ability to pass on any increase in the prices of raw materials to consumers directly. Operating margin in fiscal 2023 had fallen from all-time high of 19.7% and 18.3% in fiscals 2021 and 2022, respectively. This was primarily due to reducing yarn prices in fiscal 2023 after seeing two consecutive surge years wherein hosiery players, such as Rupa who benefitted from high year-end finished goods inventory were highly impacted by the inventory loss. Reduced sales volume and reduction in year-end prices, owing to intense competition in the highly fragmented innerwear and hosiery industry, were other factors impacting profitability in fiscal 2023. Cotton yarn prices started to come down from second half of fiscal 2023 and as such, operating margins remained around 9.70% in fiscal 2024 as against 8% in fiscal 2023 owing to greater advertisement costs to expand market share. Basis better economies of scale, reduced reliance on advertisement costs and stable raw material prices, operating margins further improved to around 10.52% in fiscal 2025. Operating margins are further expected to improve basis stable raw material prices (cooling of yarn prices), lower reliance on advertisement costs as well as better operational efficiency through increased sales volumes. Sustenance of healthy operating profitability amidst raw material price volatility and intense competition shall remain a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Bank limit utilisation is moderate at less than 60 percent for the past 12 months ended May 2025. Cash accrual are expected to be over Rs 85-100 crore which shall be sufficient against term debt obligation of less than Rs 2 crore over the medium term. Current ratio remains healthy at 2.60 times as on 31<sup>st</sup> March, 2025. Unencumbered liquid funds of more than Rs 240 crore as on 31<sup>st</sup> March 2025 provides additional cushion to liquidity and sustenance of the same will remain key monitorable, over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

The strong market position of the group and its established distribution network will continue to support business risk profile over the medium term.
