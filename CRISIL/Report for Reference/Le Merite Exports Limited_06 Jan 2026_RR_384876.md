### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has downgraded its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Le Merite Exports Limited (LMEL) to ' **Crisil BB+/Stable’** from ‘Crisil BBB-/Negative’. 

The rating action reflects the moderation in overall credit risk profile driven by increased project related risks associated with the large capital expenditure.   The company is setting up a new manufacturing unit with an estimated cost of Rs. 85-90 crores, but with less than 10% of the project completed as of December 2025, it remains exposed to implementation related risks. Further, the funding tie up is also pending and is expected to close by end of fiscal 2026. While planned equity infusion of approximately Rs. 20 crores partially mitigate funding concerns, timely completion of the project along with fund disbursement within targeted deadline without any cost overruns will be a key rating monitorable. 

In addition, revenues in the first half of fiscal year 2026 declined by ~30% as compared to same period last year, following a strategic shift towards defense products, revenue diversification towards other geographies thus reducing dependence on Bangladesh and closure of operations in one of the subsidiaries. However, overall business risk profile remains adequate, supported by improvement in operating margins leading to healthy cash accruals during the same period. Sustained improvement in scale of operations, stability in profitability and timely ramp up in the large capex remains a key rating sensitivity factor. 

The rating reflects the extensive promoter experience in the textile industry and its comfortable capital structure. These rating strengths are partially offset by moderate yet improving debt protection metrics and susceptibility of operating margins to fluctuating raw material prices along with intense competition, large working capital requirements and risk related to execution of project. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at its ratings, Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of LMEL with its subsidiary Le Merite Laxmi Spinning Private Limited, Le Merite Filaments Private Limited, Le Merite Tactical & Defence Solutions Private Limited together known as Le Merite group. Crisil considers that the subsidiary is strategically important to, and have a significant degree of operational integration with Le Merite Exports Limited. 

Unsecured loans of Rs 12.36 crores as on March 31, 2025 extended by the promoters are treated as debt.

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoter:** The promoters of the company have more than a decade of experience in the textile industry, which has helped them understand the business and industry dynamics and has also enabled them to overcome business cycles. Long-standing presence in the industry has also developed healthy relationships with customers and suppliers.  Additionally, this has also helped the company to get TOT (transfer of technology) certificate for production of textile related products for defense. Backed by the same, the group achieved revenues of Rs 535 crores in FY 2025. While the company has achieved revenues of Rs 213 crores in H1 of fiscal 2025 which is lower than the same period last year, it is largely on account of shifting focus of the group towards its own manufacturing set up, increasing exposure to defense 

related textile products and closure of operations in one of the subsidiaries of the group. Nevertheless, the business risk profile of the group will continue to be supported by the extensive experience of the promoters. 

**Comfortable capital structure:** The financial risk profile is marked with a net worth of Rs 112 crores as on March 31, 2025, led by the steady accretion to the reserves and equity infusion. The improvement in net worth has led to the improvement in capital structure leading to the gearing and total outside liability to adjusted net worth ratio of 0.79 times and 1.11 times as on March 31, 2025 (0.84 times and 1.15 times a year ago). While the group is expected to have a debt of ~50-60 crores for its ongoing capex, the capital structure is expected to remain comfortable, however, any higher-than-expected debt could impact the capital structure over medium term and hence remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate yet improving debt protection metrices and susceptibility of operating margins to fluctuating raw material prices and intense competition:** Historically, the operating margins of the group have moderated on account of sharp fluctuations and decline in the spreads between cotton and cotton yarn prices. This had resulted in moderation in the interest coverage to fall below 1.5 times in fiscal 2025. While the operating margins of the group have improved to upwards of 5% in H1 of fiscal 2026, it is primarily driven by the improvement in gross profits and higher sales from defense products. Thus, any sharp fluctuation in the raw material prices could materially impact on the operating margins of the group and thereby the debt protection metrices. Thus, the sustenance of the improved profitability levels remains monitorable. 

Additionally, the cotton trading industry is marked by huge fragmentation, given the low entry barriers. The presence of several small players catering to price-sensitive customers intensifies competition and restricts bargaining power with customers and suppliers, impacting the profitability of the company. In addition, the revenues are geographically concentrated in Bangladesh (~44% and ~35% of revenues in fiscal 2025 and H1 fiscal 2026, respectively) which exposes the company to region specific developments and risks.  Therefore, a diversification in revenues towards better margins (products related to defense) remains crucial for LMEL as witnessed in first half of fiscal 2026 thus a successful scale up of operations in this segment leading to sustenance of improved profitability levels a key rating sensitivity factor. 

**Moderate working capital requirement** : The working capital cycle of the company is moderately working capital intensive as reflected in the gross current assets of 140 days as on March 31, 2025 (143 days a year ago). This is driven by moderate debtors and low inventory of around 82 days and 8 days respectively as on March 31, 2025 (as compared to 73 and 14 days, a year ago). Although the receivables are large due to the high credit period extended to customers, inventory remains low and generally order backed. Company also has more than 6 months debtors of around Rs 15 crores, these are expected to be recovered. Additionally, given the group exposure towards Bangladesh of ~35% as of H1 of fiscal 2026 (~44% for FY 2025) could also impact the working capital operations given the geopolitical issues in the country. Although LMEL has not faced any significant challenges in receipt of the debtors from the counterparts of this region, a successful diversification in revenues and timely receipt of the outstanding debtors remains monitorable. 

**Exposure to risk related to ongoing project under execution** : The group is setting up a manufacturing unit to shift its focus from current asset lite model largely to cater to demand for defense related textile products. The project cost is estimated to be around Rs 85-90 crores as indicated to Crisil ratings by the management. The capex is expected to be funded by a mix of debt (~Rs 50-60 crores), equity (~Rs 20 crores) and internal accruals. While the execution related risk is high for the project given moderate completion of less than 10% of project cost as of December 2025, the funding related risk is also high given that the debt for the project is not yet tied up. The timely completion of the project and fund disbursement within the targeted timelines and avoiding any cost overrun or higher than expected debt levels impacting the existing operations of the business will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Stretched 

The utilization on the fund-based bank limits (Packing credit) averaged at around 73% for the past 12 months ended October 2025. The Net Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 13-14 Crores over medium term which will be sufficient to cover against the expected repayment obligations for the term loans to be taken for the capex under execution, however any larger than expected debt funding or change in the repayment terms could impact the cushion between Net Cash Accruals to Repayment obligations. Cash and Bank balance of Rs 46 crores as on September 2025 of which Rs 30-33 crores which is free supports the liquidity. Additionally, need based support from the promoters in the form of USL will support the liquidity. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes credit risk profile of Le Merite Exports Limited will be supported by its healthy capital structure and extensive promoter experience 

# **<u>Rating Sensitivity Factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

- Significant improvement in the scale of operations and sustenance of operating margins above 5-6% levels leading to higher than anticipated net cash accruals 

- Sustenance of financial risk profile especially interest coverage sustaining above 2-2.5 times 

Timely completion and successful ramp up in the planned capex and reduced geographical concentration 

**Downward Factors** 

Any higher-than-expected debt funding impacting the capital structure of the group 

Decline in revenues or operating margins below 3% leading to lower-than-expected net cash accruals Further moderation in the working capital cycle, especially debtor cycle. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

Incorporated in 2003 by the promoter, Mr Abhishek Lath, LMEL trades in cotton yarn and fabric. The Mumbai-based company derives bulk of its revenue from exports. Since June 2020, the company started manufacturing cotton yarn in its leased unit. Manufacturing facilities are located in Nagpur, Dhamangaon and Nanded (all in Maharashtra) 

Le Merite Laxmi Spinning Private Limited is a 51% owned subsidiary of Le Merite Exports Limited engaged in the manufacturing of cotton yarn in its leased unit. The company is now non operational. 

Le Merite Filament Private Limited is a majority owned subsidiary of LMEL however is non operational. 

Le Merite Tactical & Defence Solutions Private Limited is a newly incorporated company with LMEL having 51% of Share holding. It is currently doing a greenfield project of setting up manufacturing unit in Amravati for defense related textile products
