### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities and commercial paper programme of Alembic Pharmaceuticals Ltd (Alembic Pharma). 

The ratings continue to reflect the strong position of Alembic Pharma in the domestic formulations market, its diversified revenue profile with growing presence in the international generics segment and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by modest profitability due to increased operating expenses from new manufacturing facilities, which are yet to operate at optimal capacity, and exposure to pricing pressure and regulatory risks in the pharmaceutical (pharma) industry. 

Consolidated revenue grew ~10% on-year in fiscal 2026, supported by healthy growth in the US and ex-US formulations, while domestic formulations and active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) registered moderate growth. US formulations grew 13%, supported by new launches, volume growth and market share gains despite price erosion. Ex-US formulations grew 20%, aided by product additions, partner-led expansion and geographic diversification. Domestic formulations grew 5%, driven by Animal Health and select specialty therapies, though human branded formulations remained subdued due to weak volume traction, mature legacy brands, slower growth in anti-infectives. Over the medium term, revenue growth is expected to remain healthy, supported by US launch momentum, scale-up of complex products, continued ex-US growth, steady domestic formulations growth and gradual growth in APIs post the recovery in fiscal 2026. 

Operating margin stood at 15.9% in fiscal 2026, against 15.5% in fiscal 2025, supported by operating leverage, cost optimisation and better utilisation of newer facilities. However, margin expansion remained constrained by higher research and development (R&D) spend, US generics price erosion, API pricing pressure and costs related to the US branded business. The operating margin is expected to improve gradually to 16–17% over the medium term, supported by better facility utilisation, cost-control measures and launch-led revenue growth, though costs related to the recently launched branded drug, Pivya, may constrain any material improvement in fiscal 2027. 

The company’s working capital intensity remains high, with gross current assets at 224 days as on March 31, 2026, compared with 222 days as on March 31, 2025. Net working capital increased by around Rs 330 crore, primarily on account of elevated inventory. The inventory build-up is owing to planned launches and stocking of key APIs and related inputs to ensure uninterrupted operations amid geopolitical uncertainties. Consequently, short-term borrowings remained high at Rs 1,239 crore as on March 31, 2026, broadly similar to Rs 1,196 crore as on March 31, 2025, but significantly higher than Rs 430 crore as on March 31, 2024. Total debt, including lease liabilities of Rs 69 crore, stood at Rs 1,430 crore as on March 31, 2026. With planned product launches and gradual inventory liquidation, working capital is expected to improve, but the pace of inventory reduction remains monitorable. 

The company has undertaken sizeable capital expenditure (capex) over the past few fiscals towards specialised generics, injectables, ophthalmics, oncology, peptides and domestic formulation capacity. Annual capex is expected to remain moderate at Rs 400 crore, primarily towards maintenance, debottlenecking and capacity enhancement. The capex is 

expected to be funded through internal accrual. Resultantly, the financial risk profile will remain healthy, supported by comfortable capital structure and debt protection metrics, though elevated working capital borrowings will be monitorable.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Strong position in the domestic formulation market** 

Alembic remains an established domestic formulations player, with fiscal 2026 domestic branded revenue of Rs. 2,458 crore, over 200 brands, 5,500+ MRs, and a strong prescription base. The company had around 1.2% market share as per IQVIA MAT Mar-26. Domestic growth was moderate at 5% during the year, with the Animal Health portfolio driving growth, while human branded formulations remained subdued due to weak volume traction, mature legacy brands. Over the medium term, growth is expected to be supported by chronic/specialty therapies, Animal Health, focus-brand execution and regular launches. 

# **Diversified presence in the international market** 

International business, including API, accounted for around 67% of revenues in fiscal 2026, with the US remaining the key market at around 30% of revenue. Alembic also has sizeable presence across Europe, Australia, Canada, Brazil, Chile and South Africa. US formulations grew 13% in FY26, supported by new launches, volume growth and market-share gains despite continued price erosion. The company commercialised 15 products in fiscal 2026, taking cumulative US commercialised products to 178. As of March 31, 2026, Alembic had filed 274 ANDAs, with 235 approvals including tentative approvals. Over the medium term, regulated-market growth should be supported by complex products, new launches, better utilisation of newer facilities, entry into US branded formulations through Pivya and long-term customer relationships; however, price erosion and regulatory compliance remain key monitorables. 

# **Healthy financial risk profile** 

Financial risk profile remained strong in fiscal 2026, supported by net worth of nearly Rs. 5,400 crore, comfortable gearing of around 0.27x, and healthy debt protection metrics, with interest coverage of around 12.5x. Capex is expected to remain moderate at up to Rs. 400 crore annually, mainly towards debottlenecking, capacity enhancement, replacement capex and selective in-licensing opportunities, and is expected to be funded through internal accruals. Debt metrics are expected to remain comfortable over the medium term, though any major capex or debt-funded acquisition remains a key rating sensitivity factor. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Profitability remains constrained by sub-optimal utilisation of new facilities, price erosion in US markets** 

Operating margin stood at 15.9% in fiscal 2026, supported by cost optimisation, operating leverage and better utilisation of newer facilities. However, profitability remains constrained by sub-optimal utilisation of new facilities, particularly F2 and F3 , continued US generics price erosion, API pricing pressure, elevated R&D spend and launch-phase costs for the US branded business.  Utilisation of F2 and F3 facilities improved during fiscal 2026 and is expected to ramp up gradually with new launches, out-licensing and CMO opportunities this fiscal.. 

The share of R&D in sales has eased from 12-14% in the past to 9-10% at present and this, coupled with cost optimization measures, focus on high-margin products, is expected to help improve operating margin in the 16-17% range over the medium term, with any significant improvement hinging on pace of improvement in facility utilisation and the ability to absorb Pivya-related costs. 

# **Exposure to intensifying pricing pressure and regulatory risk** 

The company remains exposed to risk posed by regulatory changes in India and the overseas markets as reflected in increasing scrutiny and inspections by authorities, including the US Food and Drug Administration, European Medicines Agency and TGA Australia. Any adverse findings from these regulators remain monitorable. In the domestic market, the regulatory impact of Drug Pricing Control Order and ban on some fixed-dose combinations adversely affected revenue and profit in the past and will also be monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

The company had liquid surplus of Rs.203 crore as on March 31, 2026. Annual cash accrual is expected at Rs.700-800 crore over the medium term and will be sufficient to cover routine capex of around Rs.400 crore and service debt repayments of less than Rs.75 crore over the next two years. Further, utilisation of fund-based bank facilities was modest at 27% during six months ending March 2026. Comfortable debt protection metrics enhance the company’s fund raising ability. 

# **<u>ESG Profile</u>** 

The environment, social, and governance (ESG) profile of Alembic Pharma supports its strong credit risk profile. 

The pharma sector can have a significant impact on the environment owing to greenhouse gas emissions, water use and waste generation. The sector’s social impact is characterised by the impact on the health and wellbeing of consumers due to its products and on employees and the local community on account of its operations. 

# **Key ESG highlights** 

- Alembic Pharmaceuticals Ltd (Alembic Pharma) targets to achieve net zero emissions by 2050 (verified and validated by Science Based Target initiative [SBTi]). Against this target, the company has made progress in reducing its direct emissions by ~10% in fiscal 2025 (compared to fiscal 2021 baseline) 

- The company's transition to renewable energy gained momentum with commissioning of another 12 MW solar power plant (to its existing 12 MW facility), boosting the share of renewables in its energy mix to ~20% in fiscal 2025 from ~5% in fiscal 2023 

- Alembic Pharma has aimed to attain water neutrality by 2027 and has developed 102 water recharge wells for groundwater recharge through which it has achieved 85% water neutrality in fiscal 2025 

- Lost time injury frequency rate for employees and workers is lower (at 0.03 and 0.07 per million person-hours worked, respectively) compared with the peer average 

- Further, the attrition rate of permanent employees of the company declined to 26% in fiscal 2025 (broadly in-line with the peers), from 30% in fiscal 2024. Also, it reported zero workforce fatalities in fiscal 2025 and targets to maintain zero fatality at all plants and locations 

- Its governance structure is characterised by 50% of its board comprising independent directors, 13% woman directors, dedicated investor grievance redressal system and extensive financial and ESG disclosures, demonstrating transparency and accountability. 

There is growing importance of ESG among investors and lenders. Alembic Pharma’s continued commitment to ESG principles will play a key role in enhancing stakeholder confidence and ensuring ease of raising capital from markets where ESG compliance is a key factor. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The business risk profile is likely to benefit from the diversified segmental and geographical presence of the company in the pharma space. However, the operating margin will remain average amid gradual ramp up in the new facilities, intense competition, price erosion in US, inflation driven cost and R&D spends. Healthy cash generation and low leverage in the capital structure will help sustain healthy financial risk profile over the medium term. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Double-digit revenue growth, supported by new product launches and better diversity in revenue streams with operating profitability improving above 20-22% on a sustained basis, resulting in better-than-expected cash generation Prudent capital spending and working capital management along with healthy cash generation will ensure maintenance of healthy financial risk profile and comfortable debt protection metrics 

# **Downward factors** 

- Lower-than-expected revenue growth and operating profitability remaining below 14-16% on a sustained basis, also impacting cash generation 

- Larger-than-expected working capital requirement or capex and acquisitions, leading to moderation in debt protection metrics 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

The pharma business of Alembic Ltd, comprising domestic formulations, international generics and APIs, was transferred to Alembic Pharma, following the latter’s demerger from Alembic Ltd effective April 1, 2010. Vadodarabased Alembic Pharma manufactures a range of formulations and bulk drugs for the domestic and international markets. It has nine manufacturing facilities for formulations and APIs and three R&D facilities. Alembic Pharma is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange.
