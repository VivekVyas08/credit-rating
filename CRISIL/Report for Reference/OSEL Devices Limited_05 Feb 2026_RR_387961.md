### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its rating on the long-term bank facilities of OSEL Devices Limited (OSEL) at ‘Crisil BBB/Positive’. 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facility of OSEL to ‘Positive’ from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at ‘Crisil BBB’ on November 28, 2025. 

The revision in outlook reflects the expected improvement in the business risk profile. Revenue grew by 41% on-year in fiscal 2025 to Rs 186 crore driven by volume growth on the back of strong and diversified clientele. The growth is expected to sustain in fiscal 2026; the company has achieved revenue of Rs 147 crore in the first six months of fiscal 2026 (Rs 87 crore in the corresponding period of fiscal 2025) and is expected to achieve revenue of Rs 300-310 crore for full fiscal 2026. The company has a diversified product mix, consisting of multiple SKUs (stock keeping units) of hearing aids and light-emitting diode (LED) display systems. Introduction of feature phones through partnership with Philips will strengthen the business risk profile and support revenue growth in the near to medium term. Operating margin was 17-18% over the two fiscals through 2025 (17.9% in fiscal 2025) driven by better absorption of fixed cost and healthy profitability in the hearing aid segment. The operating margin is expected to sustain at 17-18% in fiscal 2026. 

The rating also factors the increase in receivables to 128 days as on March 31, 2025, from 73 days as on March 31, 2024, as the company has extended additional credit to customers, leading to increased utilisation of the working capital limit. Improvement in revenue, stable operating margin at around 17% and better working capital cycle will remain key rating sensitivity factors. 

The financial risk profile strengthened following the initial public offering (IPO) in September 2024, when the company raised funds of Rs 63 crore leading to networth improving to Rs 108 crore as on March 31, 2025 (Rs 25 crore as on March 31, 2024). The funds have been utilised for reduction of debt, working capital requirement and general corporate purposes. In September 2025, the company raised Rs 54 crore through preferential issue of fully convertible warrants, which will be utilised for meeting the working capital requirement. Networth is expected to be Rs 190-195 crore as on March 31, 2026. 

The rating continues to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the healthcare equipment industry, the improving scale of operations of the company and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to risk of technological obsolescence and raw material price fluctuations, and large working capital requirement. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of OSEL. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoters and improving scale of operations:** The promoters have experience of around two decades in the healthcare equipment industry. The company was engaged in trading until 2019 and subsequently started manufacturing LED display systems and hearing aids. The expertise of the promoters has given them understanding of market dynamics and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. The LED display systems and hearing aids contributed ~46% and ~54% to revenue, respectively, in fiscal 

2025. Operating income has been growing at a healthy compound annual rate of 42% for the three fiscals through 2025. The operating income is expected to be Rs 300-310 crore in fiscal 2026 driven by increased capacity utilisation amid rising demand for products, given the increased market penetration. The introduction of feature phones through partnership with Philips will strengthen the business risk profile and support revenue growth in the near to medium term. The company has a strong clientele, including private and government customers, which also support revenue growth. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth is expected at Rs 190-195 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 108 crore as on March 31, 2025), driven by healthy accretion to reserve and funds infused through preferential issue of fully convertible warrants. The capital structure will be healthy, with gearing expected below 0.5 time as on March 31, 2026 (0.46 time as on March 31, 2025). Interest coverage ratio is expected at 7-8 times in fiscal 2026 (9.6 times in fiscal 2025) driven by healthy and sustained profitability and improving scale of operations. The financial risk profile will likely improve over the medium term driven by no major debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) and healthy accretion to reserve. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to technology obsolescence risk and raw material price fluctuations:** Intense competition has led to rapidly evolving products and innovations and thus high technology obsolescence risk. This affects the bargaining power of the company, and the operating margin fluctuates based on tenders. Also, the company procures some of its key raw materials from China and hence any volatility in price or supply chain issues could impact operations. 

**Large working capital requirement:** Operations were working capital intensive, as reflected in gross current assets (GCAs) of 350 days as on March 31, 2025 (219 days a year earlier), driven by inventory of 97 days (85 days a year earlier). Inventory includes raw materials imported from China, and the company tries to maintain adequate inventory to cater to unprecedented orders. Furthermore, it has multiple SKUs and does customisation for both product segments. Receivables were at 128 days as on March 31, 2025 (73 days a year earlier). The working capital cycle is partly supported by payables of 60-90 days as on that date. The GCAs are expected at 370-380 days as on March 31, 2026, driven by large cash and bank balance and receivables and inventory of 90-110 days. Improvement in receivables will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Expected net cash accrual of Rs 30-35 crore per annum will sufficiently cover working capital requirement and yearly term debt obligation of Rs 0.5-1.0 crore over the medium term. Cash and bank balance of Rs 32 crore as on September 30, 2025, supports the liquidity. Bank limit utilisation averaged 77% for the 12 months through September 2025. Current ratio is expected at 2.1 times as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

Crisil Ratings believes the business risk profile of OSEL will continue to improve driven by its diversified product portfolio, the extensive experience of the promoters and its established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Increase in revenue to around Rs 300 crore and stable operating margin of around 17% leading to higher cash accrual Improvement in the working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue and/or fall in operating margin below 13% leading to lower net cash accrual 

Further stretch in the working capital cycle or large, debt-funded capex weakening the liquidity and financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 2006, OSEL manufactures and assembles electronic equipment such as LED screens and hearing aids. Its manufacturing facility is in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, and has production capacity of 15,000 sq ft per annum of LED displays and 400,000 units per annum of hearing aids. 

The company is owned and managed by Mr Rajendra Ravi Shanker Mishra, Ms Jyotsna Jawahar and Mr Mukesh Kumar Sinha.
