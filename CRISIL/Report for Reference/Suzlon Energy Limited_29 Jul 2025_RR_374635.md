### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Suzlon Energy Ltd (SEL) to **‘Crisil A+/Stable’** from ‘Crisil A/Positive **’** and reaffirmed the short term rating at ‘Crisil A1’. 

The upgrade reflects SEL’s consistently improving scale of operations driven by execution of the increasing order book which provides revenue visibility, stable cash flow from the O&M services business and a strong established market position in the wind turbine generators industry while maintaining its healthy financial profile and superior liquidity profile. 

Revenue grew by 67% in FY 2025 driven by WTG deliveries of 1.55 GW with a strong growth momentum going ahead driven by an order book of the WTG business of 5.5 GW as in May 2025 against ~5.1 gigawatt (GW) as in October, 2024. Overall EBITDA margins improved to 17.1% in FY 25 from 15.8% in FY 24, with margins expected in similar range going ahead. WTG business has been able to achieve earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) of Rs ~965 crore in FY25 (11% EBITDA margin) with delivery volume of 1550 megawatt (MW) against 4.6% and 2.2% in EBITDA margin in fiscals 2024 and 2023, respectively, primarily driven by operating leverage. Although OMS division continued to maintain ~40% EBITDA margin in FY25 in line with expectations, the absolute EBITDA grew by 7% to Rs 771 cr due to some delays in installations. 

The financial risk profile of SEL is marked by strong networth of Rs 6,106 crore at the end of fiscal 2025, growing accruals, priority working capital and no debt at standalone levels. Although the company had Rs 283 crores in borrowings at consolidated level at the end of fiscal 2025, debt protection metrics are comfortable with interest cover of 7.3x. 

These strengths are partially offset by the relatively high operating leverage in the WTG business, large working capital requirement and financial history of the company. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of SEL and its subsidiaries, including SE Forge Ltd and Renom Energy Servicese. These entities, collectively referred to as the Suzlon group, sell WTGs and provide related services and components, with significant operational synergies and some common members eon the Board. 

SEL is expected to provide operational and financial support to its subsidiaries Renom Energy Services and SE Forge if needed.

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Leading market position in the wind turbine segment and healthy order book:** The group has a successful track record of project execution with technical expertise, evident from the healthy market share of over 30% in the WTG business in India over the past many years in cumulative installed capacity. SEL’s order book stood at ~5.5 GW (as in 

May, 2025), to be executed till fiscal 2027. The company has been able to overcome the dependence on customerbacked financing to execute orders, which had constrained the growth previously. 

**Stable cash flow from the O&M services business:** The Suzlon group had about 15.1 GW of installed fleet under the O&M business in India at the end of fiscal 2025. While the fleet under O&M reduces with decommissioning of WTGs post completion of the design life, new WTGs delivered and commissioned get added to the fleet every fiscal. Revenue from the O&M services has been steady as this is contractual activity over a fixed timeframe and at a contracted price. Also, escalation in revenue is inbuilt into the contracts, ensuring stability of operating margin over a period. The group has demonstrated stability in revenue and profitability of the O&M services business even in stressful times in the past. Stable cash flow with EBITDA above Rs 750 crore per fiscal from the O&M services business is expected going forward. 

**Healthy financial risk profile** : The company has a healthy financial risk profile with a networth of Rs 6,106 crore as on March 31, 2025.  Post restructuring,  steps were undertaken to reduce debt liability over the last few years, the company on a standalone level is debt free and at a consolidated level has a debt of Rs. 283 crore as on March 31, 2025. 

Further, SEL does not plan to have any material debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) over the near term. Crisil Ratings understands from management articulation that the debt/EBITDA threshold wouldn’t go beyond 1x. The company also had a free cash balance of Rs 1,112 cr at the end of fiscal 2025 

SEL has also raised Rs. 5,906  crores of lines from REC Limited to meet its working capital requirements (the facility is in form of Letter of Comfort, based on which LC/BG is issued by fronting banks.) 

# **Weaknesses:** 

- **Operating leverage in the WTG business:** High operating leverage exposes the WTG business to losses in the event of curtailed offtake. Sustenance of revenue in the WTG business is extremely critical on account of operating leverage arising from substantial fixed costs. In the past, on account of these fixed costs, the group has suffered substantial operating losses due to  slowdown in revenue from this business. Crisil Ratings understands the group had undertaken cost reduction activities in operations, manpower and other expenses, resulting in reduction of fixed costs. These efforts have culminated in the reduction of breakeven of WTG division to ~600 MW. However, any delay in execution of orders will result in fixed costs under recovery adversely impacting cash flow. While the measures undertaken may reduce operating leverage, it is expected to remain high and expose operating profitability to variability in revenue. 

- **Competitive intensity in wind industry:** The business environment for the wind energy sector continues to be challenging. While SEL is one of the prominent domestic players with over 30%+ cumulative market share as on March 31,2025 in terms of installed capacity, it faces intense competition from certain domestic players and foreign players. 

- **Weak financial history:** SEL defaulted in the past and underwent restructuring. The last restructuring was in June 2020, post which the company went for refinancing in May 2022. The 16-lender consortium was replaced with two lenders and unsustainable debt was extinguished through conversion to equity. The company also had a negative networth due to past losses till fiscal 2022. This has impacted the financial flexibility of the company with certain lenders. Nevertheless, with prepayment of the entire debt, other lenders have reached out to fund the working capital requirement of SEL. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Adequate</u>** 

The company’s unencumbered cash reserves stood at about Rs 1,112 crores as of March 31, 2025 up from Rs 426 crores as of March 31, 2024. The company does not have any debt service obligation, given that the term debt has been fully repaid. Furthermore, its O&M division is expected to generate an EBITDA of over Rs 750 crore every year, thereby supporting the overall cash flow. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil ratings maintains stable outlook on the ratings of SEL on the expectation of sustenance of business and financial risk profile of the company driven by healthy order of 5.5 GW and low debt. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained and better than expected EBITDA in the WTG division along with management of working capital cycle Increase in and sustenance of delivery volumes of WTG to about 1.5-2 GW annually 

- Track record of improving financial flexibility at group level through increased access to banker fund and non-fund based lines. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Impact on order book or execution volumes, resulting in operating margin being lower than 10-12% on a sustained basis Higher-than-expected debt-funded acquisitions/capex leading to significant deterioration in key credit metrics Sharp deterioration in liquidity 

**<u>About the Company</u>** 

Founded in 1995, SEL is one of the leading global renewable energy solutions providers. Over the past 29 years, the group has installed over 20 GW of wind energy in 17 countries across six continents. The group comprises SEL and its various subsidiaries. Its manufacturing footprint is spread across India. It is a vertically integrated WTG manufacturer. It also undertakes installation and O&M of all WTG sales. Operations include design development and manufacturing of all major components, including rotor blades, tubular towers, generators, control equipment, gears and nacelles. Apart from manufacturing, it offers a full gamut of wind project planning and execution services, including wind resource assessment, infrastructure and power evacuation, technical planning and execution of wind power projects. It also offers O&M services in India and overseas.
