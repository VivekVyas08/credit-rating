CRISIL has suspended its ratings on the bank facilities of Asian Granito India Ltd (AGIL). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by AGIL with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, AGIL is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess AGIL's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings'. 



### **_About the Company_** 

AGIL, incorporated in 1995, is a leading ceramic player; it manufactures various kinds of ceramics tiles such as vitrified tiles, wall tiles and floor tiles, and agglomerated marbles. The company is promoted by Mr. Kamleshhai Patel and Mr. Mukeshbhai Patel, and their family members. The company is listed on Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange.
