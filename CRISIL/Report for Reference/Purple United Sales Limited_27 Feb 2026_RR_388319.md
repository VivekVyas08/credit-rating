### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil BBB-/Stable’** rating to the long term bank facilities of Purple United Sales Limited (PUSL). 

The ratings reflect extensive experience of PUSL’s promoters leading to strong brands and extensive distribution network and moderate financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by moderate scale and working capital intensive nature of operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of PUSL 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Extensive experience of the promoters:** 

Promoter’s of the company have extensive experience spanning over 3 decades in the apparel and footwear industry. This has enabled the company to establish five brands named Toothless, Boltzy, That’s His Style, That’s Her Style and Striders under the main brand ‘Purple United Kids’ within one decade of incorporation. 

Promoter experience has also led to long standing relationships with suppliers and deep understanding of market dynamics which resulted in 105 exclusive brand outlets as on February 2026. 

### **Extensive distribution network:** 

PUSL’s distribution network comprises exclusive brand outlets (EBOs), multiple shop-in-shops, and a robust online presence through its website and major e-commerce platforms such as Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, FirstCry, AJIO. This dynamic approach allows the company to maintain high purchase frequency and cater to repeat customers while continually expanding its reach. 

### **Moderate financial risk profile:** 

PUSL has a moderate financial risk profile marked by comfortable networth of Rs 66 Cr as on September 30,2025. Moderate reliance on debt has led to a moderate capital structure marked by gearing of 0.68 times. Debt protection metrics of the company are comfortable with interest coverage and net cash accruals and adjusted debt at 4.27 times and 0.30 time for fiscal 2025. Interest coverage moderated to 3.4 times   in H1 of fiscal 2026. 

Financial risk profile of the company is estimated to be supported by planned infusion of funds through preferential issue of equity shares of Rs 70 Cr which will be utilised for capex and working capital requirements 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

### **Moderate scale of operations:** 

Presence of few organized and many unorganized players in the kid’s apparel industry has led to a fragmentation in the industry resulting into intense competition amongst players. This has restricted the scale of operations of PUSL. Company clocked in a revenue of Rs 103 Cr in fiscal 2025 and has already achieved revenue of Rs 61 Cr in the first half of fiscal 2026. Sustained increase in the scale of operations on the back of expansion of new stores will remain monitorable. 

### **Working capital intensive nature of operations:** 

Operations of the company are working capital intensive as reflected in 387 Gross Current Asset (GCA) Days as on March 31, 2025. 

High GCA of the company is emanating from high debtor and inventory days, which stood at 214 days and 77 days respectively as on March 31, 2025. While the debtor days of the company have reduced to 158 days as on September 30, 2025, inventory days of the company have increased as on September 30, 2025, and are estimated to increase further with the increase in number of stores. The company is in process of raising funds through preferential issue of equity shares which will be used to meet incremental working capital requirements arising out of new stores. 

Their working capital requirements are met by bank lines and creditors. Working capital management, particularly inventory levels, will remain a key monitorable in light of opening of new stores. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Liquidity position of the company remains adequate marked by estimated net cash accruals over Rs 23 Cr as against repayment obligations of Rs 2.75-3 Cr. Bank limit utilization of the company averaged at 93% for the last twelve months ended December 2025. Company has got enhancement of Rs 21 crore in month of December 2025. Company had free cash and cash balance over Rs 6.5 Cr as on December 2025. Company is also in the process to raising capital through preferential issue of equity shares which will be critical for funding of panned capex and increasing working capital requirements. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes PUSL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained increase in scale of operations along with sustenance of operating margins leading to higher net cash accruals. 

Improvement in working capital cycle leading to lower GCA days leading to an improvement in financial risk profile and improvement in liquidity position with BLU averaging below 85%. 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margins leading to lower than expected net cash accruals 

- Stretch in working capital cycle with GCA days remaining over 350 days leading to a moderation in financial risk profile and liquidity position 

Higher than expected debt funded capex leading to a leveraged capital structure and stretch in liquidity. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

PUSL, formerly known as Purple United Sales Private Limited, was incorporated in 2014. PUSL is engaged in retailing of kids apparels, footwear and fashion accessories. PUSL has 105 showrooms (exclusive brand outlets - EBO) under the brand name “Purple United Kids” across various cities in India, built over around 1,50,000 sq. feet approximately. The company’s corporate office is located in Noida, Uttar Pradesh.
