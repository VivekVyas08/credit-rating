### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A+/Stable/Crisil A1’ ratings on the bank facilities of Plastiblends India Limited (PBIL). 

The ratings continue to derive strength from PBIL's established market position in the masterbatch industry, diverse product portfolio and strong financial risk profile, backed by negligible debt and healthy liquidity. These strengths are partially offset by the exposure to volatility in raw material prices and intense competition. However, for the major raw materials, the company is able to pass on the cost to its customers. 

Revenue is expected to grow by around 9% in fiscal 2027 on account of steady demand growth for black masterbatches and gradual volume offtake in engineering masterbatches. This will be followed by 1.4% revenue growth to Rs 789 crore in fiscal 2026, driven by realisation growth of ~2% despite moderation in the volume growth due to weak demand in the irrigation segment and gradual shift from Polywhite segment. 

In the medium term, high realisation will support the revenue growth, driven by increasing contribution from high margin products such as black and engineering masterbatches. 

The operating margin is expected to remain at 7-7.5% over the medium term owing to favourable product mix and cost optimisation measures initiated by the company. The operating margin improved to 7.8% in fiscal 2026 from 7.4% in fiscal 2025 on the back of marginal one-time inventory gain in the fourth quarter of fiscal 26 due to steep increase in raw material prices of polymer, which is directly linked to crude. 

For FY27, the company shall incur capital expenditure (capex) spends of around Rs. 15-20 crore towards installing solar power panels to increase renewable energy consumption from the existing 1.4MW to 6MW which will save the power cost and support margins in the medium term. 

The financial risk profile continues to remain strong on the back of robust capital structure reflected by the absence of longterm debt obligations coupled with the minimal reliance on working capital debt and strong debt protection metrics. Capital structure marked by total outside labilities to adjusted networth (TOL / ANW) continued to remain strong at 0.24 time as on March 31, 2026, and is expected to remain below 0.24 times in the medium term. The interest coverage in FY26 was 30.8 and will be above 32 times in the medium term. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the business and financial risk profiles of PBIL on a standalone basis. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Established market position in the masterbatch segment** 

PBIL is among the largest manufacturers in India for the organised domestic masterbatch industry. The company’s units manufacture a diverse product portfolio catering to varied customer bases across domestic and overseas markets. The company manufactures white, black, and coloured masterbatches and compounds used primarily as colouring agents in plastics. The company has also forayed into engineering masterbatches, which have high application in the automotive and white goods product categories, and the management expect healthy volume offtake during the current fiscal. 

Apart from the domestic presence, the company also benefits from moderate export presence in the Asian, African, and LATAM markets. Exports contribution in overall revenue mix stood at 19% during fiscal 2026 though reduced from ~22% in the past, the management is focusing on improving its export share in the overall revenue mix by penetrating the Southeast Asian markets. 

# **Strong financial risk profile** 

The strong financial risk profile of PBIL is driven by a robust capital structure owing to the minimal dependence on external sources of funds. While the company has maintained an unlevered capital structure since fiscal 2020 owing to more than sufficient coverage of capex spends through internal accruals, the reliance on working capital debt has also been negligible owing to the efficient working capital management. Gross current assets (GCA) days (net of cash) has increased to 145 days in FY26 from 125 days in FY25 due to higher inventory levels. This was driven by a rise in crude-linked raw materials amid the west Asia crisis, which increased the value of the inventory held. Additionally the company strategically built up inventory to ensure supply continuity, which further increased the GCA days. As a result of a robust capital structure followed by efficient working capital management, TOL / ANW stood at 0.24 time as on March 31, 2026, and over the medium term is expected to be at similar levels. The financial risk profile is further strengthened by healthy unencumbered cash surplus of Rs. 47 crore as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Profitability susceptible to fluctuation in raw material prices** 

Profitability is susceptible to the prices of polymer having direct linkages with crude, and accounts for 45-50% of the total raw material cost. In addition, the company is not able to fully pass on the cost escalations due to intense competition. Operating margin have improved to 7.8% in fiscal 2026 from 7.4% in fiscal 2025 due to one-time inventory gain in quarter four of fiscal 26 due to steep increase in raw material prices – of polymer. That said, the management is taking various initiatives to improve profitability margins such as improving the product mix towards higher margin products and also optimising the cost structure by increasing the adoption of renewable energy. 

# **Exposure to intense competition** 

The masterbatch industry in India is characterised by the presence of a large, unorganised sector, accounting for almost 35% of the market share. The consequent intense competition along with the commoditised nature of products and limited product differentiation may continue to constrain scalability, pricing power and scalability. However, company is moving away from Polywhite segment to engineering masterbatches where competition is less intensive. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Expected annual net cash accrual of Rs 45-50 crore per annum over the medium term and unencumbered cash surplus of around Rs 47 crore as on March 31, 2026, will sufficiently cover working capital requirement and yearly capex spends of Rs 15-20 crore with limited dependence on external borrowing requirement. Besides, the company has access to fund-based limit of Rs 94 crore out of which approximately 21% was utilised for the six months ended March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The business risk profile of PBIL shall remain stable over the medium term, backed by its established market position in the masterbatch industry and improving product mix towards higher margin products. The financial risk profile shall also continue to remain strong owing to the sustenance of robust capital structure and strong debt protection metrics on the back of healthy cash accrual & reserves as against yearly capex and working capital requirement. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Substantial increase in scale of operations with operating margins improving to 11-12% on a sustained basis Sustenance of maintenance of strong financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors** 

Weakening of business performance or the company continuing to operate at below 7% operating margin on a sustained basis 

- Any large, debt-funded capex or sizeable stretch in the working capital cycle, leading to material impact on the debt protection metrics or capital structure 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1991, PBIL is a part of the Kolsite group of companies. The company manufactures white, black and coloured masterbatches and compounds used primarily as colouring agents in plastics. The masterbatches are marketed under the trade names—Polyclear, Polynuc, Polyultra, Antimicrobial, Polyrodent, PolyFR and Anti-fibrillation. PBIL’s plants in Daman; Roorkee, Uttarakhand; and Surat, Gujarat; have combined manufacturing capacity of around 125,000 tonne per annum.
