### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable’ rating on the long-term bank facilities of S H Kelkar and Company Limited (S H Kelkar; part of the Keva group, formerly known as the Kelkar group). 

The rating continues to reflect the established position of the Keva group in the flavours and fragrances industry, the extensive experience of the promoters, strong research and development (R&D) capabilities and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition and moderately high working capital requirement. 

The operating performance of the Keva group will remain healthy, supported by growth in the flavours segment in international markets, continued client addition and contribution from Creative Flavours and Fragrances SpA (CFF) and Holland Aromatics BV. Operating income grew 15% y-o-y to Rs 2125 crore in fiscal 2025 driven by increased contributions from new accounts, higher traction from mid-sized SME’s (including e-commerce & start-ups), and stable growth in exports. Revenue from the Indian region grew by 22%, Europe by 7% and ROW by 8%. Further, Company reported Rs. 579 crores in Q1 FY26, achieving growth of ~ 14.8% over the H1 FY2025 average, given that Q1 FY2025 was impacted by the firerelated disruption. Revenue is expected to continue grow in double digits in medium term, with stable demand, continued addition of new accounts and increase in capacities as company is in the midst of brownfield expansion in Holland and plants in India (Vanavati and Vaishvali). Operating margin stood at 14% in FY25 vs 16% in FY24, due to the fire incident, increase in employee cost on account of new Creative Development Centers as well as higher raw material cost in H1FY25 owing to supply constraints. With input costs stabilizing, price hikes taken towards end of Q4FY25, and operating leverage expected to improve, margins are anticipated to improve from the H2FY2026 and more meaningfully from FY2027 onwards post capex to 15-16%. 

The financial risk profile of the company is marked by strong networth of over Rs 1000 crore and healthy debt protection metrics with gearing less than 1x and interest coverage of 6 times. In the interim, debt (excluding lease liabilities) increased to Rs 745 crore (from Rs 617 crore)owing to rise in working capital borrowings and term loans due to fire incident happened in April 2024 at Vaishavali plant, primarily to refurbish inventories at different locations and restoration of plant & machinery. The Company has planned capex of Rs 200 crores, primarily towards reinstatement of Vashivali facility, and capacity expansion in Holland and Vanavate facility. The borrowings are expected to moderate once the pending insurance claims are realized and capex at Vashivali plant is completed by first quarter of fiscal 2027. Healthy internal accruals estimated at over Rs 200 crore each fiscal, liquid surplus and unutilised bank lines are more than sufficient to support repayment obligation, incremental working capital requirements and maintenance capex over the medium term. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of S H Kelkar, its subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries. This is because all the entities, collectively referred to as the Keva group, have common management and significant business and financial linkages. 

Additionally, Crisil Ratings has amortised goodwill on acquisitions for five years. With adoption of Ind AS 116 with effect from April 1, 2019, lease liabilities are being treated as debt while related adjustments are made in depreciation and amortisation and interest cost components.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

- **Established player in the flavours and fragrances industry and extensive experience of the promoters:** The Keva group commenced operations as a partnership firm and has longstanding presence of ten decades in the flavours and fragrances industry, spanning over three generations. It has an established clientele across industries such as fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), dairy, beverages, pharmaceuticals and tobacco with over 4,100 customers. Mr Kedar Vaze, the promoter and CEO, has over 20 years of experience. 

- **Strong R&D capabilities:** Robust R&D capabilities enable the group to continuously innovate and develop new products as per the changing requirement of the end-user FMCG industry. The group has 7 creation and development centres in Netherlands, Germany, Mumbai and Singapore with 20+ perfumers, 5+ flavourists, evaluators and application executives. It also has a dedicated research team of 29+ scientists. 

- **Healthy financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile of the company is marked by strong networth of over Rs 1000 crore and healthy debt protection metrics with gearing less than 1x and interest coverage of 6 times. In the interim, debt (excluding lease liabilities) increased to Rs 745 crore (from Rs 617 crore), primarily the company witnessed a rise in working capital borrowings and term loans due to fire incident happened in April 2024 at Vaishavali plant, primarily to refurbish inventories at different locations and restoration of plant & machinery. The Company has planned capex of Rs 200 crores, primarily towards reinstatement of Vashivali facility, and capacity expansion in Holland and Vanavate facility. These borrowings are expected to moderate once the pending insurance claims are realized and capex at Vashivali plant is completed by fiscal 2027. Healthy internal accruals estimated at over Rs 200 crore each fiscal, liquid surplus and unutilised bank lines are more than sufficient to support repayment obligation, incremental working capital requirements and maintenance capex over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

- **Exposure to any changes in off-take by users, especially in the wake of increasing competition:** Demand for flavours and fragrances moves in line with tastes and preferences of consumers. With changing tastes and preferences of consumers and increasing competition, FMCG companies are under pressure to constantly innovate and develop affordable products. Accordingly, the Keva group has to market its products in cost-effective ways. Furthermore, it faces competition from international players, such as Givaudan SA and International Flavors and Fragrances unorganised local players and other specialty chemicals makers. Operating performance is susceptible to changes in offtake by enduser industries and intensifying competition. 

- **Moderately high working capital operations:** Operations are working capital intensive, as reflected in gross current assets of 261 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by inventory of 144 days. Though the company strategically maintains higher inventory to secure supply of key raw materials and offset price escalations, it remains vulnerable to volatility in raw material prices. Besides, as a portion of the raw material requirement is met through imports, sharp foreign exchange (forex) movements can result in inventory loss, despite a natural hedge by way of export of finished products. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Healthy internal accruals estimated at over Rs 200 crore each fiscal, liquid surplus and unutilised bank lines are more than sufficient to support repayment obligation, incremental working capital requirements and maintenance capex over the medium term. SHK (India) access to fund-based limits of Rs 397 crore, which were utilised at 54% on average in the 12 months through June 2025. 

# **<u>ESG profile</u>** 

The environment, social and governance (ESG) profile of S H Kelkar supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

# **<u>Key ESG highlights:</u>** 

In light of the company’s commitment to reduce its carbon footprint, SHKCL reported a declining trend in scope 1 and 2 emissions intensity, which fell by ~25% CAGR[1], and remained below than the peer average at ~2 tCO2e per Rs crore of revenue 

- On the social front, diversity in its workforce increased to ~31% in fiscal 2024 from ~19% in fiscal 2022. Further, the gross wages paid to females as % of total wage cost was also higher (~24%) as compared to peers With respect to workplace safety, the company reported nil fatalities and lost time injury frequency rate (LTIFR) in this fiscal year. Additionally, entire workforce had been trained on skills upgradation and health and safety measures The company’s governance structure is characterized by 57% of its Board comprising of independent directors and 29% women directors, a ~100% investor complaint redressal rate, and extensive financial disclosures

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

Keva Group was set up in 1922 as a partnership firm by Mr Sadashiv Haribhau Kelkar (renamed Vinayak Ganesh Vaze once adopted by the Vaze family) and his brother, Mr Damodar Vaze. The firm was reconstituted as a private limited company in 1955. S H Kelkar is the flagship company of the Keva group. The group manufactures flavours and fragrances for application in varied sectors such as FMCG, dairy, beverages, pharmaceuticals and tobacco. It has eight manufacturing units (five are in India while the remaining three are overseas) and seven creation and development centres.
