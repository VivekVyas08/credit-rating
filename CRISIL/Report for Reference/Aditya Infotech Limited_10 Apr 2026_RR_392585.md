### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revoked the suspension of its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Aditya Infotech Limited (AIL) and assigned its ‘ **Crisil A/Stable** ’ rating to the facilities. Crisil Ratings had suspended the rating on June 18, 2015, as AIL had not provided necessary information required to maintain a valid rating. AIL has now shared the requisite information, enabling Crisil Ratings to assign a rating to the bank facilities. 

The rating reflects the group's strong brand supported by established presence domestically, the strong geographical diversification and distribution network in the domestic market, and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to competition and volatility in raw material prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates, and large working capital requirement. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of AIL and its subsidiaries, AIL Dixon Technologies Pvt Ltd, Shenzhen CP Plus International Ltd and Aditya Infotech Taiwan Co Ltd, collectively referred to as the Aditya Infotech group. 

Unsecured loan of Rs 1.35 crore as on March 31, 2025, from related parties has been treated as debt as the same has been repaid during the fiscal 2026.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established brand and presence domestically:** AIL has strong market position in the electronics security and surveillance industry (closed-circuit television [CCTV] cameras, recorders and other surveillance equipment’s) under its own brand, “CP Plus” and also earns revenue from service business. The company is a distributor of electronic security products of the Dahua Technologies group (under the brand Dahua). The three-decade-long experience of the promoters, their strong understanding of market dynamics and healthy relationships with suppliers and customers will continue to support the business. This has allowed the company to command premium in the branded communication equipment industry. Furthermore, to target the mass market and unorganized segments, the company is launching two new brands: EYRA and NEXIVUE. In the first nine months of fiscal 2026, the company has already achieved revenues of more than Rs 2798 crore as compared to Rs. 2134 crores a year ago and is expected to post healthy revenue growth for fiscal 2026. 

**Strong geographical diversification and distribution network in the domestic market:** The group has a strong marketing network across India. It has over 800 distributors in more than 500 cities, including tier I, II and III cities, and 10 warehouses across Delhi, Gurugram and Noida in north India; Bhiwandi, Ahmedabad, and Indore in West India; Kolkata, and Guwahati in east India; as well as Chennai, and Bengaluru in south India. 

Though majority of revenue comes from cameras and recorders, the company is expanding its product portfolio by adding other products such as video door phone, time and attendance recorders and digital door locks. The group will continue to innovate and manufacture products to suit customer requirements over the medium term. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** With steady accretion to reserves and fund raising through initial public offering (IPO), the financial risk profile is strong, supported by adjusted networth of more than Rs 1,150 crore as on March 31, 2026 (compared 

with Rs 458 crore as on March 31, 2025). With moderate reliance on external debt, the capital structure is comfortable, as reflected in estimated gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio of 0.2–0.3 time and 1.4–1.5 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (1.33 times and 4.66 times, respectively, a year earlier). Despite capital expenditure (capex) plans of Rs 100–150 crore currently, the capital structure is expected at similar levels over the medium term, backed by healthy accretion to reserve. Debt protection metrics are healthy, as indicated by estimated interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of over 10 times and 1.0–1.1 times, respectively, in fiscal 2026 (6.32 times and 0.19 time, respectively, in fiscal 2025) and are expected to remain comfortable over the medium term. The financial risk profile will likely remain strong with healthy accretion to reserve and moderate reliance on external debt. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to competition and risks relating to raw material price fluctuations:** The electronics security products industry is competitive with fragmented market. The competition and technology changes has led to rapidly evolving products and innovations and thus high technology obsolescence risk. Also, the company sources key materials such as chip sets, printed circuit boards, copper and aluminum from domestic and foreign suppliers, and their prices are volatile, thus affecting the operating margins. 

**Vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuations in forex rates:** As majority of the raw material is procured from the international market, mainly China and Taiwan, any sharp fluctuation in forex rates affects procurement cost and accrual. To minimize this risk, the company enters hedging contracts and increases product costs. 

**Working capital-intensive operations** : Gross current assets (GCAs) were at 190–246 days over the three fiscals through 2025. The GCAs (excluding cash) was high at 246 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by large receivables and inventory. The receivable and inventory days as on September 30, 2025 are at 115 and 116 days respectively with higher quarter end sales. AIL provides a credit period of around 90-100 days to its customers and is expected to remain in a similar range going forward. Furthermore, due to its business need, it holds large work in process & inventory. The working capital cycle will remain stretched over the medium term, considering moderate demand growth. Improvement in the working capital cycle will remain a key rating sensitivity factor over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Net cash accrual, expected at Rs 275–400 crore per annum, will sufficiently cover yearly debt obligation of Rs 21– 35 crore over the medium term. With prepayment of loans through IPO proceeds, the current ratio is estimated at a comfortable over 1.5 times as on March 31, 2026. The company had cash and bank balance of around Rs 25 crore as on September 30, 2025. Internal cash accrual, cash and equivalent and bank lines will be adequate to meet the debt obligation and working capital requirement over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the group will continue to benefit from the established brand presence and distribution network. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Improvement in scale of operations and sustenance of operating margins leading to higher cash accruals Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and improvement in the working capital cycle, with GCAs at less than 180 days and reduction in the higher than six months receivables. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in net cash accruals on account of fall in revenues or operating margins 

Further stretch in the working capital cycle or large, debt-funded capex weakening the liquidity and financial risk profile, with the TOLANW ratio above 2 times 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

AIL was incorporated in 1995, the company having registered office  located at New Delhi. AIL is engaged in manufacturing and assembly of security and surveillance equipment’s & components such as closed-circuit television (CCTV) camera , surveillance software,  recorders , video door phone, time & attendance recorders, digital door lock, etc. under its own brand name “CP Plus” 

It is also engaged in trading of security equipment’s & components of Dahua Technologies Group. 

AIL is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange Ltd and the National Stock Exchange of India. The company is promoted and managed by Aditya Khemka (Managing Director), Hari Shankar Khemka. 

AIL Dixon Technologies Pvt Ltd was set up as a joint venture between AIL and Dixon Technologies (India) Ltd in May 2017. In September 2024, it was acquired by AIL and became a wholly owned subsidiary. It manufactures CCTVs cameras and recorders under brand “CP Plus”. 

Shenzhen CP Plus International Ltd was incorporated in 2016. The company is a wholly owned subsidiary of AIL and based in China. It provides rendering assistance/support for procuring raw materials/spare parts/finished goods for surveillance and solutions, which includes quality control services, testing and analysis. 

Aditya lnfotech Taiwan Co Ltd was incorporated as a wholly owned subsidiary in Taiwan on February 2, 2026. The newly incorporated subsidiary will be engaged in the research and development of security and surveillance equipment.
