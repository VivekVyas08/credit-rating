### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed the rating on the bank facilities of Insecticides (India) Limited (IIL) at ‘Crisil A+/Stable/Crisil A1’. 

The ratings reflect the established market position of IIL through diversified product portfolio and wide distribution network, and robust financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by working capital-intensive operations, and exposure to fluctuations in raw material prices, acceptance of new products, and to risks inherent in the agrochemicals market 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of IIL. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u> Strengths:** 

- **Established market position through diversified product portfolio and wide distribution network** : The business risk profile of IIL continues to be supported by its established market position, long-term relationships with customers and suppliers, and its focus on consistently launching new products to meet the continuously changing needs of the Indian agricultural market. The company’s operating income registered a CAGR of 10% during the three fiscals through 2025 on account of growth witnessed in brand formulations and institutional sales. The company’s focus on sales of key products under the business to consumer (B2C) segment shall continue to support the business risk profile as revenue growth of 8-10% is expected in fiscal 2026. The company has developed a large portfolio of agrochemical products with over 140 formulations of insecticides, herbicides and fungicides. Furthermore, the distributor network is fairly diversified and comprises over 7,500 dealers spread across India. Moreover, the brand has strong recall in the domestic agrochemicals industry with a three-pronged marketing strategy. Recently acquired brands are expected to aid the business risk profile and their contribution in the overall revenue mix will remain monitorable. 

**Robust financial risk profile:** Gearing is estimated to be strong at below 0.1 time as on March 31, 2025, supported by sizeable networth of Rs 1,120-1,140 crore and limited dependence on debt. Furthermore, in the absence of any further debt-funded capex, gearing is likely to remain stable over the medium term. Debt protection metrics are estimated to remain strong, with interest coverage ratio of 13-14 times in fiscal 2025, backed by steady profitability and improvement in overall business risk profile. With no further debt-funded capex planned, the financial risk profile is expected to remain strong over the medium term. 

### **Weaknesses:** 

**Working capital-intensive operations:** Gross current assets are estimated at 240-250 days as on March 31, 2025 (230 days previous fiscal), because of inventory (both raw material and finished goods) and receivables of 180185 days and 65-70 days, respectively (164 days and 55 days, respectively, as on March 31, 2024). Furthermore, seasonality and irregular demand because of uneven monsoon, along with dependence on imported raw materials, leads to large inventory. The working capital cycle was aided by credit of 90-120 days from suppliers. The operations are likely to remain working capital-intensive owing to the commoditised nature of business and healthy growth prospects and shall be closely monitored. Efficient management of inventory, helping sustain operating profitability amid business growth, will also remain monitorable. 

**Exposure to fluctuations in raw material prices, acceptance of new products, and to risks inherent in the agrochemicals market:** The agrochemicals industry is intensely competitive and fragmented in nature and is susceptible to climatic conditions. Operations are also exposed to government regulations as it may restrict or ban the use of certain agrochemicals due to environmental concerns. Hence, the need to continuously develop and launch new products remains critical. Furthermore, the company, being a formulator in the agrochemicals value chain, raw material price movement _(_ active ingredients/technicals _)_ is directly linked to realisations, resulting in volatile operating profitability. Consequently, operating margin reduced to 6.8% and 8.5% in fiscals 2023 and 2024, respectively, from 11% in fiscal 2022 due to decline in realisations. However, this risk is partially aided by diverse product profile and prudent procurement measures. Going forward, sustenance of the operating margin, amidst business growth, will remain a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

IIL is expected to generate net cash accrual of Rs 160-180 crore per annum, which will be sufficient to meet annual debt obligation of Rs 6-7 crore over the medium term. Unencumbered cash and equivalent stood at ~Rs 50 crore as of March 2025, which is expected to improve crore over the medium term amidst absence of sizeable debt funded capex and expected accretion to reserves. IIL also has access to fund-based working capital limit, which was utilised at 40% on average during the 12 months through March 2025. Crisil Ratings expects internal accrual, cash and equivalent, and unutilized bank limit to be sufficient to meet the debt obligation as well as incremental working capital requirement. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

IIL will continue to benefit from its established market position in the domestic agrochemicals industry. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

- Sustained and significant increase in operating income and improvement in operating margin to over 13-14% leading to higher-than-expected net cash accrual 

- Efficient working capital management with GCAs below 220 days, leading to lower reliance on bank debt; thereby further strengthening the financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors:** 

- Decline in operating income or fall in operating margin below 7-8% on a consistent basis, leading to lower-thanexpected net cash accrual 

Any further stretch in the working capital cycle or large, debt-funded capex weakening the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1996 and promoted by Mr Hari Chand Aggarwal and Mr Rajesh Kumar Aggarwal, IIL commenced operations in 2002. The company manufactures formulations and technicals for plant protection chemicals and household pesticides. Its facilities are in Chopanki, Rajasthan; Samba and Udhampur, Jammu and Kashmir; and Dahej, Gujarat. It had an initial public offering in 2007 and its shares are listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange.
