### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its 'Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+’ ratings on the bank facilities of PPAP Automotive Limited (PPAP; part of the PPAP group) **.** 

Crisil Ratings has taken note of the announcement made by PPAP group dated 07<sup>th</sup> January 2025 for the for the sale of its entire 50% stake in PPAP Tokai India Rubber Private Limited, a joint venture between PPAP and Tokai Kogyo Co. Ltd. The sale was agreed upon on 1<sup>st</sup> January 2026 and is aligned with the long-term vision of the two involved entities. Furthermore, the sale is expected to bring a consideration of Rs 100 crore by the end of February 2026. Furthermore, final clarity on allocation of funds is awaited. Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor developments regarding the aspects. 

The ratings continue to reflect PPAP's leadership position in the polyvinyl chloride (PVC)/thermos plastic olefins (TPO) auto component industry along with strong relationships with large original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by customer concentration in revenue and exposure to volatility in raw material prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of PPAP and its wholly owned subsidiaries: Avinya Batteries Ltd (formerly known as PPAP Technologies Ltd) and ELPIS Automotives Pvt Ltd. This is because all these entities, collectively referred to as the PPAP group, are under a common management and have financial linkages. 

As PPAP is selling their entire 50% stake in the PPAP Tokai India Rubber Private Limited, Crisil Ratings will not be consolidating the financial performance of PPAP Tokai India Rubber Private Limited with the PPAP Group.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Leadership in the PVC/TPO profiles segment and strong relationships with large OEMs:** PPAP is one of the largest players in the PVC/TPO-based profiles segment for the auto industry and is associated with marquee OEMs as its clientele, strengthening the market position of the company. The group has been gradually diversifying its customer base and now caters to a diverse set of OEMs. Revenue marginally improved from Rs 520.94 crore in fiscal 2024 to Rs 552.06 crore in fiscal 2025, supported by increase in price realisation. With the orders for new model from the OEMs and expected ramp-up of the business in the subsidiary companies, the group’s revenue is likely to grow by 5-7%. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth was at Rs 275.83 crores as on March 31, 2025 (Rs 271.47 crore a year earlier; dip in networth due to net losses). Total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio was at 1.01 times as on March 31, 2025. Improvement in profitability resulted in interest coverage ratio reviving from 2.71 times in fiscal 2024 to 3.41 times in fiscal 2025. The group has also controlled reliance on external debt as reflected in average bank limit utilisation of 64% for the 12 months ended June 30, 2025. With no major, debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) planned for the near-to-medium term, the financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Customer concentration in revenue:** The top two customers accounted for ~68% of revenue in fiscal 2025. Moreover, strong and preferred relationships with customers have supported and protected the group from any customerrelated challenges. The group’s strategy of entering into new areas of growth such as expansion in battery segment, aftermarket segment and industrial products is expected to bring diversification and reduce customer concentration in the revenue profile. Adequate customer diversification, leading to reduction in client concentration risk, will be monitorable. 

**Exposure to volatility in raw material prices and forex rates:** The key raw materials, PVC and other petrochemical derivatives, are crude derivatives and hence the prices are volatile. Besides, as some raw materials are imported, the company is susceptible to fluctuation in forex rates. Though imports have reduced gradually over the past few years, 2025% of raw material is still imported and hence the group remains vulnerable to volatility in raw material prices and forex rates. The group is partially able to pass on the price hike to customers due to which the operating margin was at 10.4% in fiscal 2025. Improvement in operating income and revival of the operating margin will remain monitorable over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation was moderate at 62.4% on average for the 12 months ended June 30, 25. Annual cash accrual is expected at Rs 41-50 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 22-33 crore over the medium term and will cushion liquidity. The current ratio was low at 0.89 time as on March 31, 2025. 

The liquidity is expected to get additional support from the sale of PPAP’s stake in the PPAP Tokai India Rubber Private Limited JV going forward. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

PPAP will continue to benefit from its established position in the market for PVC/TPO profiles for the auto industry. 

# **<u>Rating Sensitivity Factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Steady revenue growth, driven by improved volume and operating margin sustaining at 10-11% over the medium term, leading to increase in cash accrual 

Stable financial risk profile of the company with gearing levels remaining below 0.6 time 

# **Downward factors:** 

- Operating margin declining to below 7% at the group level, further weakening of net cash accrual thus impacting the liquidity profile 

Large, debt-funded capex, weakening the capital structure 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

Set up in 1978 as a partnership firm by D C Jain, Asha Jain and Vinay Kumari Jain, PPAP was reconstituted as a public limited company, Precision Pipes and Profiles Company Ltd, in 1995. The company got its present name in May 2014. PPAP manufactures auto sealing systems and interior and exterior injection moulded products. The company was listed on the National Stock Exchange and Bombay Stock Exchange in January 2008. PPAP acquired Avinya Batteries Pvt Ltd (formerly known as PPAP Technologies Ltd)  in 2019. The said company was acquired to start a new business of manufacturing of lithium battery for electric cars. Elpis Automotives Pvt Ltd commenced its operations from November 2019. The group is expanding its business opportunities into new market in the field of automotive component and accessories. It is mainly involved in the business of trading of auto components. It has created a new network/dealer channel under this company and would purchase components from PPAP.
