### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Vascon Engineers Ltd (VEL). 

The engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) and real estate segments reported 44% growth in the operating income to Rs 1,079 crore in fiscal 2025 (from Rs 748 crore in fiscal 2024), driven by healthy execution of EPC projects. Total Orders worth Rs 2,838 crore as on September 30, 2025, should provide adequate revenue visibility to the EPC segment over the medium term. 

Total collections in the real estate segment declined to Rs 58 crore in fiscal 2025, from Rs 127 crore in fiscal 2024, due to lower-than-expected sales velocity and collections from the Orchids project in Santa Cruz (Mumbai) and delay in commencement of the project in Powai (Mumbai). Cash flow expected to improve with collections from ongoing/completed projects and the launch of saleable area of 0.82 million sq ft, with sales value of Rs 1,100 crore. 

Consolidated margin (including EPC and real estate businesses) is expected to remain healthy in the medium term. Profitability of the real estate segment will be aided by revenue booked for completed projects. On the other hand, operating margin for the EPC segment may remain rangebound at 9-10%, supported by existing orders, though increased competition may exert pressure on the margin for new orders. 

Consolidated capital structure remains healthy, with total networth of Rs 1,093 crore as against debt of Rs 205 crore. Gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth (TOL/TNW) ratios stood at 0.19 time and 0.95 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025 (0.16 time and 0.90 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2024). Adjusted interest coverage ratio stood at 3 times for fiscal 2025 (3.5 times in fiscal 2024). Absence of any major capital expenditure (capex) plans and repayment of long-term debt should help the financial risk profile improve. Debt under the real estate segment rose to Rs 147 crore in fiscal 2025, from Rs 88 crore in fiscal 2024, mainly due to lower collections. Liquidity is further aided by the adequate buffer available in the fund-based and non-fund-based facilities. Unencumbered cash and equivalent stood at Rs 118 crore as on March 31, 2025. 

The ratings also factor in the extensive experience of the promoters in the EPC and real estate domains, the company’s healthy execution track record in the civil construction business, and its reputed clientele, comprising public sector entities ensuring moderate counterparty risk. 

These strengths are partially offset by the large working capital requirement, which is deployed in the real estate segment for joint development agreement (JDA) commitments, slow-moving inventory in some projects, certain units kept as collateral, and significant unbilled revenue and retention money in the EPC segment. The company is also exposed to intense competition and cyclicality in the EPC segment. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the consolidated financial and business risk profiles of VEL, along with its subsidiaries, given the common management and business synergies between the entities. 

Subsidiaries include Marvel Housing Pvt Ltd and Vascon Value Homes Pvt Ltd. Associates and joint ventures include Phoenix Ventures, Vascon Saga Construction LLP, Vascon Qatar WLL, Ajanta Enterprises, Vascon developers LLP, DCS Conventions and Hospitality Pvt Ltd and Mumbai Estates Pvt Ltd. 

Interest-bearing mobilisation advances have been treated as debt.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Sustained financial risk profile marked by stable debt indicators** 

Total networth rose to Rs 1,093 crore as on March 31, 2025, from Rs 981 crore a year before, aided by steady execution and operating margin in the EPC segment, leading to healthy accretion to reserve. Presence of price-escalation clauses in most contracts also reduces the exposure to volatility in raw material prices. 

A strategic realignment with an exit from all non-core businesses, has led to sharper focus on EPC and real estate segments. Debt protection metrics remain healthy, with net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratio at 0.66 time for fiscal 2025, aided by proceeds of Rs 74.79 crore as profit on sale of GMP Technical Solutions Pvt Ltd. The NCAAD ratio is expected to be over 0.4 time, and the NCA to repayment obligation ratio at over 1 time, in the medium term. 

# **Established position in the EPC (buildings) segment, supported by healthy order book and counterparty profile** 

VEL benefits from the management’s established track record in executing construction contracts for the infrastructure, real estate and manufacturing segments on an EPC basis. The company also has strong technical capabilities and domain expertise in the construction and execution of complex projects. 

The company had external orders worth Rs 2,377 crore as on September 30, 2025, and will continue to focus on government projects. Government entities accounting for nearly 78% of orders, will lend stability to the operating performance and ensure uninterrupted cash flow over the medium term. 

Vascon has entered into a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Adani Infra Ltd to collaborate as an execution partner under an early engagement model for the next five years. This should further boost the order book and revenue growth in the long term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Large working capital requirement** 

Operations are working capital intensive, owing to inventory held as collateral in the real estate business, and unbilled revenue and retention money in the EPC segment. Huge backlog of unsold real estate units (~Rs 320 crore, excluding Rs 220 crore as land and development potential) as on March 31, 2025, has led to a large inventory. 

In the EPC segment, the unbilled revenue formed ~18% of the order book and retention money, around 40% of overall receivables. However, receivables have been improving in the EPC segment (72 days as on March 31, 2025, as against 88 days a year before) as government bodies are the main counterparty. Receivables are likely to range within 70-80 days in this segment as proportion of revenue from the EPC business should remain stable. However, liquidation of long stuck inventory in the real estate segment will remain monitorable. 

# **Exposure to risks related to saleability of real estate projects** 

Saleability and implementation risk will persist for the real estate segment, as reflected by sharp fluctuation in income, sales and collections, noted over the past few fiscals. Certain projects (such as Vascon Goodlife in Pune) have witnessed limited progress amid muted demand. Additionally, launches in fiscal 2025 were less than expected. The Powai project was scheduled to be launched in fiscal 2025, and is now likely to be launched in April 2026. 

Timely launch of real estate projects will be monitorable. Ability to liquidate and reduce real estate inventory (worth ~Rs 320 crore as on March 31, 2025; excluding Rs 220 crore as land and development potential), delay in completion or launch of projects, and additional debt raised to support any cash flow mismatch, will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

With a healthy profit margin and comfortable leverage, cash flow should suffice to cover the debt obligation and regular capex. Cash accrual of Rs 80-100 crore expected per annum, along with cash generated in the real estate business, will cover the yearly debt obligation of Rs 60-64 crore over the medium term. Unencumbered cash and equivalents stood at Rs 118 crore as on March 31, 2025. Utilisation of the fund-based bank limit of Rs 95 crore averaged 58% during the 12 months ended September 30, 2025. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The credit risk profile of VEL will continue to benefit from its healthy order book, improving operating performance, healthy capital structure and adequate liquidity. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u> Upward factors** 

- Substantial scale up in EPC and real estate segments, along with a steady operating margin of 9.5-10% in the EPC segment 

Improvement in working capital cycle and liquidity 

# **Downward factors** 

- Weaker operating performance, marked by lower-than-expected net collections in the real estate business (below Rs 58 crore) 

Substantial delay in execution of orders or increase in real estate inventory, which may translate into increase in debt 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

VEL, based in Pune, is engaged in the EPC and real estate development businesses. The company was incorporated in January 1986, by Mr R Vasudevan and commenced operations with the construction of Cipla’s factory in Patalganga (Maharashtra) in November 1986. Until 1998, the company undertook third-party contracts in the real estate segment. 

VEL now undertakes construction of residential and office complexes, along with information technology parks, industrial units, shopping malls, multiplexes, educational institutions and hotels. Under the EPC segment, VEL has executed construction contracts. It primarily caters to government departments and authorities.
