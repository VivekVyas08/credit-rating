### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A+/Stable’ rating on the long-term bank facilities of Park Medi World Ltd (PMWPL; Formerly known as Park Medi World Private Limited; part of the park group). 

The rating continues to reflect the established market position and brand recall of Park group of hospitals and its strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by scale of operations and exposure to risks related to implementation and timely stabilisation of operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of PMWPL and its wholly owned and step-down subsidiaries as they have significant business and financial risk linkages. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position and brand recall of Park group of hospital:** The Park group is an established player in northern India, operating 14 large multi-specialty hospitals with approximately 3,000 operational beds. The business was started by Dr. Ajit Gupta and his son, Dr. Ankit Gupta, over three decades ago as a clinic in New Delhi, and since then, the promoters have built the brand organically through prudent bed addition and a focus on running operationally efficient hospitals. Over the years, the group has added multiple hospitals, both organically and non-organically, and, given their market presence and experience, they have been able to turn profitable in a short span of time. Furthermore, the hospitals are well-diversified, with no single specialty accounting for more than 16% of revenue in fiscal 2025. This experience of building a recognised brand and running multiple large hospitals is expected to support the business risk profile over the medium term. The group’s revenue is expected to improve to Rs. 1,700 crores in fiscal 2026 supported health growth from recently acquired hospitals and growth from the existing hospitals. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile is strong, marked by a net worth of Rs. 987 crores as of March 31, 2025, and with healthy assertion to reserve, pre-IPO funding of Rs. 100 crores, and IPO plans of the management; the net worth is likely to further strengthen over the medium term. The group has been prudent in financial risk planning, which is evident from its low gearing and leverage position _(total outside liabilities to adjusted debt)_ below 1 time. Going forward, given the acquisition plans for setting up greenfield and brownfield hospitals in various cities, the financial risk profile continues to remain comfortable, as most of the projects will be funded through internal accruals and free liquidity available, with limited dependence on external debt. The debt protection metrics were robust due to low leverage and healthy profitability: interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios are expected to be more than 8 times and more than 0.6 times respectively, for fiscal 2026 (6 times and 0.44 times, respectively, previous fiscal). The metrics are expected to improve further over the medium term, supported by healthy profitability. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Scale of operations:** Despite an improvement in revenue to Rs. 1,393 crores in fiscal 2025—and further expected growth to Rs. 1,700 crores in fiscal 2026—the scale remains moderate. The growth is supported by improving occupancy across the new hospital's facilities, leading to a healthy average revenue per occupied bed (ARPOB) of around Rs. 26,000. However, the group is undertaking sizable capital expenditures to set up new hospitals through both greenfield and brownfield projects; any delay in ramping up these operations can impact revenue growth. Going forward, timely 

stabilization of operations in all hospitals, leading to further growth in the scale of the group's operations, remains a key monitorable. 

**Exposure to risks related to implementation and timely stabilisation of operations** : The group is undertaking a sizeable capital expenditure (including acquisitions) of around Rs. 150–200 crore in fiscal 2026. The group is in the process of setting up new hospitals in Panchkula and undertaking brownfield expansions of a hospital in Narela, Kanpur, etc. Considering multiple expansion plans through greenfield and brownfield projects, newer hospitals take some time to scale up and reach similar levels compared to other hospitals. This may moderate operating profitability, as evident with operating margins declining from 31% in fiscal 2023 to 26.8% in fiscal 2025; the deviation is due to moderate occupancy in recently acquired hospitals in Patiala and Mohali, leading to low absorption of fixed costs, also due to changes in accounting to IND AS. Timely ramp-up and stabilization of operations in the new hospitals, supporting revenue growth and operating profitability, will remain key rating sensitivity factors. However, the ability of the Park Group to set up and stabilize greenfield projects and turn around brownfield projects provides comfort. However, timely implementation without significant time and cost overruns will be a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

The sanctioned limit of Rs. 362 crore was utilized at nearly 25% on average over the 12 months through June 2025. Expected cash accrual of over Rs 300-320 crore will comfortably cover yearly debt obligation of Rs. 80-100 crore in fiscal 2026. The group also has unencumbered cash of ~Rs 310 crore as of September 2025, which supports liquidity. Furthermore, the group maintains sufficient liquidity in each hospital to account for any exigencies in any particular hospital. Despite plans for greenfield and brownfield expansions over the medium term, Crisil Ratings expects the group to maintain its sizeable liquidity over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the Park group will benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and established market position. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Timely commercialisation and stabilization of new hospital leading to operating profitability at 25-26%. Sustained revenue of over Rs 2,500 crore leading to higher-than-expected cash accrual. 

Efficient management of working capital cycle leads to improvement in financial risk profile and liquidity. 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin to below 23% leading to lower-than-expected cash accrual Significantly higher-than-expected debt-funded capex impacting the financial and liquidity profiles. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in January 2011, PMWPL is a part of the Park group, which operates a chain of hospitals. The group has a total of 14 hospitals in cities such as Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Mohali, Jaipur and Behror, among others.
