### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Pavna Industries Ltd (PIL) to ‘ **Crisil BBB/Stable** ’ from ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable’. 

The upgrade factors in the healthy business risk profile of the group supported by their established relationships with original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). Operating income has remained stable, ranging between Rs 310 crore to Rs 320 crore over the past two fiscals, with Rs 309 crore reported in fiscal 2025. This stability is attributed to the group's strategic shift towards the non-casting business, which experienced growth of over 18% in fiscal 2025, driven by the addition of new customers, resulting in volumetric growth. The company's strong relationships with OEMs is expected to continue driving growth, with operating income projected at Rs 310-315 crore in fiscal 2026. The addition of new customers to the portfolio is expected to lead to incremental revenue from the casting business, supporting the company's revenue growth. In the first three months of fiscal 2026, the group achieved revenue of Rs 60.4 crore, with operating margin of approximately 8.3%. 

Operating margin sustained at around 11% in fiscal 2025 (11.7% in fiscal 2024), driven by increased sales through the noncasting business which fetches a higher margin. Going forward, as the group is reducing its casting business, the margin is expected to remain at 10-11%. 

The rating also reflects the healthy financial risk profile of the group with networth expected at Rs 255-260 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 212 crore as on March 31, 2025) driven by healthy accretion to reserves and issuance of pending share warrants. Networth improved to Rs 212 crore as on March 31, 2025 (Rs 84 crore as on March 31, 2024) driven by issuance of preferential equity shares and share warrants of Rs 120 crore in fiscal 2025. The proceeds have been utilised to repay the long-term debt and reduce working capital utilisation. Earlier, the working capital utilisation averaged more than 90%; it has reduced to 68% on average for the 12 months through June 2025. Gearing is expected to be less than 0.15 time as on March 31, 2026 (0.17 time as on March 31, 2025). 

The rating reflects PIL’s established market position and healthy relationships with customers in the automotive (auto) components industry and robust financial risk profile of the group. These strengths are partially offset by customer concentration in the revenue profile and large working capital requirement. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of PIL and its subsidiaries, Pavna Marketing Pvt Ltd, Pavna Sunworld Autotech Pvt Ltd, Swapnil Switches Pvt Ltd and Pavna Auto Engineers Pvt Ltd. This is because all these entities, collectively referred to as the Pavna group, are in the same business and have strong business and financial links. 

Now, Crisil Ratings is also consolidating the joint venture (JV) between PIL and SmartChip Microelectronic Corporation (PILSMC JV) will be 80% owned by PIL.

### **<u>Key rating drivers and detailed description</u> Strengths:** 

**Established market position and relationships with customers** : The group is an established player for locks and switches in the auto component industry, which is reflected in its diversified product profile and longstanding 

relationships with reputed clients. It is the sole supplier to most of its clients and is already associated with all key customers. The products cater to the 2W (two wheeler) and 3W (three wheeler) segments, resulting in a diversified customer and segment base. The promoter has nearly five decades of experience in the auto components industry; his strong understanding of market dynamics and healthy relationships with principal suppliers will continue to support the business. 

**Healthy financial risk profile** : Networth of the group is expected to be Rs 250-260 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 212 crore as on March 31, 2025) driven by healthy accretion to reserves and issuance of share warrants. The group’s capital structure has improved after issuance of equity as reflected in expected gearing of below 0.2 time as on March 31, 2026 (0.17 time as on March 31, 2025). Interest coverage ratio is expected to be 7-7.5 times in fiscal 2026 (3.1 times in fiscal 2025) driven by sustained profitability and reduction in debt. The group is expected to convert some part of the remaining share warrants in fiscal 2026 and the rest in fiscal 2027. 

# **Weaknesses:** 

- **Exposure to high customer concentration risk** : Although improving, the top customer contributed 51% of revenue in fiscal 2025 (60% in fiscal 2024), thereby exposing the business to intense customer concentration risk. However, this risk is partially offset by longstanding relationship with the customer. 

- **Large working capital requirement:** The group has to maintain inventory of 130-150 days due to a diversified product portfolio. Gross current assets (GCAs) were 202 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by huge inventory of around 152 days and debtors of 51 days. However, the working capital requirement is partially supported by payables of 36 days, internal accrual and bank limits. The GCAs are expected to be 230-240 days over the medium term, driven by large cash and bank balance and high inventory. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Adequate</u>** 

Expected net cash accrual of Rs 25-35 crore annually will be sufficient to meet incremental working capital requirement and cater to annual debt obligation of only Rs 1.5-2 crore over the medium term. Fund-based bank limit utilisation was 68% on average for the 12 months through June 2025 which was earlier more than 90%. The current ratio is expected to be 3.4-3.5 times as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

The Pavna group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter and established relationships with OEMs. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Revenue of over Rs 350 crore and operating margin of 10-11%, resulting in higher-than-expected net cash accrual Improvement in the working capital cycle and sustenance of healthy financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue and operating margin dropping below 9%, leading to lower-than-expected net cash accrual Large, debt-funded capital expenditure or stretched working capital cycle weakening the liquidity and financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the group</u>** 

Set up in 1971 by Mr Pawan Jain, PIL manufactures locks and ignition switches for two-wheelers. It got listed on the NSE Emerge platform in March 2021 and got listed on BSE and migrated to NSE Main board in June 2023. Current operations are being managed by Mr Swapnil Jain. 

Pavna Auto Engineering Pvt Ltd was incorporated on December 19, 1994, and is a subsidiary of PIL. It manufactures fuel cocks and die cast components. 

Pavna Sunworld Autotech Pvt Ltd was incorporated on Januaryr 23, 2018, and is a subsidiary of PIL. It manufactures chain tensioners, oil pumps, throttle body and fuel pumps. 

Swapnil Switches Pvt Ltd was incorporated on January 22, 1991, and is a subsidiary of PIL. It manufactures electromechanical switches. 

Pavna Marketing Pvt Ltd was incorporated on April 23, 2019, and is a wholly owned subsidiary of PIL. It serves the secondary spare market for locks, switches, fuel tank cap, motors and controller of electric vehicle, engine parts such as chain tensioner, oil pump and water pump. 

PILSMC JV will be 80% owned by PIL and will manufacture electronics locks.
