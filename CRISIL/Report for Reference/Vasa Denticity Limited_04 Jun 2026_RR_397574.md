### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has removed its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Vasa Denticity Limited (VDL; part of the Vasa group) from ‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications' and has reaffirmed the rating at ‘ **Crisil BBB** ’ while assigning a ' **Stable** ' outlook. 

Crisil Ratings had initially placed the rating on watch developing following the corporate announcement made by VDL on August 01, 2025, regarding the proposed acquisition of a controlling stake of 51% in IDS Denmed Private Limited (IDS) for a total consideration of Rs 128 crore. Subsequently, on May 08, 2026, the company announced that it would not proceed with the proposed acquisition as the two businesses were at meaningfully different stages of operation and digital maturity. Accordingly, Vasa and IDS decided not to proceed with the proposed transaction. 

Subsequently, Crisil Ratings sought clarity whether any financial obligations and/or legal implication would arise from the termination of the proposed acquisition. Crisil Ratings has received clarity as well as financial performance update and understood that no financial cost or legal implication would arise on account of the termination of the proposed acquisition. Accordingly, the rating has been removed from ‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications’. 

The stable outlook reflects the sustenance of the company’s revenue profile and healthy financial risk profile. The group’s revenue growth continues to remain healthy, supported by increasing product offerings, growing customer base and continued traction in its online distribution platform. Revenue is expected to grow to over Rs 270 crore in fiscal 2026 from Rs 249 crore in fiscal 2025. Operating margins moderated in fiscal 2026 to ~4% owing to high input costs, rupee depreciation, and loss of revenue arising on account of stock-outs. While price increases were enacted across the platform, a lag in execution resulted in temporary pressure on margins. The company is implementing corrective measures, including stabilisation of inventory levels to reduce lost sales, implementing operational efficiencies, improving the product mix towards a higher share of retail customers, and enforcing better cost control alongside normalised forex volatility. Recovery in operating margins following these measures will remain a key monitorable and a key rating sensitivity factor. 

The ratings continue to reflect the VDL group’s established distribution network in the dental supplies segment, extensive product portfolio, strong technology-enabled operating platform, experienced management team, and healthy financial risk profile supported by comfortable capital structure and adequate liquidity. These strengths are partially offset by the group's growing but modest scale of operations relative to larger industry participants and exposure to intense competition in the fragmented dental products distribution industry.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established distribution network and wide product portfolio:** The VDL group operates both online and offline, housing ~470 domestic and international brands with a comprehensive range of more than 23,000 dental products. The online channel is operated through its website, Dentalkart.com, and mobile application, Dentalkart. It provides a one stop solution 

to dental professionals, private clinics, hospitals and medical colleges for product needs. This, along with the decade long experience of the promoters in the dental industry, their strong understanding of market dynamics, established relationships with diverse suppliers and customers and multi-brand products will continue to support the business risk profile over the medium term. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth is expected to grow to around Rs 165-170 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 127 crore as on March 31, 2025) owing to steady accretion to reserves and infusion of equity from conversion of share warrants amounting to Rs 30.9 crore in H1-FY2026 and further the last tranche of Rs 6.5 crore was converted in May 2026. Capital structure continues to be healthy owing to long-term debt free status of the company as reflected in gearing of 0 times and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) of around 0.1 time as on March 31, 2026 (0.13 time in previous fiscal). Debt protection metrics have also been comfortable due to low leverage, reflected in expected interest cover of around 83 times in fiscal 2026 (253 times in fiscal 2025). 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Growing, but modest, scale of operations** : Revenue recorded a compound annual growth rate of around 31% over the three fiscals ended March 2026, driven by the increasing number of stock-keeping units, healthy order inflow and wide customer base. Revenue is expected to grow to over Rs 270 crore in fiscal 2026 from Rs 249 crore in fiscal 2025. Average revenue per order and customers has been growing consistently over the years. Nevertheless, sustained growth in scale is monitorable. 

**Exposure to intense competition** : The Indian dental supplies market remains highly fragmented and group faces competition from direct sellers/manufacturers and other unorganized players. Accordingly, it provides periodic discounts and attractive schemes to stave off competition and retain customers. Aggressive expansion by existing competitors and the emergence of large players in e-commerce may impinge upon profitability and scalability of the group and will remain a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Liquidity is adequate, marked by sufficient net cash accruals around Rs 12 crore in fiscal 2026 and Rs 20-25 crore annually thereafter- against nil debt repayment obligations, as it is currently long-term debt free. Liquidity is further supported by cushion in bank limits, which remain largely unutilized, with an average utilization of 10% over the eight months ended March 2026. Additionally, liquidity is supported by liquid investments in the form of arbitrage Mutual Funds of around Rs 42 crore as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the VDL group will continue to benefit from its established distribution network and wide product portfolio in the e-commerce space. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

Steady increase in revenue and sustenance of operating margin at 9-10%, leading to higher-than-expected net cash accruals 

Sustenance of financial risk profile and liquidity profile 

# **Downward Factors** 

Decline revenue and/or decline in operating margin to below 4% resulting in lower-than-expected net cash accruals. Sizeable stretch in the working capital cycle or any large, debt-funded capital expenditure impacting the financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

VDL (formerly, Vasa Denticity Pvt Ltd) was incorporated in 2016 and is promoted by Dr Vikas Agarwal and Mr Sandeep Aggarwal. The company is engaged in marketing and distribution of a comprehensive portfolio of dental products, including consumables, instruments, equipment and accessories for diagnosing, treating and preventing dental conditions as well as improving the aesthetics of the human smile through its online portal, Dentalkart.com, and mobile application, Dentalkart. VDL operates an online channel, housing ~470 domestic and international brands with a comprehensive product range of more than 23,000 dental products. 

Waldent Innovations Pvt Ltd, established in August 2023, is in the same business. 

Smileworks Pvt Ltd, incorporated in November 2024, delivers dental prostheses and restorations.
