### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its **‘** Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’ ratings on the bank facilities of Goyal Salt Limited (GSL). 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the salt manufacturing and trading business, favourable location of the plants, the moderate working capital cycle and healthy financial risk profile of the company. These strengths are partially offset by the moderate scale of operations and vulnerability to climatic changes and natural phenomena. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of GSL with its wholly owned subsidiary Goyal Exim Pvt Ltd (incorporated in March 2026). 

Unsecured loan from the promoters (Rs 0.7 crores as on March 31,2025) has been treated as debt. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoters and favourable location of the plants:** The two-decade-long experience of the promoters, Parmesh Goyal and Rajesh Goyal, in the salt manufacturing and trading industry, has enabled them to gain a strong understanding of market dynamics and build longstanding relationships with key customers and suppliers. Moreover, the favourable location of the plants, one each in Gujarat and Rajasthan, facilitates easy access to raw material and minimises transportation cost. Revenue has grown steadily from Rs 118 crore in fiscal 2024 to Rs 199 crore in fiscal 2026, backed by continued diversification in the product profile and customer base. Furthermore, continued ramp up of the manufacturing facility in Gandhidham, Gujarat, with focus on  own branded sales and exports, should aid healthy revenue growth over the medium term. 

**Moderate working capital cycle:** Gross current assets stood at 110 days as on March 31, 2026 (84 days a year ago), driven by receivables of 40-50 days and moderate inventory of 40-60 days. Customers are offered moderate credit period of 30–90 days and payments are collected on time. Inventory is maintained as per the seasonal and business requirements and tends to be higher during procurement season but the same is sold by the year end. This has also resulted in high dependence on bank lines. The working capital cycle is partially also supported by credit offered by suppliers. Working capital cycle should remain efficiently managed over the medium term. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth was moderate at Rs 65-70 crores as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 53 crores a year ago) and has steadily increased due to accretion to reserve and equity infusions. Capital structure is moderate as reflected in total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) and gearing ratio estimated at around 0.8 times and 0.7 times respectively as on March 31,2026 (around 0.7 time and 0.4 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025) aided by moderate reliance on external debt for capital expenditure (capex) and working capital requirements. Debt protection metrics are adequate as reflected in interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of above 7 times and 0.4 times, respectively, for fiscal 2026. Financial risk profile should remain healthy over the medium term, backed by healthy profitability, with no further debt-funded capex planned for the near term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate scale of operations:** Despite longstanding presence in the salt manufacturing and trading business, GSL operates on a moderate scale, as reflected in revenue of Rs 199 crores for fiscal 2026. This constrains scalability and operating efficiency. Continued ramp up of the newly expanded manufacturing facility, yielding incremental revenue, along with healthy demand from the existing plant, remains a key rating sensitivity factor. 

**Vulnerability to climatic changes and natural phenomena** : As the manufacturing facilities are in Rajasthan and Gujarat, the company remains exposed to adverse weather conditions and natural occurrences, and other regulatory, economic and demographic factors. Non-seasonal rainfall could also affect procurement of the principal raw material, raw salt. Though this risk is mitigated with GSL’s entry into a new state, sudden climatic changes and natural phenomena could impact revenue and profitability and this remains monitorable for the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation was moderate at more than 85% on average for the 12 months through March 2026, however, exceeded 95% in some months. While the company has received enhancement, moderate utilisation of working capital limits remains monitorable. Annual cash accrual is expected to be Rs 15–17 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 4 crore over the medium term and the surplus will cushion liquidity. Furthermore, the company has unencumbered cash and other equity instruments of Rs 4–5 crore as of March 2026. Low gearing and moderate networth support financial flexibility and overall liquidity. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes GSL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters in the salt manufacturing and trading business, their established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained growth in revenue  and sustenance of the operating margin, leading to high net cash accrual Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and prudent working capital management, aiding liquidity with reduced utilisation of bank lines below 85% on a sustained basis 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin, leading to net cash accrual below Rs 6.5 crores 

Further stretch in the working capital cycle or larger-than-expected debt-funded capex, weakening liquidity with bank limit utilisation sustained above 95% 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

GSL, formerly Goyal Salt Pvt Ltd, was incorporated in 2010, by Kunj Goyal and was reconstituted as a limited company in 2023. The company is engaged in refining raw salts procured from sub-soil brine, for use as industrial salt and edible salt. Products include refined free-flow iodised salt, refined free flow industrial salt, double fortified salt and refined half dry salt. Operations are managed by second-generation promoters, Parmesh Goyal and Rajesh Goyal. 

The company is listed on the SME platform of the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.
