### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Cyient Limited (Cyient). 

The rating continues to reflect the company’s strong financial risk profile and healthy business risk profile, underpinned by its diversified presence across engineering services, established clientele, and benefits derived from strategic acquisitions. These strengths are partially offset by moderation in operating profitability and relatively modest scale, along with execution risks associated with expansion into new growth areas. The rating is further constrained by exposure to customer and segment concentration risks, as well as the inherent susceptibility of its end-markets to macroeconomic cyclicality. 

On 30<sup>th</sup> May 2026, Cyient announced the acquisition of TAO Digital Limited Inc. (TAO), a US-based data engineering and AI-native digital solutions company (CY25 revenues of ~USD 80 million, EBITDA margin ~20%), for a cash consideration of USD 218 million. The acquisition involves an upfront payment of USD 130 million, with the balance payable over the next two years, linked to EBITDA-based earnouts. The transaction is expected to be primarily debt-funded. Further, in April 2026, the company also completed the acquisition of a 74% stake in Kinetic Technologies, strengthening its presence in power semiconductors. The acquisitions are expected to enhance Cyient’s capabilities across digital and semiconductor segments, enabling cross-selling opportunities, strengthening access to high-growth end-markets, and improving its overall business mix. While the increase in debt will moderate the capital structure, the impact on the financial risk profile is expected to remain manageable, supported by strong accruals and expected healthy cash flow generation from Cyient as well as the acquired entities. 

Operating performance remained muted in fiscal 2026, with revenues declining marginally by 1% due to macroeconomic uncertainties, delays in project execution, and demand softness across select end-user segments. Growth in the DET segment (~6%) was offset by a decline in the DLM segment (~17%) owing to a high base and moderation in execution. The semiconductor business reported revenue of ~USD 25.7 million, with gradual improvement registered over the course of the year.. Operating margin moderated to 12.9% from 15.6% in fiscal 2025, primarily due to losses in the semiconductor segment and subdued operating leverage, while margins in DET and DLM remained relatively stable. 

Over the medium term, revenue growth is expected to recover, supported by continued deal wins in DET wherein synergies with TAO are likely, healthy order book and visibility in DLM, and scaling up of the semiconductor segment, including contributions from Kinetic Technologies. Operating profitability is likely to remain range-bound in the near term but is expected to improve over the medium term, aided by improvement in business mix, integration of TAO which is a higher margin business, and continued cost optimization and efficiency improvements, partly offset by ongoing investments in the semiconductor business. 

The financial risk profile remains strong, supported by a healthy net worth of over Rs 6,100 crore as on March 31, 2026, and steady annual accruals. The company was net debt-free as on March 31, 2026, with a conservative capital structure. While leverage is expected to increase due to the acquisitions of TAO Digital and Kinetic Technologies and investments in the semiconductor business, the impact is mitigated by expected cash flow generation from acquired entities, staggered payment structures, and access to low-cost funding with favourable repayment schedules. Debt protection metrics are commensurately expected to remain comfortable, with interest coverage above ~12 times and gearing below 0.50 times over the medium term, supported by strong accruals and prudent financial policy.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Healthy business risk profile, supported by diversified presence across engineering services, established clientele and strategic acquisitions** 

Cyient offers niche product and process engineering services in domains such as aerospace and transportation, communication, mining, energy and utilities. Also, through Cyient DLM, it imparts system integration and prototyping capabilities to its engineering services, thus enabling it to provide design-to-production solutions to clients. Furthermore, the semiconductor business, though currently small, is expected to enhance diversification over the medium term through offerings including turnkey ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit) solutions and service options. 

Strategic acquisitions, including the proposed acquisition of TAO Digital Solutions and acquisition of Kinetic Technologies, are expected to strengthen capabilities in high-growth areas such as data engineering, AI, and semiconductor solutions. These acquisitions are aligned with Cyient’s strategy to enhance its digital and IP-led offerings within the DET segment and improve service mix. The focus on acquiring niche, high-margin businesses is also expected to support margin expansion and deepen client engagement over the medium term, although timely integration and realisation of synergies will remain monitorable. 

# **Strong financial risk profile** 

Cyient’s financial risk profile remains strong, supported by sizeable net worth of over Rs. 6,100 crore as on March 31, 2026, and healthy cash accruals. The company remained net debt-free as on March 31, 2026, with modest debt levels of Rs. 431 crore, including financial lease liabilities, lower compared to Rs.514 crore as on March 31, 2025. Although leverage is expected to increase due to debt-funded acquisitions (including TAO Digital and Kinetic) and growth investments in semiconductor business, the capital structure is expected to remain comfortable, with gearing likely to stay below ~0.5x over the medium term. Debt protection metrics are also expected to remain strong, with interest coverage above ~12x, supported by steady accruals and relatively low-cost acquisition financing. 

Liquidity remains comfortable, with cash and equivalents  of ~Rs.1,500 crore as on March 31, 2026, and sufficient internal accruals to fund moderate organic capex, shareholder returns (including announced buyback of Rs.720 crore), and debt servicing obligations. The staggered structure of TAO’s acquisition payout and expected funding through acquired entities’ cash flows provides additional comfort. The company also benefits from strong financial flexibility, aided by its listed status and access to capital markets. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Exposure to customer and segment concentration risks, and susceptibility to macroeconomic cyclicality** 

Despite diversification across segments, Cyient remains exposed to concentration risks, with the company deriving about 26% from the transportation segment, while top five customers consistently accounted for over 30% of revenues throughout fiscal 2026. Any slowdown in key segments or delay in client spending, especially in the DET business, can impact growth as the engineering services industry remains sensitive to global macroeconomic conditions, including slowdown in discretionary technology spending. This was evidenced in fiscal 2026, where revenue decline was essentially driven by delayed execution, budget deferrals, and geopolitical uncertainties. 

# **Moderation in operating profitability and relatively modest scale, with execution risks in new growth areas** 

Cyient remains a mid-sized tier II player in the engineering services space, which constrains its ability to undertake large and complex deals, given the industry preference for vendors with greater scale and end-to-end delivery capabilities. Parallelly, revenue growth has also remained subdued in recent periods due to macroeconomic headwinds and execution delays, impacting operating leverage. Consequently, operating profitability moderated to 12.9% in FY26 from 15.6% in FY25, owing to weak revenue growth and continued losses in the semiconductor segment, which remains in an investment phase. 

While strategic acquisitions are expected to support scale, enhance capabilities in high-growth segments, and improve service mix over the medium term, the benefits are likely to be gradual. Timely integration, scaling up of acquired businesses, and achievement of breakeven in the semiconductor segment will remain key monitorables. Any delay in realisation of expected synergies or slower ramp-up in semiconductors may continue to weigh on profitability in the near term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity is expected to remain strong, supported by cash and equivalents of ~Rs.1,500 crore as on March 31, 2026 and steady cash accruals of over Rs.750 crore annually over the medium term. While liquidity may be partly utilised towards the announced share buyback, incremental funding requirements for acquisitions as well as ongoing investments in the semiconductor segment are expected to be largely met through low debt. Nevertheless, internal accruals are expected to remain sufficient to meet debt servicing obligations (~Rs. 200-300 crore annually) and fund moderate capex (~Rs.100–200 crore annually). 

# **<u>ESG Profile</u>** 

The environment, social and governance (ESG) profile of Cyient supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

The IT sector has a low impact on the environment because of the inherent nature of digital services, core operations as well as products. The sector has a social impact because of its large workforce. Cyient has continuously focused on mitigating its environmental and social impact. 

# **Key ESG highlights:** 

The company aims to achieve 50% reduction in direct emissions by 2040 (compared with its fiscal 2023 base), and has committed to achieve net-zero emissions in alignment with United Nations Sustainable Development Goals as well as increase in renewable energy utilisation at owned sites to 75% by fiscal 2040 

Emissions stood ~1 tCO2e per employee in fiscal 2025, which increased ~6% from fiscal 2023 level 

- Cyient has approximately 25% of females in the workforce and intends to achieve ~39% female representation in the workforce by fiscal 2030 

- In the last three fiscals, there were zero reported instances of material data breaches and zero reported stakeholder complaints (customers and value chain partners) in fiscal 2025 

- The company’s governance structure is characterised by ~56% of the board comprising independent directors, 22% women directors and presence of board-level ESG committee, and extensive financial disclosures 

There is growing importance of ESG among investors and lenders. Cyient’s commitment to ESG principles will play a key role in enhancing stakeholder confidence, given the high share of market borrowing in its debt and access to both domestic and foreign capital markets. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes Cyient will continue to benefit from its diversified business profile, supported by established client relationships, healthy order book, and increasing focus on digital, AI-led and semiconductor segments, aided by recent acquisitions. The financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable despite higher debt on account of acquisition-led investments and ongoing spend towards the semiconductor business, supported by steady cash accruals, adequate liquidity, and a prudent financial policy. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained substantial revenue growth combined with increased customer and market segment diversification Steady improvement in operating efficiency with EBITDA margin remaining higher than 18% on a sustained basis Healthy financial risk profile in absence of any large debt-funded acquisition 

# **Downward factors**

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Cyient (formerly Infotech Enterprises Ltd) was set up as a private limited company in 1991 by Mr B V R Mohan Reddy, the executive chairman. The company commenced operations in September 1992. Cyient was reconstituted as a public limited company in April 1995 and came out with its IPO in March 1997. 

Cyient started operations by providing geographic information systems services. In May 2000, the company diversified into engineering services. It operates through eight business units: aerospace and defence; transportation; industrial, energy and natural resources; semiconductors, internet of things and analytics; medical and healthcare; utilities and geospatial; communications; and design-led manufacturing (Cyient DLM). Cyient DLM (earlier Rangsons) provides design integration and production facilities. Cyient has operations across the globe. 

Cyient carved out its semiconductor business in April 2025 into a standalone subsidiary, Cyient Semiconductors Pvt Ltd. The primary focus will be on providing end-to-end ASIC turnkey solutions.
