### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Galaxy Surfactants Ltd (Galaxy; a part of the GSL group). 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position of the group in the specialty chemicals sector, supported by the diversified customer base, segmental coverage and geographic reach, and longstanding relationships with leading global and national fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) players, particularly in the home and personal care (HPC) segment. The ratings also factor in the healthy operating capabilities and strong financial risk profile of the company. These strengths are partially offset by vulnerability of the operating margin to macroeconomic headwinds and volatility in raw material prices. 

Revenue grew by 11% year-on-year to Rs 4224 crore in fiscal 2025, driven by a 3% increase in volumes, primarily from the performance surfactants segment, and the remainder from increase in realisations as raw material costs primarily fatty alcohol prices nearly doubled during fiscal 2025. Volume growth was supported by the robust performance in the Rest of the World (ROW) markets, which accounted for 36% of total revenue, while India and AMET regions witnessed a flat performance. Due to higher volatility in input price, EBITDA margins moderated to 11.5% in fiscal 2025 from 12.7% YoY. The momentum continued into Q1 FY26, with revenue rising 31% YoY, led by strong demand from the ROW region. However, EBITDA margin moderated to 9.7%, compared with 12.7% in Q1 FY25. In medium term, the revenue growth is expected to be in early double digit, supported by volume expansion with stable demand across three key regions and continued product innovation. Gradual stabilization in raw material prices is likely to aid EBITDA margins. 

The recent imposition of 50% US tariffs is not expected to have significant impact on financial and business profile, supported by strong customer stickiness and a diversified global presence. Demand from the US market is likely to remain steady, given the essential nature of products catering to the Home and Personal Care segment of the FMCG. That said, the company’s cost competitiveness may allow it to absorb a portion of the tariff impact and remain competitive in the US market. On a steady state basis, the ability and extent of pass through of incremental costs to US customers, realignment of supply chains and ability of company to scale up their business in other geographies leveraging its strong distribution network will be monitorable. 

The financial risk profile remains healthy, marked by strong debt protection metrics. Gearing should remain below 0.10 times while interest coverage is likely to be healthy over 25 times in the medium term. Steady cash accrual and prudent funding of capital expenditure (capex) are expected to keep debt levels under control and strengthen the financial risk profile of the group.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established position in the HPC intermediates market:** The GSL group has been engaged in manufacture and sale of surfactants and specialty chemicals used as intermediates by the HPC industry for more than four decades. The group caters to over 1,450 customers across more than 80 countries, including reputed multinationals and domestic customers, and is one of the leading players globally. India accounted for 40% of revenue in fiscal 2025, followed by Africa, Middle East and Turkey (AMET: 24%) and rest of the world (ROW: 36%), which comprises the US, Europe and Asia-Pacific. Wide geographic and customer diversification de-risk the exposure to a single region. Products find applications across mass, mid-priced and premium range of customers. 

Longstanding association with clients and strong research and development (R&D) capabilities should enable the group to maintain its leadership position. Overall revenue is likely to grow at a steady rate over the medium term, in line with demand from end-user segments. 

**Healthy operating capabilities:** The group is integrated across the full value chain of the HPC industry. It has seven strategically located facilities with in-house project execution capabilities, five in India, and one each in Egypt and the US. The group has a strong R&D focused team. As of March 2025, the group has 111 approved patents and has applied for 32 more patents, which will support its growing product base (235+) over time. Through cost optimisation and focus on increasing the share of value-added products, the group was able to increase its earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) per tonne from Rs 15,000 in fiscal 2018 to over Rs 18500 in fiscal 2025. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile of the GSL group is marked by healthy networth, improved gearing and strong debt protection metrics. Steady cash accrual and prudent funding of capex have enabled the group to maintain debt levels. Gearing remained stable at 0.09 time as on March 31, 2025 continued due to lower reliance on bank limit. Interest coverage ratio remained healthy at over 25 times in fiscal 2025. The group is expected to incur capex of Rs 150-175 crore per annum over the medium term, funded entirely through internal accrual. With steady business performance, debt protection metrics will remain strong over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate working capital intensity in operations:** Gross current assets were high ranging from 130 to 155 days over the three fiscals ending March 31, 2025, led by inventory of 65-75 days and receivables of 60-65 days. Working capital requirement is met via creditors (65-75 days) and bank debt. Also, the strong credit profile of customers provides the group flexibility to discount receivables and generate liquidity, if required. 

**Partial susceptibility of operating profitability to volatility in raw material prices:** Cost of lauryl alcohol forms majority of raw material cost. The group derives bulk of its revenue through the cost-plus model. Under the non-cost-plus model Operating profitability remains partially susceptible to volatility in prices of key raw materials, such as fatty alcohol, fatty acids, ethylene oxide, phenol, and linear alkyl benzene, as witnessed in the current fiscal. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Expected cash accrual of over Rs 300 crore per annum and cash and cash equivalents (including investments) of Rs 510 crore as on March 31, 2025 (Rs 440 crore as of March 31, 2024) aid liquidity. Utilisation of the bank limits was low, averaging 28% over the 12 months ended May 2025. The group has long-term debt obligations of average Rs 10 crore per fiscal during 2026-2027. Internal accrual, cash and cash equivalents, and unutilised bank limit should suffice to cover the debt obligation. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The GSL group will continue to benefit from its strong market position and established clientele in the domestic and global markets. The financial risk profile should remain healthy, aided by steady cash accrual.

# **Upward factors** 

Double digit growth in revenues supported by volumes with gradually improving operating margins Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and debt protection metrics 

# **Downward factors** 

Deterioration in operating performance, leading to cash accrual below Rs 150 crore 

Any large, debt-funded capex or sizeable stretch in the working capital cycle, constraining the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

The GSL group, set up in 1980, manufactures, sells and distributes surfactants and specialty chemicals, which are used as intermediate raw materials in HPC products. GSL has its facilities at Tarapur and Taloja in Maharashtra, which have an installed capacity of around 1.91 lakh MTPA, and a 1.33 lakh MTPA ethoxylation (intermediate process) plant at Jhagadia, Gujarat. 

Galaxy Chemicals (Egypt) SAE, a step-down subsidiary of GSL, set up a greenfield project in Suez, 140 km from Cairo in Egypt, where it operates a manufacturing plant with an installed capacity of around 1.18 lakh MTPA. 

Tri-K, a step-down subsidiary of GSL, markets the group's products in the US, and manufactures proteins for the global cosmetic and personal care industry.
