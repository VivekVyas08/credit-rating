### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

CRISIL Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank loan facilities of Shree Rama Multi-Tech Ltd (SRMTL) to **‘CRISIL BBB/Stable/CRISIL A3+’** from ‘CRISIL BBB-/Stable/CRISIL A3’. The ratings are simultaneously **withdrawn** upon company’s request and on receipt of required documents. The withdrawal is in line with CRISIL Ratings withdrawal policy. 

The upgrade reflects the improved capital structure of the company with the successful completion of its long-standing right issue of shares and allotment of preference shares. The company raised Rs.63 crores via the right issue and the proceeds were used to repay Rs. 61.72 crores of principal amount of its outstanding loans / debentures. The company also completed allotment of 15% cumulative preference shares and raised Rs. 7.67 crores which were used to redeem preference shares of Rs. 6.67 crores along with unpaid dividend of Rs. 1 crore. 

Post issue of right shares, outstanding interest of Rs. 183.36 crores on the borrowings was also waived off. Also, post allotment of preference shares, the preference shareholders waived off their right to claim accumulated dividend and accumulated interest on delayed payment. This substantially reduced the contingent liability on the company. 



### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, CRISIL Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SRMTL. CRISIL Ratings has also applied its group notch-up framework to factor in the extent of support available to SRMTL from the Nirma group. 



### **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1993, SRMTL is a Gujarat-based integrated packaging company that manufactures laminated tubes and specialty packaging products. It has installed capacity of 9,514 lakh multi-layer tubes. It has more than 150 clients in India and abroad. SRMTL is a part of the Nirma group, which holds 61.57% stake in the company. 



### **<u>About the Nirma Group</u>** 

Nirma is a diversified group with notable presence in fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), inorganic chemicals, cement and pharmaceuticals. It manufactures and sells detergent and common salt under the popular brand Nirma. It is also a leading producer of soda ash in the country. 

The group company Nuvoco Vista Corp Ltd is among the top five cement manufacturers in India with strong presence in north and eastern markets. The Nirma group recently completed the acquisition of Glenmark Lifesciences Ltd to strengthen its presence in the pharmaceutical business in addition to the existing Aculife Healthcare Pvt Ltd.
