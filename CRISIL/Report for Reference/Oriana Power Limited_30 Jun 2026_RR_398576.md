### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil A-/Stable’** rating to Rs.75 Crore non-convertible debentures of Oriana Power Limited (OPL; part of the Oriana group) and reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+’ ratings on the existing bank facilities and the nonconvertible debentures of OPL. 

The ratings reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the solar equipment industry, supported by strong customer profile and healthy order book, and the company’s healthy financial risk profile and operating margin. These strengths are partially offset by significant portion of networth invested in special purpose vehicles (SPVs) and susceptibility to risks related to availability of cells and regulatory changes. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has moderately consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of OPL and its subsidiaries/associates. In line with this, the equity requirement in under-implementation projects has been factored into the financials of the company. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoters, supported by strong customer profile and healthy order book:** The promoters' experience of over a decade in undertaking solar EPC (engineering, procurement and construction) projects will help OPL focus on sustainable growth in the solar product EPC industry. Healthy relations with suppliers and customers should aid the business over the medium term. As of March 2026, the company had delivered solar projects of more than 835 megawatt (MW) in a timely manner and within the stipulated cost, and had ongoing projects of 700+ MW (including floating solar plants and rooftop solar projects). The growth in operations is driven by strong clientele, including both government and private parties. The order book as of March 2026 comprised solar projects of over Rs 6,612 crore with orders of over Rs 3,000 crore in the pipeline, providing healthy revenue visibility for the medium term. With this, the operating income is likely to grow substantially to above Rs 2,000 crore annually in medium term from Rs 1,820 crore in fiscal 2026. Diversification into battery energy storage systems (BESS), and green hydrogen, wherein OPL has received significant orders, shall help improve the business risk profile over the medium term. Timely execution of the order book leading to sustained revenue growth will remain monitorable. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** The financial risk profile is likely to remain healthy, backed by strong networth, expected to increase above Rs 1,000crores as on March 31, 2027, from Rs 785.6 crore as on March 31, 2026, because of higher accretion to reserve. On a standalone level, OPL had debt of Rs 701.2 crore and contingent liability of Rs 261.2 crores as on March 31, 2026. Despite this, the capital structure of the company has remained comfortable and gearing is expected to remain below 1 time as on March 31, 2027 (1.2 times as on March 31, 2026). Improving operating margin over the past few fiscals, despite moderate dependence on bank debt, has kept debt protection indicators healthy. The interest coverage is expected at 10–12 

times for fiscal 2027 (8.75 times in fiscal 2026). In the absence of any debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) plans in OPL, the financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term. 

**Healthy operating profitability:** The company offers end-to-end services for solar EPC projects, right from land identification to project commissioning. The company's service portfolio includes understanding client requirements and providing connectivity beyond standard EPC services. This led to an improvement in the operating margin to 22.34% in fiscal 2026. The margin will be shielded from any sharp volatility in raw material prices as the company hedges its inventory requirement at the time of order booking. The operating margin is expected to remain healthy at 18–20% over the medium term. With the commencement of BESS operations, the operating profitability is likely to improve on account of low operating expenses in the segment, which will be monitorable. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Significant portion of networth invested in SPVs:** OPL invested Rs 553 crore (70.4% of its net worth) in its subsidiaries under the independent power purchase (IPP) model in fiscal 2026. The company is likely to invest an additional Rs 62–64 crore in fiscal 2027 in its special purpose vehicles (SPVs) under the IPP model, taking the total investment to Rs 615-620  crore (58 % of its net worth), with only 15-16% of its net worth allocated to under-construction projects, the funding for which will be monitorable while the remaining 55–56% of networth is in operational projects. . The company is likely to continue investing in its SPVs, with plans to set up solar plants and diversify into green hydrogen, battery energy storage systems (BESS), in fiscals 2027 and 2028. OPL has also provided a corporate guarantee for the term debt of its subsidiaries. A large project for green ammonia, valued at Rs 3,009 crore, has been awarded to OPL and is expected to be commercialized from April 2028. The project’s equity is expected to be mobilized from multiple sources such as from company’s assets held for sale and other suitable instruments or strategic investors. However, given the track record of timely completion of previous SPV projects, the timely infusion of availability and deployment of equity from the stated sources, without any further cost or time overruns, shall remain a key rating sensitivity factor. 

**Susceptibility to risks related to availability of solar cells and regulatory changes:** China dominates the photovoltaic (PV) solar cells supply chain with a 70–80% share in the global market. Though India has achieved self-sustainability for solar panels driven by the ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) policy, the dependence on uninterrupted import of solar cells (raw material for manufacturing solar panels) remains high. Any trade disruption on account of macroeconomic factors shall have a direct impact on EPC players and original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) of solar panels. The government directives and likely imposition of ALCM (Approved List of Cell Manufacturers) shall continue to tighten supply and potentially constrain contractors from importing cells. Hence, the availability of raw material remains vulnerable to changes in government policies and any disruption can cause an increase in the cost of solar EPC or have an adverse impact on timely execution of orders. OPL has a strong operational policy ensuring the availability of critical components for project execution, leading to timely completion of projects. However, timely execution of orders while sustaining profitability remains monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity should remain supported by the ample surplus available in cash accrual and bank lines. Bank limit utilisation was moderate, at 57.4% on average for the 12 months through April 2026. Cash accrual of Rs 250–300 crore over the medium term will be sufficient to meet negligible term debt obligations. The current ratio remained moderate at 1.47 times as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

The group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters, horizontal business integration, enabling consolidation of resources for expansion within the industry, along with healthy order book.

# **Downward factors** : 

Delay in monetization of the SPVs resulting in weakening of liquidity risk profile 

Further stretch in working capital requirements leading to TOL TNW ratio above 2.5 times Decline in revenue or fall in operating margin leading to lower-than-expected cash accrual 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Based in Noida (Uttar Pradesh), OPL was incorporated as private limited company in 2013 and reconstituted as a closely held public limited company in 2023. Promoted by Parveen Kumar, Rupal Gupta and Anirudh Saraswat, OPL, along with its subsidiaries, has two main business verticals: offering renewable energy solutions on BOOT (build, own, operate, transfer) and on EPC basis. OPL is listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) SME platform.
