### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’** ratings on the bank facilities of Vinyas Innovative Technologies Limited (VITL). 

The rating reflects VITL's established track record and extensive industry experience of the promoters, strong order book position and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by working capital intensive operations and Import reliance on key raw materials. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of VITL. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Established track record & extensive industry experience of the promoters and diversified product portfolio and** 

**end user industries:** The promoters have experience of over two decades in electronic component manufacturing industry and it caters to the broad range of end user industries including aerospace & defense, medical equipment and industrial segments. Over the decades, VITL has developed strong engineering and execution capabilities, which has helped to establish themselves as a reliable partner in designing and manufacturing electronic components. Crisil Ratings believes that the experience and expertise of the promoters should support revenue growth and operating margins over the medium term. VITL continues to focus on complex – low to medium volume, high-tech and high-mix business. Furthermore, it largely manufactures safety critical components for its customers across various sectors. The company’s execution track record and ability to provide integrated solutions enables it to provide one stop solutions for its clients, which should support business growth and profitability over medium term. 

**Strong order book position:** VITL has a healthy order book of around ~Rs.1000 crore (as of July 2025), to be executed over a period of 18-36 months. Orderbook has grown significantly as compared to previous fiscal wherein it stood at Rs 520 crores as of April 2024. Timely execution of the orderbook while improving the current order position will remain monitorable. 

**Healthy financial risk profile: V** ITL has a healthy financial profile marked by networth of Rs 147 crore as on March 31<sup>st</sup> 2025 with low gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted tangible networth (TOL/ANW) of 0.70 times and 1.24 times respectively as on March 31<sup>st</sup> , 2025. Capital structure is expected further improve backed by equity infusion in current fiscal and steady accretion to reserves. Debt protection measures have also been at healthy levels with interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratio are at 3 times and 0.24 times for fiscal 2025. VITL’s debt protection measures are likely to further improve over medium term with improving profitability. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Working capital intensive operations:** Its intensive working capital management is reflected in its gross current assets of 263 days as on March 31, 2025. Working capital cycle is marked by moderately high inventory levels and higher receivables at the end of the 4<sup>th</sup> quarter. Working capital profile is expected to remain capital intensive; given the nature of business and turnaround time required in the end user projects. 

**Import reliance on key raw materials, exposure to forex fluctuations and volatility in input prices:** The company has significant reliance on imports to meet its raw material requirement, with 70-75% sourced from the USA, Taiwan, Singapore, Israel and other foreign countries. This makes the company’s operations vulnerable to supply chain disruptions caused by any bottleneck or geopolitical risk. Raw material cost forms a sizeable chunk of the cost of production and any sharp fluctuation in prices of these components or forex rates could impact profitability adversely over the medium term, and hence, remains a key monitorable. Nonetheless, there exists price parity variation clause and Foreign Exchange Rate Variance in most of the contracts. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Adequate</u>** 

Bank limit utilization is low at around 83 percent for the past twelve months ended August 2025. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 25-33 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 4- 6 crore over the medium term. Current ratio is healthy at 1.67 times on March 31, 2025. Moderate cash and bank balance of around Rs.11 crore as on March 31, 2025. Sizable equity infusion in current fiscal, availability of sufficient non-fund-based limits and support from promoters provide further comfort. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes VITL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Improved revenue growth while maintaining operating margin in the range of 11% resulting in better net cash accruals. Sustenance of financial risk profile and improvement of working capital management. 

# **Downward factors** 

- Decline in operating margins below 6% leading to lower than expected net cash on account of decline in revenue or operating profits. 

Large debt-funded capital expenditure weakens capital structure or dividend payouts leading to stretch in liquidity. Continuous stretch in working capital cycle driven by higher inventory/debtor levels. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

VITL was incorporated in 2001. VITL is engaged in designing, engineering and electronics manufacturing services catering to global Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Original Design Manufacturers (ODMs) in the electronic industry. The company provides end-to-end solutions ranging from design for manufacturability, supply chain management, PCB assembly, advanced test solutions, product integration, and after-market support. VITL has its manufacturing facilities in Mysore, Karnataka. VITL’s shares are listed on the NSE Emerge platform. VITL is promoted by Mr. Narendra Narayanan, Mr. Sumukh Narendra and Mrs. Meera Narendra.
