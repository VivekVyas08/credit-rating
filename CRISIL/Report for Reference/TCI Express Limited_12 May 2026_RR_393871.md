### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable’ rating on the long-term bank facilities of TCI Express Limited (TCI Express). 

The rating continues to reflect the company’s strong financial risk profile, healthy market position in express logistics, diversified customer base and differentiated offerings. The company has witnessed a moderation in its business risk profile due to flattish revenue growth and profitability in fiscal 2026 compared to fiscal 2025. Revenue and operating profitability are expected to rebound this fiscal, driven by an increased focus on new sectors such as defence, electric vehicles (EVs), and solar energy, as well as diversification into other premium services in rail, air and customer-to-customer (C2C) businesses, and price hikes taken in this fiscal. These strengths are partially offset by the modest scale of operations, low cash flow diversity and exposure to intense competition. 

In the first nine months of fiscal 2026, revenue growth remained flattish, primarily due to moderation in the road segment (81-82% of revenue), which was affected by lower demand in industrial and export-linked sectors such as lifestyle and textiles, resulting from higher US tariffs and intense competition. However, other segments, including rail, air, C2C, and e- commerce have grown and are likely to contribute a higher share in the medium term, thereby reducing dependence on the road segment. The company's topline is expected to rebound in fiscal 2027 with 12-14% year-on-year growth, driven by improving diversification, a focus on emerging industries—such as defence, EVs, and solar energy—price hikes, and improving contribution from small and medium enterprises (SMEs). 

Operating margin remained moderate at 10.4% in the first nine months of fiscal 2026, compared to 14-15% previously. This was mainly due to lower fleet utilisation (~83%) driven by weaker demand in the lifestyle and textile sectors, and higher air costs that were not fully passed on to customers. Additionally, increased toll, labour, fixed costs have also affected profitability. Operating margin is expected to increase to 11.5-12.0% in fiscal 2027, driven by several key factors, including increasing fleet utilisation to 84-85% providing operating leverage, price hikes of ~3%, and passing on of higher air costs. Additionally, the company aims to grow its share of SMEs, which have higher margins than large corporates. It also plans to increase its share of high-margin segments like rail, air, and C2C to around 20%. 

The financial risk profile remains strong, marked by estimated healthy networth of Rs 800-830 crore with negligible debt as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics are expected to remain strong on account of negligible debt and healthy accruals from operations over the medium term. The company is expected to generate cash accruals of Rs 100-120 crore in the medium term, which along with a healthy free cash and equivalent of Rs 146 crore as on December 31, 2025 will be sufficient to fund incremental capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 80-100 crore and working capital requirements. The company has capex plans of Rs 80-100 crore per annum in the medium term, primarily towards branch expansion, sorting centre infrastructure and information technology (IT) upgrades.

**-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Strong market position** 

TCI Express is a group company of Transport Corporation of India Ltd (TCIL; rated ‘Crisil AA/Stable/Crisil A1+’), which is one of the largest integrated service providers in the logistics industry. TCI Express has a healthy market position in the express delivery business, aided by reputed brand and the promoter’s experience of more than six decades in the logistics industry. The company is among the major players in the road express delivery industry and has a market share of 6-7% (by value of goods transported). They provide diversified portfolio of express services, with a dominant presence in road transport (81-82% of revenue) and a growing presence in other segments, including air, rail, C2C, and Ecom, which contribute 18-19% to revenue. The company has invested heavily in multimodal capabilities, including rail, air, and C2C businesses, which have shown healthy growth and are expected to increase their share to ~20% in the next 1-2 years. Investments in infrastructure, including expanded branch networks (for targeting SMEs and other multi-modal segment clients (ex-road)), air networks (doubled in last 2 years), and automation of sorting centres, will also support growth. 

# **Steady operating efficiency, supported by stable working capital cycle; despite temporary moderation in operating margin** 

The company's operating efficiency remains steady, underpinned by its asset-light model, which enables it to maintain low debt levels and healthy returns on capital employed (RoCE). However, it has witnessed some moderation in the last two fiscals due to decline in profitability. Operating margin is estimated to remain at 10.3-10.5% in fiscal 2026 compared to 10.4% in fiscal 2025 and 14-15% in the past. In the last two fiscals, the margins have been impacted due to lower fleet utilisation, softer demand, higher air business costs, and increased toll and labour expenses, which were partially passed on by the company. The margins are expected to gradually improve with improving fleet utilisation, amid diversification to newer sectors like defence, EVs, and solar energy, price hikes taken to pass on increased costs, and a focus on increasing the share of higher-margin segments (rail, air, C2C) in the medium term. 

Operating efficiency is supported by a flexible hiring model, strategically located sorting centres, and long-standing relationships with a diverse client base. Furthermore, with planned capex focused on enhancing infrastructure, the company is poised to maintain a healthy RoCE of 15-20% in the medium term. Additionally, working capital requirements remain low, reflected in gross current assets (GCAs) of 80-90 days. 

# **Strong financial risk profile** 

The financial risk profile remains strong supported by estimated healthy networth of over Rs 800 crore with negligible debt, as on March 31, 2026. The debt protection metrics are expected to remain strong over the medium term on account of negligible debt and healthy accrual from operations. The company is expected to generate cash accrual of Rs 100-120 crore over the medium term, which will be sufficient to fund the capex plans. It is further supported by cash and equivalent of Rs 146 crore as on December 31, 2025 and bank lines of Rs 40 crore with average utilisation of ~3% in the 12 months through February 2026. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Modest scale of operations** 

The turnover is estimated to remain modest at Rs 1230-1250 crore in fiscal 2026. Around 81-82% of the revenue comes from road transportation of express cargo. Due to high concentration in revenue, cash flow is susceptible to any slowdown in the road express logistics industry. However, clientele is diversified, with the top 10 customers contributing to around 10% of revenue and SMEs to around 49%. The company is also fairly diversified across end user industries. Nonetheless, any slowdown in key client industries, adversely impacting revenue and operating profitability, will be a key rating sensitivity factor. 

# **Exposure to intense competition** 

Intense competition from large, organised players, such as Blue Dart, DTDC Express, Gati Ltd; and unorganised players restrict the growth in market share and the ability to fully pass on price increases to customers. The ability to sustain healthy revenue growth amid intense competition will be monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Liquidity is healthy as reflected in cash and equivalent of Rs 146 crore as on December 31, 2025, and bank limits of Rs 45 crore against which utilisation remained low at ~3% for the 12 months through February 2026. Expected annual cash accrual of Rs 100-120 crore, over the medium term, will adequately cover capex and working capital requirements in the absence of term debt obligation. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the business and financial risk profiles of TCI Express will remain strong over the medium term, supported by its strong market position in the road express segment, longstanding client relationships and asset light model.

# **Downward factors**

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

TCI Express started independent operations on April 1, 2016, in line with TCIL’s strategy of demerging the express division (XPS) into a separate business entity. The division was operating as a business unit of TCIL since 1996. TCI Express caters to diverse express delivery requirements, including domestic and international parcel services, with connectivity across road, rail and air. 

TCIL was established by P D Agarwal in 1958. From a conventional transportation company, it has become India’s largest integrated logistics service provider. TCIL has diversified its revenue streams over the years and at present operates through multiple divisions—freight, supply chain solutions and seaways.
