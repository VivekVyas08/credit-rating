### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisl Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of NBCC (India) Ltd (NBCC) to **‘Positive’** from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at **‘Crisil AA’.** 

The revision in outlook factors in the continuous improvement in the business risk profile of the company, supported by healthy operating performance and a 1.4 times increase in order book with majority of the orders coming for higher-margin redevelopment projects. With the improved order book, revenue is expected to increase at a compound annual growth rate of 11-13% over the medium term, providing adequate revenue visibility. The first half of fiscal 2026 witnessed a strong revenue growth of ~15%, compared with the corresponding half in the previous fiscal. The ability to secure new orders and manage project execution within time will remain monitorable. 

The operating margin was ~4% in the first half of fiscal 2026 and is expected to remain at 5-6% over the medium term, driven by improving project order mix towards higher-margin redevelopment projects. Redevelopment projects now constitute ~53% of the order book, from ~41% in fiscal 2024. Healthy order book translating to revenue growth and high profitability is expected to strengthen net cash accrual over the medium term. The financial risk profile remains robust, supported by the company’s debt-free position, with no plans to undertake any debt over the medium term due to its assetlight business model wherein it operates on a back-to-back subcontracting arrangement minimizing capital expenditure (capex) and working capital requirements. Cash accrual is projected at Rs 600-630 crore per annum against nil debt obligation and negligible capex over the medium term. Financial flexibility is supported by minimal working capital requirements and unencumbered cash and equivalent of over Rs 220 crore as on September 30, 2025. 

The rating continues to reflect the long track record and established market position of NBCC in the construction and project management business. The rating also takes comfort from NBCC’s status as a Schedule A Navratna company, due to ownership by the Government of India under the administrative control of Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs. These strengths are partially offset by modest operating profitability and exposure to execution risk, as most of the projects are yet to start, and risks related to contingent liabilities. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of NBCC and its subsidiaries/joint ventures, except NBCC-RK Millen and HSCL-SIPL, as these entities do not have any transactions and NBCC has already provided for their losses.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **PSU track record and strong market position in the PMC segment** 

NBCC, a Navratna public sector undertaking (PSU), was incorporated in 1960 for undertaking projects from central and state government organizations on a nomination basis, as well as through competitive bidding. NBCC is also designated as an implementing agency for several government schemes. The company has completed and is in the process of executing various redevelopment projects across Delhi/ National Capital Region including stressed real estate projects. It has also been awarded major redevelopment projects across Maharashtra as well as Jammu & Kashmir further strengthening geographical diversification. As of October 2025, the consolidated order book stood at Rs 1,28,000 crore, with order book to 

revenue ratio of around 10 times, providing healthy medium-term revenue visibility. The company was awarded contracts worth Rs 10,000 crore in the first half of fiscal 2026 and it tendered projects worth ~Rs 3,800 crore during the same period. Orders worth Rs 34,000 crore are under execution and projects of Rs 10,000-12,000 crore are in the tendering phase. Thus, revenue growth is expected at above 20% and cash accrual is expected at more than Rs 650 crore over the medium term. 

# **Nil debt and strong liquidity** 

The company has healthy capital structure with no external debt, leading to low financial charges (only financial charges being commissions on bank guarantees). Liquidity was strong with cash and bank balance of ~Rs 4,866 crore with unencumbered cash balance around Rs 220 crore as on September 30, 2025, on consolidated basis. NBCC is expected to generate cash accrual of Rs 600-630 crore per annum over the medium term against nil debt obligation and limited capex. Despite being in the highly working capital-intensive construction industry, the working capital cycle has been moderate as the business model entails execution of project management consultancy (PMC) projects against customer advances. NBCC operates on an asset-light business model and transfers most of the risks with respect to execution and cost passthrough to subcontractors through back-to-back arrangements and bank guarantees. This also leads to sizeable liquid surplus and non-operating cash flow. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Modest operating profitability** 

Operating margin fluctuated in the past few fiscals owing to lower operating income due to stuck projects while overheads (like in the form of salaries) remained high. Additionally, margin on PMC contracts reduced to 5-8% (from 7-10% earlier) owing to a policy drive for cost efficiency in government-funded projects. However, the operating margin is expected to improve to 5-6% over the medium term, backed by healthy execution of high-margin redevelopment projects and benefits of operating leverage with higher scale. Sizeable book position (~Rs 1,28,000 crore as of October 2025 at consolidated level) also provides medium-term revenue visibility, though ramp up in execution remains monitorable. 

# **Exposure to execution risk and risks related to contingent liabilities** 

NBCC is exposed to execution risk as most of its projects in the current order book are yet to start. The sector is subject to cyclicality as it is dependent on economic growth. Owing to the nature of business, NBCC has sizeable off-balance sheet liabilities, primarily claims pending from clients as well as bank guarantees given to clients. Consequently, the company is exposed to liquidity risk, which may arise out of contingent liabilities. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash accrual is projected at Rs 600-630 crore per annum against nil debt obligation and negligible capex over the medium term. The company has nil debt and does not plan to undertake debt in the near term. Cash and cash equivalents stood sizeable at Rs 4,866 crore and unencumbered cash balances at around Rs 220 crore as on September 30, 2025. The backto-back arrangements with subcontractors help to keep the working capital requirement minimal and maintain strong liquidity. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

The business risk profile of NBCC will further strengthen from sizeable growth in the order book, enabling sustenance of strong operating performance with improving margin driven by a higher mix of redevelopment PMC projects earning better margins. The financial risk profile is expected to remain strong with nil debt and strong liquidity, aided by low working capital and capex requirements. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Strong operating performance, with return on capital employed above 25% on a sustained basis 

- Sustained growth in orders providing medium-term revenue visibility 

- Sustenance of financial risk profile and cash surplus while maintaining the working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors** 

- Operating profit before depreciation, interest and tax margin remaining below 4% on a sustained basis 

- Weakening of business risk profile because of lower order inflow or slow pace of execution 

- Weakening of capital structure or increase in working capital intensity 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1960, NBCC is a central government enterprise under the Ministry of Urban Development and is a Schedule A Navratna company. The company was set up as a PMC to undertake civil and industrial infrastructure projects for central and state governments. Under this arrangement, projects are awarded on a nomination basis to NBCC, which then assigns the projects to third-party contractors. The company also secures jobs from the government and public sector clients through tenders and develops commercial and residential projects.
