### **<u>Detailed rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of Amanta Healthcare Ltd (AHL) to **‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’** from ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’. 

The rating upgrade reflects improvement in the financial risk profile of the company, driven by increase in networth from Rs 96 crore as on March 31, 2025, to Rs 211 crore as on September 30, 2025, backed by equity funds raised through initial public offering (IPO) of around Rs 126 crore. Proceeds from IPO is being used towards capacity expansion of Steriport and SVP brands.The expanded capacity is expected to commence from April 2026, leading to healthy growth in medium term. The business risk profile remains healthy with stable operating margin of 21-22%. 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position of AHL in parenteral business, the large distribution network, wide product profile and sound operating efficiency. The ratings also factor in extensive experience of AHL promoters in the parenteral business. These strengths are partially offset by modest, albeit improving, financial risk profile, exposure to intense competition and susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in prices of packing material. 

### **<u>Analytical approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of AHL. 

### **<u>Key rating drivers - Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position and extensive experience of the promoters** : The promoters, members of the Patel family, have been engaged in the pharmaceutical business for over three decades and have a large network of nearly 320 distributors and a diversified profile of 41 products. Their strong understanding of industry dynamics has helped AHL become one of the leading players in the parenteral business, supported by strong brand, Steriport, and presence in the domestic and international markets such as UK, Sudan, Phillippinesm Thailand, Cambodia, Zimbabwe and African countries. Revenue of Rs 272 crore was recorded in fiscal 2025, supported by increase in volume and realisations in the large-volume parenteral and Steriport segments. Revenue is expected to grow further by 8-10% in fiscal 2026, led by increasing contribution from high value-added products, primarily Steriport. With planned capacity expansion from April 2026 in the Steriport division, high contributions from exports should drive healthy growth in sales over the medium term. 

**Healthy operating profitability:** Apart from increase in share of high-margin products, other factors such as cost optimisation have helped improve profitability. While the operating margin was around 21.9% in fiscal 2025, it improved to around 22% in the first six months of fiscal 2026. Growth in realisation, exports and increase in capacity of Steriport should further improve the operating margin .Despite increase in cost of plastic granules, which are linked to crude oil, AHL has been able to maintain its operating margin at above 20%. 

**Improvement in the financial risk profile supported by fund raising from IPO and debt refinancing** : The financial risk profile has improved in fiscal 2026, aided by equity infusion raised through IPO of around Rs 126 crore, which led to increase in networth from Rs 96 crore as on March 31, 2025, to Rs 211 crore as on September 30, 2025. Gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio are expected to improve in fiscal 2026 with the equity infusion. Gearing and TOLANW improved to 0.84 time and 1.32 times, respectively, as on September 30, 2025, from 2.02 times and 2.99 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. 

# **<u>Key rating drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderately high working capital cycle:** The operations are working capital intensive with gross current assets (GCAs) of 170-180 days, driven by receivables of 60-65 days and inventory of 90-110 days. The working capital requirement also arises from high finished goods inventory as the company has more than 50 stock keeping units. 

**Exposure to intense competition and volatility in prices of packing material:** AHL draws 55-60% of its revenue from the high-volume and low-value parenteral division. The company faces intense competition both in the domestic and overseas markets and has low bargaining power with its customers. However, focus on high-value specialty products such as Steriport, whose contribution to revenue has increased over the years, has led to high negotiating power, and the scope to build brand equity. Imported plastic granules are the primary raw material for AHL. As plastic granules are derived from crude oil, fluctuation in prices of crude oil could weaken profitability, as passing on the cost may take some time though industry has always been able to pass on the cost hike to their dealers. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** <u>Adequate</u> 

Bank limit utilisation was low at 58.37% on average for the 11 months ended October 31, 2025. Annual cash accrual is expected at Rs 40-45 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 21-22 crore over the medium term and will cushion liquidity. 

The current ratio was moderate at 1.23 times as on March 31, 2025. The working capital requirements due to expansion can be met from additional internal accruals and thereby not affecting liquidity comfort. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** <u>Stable</u> 

Crisil Ratings believes AHL will maintain its established position in the parenteral segment, aided by sustained growth in revenue and healthy operating margin. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

Sustained revenue growth and operating margin, leading to net cash accrual above Rs 50 crore Reduction in the borrowing cost, leading to improvement in net cash accrual and financial risk profile 

# **Downward Factors** 

Sluggish revenue growth and significant decline in operating margin, leading to low cash accrual 

Any larger-than-expected debt-funded acquisition or capital expenditure or stretched working capital cycle, weakening the debt protection metrics and leading to TOLANW ratio above 2.5 times. 

# **<u>About the company</u>** 

AHL is a listed company originally promoted by Rohit J Patel, Bhavesh G Patel and other family members in 1995. The product portfolio comprises both large volume parenteral (LVP) and small volume parenteral (SVP) for fluid therapy, formulations, antibiotic, anti-fungal, ophthalmic, diuretics and other injectables. As on March 31, 2024, AHL had an installed capacity of 675 lakh bottles (LVP) and 2,400 lakh bottles (SVP) per annum, 849 lakh bottles of Steriport at it ISO-certified and cGMP compliant manufacturing facilities at Kheda, Gujarat.
