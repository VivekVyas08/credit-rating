### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Stable/Crisil A1+' ratings on the bank facilities of Bajaj Electricals Limited (BEL). Crisil Ratings has also **withdrawn** its rating on Rs 600 crore of proposed non-fund-based facilities at the company’s request on account of the company’s decision to optimize its limits by Rs.600 crores. This is in line with Crisil Ratings’ withdrawal policy. 

The consolidated revenue declined by 9.6% to Rs.3223 crores in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 as early on-set and prolonged monsoon impacted the sale of seasonal products in H1-fiscal 2026. Further, the company focused on inventory rationalization which impacted sales in Q3-fiscal 2026, however, the same is expected to improve company’s channel health and working capital management going forward. Despite the challenges, BEL has managed to maintain the market share in small appliances segment. Revenue growth is expected to recover next fiscal after 8-10% decline in fiscal 2026 with revival of demand in consumer products, along with price hikes. Lighting segment to show a healthy volume and realisation growth along with new product launches to support revenue. Rural demand expected to pick up post good rain. Urban demand is also expected to improve with high liquidity in the hands of consumers with increased discretionary spending. 

Operating profitability is expected to decline in fiscal 2026 mainly on account of lower sales resulting in operating deleverage and inventory rationalization. In 9m-fiscal 2026, EBIDTA margins declined to 3.4% compared to 6.4% in fiscal 2025. Operating profitability is expected to improve in the medium term, driven by cost and process optimisation through Value Addition Value Engineering (VAVE) initiatives, reduction in logistics costs, price hikes and operating leverage. BEL is working at product design level to reduce the cost of manufacturing by benchmarking its products against that of the competitors and reduce extra costs without compromising on quality. 

It launched 250-300 SKUs in fiscal 2025 and 140-150 SKUs in fiscal 2026 with different design, capacity, colour and features across the product segments. Focus is on better brand positioning to raise the brand’s recall on income pyramid by premium offering, while continuing the long-established value and mass market brand. BEL has also entered into the switchgears, solar solutions, and wires business in the lighting segment. 

The financial risk profile continues to remain strong with nil debt, healthy debt protection metrics and comfortable liquidity. The interest coverage ratio is expected to remain at 5-6 times over the medium term owing to steady accretion to profits compared with 5.1 times in fiscal 2025. Liquidity should remain strong with cash balance of Rs 602 as on Dec 31, 2025, and largely unutilized fund-based limit of Rs 150 crore. 

The ratings continue to reflect the healthy business risk profile of BEL, driven by its leading market position in few categories in the consumer electronics and durables industry in India; diversified product range; and improving financial risk profile, including strong liquidity. The company will continue to benefit from the financial flexibility derived from being a part of the Bajaj group. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to volatility in commodity prices and intense competition. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has changed the analytical approach from standalone and considered the consolidated the business and financial risk profile of BEL. The subsidiary Nirlep Appliances Pvt Ltd was merged into the company in fiscal 2024. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Established presence in the consumer appliances and lighting segments** 

BEL has leadership position in the consumer electronics industry. Apart from being the top player (in terms of sales volume) in the mixer grinder, the company is also among the top 2-3 players in other appliances, water heaters, coolers and dry iron segments and among the top five in fans and lighting categories. It has a vast network of 650-700 distributors and over two lakh retail outlets across India. 

The company derives strength from its keen focus on research and development, resulting from consistent investment in people and infrastructure and sound product development capabilities. With continued focus on strengthening the product offerings, especially in the appliances and fan categories, the company is expected to register stable growth in the consumer appliances and lighting segments over the medium term. 

As part of its strategy for lighting segment, the Company initially entered the switchgear segment in Q2, followed by foray into solar solutions in Q3, and has now launched wires in Q4-fiscal 2026. This is part of company’s focus to build an integrated portfolio, leveraging brand strength, distribution reach to drive growth over medium term. 

# **Diversified offerings in the consumer products segment** 

BEL operates across household appliances with consumer products constituting 75% of revenue and lighting segment contributing the remaining 25%. The company is focusing on better brand positioning and recall value associated with different brand products to meet the needs of different market segments. The consumer product segment includes fans, air coolers, mixers, eater heaters, irons and other kitchen and grooming appliances. 

Within the consumer products segment, the company offers kitchen appliances such as mixers, juicers and sandwich makers; and home appliances such as fans, air coolers, water heaters and irons. In the lighting segment, it is present in the light-emitting diode and lamps segments. Majority of these products are sold under the Bajaj brand. The company will increase its presence in the premium range through its Morphy Richards brand, which is witnessing healthy growth. The company has also entered into premium fans through their newly launched Nex brand. It sells cookware through the Nirlep brand. Introduction of switchgears, solar solutions, and wires in the lighting segment in fiscal 2026 expected to bolster growth. 

# **Strong financial risk profile** 

The financial risk profile is supported by the absence of debt, healthy networth and strong liquidity. capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 30-60 crores over the medium term will be funded by cash accrual. Comfortable gearing provides headroom for modest acquisition of Morphy Richards without any material impact on financial risk profile and key debt protection metrics. Any significant debt-funded capex or sizeable acquisition will be closely monitored. 

BEL derives financial flexibility from being a part of the Bajaj group. The company has received intercorporate deposits from Jamnalal Sons Pvt Ltd (key holding company of the group) in the past and through rights issue of Rs 350 crore subscribed by existing shareholders in March 2020. With improvement in the financial risk profile and nil debt, BEL may not require additional support from group entities. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Susceptibility to volatility in commodity prices and increasing competition** 

Prices of key inputs such as copper and aluminium are highly volatile. Raw materials and purchases of traded goods account for around 70% of the cost of sales. Furthermore, in order to counter competition, BEL needs to absorb part of the increase in input prices or pass it on with a lag, which constrains profitability. However, to mitigate this risk, the company has been rationalising its cost structure by adopting an asset-light production model and achieving higher economies of scale which is expected to improve gross margins by 100-150 bps in fiscal 2026. 

# **Exposure to intense competition in the domestic consumer durables sector** 

Competition in the consumer durable sector in India has intensified over the past few years with several players establishing a strong consumer connect and brand recall. BEL faces competition from players in the organised and unorganised segments, and cheap imports from China limiting its pricing power. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Cash balance was healthy at Rs 602 crore as on Dec 31, 2025. Annual cash accrual, projected at Rs 150-200 crore, should comfortably fund the capex and working capital requirement over the medium term. Fund-based limit of Rs 150 crore remained unutilised for the 12 months through Jan 2026. The company became net term debt-free in fiscal 2022 and is expected to have minimal dependence on external debt over the medium term as well. Access to need-based funding support from the Bajaj group also enhances fund-raising ability. 

# **<u>Environment, social and governance profile</u>** 

The environment, social and governance (ESG) profile of BEL supports its strong credit risk profile. 

The durables and electrical sector has a moderate impact on the environment and the society, led by its raw material sourcing strategies, waste-intensive processes and direct impact on the health and wellbeing of customers. 

**Key ESG highlights** 

- BEL has increased its renewable energy capacity, with 809KWp solar generation system which ensures effective energy conservation 

In fiscal 2025. 98% of waste recovered through recycling, reusing or other recovery operations. 

- In fiscal 2025, the share of female employees (permanent and other than permanent) increased to 8% from previous year’s ~7% (at standalone level). 

- On the safety front, BEL’s lost time injury frequency rate (LTIFR) for employees was zero in fiscal 2025 as compared to 0.34x 3 years back (reported at standalone level). The company also conducted around 285 trainings for safety and other EHS. 

- The company’s governance structure is characterized by ~50% of its board being independent directors, ~20% women board directors, split in positions of Chairperson and Managing Director on the board, and extensive financial disclosures. 

ESG is gaining importance among investors and lenders. The commitment of the company to ESG will play a key role in enhancing stakeholder confidence, given shareholding by foreign portfolio investors and access to both domestic and foreign capital markets. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

BEL will continue to benefit from its established market position across product categories and established brand position. The financial risk profile should remain strong, driven by nil long-term debt, high financial flexibility and moderate capex. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Sustained revenue growth driven by market leadership across  product segments, better product diversity and increase in market share 

- Improvement in profitability with earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation margin of 8-10% on a sustained basis 

- Sustenance of comfortable financial risk profile, including debt protection metrics; improvement in the working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors** 

- Weakness in operating performance with significant de-growth in revenue and, decline in market share in key product segments 

- Moderation in operating profitability to below 6% on a sustained basis 

- Sizeable debt-funded capex or acquisition, leading to total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio breaching above 2.0 times on a sustained basis or significant weakening of liquidity 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

BEL is an established player in the consumer electronics and durables industry and is a part of the Bajaj group led by Shekhar Bajaj. 

The company was incorporated in 1938 as Radio Lamp Works and renamed BEL in 1960. It was formed as a marketing arm for consumer durables but has now diversified into engineering projects to parlay its presence in the lighting segment to capitalise on the growing infrastructure spend in India. The engineering procurement and construction division has been demerged into a separate entity, Bajel Projects Ltd, in fiscal 2024. 

BEL sells premium range of appliances under the Morphy Richards and Nex brands. The company sells majority of its cookware under the Nirlep brand.
