### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its 'Crisil BBB+/Positive/Crisil A2’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Gala Precision Engineering Limited (GPEL) 

The ratings continue to reflect GPEL’s established market position in the springs and fastener industry, the diverse product line, healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by large working capital requirements and exposure to cyclicality in end-user industries. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of GPEL and its subsidiaries i.e Gala Springs LLP, China-based wholly owned subsidiary, Gala Precision Component Shanghai Ltd, which is strategically important to and has significant operational integration with GPEL. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position in the springs and fastener industry:** The promoters experience of over two decades in the engineering and automotive industry has helped establish strong relationships with customers leading to repeat orders. The company has an established customer base and has added clients across various industries in both domestic and overseas markets. This has led to revenue growth to Rs 238 crore in Fiscal 2025 from Rs.106 crore in fiscal 2021. Revenue is further expected to increase over the medium term driven by healthy demand, continuous addition of new SKUs, customers and increase in manufacturing capacity. 

**Diversified product profile and end-user industry:** GPEL has a diversified portfolio comprising disc & strip spring & assemblies (including Gallock washers) (DSS), special fastening solutions (SFS) and coil and spring systems (CSS) comprising of an extensive portfolio of around **750+ SKUs.** DSS, SFS and CSS products contribute around to around 49%, 32% and 19% to revenues in fiscal 2025. The company products caters to various industries such as automotive, power, renewable energy, industrial and railways. This protects revenues from slowdown in demand in a single industry. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** With continuous accretion to reserves and recent IPO the financial risk profile is healthy with networth of Rs. 233 crores as on March 31, 2025. With moderate reliance on external debt, capital structure has been comfortable with gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratios of 0.1 and 0.27 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. Despite capex plans (will be funded through IPO funds) the capital structure is expected to remain healthy at similar levels over the medium term backed by healthy accretion to reserve.  Debt protection metrics is healthy with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 13.45 and 1.51 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025.  Financial risk profile is expected to remain healthy with healthy accretion to reserve and low reliance on external debt. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Large working capital requirement:** The company has a large working capital requirement, as operations are working capital intensive. This is reflected in the gross current assets (GCAs) of 229 days as of March 31, 2025, driven by receivables and inventory of 113 days (which improved to 85 days as of September 30, 2025) and 135 days, respectively. The large inventory is due to stocking of raw material for higher variety of products (750+ SKUs), high lead time in raw material procurement, moderate manufacturing cycle and future quarters dispatch, while the company provides a moderate credit period to its customers. The working capital cycle is expected to remain in a similar range over the medium term due to business requirements. 

**Exposure to cyclicality in end-user industries** : The demand for GPEL products is correlated with the end-user industries i.e Industrial development, Renewables and Mobility (Automotive & Railways) industries. The performance of these industries are driven by factors such as economic policies, demand and supply situation, prices of raw materials. Any downturn in any of the application industries or macro-economic conditions impacting the application industries will impact the company topline and its profitability. However, over the years the company is continuously diversifying its products and improving its application industries. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

GPEL has adequate liquidity, as reflected in moderate bank limit utilisation, averaging 53% for the 12 months through June 2025. Expected cash accrual of Rs 40-50 crore per annum will suffice to cover the yearly debt obligation of Rs. 0.3-0.4 crore over the medium term. Cash and bank balance including FDs stood at around Rs 49 crore as on September 30, 2025. Crisil Ratings expects internal accrual and unutilized bank lines to suffice to cover working capital requirements over medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Positive 

Crisil Ratings believes that the company’s credit profile will continue to improve over the medium term, backed by increasing revenues, established market position and comfortable financial risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating Sensitivity Factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Improvement in scale of operations while sustaining operating margins above 17%, leading to higher cash accruals above Rs.40 crores. 

Sustenance of financial risk profile while improving working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue or profitability, leading to net cash accrual of less than Rs 25 crore Stretched working capital cycle or debt-funded capital expenditure, impacting liquidity. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

Incorporated in 2009 by Mr Kirit Gala, GPEL manufactures disc and strip springs as well as coil and spiral springs. The company also provides special fastening solutions. Its manufacturing facilities are in Wada, Maharashtra. GPEL was listed on NSE and BSE on 9<sup>th</sup> September 2024 

Gala Precision Component Shanghai Ltd market the products of GPEL in China. However currently GEPL has initiated winding-up proceedings for Gala Precision Component Shanghai Ltd and is selling its products to a local Distributor in Shanghai directly.
