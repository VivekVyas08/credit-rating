### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has downgraded it ratings on the bank loan facilities and commercial paper of Shankara Building Products Limited (SBPL; part of Shankara Building Products group) to ' **Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+'** from 'Crisil BBB+/Crisil A2' while removed the long term rating from 'Rating Watch with Developing Implications' and assigned a **'Stable** ' outlook. Crisil Ratings has also **withdrawn** it’s rating on Rs. 60 crore commercial paper based on the withdrawal request from the management as the issue was not yet placed. The withdrawal is in line with Crisil Ratings' withdrawal Policy. 

The rating action follows the completion of demerger of the marketplace division (trading business) of the group into a newly formed entity – Shankara Buildpro Limited (SBL; rated at 'Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+') with the receipt of necessary approvals and the demerger took effect from September 26, 2025. Management has provided all the necessary clarifications including restated financials for SBPL and SBL. 

The business risk profile of Shankara Building Products group has deteriorated in fiscal 2025, following the hiving off of the relatively stronger marketplace division into SBL. This has resulted in a significant decline in revenues to Rs. 1362 Crore from Rs. 4850 Crore in fiscal 2024, and operating margins have also contracted, decreasing to 1.44% in fiscal 2025 from 3.12% in fiscal 2024. The Return on Capital Employed (RoCE) has also declined substantially to 2.52% from 17.55% during the same period. The financial risk profile has also weakened, with debt protection metrics declining to 1.33 times from 4.75 times for interest coverage, and Net Cash Accruals to Adjusted Debt (NCA/AD) ratio falling to 0.17 times from 1.09 times in fiscal 2024. 

The ratings continue to factor in SBPL’s established market position and extensive experience of the promoter in the building material distribution and retail businesses and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to economic cycles and sharp variation in raw material prices. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the financial and business risk profiles of SBPL and its wholly owned subsidiaries, Taurus Value Steel & Pipes Private Limited (Taurus), Vishal Precision Steel Tubes & Strips Private Limited (Vishal), and Centurywells Roofing India Private Limited (Centurywells). This is because all these entities, collectively referred to as the Shankara Building Products group, have common management and strong operational and financial linkages.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Established market position and extensive experience of the promoter:** 

The group's longstanding presence of over three decades in the building materials industry, its wide network of dealers, over 94 retail outlets and the in-house pipe and color-coated roofing sheet processing capacity, will continue to support the business risk profile. The main promoter has around three decades of experience in the building materials industry and succession plan is already in place. Also, promoters are assisted by a competent second line of management. 

### **Comfortable financial risk profile:** 

Networth is strong at Rs 397 crore and total outside liabilities to tangible net worth ratio is comfortable at 0.41 time as on March 31, 2025. Adequate debt protection metrics marked by interest coverage of 1.33 times and net cash accruals to adjusted debt ratio of 0.17 time for fiscal 2025. Financial risk profile is expected to improve over the medium term supported by steady accretion to reserves and gradual reduction in debt levels. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Susceptibility of demand to economic cycles:** 

The group remains exposed to fluctuation in demand for real estate and home improvement. The group’s revenues were impacted in the past fiscals on account of Covid-related disruptions. Nevertheless, turnover improved steadily, aided by improvement in construction activity resulting in improved demand. 

# **Exposure to fluctuations in input prices:** 

As with any retail business, operating margin remains modest. The operating profitability has been moderately volatile and minimal at 1.44 % for fiscal 2025 led by prices as well as intense competition. In H1FY2026, the operating profitability was further low at 0.96% owing to certain one-time expenses related to the demerger. The sustenance of operating profitability above 2% will remain as the key monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation is at around 41 percent for the past twelve months ended September 2025. The group mainly depends on nonfund-based facilities for its working capital needs. Cash accruals were to be over Rs 20 crore against repayment obligation of Rs 2-4 crore over the medium term. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes that the credit profile of Shanakara Building Products group will supported by the extensive industry experience of the promoters as well as the healthy financial risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained revenue growth while maintaining operating profitability above 3% resulting in higher-than-expected cash accruals Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and working capital management to remain efficient 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenues by more than 30% or operating margin declining below 1.5% 

Increase in working capital requirement, or larger-than-expected, debt-funded capex or acquisition, or more-than-expected dividend pay-out, weakening the financial risk profile, particularly liquidity 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

SBPL Incorporated in 1995 as Shankara Pipes India Pvt Ltd, the company was renamed as Shankara Infrastructure Materials Ltd in 2011, and thereafter, as SBPL in 2016. Further, the group operates in-house pipe and colour-coated roofing sheet processing facilities through wholly owned subsidiaries: Taurus, Vishal, and Centurywells. SBPL got listed on BSE/NSE in 2017 and promoters hold 40.18% stake in the company as on September 30, 2025
