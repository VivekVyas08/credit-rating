### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘ **Crisil BBB/Stable** / **Crisil A3+** ’ ratings to the bank loan facilities of Primo Chemicals Limited (PCL). 

The rating reflects the company’s established market position in the chlor-alkali segment, integrated operations, and healthy financial risk profile supported by low leverage and strong cash accruals. The rating also factors in cost optimisation initiatives and improving operating efficiency. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicality in the chemical industry and volatility in operating margins due to power-intensive nature of operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the financials of PCL with its associate, Flow Tech Chemicals Private Limited. Proportionate consolidation has been undertaken for associate based on the extent of profit sharing, reflecting strong operational and financial linkages within the group 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position:** PCL has been operational since 1975 and is an established player in the chlor-alkali segment, with manufacturing capabilities for a wide range of products such as caustic soda lye, caustic soda flakes, hydrochloric acid, chlorine derivatives, and aluminium chloride. 

The company’s diversified product mix reduces dependency on a single product segment, with caustic soda lye and flakes contributing to ~63% and ~30% of revenue, respectively. Other products contribute smaller but stable streams. 

**Healthy scale of operations with stable growth outlook:** The company has demonstrated stable scale of operations, with operating income of Rs 575 crore in fiscal 2026 and is expected to grow at a moderate pace (~7% CAGR) over the medium term supported by volume growth and expansion into higher value-added products. 

Stable demand from downstream industries such as textiles, chemicals, paper, and water treatment supports revenue visibility. 

- **Integrated operations and cost optimization initiative:** The company benefits from integrated manufacturing operations, wherein chlorine generated in the process is further utilised for production of downstream products (HCl, SBP), improving efficiency and reducing wastage. Additionally, the company has undertaken multiple cost optimization initiatives, including Expansion of caustic soda flakes capacity, Investment in captive and renewable power (solar project), Coal-based captive power generation among others. These measures are expected to reduce power costs significantly and improve operating efficiency over the medium term. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to cyclicality in the chemical industry:** cyclical, with product prices driven by global supply-demand dynamics, input costs, and economic conditions. The company witnessed significant margin volatility, with elevated 

margins in FY23 followed by sharp correction in FY24 due to increased global supply and normalization of prices. 

**Susceptibility of operating margin to adverse movements in power cost:** The chlor-alkali manufacturing process is highly power-intensive, with electricity constituting a major component of production cost. Accordingly, operating margin of PCL is susceptible to any adverse movements in power cost. However, this risk is partly mitigated by a captive power plant of 35 megawatt (MW) and company is also in process of tie-up for power purchase agreement for solar power effectively reducing per unit cost. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Liquidity is adequate, supported by expected net cash accruals of Rs 68 crore in fiscal 2026 against debt obligations of Rs 28 crore in fiscal 2026. Moreover, the company has a moderate average bank limit utilization of 80%. 

Current ratio was low at 0.73 times on March 31, 2026. Low gearing and moderate net worth support it’s financial flexibility and provides the financial cushion available in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the company will continue to benefit from its established market position, strong profitability, and steady demand from dealers and end-user industries, while maintaining healthy cash accruals and comfortable financial risk profile over the medium term. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward Factors** 

Sustained improvement in scale of operation by 20% and sustenance of operating margin, leading to higher cash accruals of over Rs 70 crore. 

Improvement in working capital cycle. 

# **Downward Factors** 

Decline in operating profitability below 12% or decline in scale of operations leading to lower net cash accruals. Large debt-funded capital expenditure weakens capital structure. 

Witnesses a substantial increase in its working capital requirements thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

The Company was incorporated as a Public Limited Company on 1st December,1975 in the name Punjab Alkalies Limited under the Companies Act,1956. It was initially promoted by the Punjab Industrial Development Corporation Limited (PSIDC). The name of the Company was subsequently changed to Punjab Alkalies & Chemicals Limited and a Fresh Certificate of Incorporation consequent on change of name dated 19th April 1983 was obtained from the Registrar of Companies, Punjab, HP & Chandigarh, Jalandhar. 

During the year 2020, the PSIDC sold its entire equity to Private Sector. As on date, the Government has no equity holding and entire equity is held by promoters and public. 

The name of the Company has been changed from “Punjab Alkalies & Chemicals Limited” to “Primo Chemicals Limited” w.e.f. 19/12/2022 **.** The company’s registered office is situated at S.C.O. 125-127, Sector 17-B, Chandigarh-160017. 

Company is engaged in the manufacturing of caustic soda, chlorine, and related chemical products. The company operates a manufacturing facility in Naya Nangal, Punjab, with an installed capacity of ~500 tonnes per day. 

PCL’s equity shares are listed on BSE Limited (BSE) and National Stock Exchange of India Ltd (NSE). FTCPL is engaged in manufacturing Chlorinated Paraffin and Hydrochloric Acid.
