CRISIL has suspended its ratings on the bank facilities of Lokesh Machines Ltd (LML). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by LML with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, LML is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess LML's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings'. 



### **_About the Company_** 

Incorporated in 1983 and promoted by Mr. M Lokeshwara Rao, LML commenced commercial production in 1986. The company is engaged in developing, designing, and fabricating various capital goods, such as special purpose and general purpose machines, primarily used in the automotive and automotive component sector.
