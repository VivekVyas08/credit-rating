### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB/Stable’ rating on the long-term bank facilities of Wonder Electricals Ltd (WEL; part of the Wonder group). 

The rating reflects the group’s established presence in the ceiling and exhaust fan segment. Operating income is expected to decline by 15-20% in fiscal 2026 (Rs 895 crore in fiscal 2025) owing to early onset of monsoon leading to moderation in demand. The group has achieved revenue of Rs 250 crore in the first six months of fiscal 2026 against Rs 361 crore for the corresponding period fiscal 2025. Operating margin has been at 4.3-4.4% over the three fiscals ended 2025 (4.35% in fiscal 2025) and is expected to remain along similar lines in fiscal 2026. Increase in revenue along with sustenance of operating margin at 4-4.2% will remain monitorable. 

The rating also factors the leveraged capital structure of the group with expected total outside liabilities to tangible networth (TOL/TNW) ratio of 3.2-3.3 times as on March 31, 2026 (3.93 times a year earlier) driven by high payables at year-end due to seasonality in business. Networth is expected around Rs 90 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 83 crore a year earlier) driven by healthy accretion to reserve. 

The rating continues to reflect the group’s established presence in the ceiling and exhaust fans segment— backed by the extensive experience of its promoters—and average financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by intense competition in the consumer durables sector in India and working capital-intensive operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Previously, Crisil Ratings had evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of WEL. 

Now, Crisil Ratings is consolidating the business and financial risk profiles of WEL with the joint venture, Integrated Motion & Control LLP (formed in April 2025) as the same is 51% held by WEL and will have financial and operational linkages. 

Unsecured loans of Rs 5.3 crore from the promoters as on March 31, 2025 have been treated as debt.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

Established presence in the ceiling and exhaust fans segment, backed by extensive experience of the promoters: WEL is an original equipment manufacturer of ceiling, exhaust, pedestal, and brushless direct current (BLDC) fans for reputed customers. The promoters, Mr Harsh Anand, Mr Yogesh Anand and Mr Yogesh Sahni, have experience of over four decades in the industry and are actively engaged in the day-to-day operations of the company. The extensive industry experience of the promoters has enabled longstanding business association with reputable clients from whom repeat orders are received. This has also enabled the company to add new customers ultimately supporting the growth in scale of operations over the years. 

### **Average financial risk profile** 

Networth is expected around Rs 90 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 83 crore a year earlier) driven by healthy accretion to reserve. Interest coverage ratio is expected to remain around 3.3 times in fiscal 2026 (5 times in fiscal 2025), owing to expected decline in the scale of operations. TOL/TNW is expected to be 3.2-3.3 times as on March 31, 2026 (3.93 times a year earlier) driven by high payables at year-end due to seasonality in business.  Improvement in the capital structure of the group will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Intense competition in the consumer durables sector in India** : With the entry of several large players over the past few years, there has been a significant increase in price competition, which has adversely affected the operating profitability of most players. Additionally, raw material price fluctuations accentuate the pressure on profitability. Therefore, profitability will remain a challenge for most players in the industry on account of intense competition and consolidation witnessed across large consumer durables players in the domestic market. 

**Working capital-intensive operations:** Operations are working capital intensive but have improved as indicated by gross current assets (GCAs) of 142 days as on March 31, 2025 compared to 190 days a year earlier. The same has improved on account of improvement in receivables to 114 days from 145 days and inventory to 25 days from 42 days. The group’s large working capital requirement arises from its high receivables due to higher sales at year-end. The group is required to provide extended credit to its customers. Payables of 90-100 days will support working capital in the near-tomedium term. GCAs are expected to remain 160-170 days in the near term and any improvement therein will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** <u>Adequate</u> 

Bank limit utilisation averaged a moderate 65% for the 12 months ended July 2025. Annual net cash accrual is expected at Rs 15-19 crore against yearly debt obligation of Rs 2-3 crore. The current ratio was healthy at 1.19 times as on March 31, 2025. Unsecured loans from promoters will continue to support the liquidity profile of the group. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes the Wonder group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters, and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating Sensitivity Factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained increase in revenue, with operating margin of 4.0-4.2%, leading to higher net cash accrual Sustenance of the working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in net cash accrual to below Rs 10 crore due to fall in revenue and/or operating margin 

Large, debt-funded capital expenditure weakening the capital structure and/or substantial increase in the working capital requirement weakening the liquidity and the financial risk profiles. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

WEL (earlier known as Wonder Fibromats Ltd) manufactures ceiling fans, exhaust, pedestal, and BLDC fans. Incorporated in 2009 as a private limited company, it was reconstituted as a limited company in July 2018. And with a revision in its name to the present one, the company was also migrated to the main board of the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) in 2022. 

WEL acquired the business of its group concern Uttaranchal Industries, effective November 2023. UTI was a partnership firm of the promoters of WEL. It has manufacturing plants in Roorkee, Hyderabad and Haridwar. It is promoted by Mr Harsh Anand, Mr Yogesh Anand, Mr Yogesh Sahni and their family members.
