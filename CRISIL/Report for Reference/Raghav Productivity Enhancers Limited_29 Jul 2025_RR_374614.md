### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Raghav Productivity Enhancers Ltd (RPEL; a part of the Raghav group)) to **‘Positive’** from ‘Stable’ and reaffirmed the rating at **‘Crisil A-’.** 

The revision in outlook reflects Crisil’s belief that the business risk profile of the group will continue to be benefitted by its strong market position in the ramming mass industry, with presence in both domestic and export market, thereby aiding the revenue growth over the medium term, Furthermore, operating profitability is likely to remain strong too, estimated to sustain at 27-30% over the medium term. Revenue, though modest, improved to Rs. 200 crores in fiscal 2025 (Rs 133 crore during fiscal 2024), driven by improved realization and volumetric growth of 37%, on account of the addition of new customers and superior quality of product offered by the group. Deeper penetration into export market amidst its improving demand, repeat order flow from existing customers and addition of new customers etc. are likely to further aid the revenue growth of 15-20% (on year) during fiscal 2026. That said, sustenance of revenue growth amidst steady realization to remain a key monitorable. 

Operating profitability, on the other hand, though moderated in fiscal 2025 to an estimated at 27% (~30% during previous fiscal) amidst moderation in freight cost, which is fully passed on, continues to remain strong. This, along with low dependence on external debt further resulted in improved return on capital employed (ROCE) at ~26% during fiscal 2025 (~23% a year ago). Going forward, Crisil Ratings believes that the group will continue to benefit from the increase in capacity, and increased demand for induction furnaces in the steel industry owing to low carbon emissions and precise temperature control, and the same shall continue to support the business risk profile. 

The rating also factors in the company’s strong financial risk profile, driven by sizeable net worth and limited reliance on external debt. Also, steady operating profitability has resulted in the continuation of healthy debt protection indicators, which shall continue over the medium term as well. Liquidity, on the other hand, remains healthy too, backed by sizeable net cash accruals against maturing debt, unutilized bank lines and excess reserves available to encounter exigencies (if any). 

The rating continues to reflect established market position, high operating efficiency and healthy financial risk profile of the group. These strengths are partially offset by modest scale of operations and exposure to intense competition and limited revenue diversity 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of RPEL and its wholly owned subsidiary, Raghav Productivity Solutions Pvt Ltd (RPSPL). This is because both the companies, together referred to as the Raghav group, have common management and significant business synergies.

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Established market position and customer base:** Despite intense competition, the group has established its position pan-India as a market leader in the silica ramming mass manufacturing segment, with superior product quality resulting in higher-than-average realizations. Longstanding experienceof the promoters, their strong understanding of market dynamics and healthy relationships with customers and suppliers will continue to support the business risk profile. Clientele is also well diversified, with the top five customers contributing only around 20% to the operating income in fiscal 2025, similar to fiscal 2024 levels. Further, with increase in capacities the group witnessed volumetric growth of 37% in fiscal 2025, on account of the addition of new customers and superior quality of product offered by the group. Regular demand from existing customers along with the addition of new clients in the export market may result in volumetric growth of 15-17% in fiscal 2026. 

**Healthy operating efficiency** Return on capital employed was comfortable at 26% in fiscal 2025 and is projected at a similar level in fiscal 2026 as well owing to high economies of scale. Though the operating profitability moderated to 27% in fiscal 2025 (30% a year ago), owing to moderation in freight cost, the same continues to remain strong, led by unique product offerings, higher price realizations and better margins available in the export market, which contributes 30-32% to total revenue. With this, operating profitability is expected at 27-29% in fiscal 2026. Thus, the business risk profile shall continue to remain supported by steady operating efficiency. 

**Strong financial risk profile** The financial risk profile should remain supported by healthy accretion to reserve, limited reliance on external debt and the absence of any large, debt-funded capex. The capital structure has been comfortable, as reflected in gearing of 0.03 time and net worth of Rs 191 crore as on March 31, 2025. Gearing is expected at 0.02-0.03 time as on March 31, 2026, providing headroom to take additional debt for business requirements. Debt protection metrics may continue to be robust, with interest coverage ratio of 65.67 times and net cash accrual to an 

adjusted debt ratio of 7.26 times for fiscal 2025. 

# **Weaknesses:** 

- **Modest scale of operations** Despite reporting a CAGR of 26% for the three fiscals through 2025, revenue has been Rs 200 crore in fiscal 2025. Going forward, Crisil Rating believes that the group will continue to benefit from the increase in capacity, and increased demand for induction furnaces in the steel industry owing to low carbon emissions and precise temperature control. Deeper penetration into the export market amidst its improving demand, repeat order flow from existing customers and addition of new customers etc. are likely to further aid the revenue growth of 1520% (on year) during fiscal 2026. and the same shall remain a key rating sensitivity factor. 

- **Exposure to intense competition and limited revenue diversity:** The ramming mass industrycomprises numerous unorganized players, Hence, despite the high quality of products being offered, intense competition may continue to constrain scalability, pricing power and profitability. Further, as the group entirely derives revenue from silica ramming mass and related refactory products, the business remains exposed to concentration risk. However, healthy market position should mitigate these risks to an extent. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Fund-based bank limit of Rs 20 crore remained unutilized during the 12 months ending March 31, 2025. Further, in the absence of any yearly maturing debt over the medium term, cash accrual -- expected at Rs 45-50 crore per annum – will act as a cushion to liquidity. The current ratio was healthy at 5.39 times as on March 31, 2025, and is likely to be at a similar level on March 31, 2026, as well. Liquid investments worth Rs 34 crore as on March 31, 2025, also support liquidity. 

# **<u>Outlook: Positive</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes that the business risk profile will further improve, amidst volumetric growth. Increase capacities and ramp up of will remain a key monitorable. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained growth in revenue driven by volumetric growth of 20-25%, leading to higher than expected net cash accruals Sustenance of strong financial risk profile amidst efficient working capital management 

# **Downward factors:** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin dropping to 22%, leading to lower-than-expected net cash accrual Sizeable stretch in the working capital cycle or any large, debt-funded capex 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

RPEL (erstwhile, Raghav Ramming Mass Ltd), incorporated in 2009, manufactures ramming mass for induction furnaces and quartz powder. The company, located in Jaipur (Rajasthan), was listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange in April 2016. Its facility at Newai (Rajasthan) had capacity of 1,44,000 metric tonne per annum (MTPA) for ramming mass as on March 31, 2025. 

RPEL set up a manufacturing plant for silica ramming mass, which is a greenfield expansion of its existing plant at Newai, under its wholly owned subsidiary, RPSPL. The plant has manufacturing capacity of 2,70,000 MTPA, thereby increasing the combined capacity of the group to 4,14,000 MTPA. The subsidiary commenced operations in 2024.
