### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **'Crisil A1'** rating to the short-term bank facility of Paushak Ltd (Paushak) and reaffirmed its 'Crisil A/Stable’ rating on the long-term bank facilities of Paushak. 

The rating continues to factor the established market position in phosgene based chemicals, strong operating margins and a healthy financial risk profile of the company. Moreover, the ratings also factor in the strong support from Nirayu Limited (Nirayu, rated ‘Crisil AA+/Stable’), the holding company of the Alembic group which overall holds a 53% stake in the company, in form of clear intent to extend need-based financial support. These strengths are partially offset by the moderate scale of operations and susceptibility to fluctuations in input prices & demand sentiments. 

Revenues in FY25 remained flat at ~Rs. 211 crores, compared to Rs. 206 crores in FY24. The growth was restricted due to continued pricing pressures from Chinese competition, and a sub-optimal product mix. Revenues in H1FY26 also registered steady growth of 5% YoY to Rs. 115 crores. With delay in new plant commissioning, now expected to be completed by Q4, fiscal 2026, overall revenues for current fiscal expected to be flat on-year. Thereafter, the revenues are expected to grow in the medium term with the commissioning and gradual scaling up of the new facility. 

Operating margins moderated to 28.3% in FY25 from 31.3% in FY24 owing to the ongoing shift to a more semi-specialized product portfolio along with the sustained pricing pressures. Margins improved to ~28.5% in H1FY26 (vs 26.7% in H1FY25) owing a favorable product mix. Over the medium term, further expansion into semi-specialized products through new capacities along with overheads from new facilities will lead to slight moderation in margins but are expected to sustain at healthy levels of over 20%. 

The company is undertaking a capex of ~Rs 250 crore towards further increasing its downstream capacities, upgrading its R&D facilities, enhancing its utilities (steam and electricity) and safety standards and expand into semi-specialized products, expected to be commissioned by Q4, fiscal 2026. This capex has been funded through a mix of internal accruals and debt, with Rs. 60 crore of debt drawdown till September, 2025. 

Despite said debt, credit metrics are expected to remain comfortable over the medium term with gearing expected below of 0.15 times and interest coverage above 11 times over the medium term. Liquidity remains sufficient with expected annual cash accrual of Rs 40-50 crore and repayment obligations of ~Rs.15 crores and routine capex requirements of ~Rs. 15-20 crores annually over the medium term. Besides, company has access to fund-based working capital lines of Rs. 35 crores, which remain negligibility utilised for the six months ended September 30, 2025. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has applied its parent notch-up framework to factor in the expected need-based support from Nirayu. Moreover, Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of Paushak. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position in the speciality chemicals industry:** Paushak has an established market position, backed by its presence of over four decades in the phosgene-based intermediates segment. The company derives revenue from 

chloroformates, isocyanates, specialty chemicals, carbonates and phosgene gas. These products find application across industries, primarily pharmaceuticals and agro- chemicals. The company is one of the few players licenced to manufacture phosgene gas, which is highly restricted by the government. Paushak has established itself as the largest specialty and customized phosgene derivative producer in India, boasting a cutting-edge phosgene plant with a capacity of 14,400 MT per annum. 

**Strong operating efficiency:** Backward integration of operations has led to robust operating margins (28% in fiscal 2025 and 31% in fiscal 2024). Return on capital employed was healthy at 11% in fiscal 2025 (15.8% in fiscal 2024; lower due to high capex and lower profitability). Furthermore, the working capital cycle is moderate, as reflected in receivables and inventory of 95 and 52 days, respectively, as on March 31, 2025 (90 and 56 days respectively as on March 31, 2024). 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth has grown steadily, aided by accretion to reserves, and was healthy at Rs 480 crore as on September 30, 2025. The company is undertaking a capex of ~Rs 250 crore over fiscals 2025 and 2026 towards further increasing its downstream capacities, upgrading its R&D facilities, enhancing its utilities (steam and electricity) and safety standards and expand into semi-specialized products. The commissioning of this facility has been delayed by 5-6 months and is now expected to be completed by March 2026. The company has secured a sanctioned term loan of Rs. 100 crores out of which it has drawn down around Rs. 60 crores as of September 2025,. However, with a strong balance sheet, debt protection metrics are expected to remain strong over the medium term 

**Strong parentage and expected financial support from Nirayu:** Paushak benefits from the strong parentage and needbased support of Nirayu, which has healthy financial flexibility. This arises from Nirayu’s ability to raise additional funds on account of its’ holding of equity shares of Alembic Pharmaceuticals Ltd (Alembic Pharma; rated ‘Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’). As on December 29, 2025, the combined market value of Nirayu’s shares in Alembic Pharma, Alembic Ltd and Paushak was over Rs 11,200 crore. While Paushak’s management is largely independent, the company remains critical to Nirayu and underlying comfort support is expected to be forthcoming should it be required for capital expenditures or exigencies. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate scale of operations:** Despite being in existence for nearly five decades, the company operates on a moderate scale. Revenue growth has picked up in the past six years, driven by diversification of customer base and product portfolio. Apart from pharmaceuticals, the company now caters to other sectors such as agro-chemicals and performance-based materials. With downstream capacities running at optimal capacities currently, the company is expected to gradually improve its capacities over the medium term. Timely execution and commercialisation of any planned capex will be critical for a faster growth momentum. 

**Susceptibility to fluctuations in input prices and demand sentiments:** Paushak’s profitability, similar to other players in the specialty chemicals industry, remains susceptible to movement in the prices of key raw materials and end-user demand especially in the agrochemical and pharmaceutical industries. A dip in demand owing to destocking up the value chain, combined with some pricing corrections owing to macroeconomic headwinds led to margins moderating in fiscal 2024 and in fiscal 2025. The ability of the company to sustain its operating margins amidst the pricing volatility seen in the industry would be a key monitorable. However, a strong degree of backward integration and low fixed cost structure lends support. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Expected annual cash accrual of Rs 40-50 crore shall be adequate for annual repayment obligations of ~Rs.15 crores and routine capex requirements of ~Rs. 15-20 crores over the medium term. Liquidity is marked by cash equivalents of ~Rs 1 crore as on September 30, 2025, and average bank limit utilisation of 1% on fund-based lines of Rs. 35 crores for six months ended. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings expects Paushak will continue to benefit from its established market position, diverse product profile and strong operating efficiency. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

- Improvement in product mix and a strong revenue growth and operating profitability of 30%, resulting in higher cash accrual 

- Sustenance of robust financial risk profile and debt metrics, supported by prudent capex spend and working capital management 

- Improvement in credit risk profile or a stronger support stance from Nirayu 

# **Downward factors** 

Sustained decline in operating profitability to below 20% 

Time or cost overruns in capex, or large debt-funded capex or acquisition, weakening the key credit metrics Deterioration in Nirayu’s credit risk profile or an adverse change in its support stance towards Paushak 

**<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1972, Paushak is managed by Mr Chirayu Amin and his family members, promoters of Alembic Pharma. Paushak manufactures phosgene-based specialty chemicals, used in the pharmaceuticals, agro-chemicals and performance-enhancement industries. 

Paushak is listed on BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited. As on September 30, 2025, the promoters and their group entities held a 67.28% stake, and the balance is with the public. 

The company recorded Rs 115 crore of revenue and Rs 21 crore of profit after taxes in the first half of fiscal 2026, as against Rs 109 crore of revenue and Rs 24 crore of profit after taxes in the corresponding half last fiscal.
