CRISIL has suspended its rating on the bank facilities of Windlass Biotech Limited (WBL). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by WBL with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, WBL is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess WBL’s ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL views information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and views non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria “Information availability risk in Credit ratings". 

### **_About the Company_** 

WBL (erstwhile sick unit) was acquired by the Windlass family of Dehradun, Uttarakhand from Uttar Pradesh Financial Corporation (UPFC) in 2001. WBL contract manufactures pharmaceutical products. The company also manufactures around 25 products under its own brand. WBL was a loss-making entity till 2002-03. In 2005-06, the company’s topline grew by more than 170 per cent over the previous year because of revenues from its new customer, Mankind Pharma Ltd. WBL is part of the Windlass group, which has diversified business interests such as manufacture of authentic historical battle arms and armour, and period clothing, and is also present in the real estate and hospitality sectors.
