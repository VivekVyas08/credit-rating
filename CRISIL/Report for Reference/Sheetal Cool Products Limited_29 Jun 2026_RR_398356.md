### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Due to inadequate information and in line with the Securities and Exchange Board of India guidelines, Crisil Ratings had migrated its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Sheetal Cool Products Limited (SCPL) to ‘Crisil B/Stable Issuer Not Cooperating'. However, the company’s management has subsequently shared the information necessary for a comprehensive review of the rating. Consequently, Crisil Ratings is migrating the rating of SCPL to ‘ **Crisil BBB/Stable** ’ from ‘Crisil B/Stable Issuer Not Cooperating'. 

The rating reflects the established market position of the company in the dairy products industry supported by the extensive experience of the promoters, and a comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by modest scale of operations amid intense competition, large working capital requirement and susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in milk prices. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SCPL. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive experience of the promoters and improvement in the revenue profile:** The promoters’ experience of more than two decades in the dairy products business, strong understanding of the market dynamics and healthy relationships with suppliers and over 400 distributors will continue to support the business. The company achieved revenue of Rs 366 crore in fiscal 2026, up from Rs 322 crore in fiscal 2025, and is expected to achieve revenue growth of 9–10% over the medium term. This growth is attributed to elevated demand across product categories, expanded distribution network and increased market penetration. Commissioning of a ready-to-eat (RTE) and ready-to-cook (RTC) manufacturing facility in Amreli, Gujarat, is likely to boost manufacturing capabilities in high-growth convenience food categories. This should strengthen the company's presence in non-seasonal product segments, thereby improving the scale of operations. Ability to scale up volume sales through ramp up of capacity and sustenance of operating efficiency will remain monitorables. 

**Comfortable financial risk profile:** Capital structure was healthy, as reflected in networth, gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio of Rs 156.41 crore, 0.32 time and 0.68 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics were robust, as indicated by interest coverage ratio of over 5.80 times in fiscal 2025. The interest coverage ratio is expected to improve to over 11.0 times over the medium term, supported by healthy profitability. Over the medium term, the financial risk profile is likely to remain comfortable backed by steady accretion to reserve and debt servicing. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Large working capital requirement:** Gross current assets (GCAs) were sizeable at 214 days as on March 31, 2026. The company holds large inventory given the seasonality of raw material. It offers credit of 45–60 days to customers. With growth in operations, the company will need to penetrate multiple geographies and hence increase its inventory. Quick conversion cycle and steady operating profitability, along with increase in return on capital employed (RoCE), will be monitorables. Ability to efficiently manage the working capital cycle with low reliance on bank limit will be monitorable. 

**Susceptibility of operating margin to volatility in milk prices:** Prices of key raw materials, milk and milk products, remain vulnerable to changes in government policies and environmental conditions. Furthermore, regulations such as the intermittent ban on export of skimmed milk powder (SMP), could hamper procurement of milk and SMP exports. Also, entities remain susceptible to shortage in milk production owing to external factors, such as cattle diseases. The operating margin was volatile 

at 11.25–13.64% over the five fiscals through 2026, and is expected at 11.0–11.5% over the medium term amid fluctuations in input prices and lag in cost passthrough. Sustenance of the operating margin will be a key monitorable over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation was moderate at 83% on average for the 12 months through April 2026. Expected cash accrual of over Rs 27 crore will sufficiently cover term debt obligation of Rs 16 crore in fiscal 2027 and Rs 5–6 crore in fiscal 2028. Current ratio was healthy at 2.23 times as on March 31, 2026. Low gearing and moderate networth provide headroom to raise additional debt in case of adverse conditions or downturns in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes SCPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of the promoters and its improving financial risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Significant revenue growth and stable operating margin over 12% leading to higher cash accrual Improvement in the working capital cycle and liquidity with adequate cushion in bank limit 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin of less than 8% leading to lower cash accrual 

Further stretch in the working capital cycle or any large capital expenditure weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

SCPL was set up as a sole proprietorship, M/s Shree Shital Industries, by late Jagdish D Bhuva in 2000. The firm was reconstituted as a partnership and renamed as Shital Cool Products on September 11, 2013. It was reconstituted as a private limited company (Sheetal Cool Products Pvt Ltd) on October 14, 2013. In 2017, the company became a public limited company with the current name (SCPL). It manufactures ice cream; milk and milk products; and food products, such as namkeens, wafers, fryums, frozen foods, bakery and sweets. It is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange.
