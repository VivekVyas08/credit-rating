### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Arfin India Limited (AIL). 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of AIL promoters in the steel industry and the company’s established and diversified clientele and strong capital structure. These strengths are partially offset by modest operating margin and large working capital cycle. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Rating has changed the analytical approach for the AIL and combined the business and financial profile Arfin Titanium and Speciality Alloys Limited (ATSAL) as it is a wholly owned Subsidiary having financial and Business linkages. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Extensive industry experience of the promoters and established and diversified clientele:** Experience of around three decades in the aluminum and ferroalloy industry has given the promoters an understanding of the market dynamics and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and diversified customers. The company’s end-user industry base includes steel, automotive, power and foundry. The revenue of the company on consolidated basis remained stable at around Rs. 618 crore in FY26 as against Rs. 616 crore in FY25. The revenue remained low in the 1st quarter ended FY26, owing to ongoing negotiations in terms and conditions with customers and also revenue was impacted in the march 2026 month due to Middle East War scenario. The revenue has again picked up in May 2026 and expected to increase over the medium term. 

**Strong capital structure:** The financial risk profile is marked by healthy net worth of the company estimated of Rs. 172 crores in FY26 which increased from Rs. 157 crores in FY25 accretion of profit to reserve. Capital structure of the company has remained comfortable with gearing and TOLTNW estimated at 0.75 times and 1.14 times as on march 31, 2026 as against 0.75 times and 1.2 times as on March 31. 2025. With no plan to avail any major external debt capital structure is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Modest operating margin:** Operating margins of the company has improvement in FY26 to 7.46% from 5.90% in FY25 with better realization and increased sales of value-added products however continue to remain at modest level. Following the change in product mix, the company has started to focus on high-margin products, which increased the margin over past two years. Sustenance of margin will remain a key rating sensitivity factor. 

**Large working capital cycle:** The operations are working capital intensive marked by GCA days estimated at high level of 160-170 days in March 31, 2026 as against 159 days as on March 31, 2025 mainly due to high inventory levels and moderate receivable. Inventory levels have increased over the last two years and currently the inventory of the company is high at around 142 days in March 31, 2026 as against 115 days in March 31, 2025 due to lower sales in the march and April 2026 due to impact of Middle East War however the sales was picked up in May 2026. Receivables have reduced as on 

March 31, 2026, at around 18 days as against 27 days as on March 31, 2025 due to reduced sales. Crisil Ratings believes AIL’s working capital cycle will reduce and will be monitorable over the medium term. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Liquidity of the company has improved with Bank limit utilization has remained at moderate level at around 84% for past 12 months through May 2026. Company is expected to generate adequate cash accrual in range of Rs. 20-26 crore against repayment obligation of Rs. 6-8 crore in FY27 and FY28 and post that minimal repayment. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes AIL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained revenue growth of more than 30% and stable operating margin, leading to high cash accrual. Improvement in the working capital cycle with decline in GCA days and better liquidity. 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in net cash accrual to below Rs 12 crore on account of low revenue or operating profit 

Further stretch in inventory or working capital requirement, weakening the liquidity and financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1992, AIL is engaged in aluminum recycling, and manufactures ferroalloys and aluminum products such as aluminum wire rods, aluminum deox, aluminum drawn wires, aluminum alloy ingots and cored wires. It markets its products under Arfin brand. The manufacturing facility at Chhatral in Gandhinagar has total installed capacity of 71,000 tonne per annum. AIL is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange. The company is promoted and managed by Mahendra Shah, Jatin Shah, Pushpa Shah and their family.
