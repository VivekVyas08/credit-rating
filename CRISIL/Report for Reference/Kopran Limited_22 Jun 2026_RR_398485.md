### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has continued its ratings on the bank facilities of Kopran Limited (Kopran; past of Kopran Group) on ‘ **Rating Watch with Developing Implications** ’. 

Crisil Ratings had placed the ratings on Watch with Developing Implications on April 01, 2025, following the announcement made by Kopran regarding the proposed scheme of amalgamation of Kopran Laboratories Limited (KLL) into Kopran. The said scheme of amalgamation has received regulatory approvals from Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and is awaiting regulatory approvals from National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor the progress of the amalgamation and will resolve the watch with appropriate rating action post completion of the same. 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position of the group, driven by extensive experience of promoters and comfortable capital structure. The strengths are partially offset by volatile operating profitability and working capital intensive nature of operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

For arriving at its ratings, Crisil Ratings continues to fully consolidate the business and financial risk profiles of Kopran and its wholly owned subsidiaries, Kopran Research Laboratories Limited (KRLL), Kopran Lifesciences Ltd, and Kopran (HK) Ltd, Hong Kong, and Kopran (UK) Ltd, collectively referred to as the Kopran group, as they are in the similar line of business and have significant operational and financial linkages.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Established market position:** 

Extensive experience of the promoters spanning over four decades in the pharmaceutical industry has led to an established market position of the group in formulations and API segment in both domestic and international markets. It caters to diverse therapeutic segments like anti-diabetic, anti-infective, anti-hypertensive amongst others. 

Promoter experience has led to successful navigation of business cycles and establishing long standing relationships with key stakeholders and a presence across geographies like Africa, Canada, Middle East, United Kingdom, Southeast Asia this has led to the group clocking in a revenue of Rs 676 Cr in fiscal 2026. 

### **Comfortable capital structure:** 

Capital structure of the group remains comfortable on the back of healthy networth and limited reliance on external debt. Networth of the group remains healthy at over Rs 498 Cr as on March 31,2026. 

Limited reliance on debt has led to gearing ratio to be comfortable at 0.31 times as on March 31, 2026. It is estimated to remain comfortable over the medium term in absence of any major debt funded capex plans. Debt protection metrics are also comfortable with interest coverage and net cash accruals to adjusted debt at 5.97 times and 0.19 time respectively for fiscal 2026. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u> Volatile operating profitability:** 

Operating profitability of the group has remained volatile between 9.14%-13.31% in the past three fiscal years ended fiscal year 2026. Profitability of the group is susceptible to raw material prices and forex fluctuations. Lower volume sales and forex losses had 

led to a PAT loss in H1 of fiscal 2026 however the same has improved and company reported a PAT of Rs 25.73 Cr in fiscal 2026 as against Rs 38.55 Cr in fiscal 2025. Sustenance of improvement in operating margins on the back of increase in scale of operations will remain a key rating sensitivity factor. 

# **Working capital intensive nature of operations:** 

Operations of the group continue to remain working capital intensive as reflected in a Gross Current Asset (GCA) of over 275 days as on March 31,2026 as against 289 days as on March 31, 2025. 

Improvement in GCA days was a result of lower inventory days which declined from 112 days as on March 31,2025 to 97 days as on March 31, 2026 driven by higher sales in last quarter. Debtor days continue to remain high and averaged in the range of 100145 days over the past five fiscal years. These working capital requirements are met by bank lines and creditors. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Liquidity position of the group is adequate, marked with an estimated net cash accruals generation of over Rs 35 Cr for fiscal 2027 as against repayment obligations in the range of Rs 14-15 Cr. Bank limit utilization is moderate and averaged at 61% for the past twelve months ended April 2026.  Kopran also has free cash and cash equivalents balance of Rs 4.5-5 Cr as on March 31, 2026. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors:** 

Sustained improvement in revenue and operating margins leading to higher-than-expected net cash accruals above Rs 60 crores. 

Improvement in working capital cycle 

# **Downward factors:** 

Lower than expected revenue and/ or significant drop in profitability constrains net cash accruals to below Rs.25 crores Stretch in working capital cycle or higher than expected debt funded capex or deterioration in debt protection metrics weakens the financial risk profile. 

# **<u>About the Group</u>** 

Kopran was incorporated in 1958, promoted by the Mumbai-based Somani family. The company, which is a part of the Parijat group, is managed by Mr. Surendra Somani. It manufactures pharmaceutical formulations such as antibiotics, anti-malarial, analgesics, and cardiovascular and bulk drugs at its facilities in Mahad and Khopoli in Maharashtra.  In fiscal 2015, the bulk drugs division was transferred to KRLL through a slump sale. Kopran Lifesciences Ltd and Kopran (HK) Ltd, Hong Kong are small entities and nearly defunct.
