### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its 'Crisil AAA/Stable/Crisil A1+' ratings on the bank facilities of ITC Limited (ITC). 

Operating income grew by 8% in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 to Rs 61,044 crore, as against Rs 56,558 crore in the same period of fiscal 2025, driven by growth in the FMCG, Cigarettes and Agri business segment. EBITDA margin moderated slightly to 33.8% from 34.4% due to short term pressures on certain input costs. Financial risk profile remains strong, supported by negligible debt and healthy debt protection metrics. 

The ratings continue to reflect ITC’s robust business risk profile, marked by its presence in diverse businesses viz. leading FMCG marketer in Cigarettes, Branded Packaged Foods, Personal Care, Education & Stationery, Safety Matches & Incense Sticks, foremost agri player pioneering rural transformation, leadership in Paperboard, Paper & Packaging delivering strong and sustainable profitability. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to risks inherent in various businesses that the company operates in. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of ITC and its subsidiaries, step-down subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures (JVs), citing significant business and financial linkages. 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Leadership position in the Indian cigarette industry** 

Strong brand, wide product portfolio, established distribution network and robust research and development capability have enabled ITC to consolidate its leadership position in the domestic cigarette market. Healthy brand loyalty of cigarette smokers has helped the company sustain its market share and profitability over the years, notwithstanding the increase in duties. ITC also exports cigarettes. 

### **Healthy revenue diversity** 

ITC has evolved from a pure tobacco company into a well-diversified business conglomerate, with a strong presence in paperboards, printing and packaging, agricultural commodities, branded packaged foods, personal care products, stationery, safety matches, _agarbatti_ (incense sticks) and other FMCG products. It has also added chocolates, _ghee_ , dairy and frozen food products to its branded packaged foods segment. 

### **Strong financial risk profile** 

Financial risk profile is marked by healthy internal cash accrual, low debt and robust liquidity. Debt was low around Rs 157 crore, against large tangible networth of over Rs 68,000 crore as on September 30, 2025. Liquidity was exceptionally strong because of cash and liquid investments (bonds, debentures, mutual funds and bank deposits) of over Rs 35,000 crore as on the same date. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

### **Exposure to regulatory risk in the cigarette business and vulnerability of other business segments to economic cycles** 

The cigarette business remains exposed to regulatory risk in the form of increase in taxes and the FMCG segment continues to face intense competitive pressure. These risks are partially offset by ITC’s focus on building cost efficiency and strong backward integration in the cigarettes business, through the leaf tobacco and packaging businesses, and through the robust supply chain and agri-farm network in the agricultural commodity and packaged food businesses. 

### **<u>Liquidity</u>** Superior 

Liquidity is driven by cash surplus of over Rs 35,000 crore and cash accrual of over Rs 5,000 crore expected over the medium term, after payment of dividend. With negligible long-term debt, cash accrual should suffice to cover the capex and working capital requirement over the medium term. 

**<u>ESG Profile</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes the environment, social and governance (ESG) profile of ITC supports its credit risk profile. The tobacco sector has a high social impact owing to health hazards associated with the use of tobacco, including smoking addiction, as well as risk of government intervention by way of restriction on sales, regulation of marketing practices and higher tax. The company’s strong focus on addressing some of these ESG risks supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

# **Key ESG highlights:** 

- As part of its _Sustainability 2.0_ agenda, ITC has set ambitious climate targets, including increasing renewable energy usage to 50% of total energy consumption, reducing specific energy consumption by 30%, and cutting specific GHG emissions by 50% by 2030, compared with the fiscal 2018–19 baseline. 

- Further strengthening its long-term climate ambition, ITC has committed to achieving net zero operations by 2050, covering Scope 1 and 2 emissions. 

- On the social front, employee well-being initiatives contributed to nearly 4 percentage point on-year decline in attrition, within the permanent workforce in fiscal 2025 

- The company’s governance structure is characterized by almost 50% of the board comprising independent directors, and extensive financial disclosures **.** 

There is growing importance of ESG among investors and lenders. Crisil Ratings believes the company’s continuous commitment to embed sustainability principles across the organization and its value chain will partly offset the inherent impact of the tobacco sector on the environment and social aspects. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes ITC will maintain its robust financial risk profile and strong market position in the various segments in which it operates, over the medium term. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Downward factors** 

Large, debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) or acquisition, weakening the financial risk profile, with gearing exceeding 0.5 time Any adverse impact of changes in regulations for the cigarette industry Considerable decline in cash and liquid investments 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

ITC operates in a variety of business segments, including Cigarettes, Paperboards, Printing and Packaging, Agricultural Commodities, Branded Packaged Foods, Personal Care Products, Stationery, Safety Matches, _Agarbatti_ and other FMCG products. The hotels business was demerged on 1<sup>st</sup> Jan’25, under a separate entity, ITC Hotels Ltd where ITC now holds 40% stake. However, cigarette manufacturing and sales remain its largest economic activity in revenue terms.
