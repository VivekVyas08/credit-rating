# **Update on Belrise Industries Limited** 

_Note: None of the Directors on Crisil Ratings Limited’s Board are members of rating committee and thus do not participate in discussion or assignment of any ratings. The Board of Directors also does not discuss any ratings at its meetings._ 

Crisil Ratings has factored in the announcement made by Belrise Industries Ltd (Belrise, part of Belrise group) on March 18, 2026, in relation to its proposed acquisition of 100% of equity share capital of Chester Hall Precision Engineering Holdings Limited along with its 100% subsidiary, Chester Hall Precision Engineering Limited through a wholly owned step-down subsidiary namely, Belrise UK Holdings Limited. The acquisitions would be incurred for a consideration of £13.2 million. 

Crisil Ratings understands that the acquisition would enable the group to expand its customer base and product portfolio, as Chester Hall Precision Engineering Limited are specialists in machining aerostructures, aero engine components and satellite parts, serving world's largest aircraft & space OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers. The acquisition shall be completed over the medium term. The financial risk profile is likely to remain strong, marked by moderate gearing projected to be below 0.5 times over the medium term. 

The acquisition is unlikely to have any material impact on the credit profile of the company
