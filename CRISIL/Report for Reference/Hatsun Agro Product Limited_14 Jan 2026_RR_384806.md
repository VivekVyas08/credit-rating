### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Hatsun Agro Product Limited (Hatsun) to **‘Crisil AA/Stable’** from ‘Crisil AA-/Positive’. 

The rating upgrade reflects sustained improvement in Hatsun’s business performance, supported by healthy revenue growth and stable profitability. Operating income grew at a CAGR of about 11% over the three fiscals ended 2025 to Rs. 8,699 crore and continued to improve in fiscal 2026, with Rs. 5,018 crore recorded in the first half.  The company’s strategy of geographical diversification has led to increasing revenue contributions from states outside Tamil Nadu, such as Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra, and Odisha, and is expected to further strengthen its market position over the medium term. 

Operating margins improved to 13.67% in H1 fiscal 2026 from 11.61% in fiscal 2025 and 9.65% in fiscal 2023, driven by a premium product mix, strong distribution network, and supply chain efficiencies and better capacity utilization. Moreover, a flush milk season has aided the operating margins with favourable input costs. The presence of over 13,100 Hatsun Milk Banks (HMBs) and Active Bulk Coolers (ABCs), along with 22 processing plants near key markets, underpin cost efficiency. Margins are expected to remain sustained at 12 to 13 percent, supported by the integrated nature of operations and expanding scale. The Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) has also remained healthy at around 11 to 13 percent in fiscal 2025, and it is expected to stay in range of 14 to 15 percent in the medium term with sustained profitability levels. 

Moreover, the financial profile remains strong, with adjusted net worth expected to exceed Rs. 1,900 crore as of March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics are comfortable with an interest coverage of around 8.6 times in H1 fiscal 2026. The TOL/TNW (including lease liabilities) stood at 1.85 times as on March 31, 2025, and is projected at 1.5–1.7 times for fiscal 2026, aided by the absence of major debt-funded capex in the current fiscal year and only a seasonal rise in short-term borrowings due to year-end inventory buildup. 

The ratings continue to reflect a leading market position in the dairy sector supported by strong brand and geographical diversification, healthy operating efficiency, backed by established procurement and distribution networks and prudent working capital management. These strengths partially offset exposure to risks related to entry into new geographies and susceptibility to changes in environmental conditions and exposure to risks related to volatility in global milk powder prices. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles HAPL with its subsidiary, Milk Mantra Dairy Private Limited (MMDPL), given significant operational and financial linkages. MMDPL has become a subsidiary of Hatsun, effective from January 27, 2025. Furthermore, the board has approved the merger of MMDPL with Hatsun, which is expected to be completed in the current fiscal year. Debt includes lease liabilities, following adoption of Ind AS 116. Crisil Ratings has further amortized the goodwill acquired in fiscal 2025 for over a period of 10 years.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position supported by strong brand and geographical diversification:** Hatsun is the largest private sector dairy company in India, with a portfolio of established brands, superior brand equity, well-spread distribution and procurement networks, and an aggressive marketing strategy. Over the last 5 years the company has been diversifying 

geographically with revenues from Tamil Nadu reducing to around 55 percent in fiscal 2025 from around 67 percent 5 years earlier. Market position to remain strong in Tamil Nadu, with gradual penetration into other states, primarily AP, Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra. With the recent acquisition of Milk Mantra acquisition in FY2025, the company is ventured into Odisha market with the Milky Moo brand. Strong market position in the milk segment is cemented by widespread presence in South India, with majority of the processing units located across Tamil Nadu. HAP’s established market position is reflected in its turnover of over Rs. 5000 crores in first half of this fiscal year. 

**Healthy operating efficiency backed by established procurement and distribution network:** Hatsun owns around 13,100 milk banks covering around 10,000 villages, with chilling and dairy units across its key operating markets. This enables it to reach out to over 5 lakh farmers. Further the company has an established network of through its network of around 4000+ own distribution outlets. The company also has renewable energy agreements which fulfill over 80 percent of power needs. This is reflected by the improving operating margin and ROCE of the company in the medium term. 

**Prudent working capital management:** Working capital cycle will continue to be managed efficiently. The gross current assets (GCA) days came down to 26 days as on September 30, 2025, as against 71 days in fiscal 2024 with reduction in skimmed milk powder (SMP) stock. The inventory is expected to increase in the second half of the year with inventory pile up of SMP with expected good season with the support of additional working capital limits, but still the TOL/TNW (including lease liability) is expected to be in range of 1.5 to 1.7 times. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to risks related to entry into new geographies and expansion plans:** Hatsun plans to further penetrate relatively nascent markets such as Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra, and Odisha, while maintaining stable growth in Tamil Nadu. The company has made significant capital investments in these geographies over the years, including entry into Odisha through the acquisition of Milk Mantra Dairy Private Limited. Marketing and overhead costs are expected to remain elevated in the near term owing to the focus on market penetration. Furthermore, intense competition from established players in these newer regions may continue to constrain scalability, pricing power, and profitability. The company is currently in a consolidation phase following sizable capital expenditures in recent years. Any large, debt-funded capital expenditure or acquisition in the medium term could impact the capital structure and will remain a key monitorable. 

**Susceptibility to changes in environmental conditions and exposure to risks related to volatility in global milk powder prices:** The company remains exposed to risks arising from fluctuations in milk and skimmed milk powder (SMP) prices, which are influenced by droughts, supply constraints, and changes in global dairy trade dynamics. Additionally, external factors such as cattle diseases could adversely affect milk production. Volatility in global SMP prices is expected to persist. For instance, a sharp decline in SMP prices during fiscal 2024 led to surplus procurement, resulting in elevated inventory levels of about 75 days and higher working capital borrowings at the fiscal year-end. Although inventory levels moderated to around 26 days as of September 2025, they are expected to remain in the range of two months by fiscal yearend if seasonal conditions remain favorable, supported by higher short-term borrowings. Any significant increase in inventory levels beyond expectations will remain a key monitorable 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Bank limit utilization of the fund based working capital limits is moderate at around 70 percent for the past seven months ending Oct 2025. Cash accruals in the near term are expected to be around Rs. 750 to 900 crores post dividend payout sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs. 330 – 400 crores over the medium term. Mutual Fund of around Rs. 10 crores as of September 2025 provides further liquidity support. The current ratio is low at 0.72 times as on March 31, 2025, but adjusted for repayment obligations and Interest free security deposits from customers, the current ratio stood adequate at more than 1 times. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes Hatsun’s business risk profile shall continue to remain strong while financial risk profile will continue to witness sustainable improvement 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factor** 

Improvement in business risk profile with improvement in topline and sustenance of operating profitability at around 1012 percent supported by diversification in terms of geography and product Improvement in financial risk profile particularly TOLTNW 

# **Downward factor** 

Decline in revenues or profitability of less than 7 percent, resulting in lower-than-expected net cash accruals Higher than expected debt funded capital expenditure resulting in deterioration in TOLTNW 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Hatsun was incorporated by Mr R G Chandramogan in 1986. The Chennai-based company processes over 40 lakh litres of milk and milk products daily, with strong market presence in Tamil Nadu. It has 22 processing facilities across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Telangana, Maharashtra and Odisha. Popular brands include Arokya & Milky Moo (For Milk); Arun (for ice creams); Hatsun (for milk products, such as SMP, curd, paneer, buttermilk, ghee, and butter); and an ice- 

cream chain under the Ibaco brand and Santosa (for Cattle Feed). Hatsun is listed on the National Stock Exchange and BSE Ltd.
