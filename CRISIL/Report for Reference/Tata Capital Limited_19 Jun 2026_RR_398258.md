### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has assigned its **‘Crisil AAA/Stable’** rating to the Rs 5000 crore non convertible debentures of Tata Capital Limited (TCL) and has reaffirmed its **‘** Crisil AAA/Crisil PPMLD AAA/Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities and existing debt instruments. 

Further, consequent to redemption, Crisil Ratings has **withdrawn** its ratings on the bank loan facilities of Rs 37,500 crore at the company request and on receipt of No-objection certificates (NOCs) from the bankers. Also, **withdrawn** its rating on Long Term Principal Protected Market Linked Debentures of Rs. 843 crore, on Subordinated Debt of Rs 290 crore and on Non-convertible Debentures Rs 3526 crore (See Annexure 'Details of rating withdrawn' for details) at the company’s request and on redemption. This is in line with the Crisil Ratings’ ratings withdrawal policy. Crisil Ratings has received independent confirmation that these instruments are fully redeemed. 

TCL along with its and its subsidiaries TCHFL, Tata Securities Ltd and Tata Capital Pte Ltd, Singapore, together referred herein as the TCL group. 

The rating continue to factor in the TCL group’s majority ownership by, and strategic importance of the financial services business to, the parent, Tata Sons Pvt Ltd (Tata Sons; rated ‘Crisil AAA/Stable’). This is in line with the group's focus on domestic consumption as a key theme in their growth philosophy. 

TCL is the third largest non-banking financial company (NBFC) in India, with consolidated AUM of ~Rs 2,77,275 crore as on March 31, 2026. The loan book of TCL is fairly diversified across various products within retail as well as small and medium enterprises (SME) and corporate segments. TCHFL’s loan portfolio stood at Rs 86,653 crore as on March 31, 2026, which comprises of housing loans, loan against property (LAP) and Builder Loans. 

The ratings also continue to factor in the comfortable capitalisation to support the medium-term growth plans of the group, diversified resource profile and comfortable asset quality metrics. These strengths are partially offset by moderate earnings profile of the group. 

The rating on the perpetual bonds reflects the extent of buffer over the regulatory capital adequacy requirements, and high financial flexibility due to the ownership of Tata Sons. Crisil Ratings believes that it will continue to maintain this cushion going forward. 

# **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of TCL and its subsidiaries TCHFL, Tata Securities Ltd and Tata Capital Pte Ltd, Singapore, together referred herein as the TCL group. This is because they have significant operational and management linkages and operate under the common brand. The ratings also factor in the strong support from the parent, Tata Sons, given the strategic importance of TCL and its subsidiaries as the principal vehicle of non-captive lending in the group and Tata Sons’ strong articulation of support. The rating on the perpetual bonds additionally takes into account restriction to TCL group from servicing these instruments if it breaches the minimum regulatory capital requirement, or if the regulator denies permission to the company to make payments of interest and principal, if it reports losses.

# **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

# **Strategic importance to, and expectation of strong support from the ultimate parent, Tata Sons** 

TCL group is expected to continue to receive strong support from the ultimate parent, Tata Sons. This is due to Tata Sons’ majority ownership in the TCL group, coupled with the increasing importance of the financial services business to the Tata group. 

TCL is a majority-owned subsidiary of TSPL (78.8% stake in TCL’s equity shares on March 31, 2026) and is strategically important to the Tata Group (85.4% stake of TCL’s equity shares on March 31, 2026) TCL, in turn, holds 100% stake in TCHFL. 

Tata Sons also has personnel from its senior management on TCL's board. Tata Sons till date has infused capital of Rs 6,097 crore in TCL 

The TCL group, as the Tata group’s non-captive lending vehicle, is the primary financial services arm, and remains critical to the group, given the growth opportunities in this sector over the medium to long term. The TCL group is also strategically important to the Tata group because it caters to the funding requirements of various entities associated with the group, such as its suppliers, vendors and dealers. 

The shared brand and infrastructural synergies with various Tata group companies strengthen the integration of the TCL group with the overall Tata group. Business synergies should increase further as TCL taps into the Tata group ecosystem as part of its growth strategy. Crisil Ratings believes that Tata Sons will continue to have majority ownership in, and management control of TCL and its subsidiaries, over the medium term. 

# **Comfortable capitalisation to support medium term growth plans** 

The TCL group holds comfortable capitalisation, with consolidated networth (including minority interest) of Rs 47,107 crore as on March 31, 2026, as against Rs 34,225 crore as on March 31, 2025. The group’s capitalisation has been supported by regular infusion from its parent to support growth, coupled with healthy internal accruals and the same is expected going forward too. The  reported consolidated gearing stood at 5.3 times as on March 31, 2026, compared to  6.7 times as on March 31, 2025.  The capital adequacy of TCL (standalone) was comfortable at 19.0% as on March 31, 2026. 

For TCHFL, as on March 31, 2026, the networth was Rs 11,325 crore and the gearing stood at 6.5 times (Rs 8,802 crore and 6.8 times as on March 31, 2025). The capital adequacy of TCHFL was comfortable, with total capital ratio of 17.55% as on March 31, 2026. 

Crisil Ratings believes that the TCL group is adequately capitalised to absorb asset-side risks. However, the TCL Group may require external capital if book growth is higher than internal accruals. 

# **Diversified resource profile** 

The TCL group has access to diversified funding mix ranging from capital market borrowings to bank loans. TCL has a diversified lender base for bank and institutional funding from private sector banks, foreign banks and PSBs/ financial institutions, and is raising NCD (non-convertible debentures) from a diversified set of investors. The group has also raised money through ECB (External Commercial Borrowings) route. As on March 31, 2026, the group held total borrowings of Rs 2,35,977 crore which comprised of term loans (40% share), NCDs and Tier II/Perpetual (35% share), working capital loans 

and commercial papers (9% share), external commercial borrowing (12% share) and from NHB (4% share). TCL and its subsidiaries can mobilise debt at competitive costs due to their association with the Tata group. 

# **Comfortable asset quality metrics** 

TCL group reported comfortable overall gross stage 3 assets (GS3) of 2.0% as on March 31, 2026, as against 1.9% as on March 31, 2025. The provision coverage ratio stood at 56.2%, translating into net stage 3 assets (NS3) of 0.90% as on March 31, 2026. 

TCHFL reported improved GS3 ratio of 0.73% as on March 31, 2026, as against 0.77% as on March 31, 2025. The provision coverage ratio for TCHFL stood at 54.8% as on March 31, 2026, against 57.8% as on March 31, 2025, translating to NS3 ratio of 0.33% as on March 31, 2026. 

The group’s portfolio consisted of retail (58.3% share), SME (27.4% share) and corporate (14.3% share) segments, with the unsecured retail segment constituting 10.3% of net AUM as on March 31, 2026. Over the near-to-medium term, the company plans on increasing focus on the unsecured segment. Resultantly, the group’s ability to contain the slippages and maintain overall asset quality metrics as the portfolio continues to scale remains monitorable. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Moderate, albeit improving, earnings profile** 

The earnings profile of the group, as seen by TCL’s profit after tax (PAT) growing ~34% to Rs 4,891 crore in fiscal 2026 from Rs 3,655 crore in fiscal 2025, was supported by improving operating expenses and controlled credit costs. As a percentage of average managed assets, the operating expenses improved to 2.2% in fiscal 2026 (2.6% from fiscal 2025) and credit costs remained controlled at 1.1% for fiscal 2026 (1.3% for fiscal 2025). Consequently , the return on average managed assets (RoMA), improved marginally to 1.8%  in fiscal 2026 from 1.7% in fiscal 2025. 

TCHFL reported standalone PAT of Rs 1,836 crore and return on managed assets (ROMA) of 2.5% in fiscal 2026 as against Rs 1,499 crore and 2.4%, respectively, in fiscal 2025. The group’s ability to improve profitability while controlling credit costs and sustain at healthier levels will remain monitorable. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Superior 

The asset liability management (ALM) of TCL and TCHFL as on March 31, 2026, shows no cumulative negative mismatches up to one year maturity bucket (the inflows include existing bank limits). In terms of liquidity, as on March 31, 2026, TCL group had total unencumbered liquidity of Rs. 14,988 crore (cash equivalents, liquid investments and unutilised working capital bank lines), which was sufficient to meet debt obligations (including interest) for the upcoming months. Furthermore, the group also has available undrawn term loans, ECB and loans from NHB aggregating to Rs. 9,311 crore, as on March 31, 2026, which provide comfort. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes that the TCL group will remain highly strategically important to Tata Sons and will continue to benefit from the strong parent support over the medium term. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Downward factors** 

Downward revision in the credit rating of Tata Sons Pvt Ltd (Tata Sons) 

- Any change in the support philosophy of Tata Sons resulting in reduced support to the TCL group 

- Sharp deterioration in consolidated asset quality with consolidated gross stage 3 assets remaining above 6% and inturn impacting capital and earnings 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

TCL is the holding company for the financial services businesses of the TCL group and is registered with the Reserve Bank of India as a systemically important, non-deposit-taking, core investment company. TCL has a diversified product portfolio, with a presence in both the wholesale and retail finance segments. The consolidated AUM was Rs 2,77,275 crore as on March 31, 2026, and Rs 2,30,455 crore as on March 31, 2025. The fee-based services distribution of mutual funds and insurance products, stock broking and investment banking are offered through the wholly owned subsidiary, Tata Securities Ltd. TCHFL is engaged in mortgage finance, with loan portfolio of Rs 86,653 crore as on March 31, 2026, as compared to Rs 67,252 crore as on March 31, 2025.
