### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A/Stable/Crisil A1’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Shivalik Bimetal Controls Ltd (SBCL). 

The ratings continue to reflect the longstanding presence of the promoters of SBCL in the bimetal industry, its leading market position due to presence in niche industry with negligible competition and strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by moderate scale of operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of SBCL and its wholly owned subsidiaries, Shivalik Engineered Products Private Limited (SEPPL; erstwhile Checon Shivalik Contact Solutions Private Limited), Shivalik Bimetal Engineers Pvt Ltd (SBEPL) and Innovative Clad Solutions Private Limited (ICSPL) collectively referred to herein as the Shivalik group. This is because SBEPL and SEPPL are wholly owned subsidiaries of SBCL and ICSPL is a JV with M/s Arcelor Mittal Stainless and Nickel Alloys with holding of 16.01%

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Strong market position and track record of management:** The promoters have experience of around four decades in the niche and highly specialized bimetal industry. Their extensive experience and the high quality of its products have helped the group develop healthy relationships with customers and ensure sustained revenue growth, despite industry downturns. The group has specialized product profile, including shunt resistors, thermostatic bimetal among others, enabling it to diversify into the electronics, automotive and medical and other industries. In the current fiscal, the company has entered in the busbar and electronics assembly segment by establishing a dedicated manufacturing facility in Pune. the company will procure other components like PCBs, wirings etc. from outside and assemble it with busbars, shunts and connectors manufactured in house, to be sold as a complete electronic solution to 2-wheeler EV manufacturers. The new segment is expected to significantly contribute to the company’s revenue and enhance export opportunity fiscal 27 onwards due to limited competition. Management’s philosophy of introducing higher value added products in niche industry with limited contribution sustains the healthy operating profitability of the group. In 9MFY26, the group has achieved operating margin of 23.28% and expected to sustain operating margins of around 23% in the full fiscal 26. However, in the medium term, operating margins may moderate minutely due to contributions from the sale of busbar assemblies to the overall revenue of the company due to lower expected gross margins in the segment.  Management’s focus on introduction of new, higher value added products leading to sustained improvement in operating profitability shall remain monitorable. 

**Strong financial risk profile:** Financial risk profile is strong marked by low gearing of 0.08 time supported by sizeable networth of over Rs 399.78 crore as on March 31, 2025 and low dependence on working capital funding requirements. The company is long term debt free as on 30<sup>th</sup> September 2025. SBCL is undertaking capex of Rs. 20 crores in the current fiscal 26 and fiscal 27 to establish its busbar assembly unit in Pune, funded entirely through internal accruals. Further, in the absence of any further debt-funded capital expenditure, gearing is likely to improve to below 0.05 time over the medium term. Debt protection metrics are to strengthen with interest coverage ratio likely to be 33-34 times in fiscal 2026, compared to 30.3 times in fiscal 2025, backed by steady profitability and improvement in overall business profile. With no further debt funded capex planned, the financial risk profile is expected to remain strong over the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Moderate scale of operations:** The company’s topline remained stable Rs 512.11 crore in fiscal 2025 due to volumetric degrowth of 5% in bimetal segment which was offset by a 6% growth in shunt sales volume. Sales growth was flat due to moderation in demand from US market. In the current fiscal, the company has generated revenue of Rs. 408 crores till 31<sup>st</sup> December 2025 supported by improvement in realizations and stable volumes and is expected to generate stable revenue of around Rs. 540-550 crores in the full fiscal 26. Volumetric growth has remained subdued in the current fiscal due to moderation in demand growth from US market and limited access to European markets. However, in FY26, the company is establishing a busbar and electrical assembly unit in Pune which is expected to significantly contribute to the company’s total revenue. Though the management is continuously diversifying into new higher value-added products and expanding the clientele, further growth in business and demand from new clients will remain monitorable. Steady order flow leading to significant improvement in the business risk profile will remain a key rating sensitivity factor 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

Bank limit utilisation is low at around 47 percent for the past twelve months ended October 2025. Cash accrual are expected to be over Rs 85 crore which are sufficient  against nil term debt obligation over the medium term. In addition, it will be act as cushion to the liquidity of the company. Current ratio are healthy at 4.01 times on March 31, 2025. High or moderate cash and bank balance of around Rs 101 crore as on September 30, 2025. Low gearing and moderate net worth support it’s financial flexibility and provides the financial cushion available in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes that SBCL will continue to benefit from its established market position and the extensive experience of its promoters 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained growth in revenue growth of over 10% with steady operating margin leading of over 22% to higher-thanexpected net cash accruals. 

Low dependence on debt leading to sustenance of strong financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in operating income or operating margins below 17-18%, leading to lower cash accruals Any large, debt-funded capex weakening the financial risk profile or liquidity 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 1984, SBCL is promoted by Mr N S Ghumman. The company manufactures thermostatic bimetal/trimetal strips, components, shunt resistors and clad metals. Its units are in Chambaghat, Himachal Pradesh. It is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange.
