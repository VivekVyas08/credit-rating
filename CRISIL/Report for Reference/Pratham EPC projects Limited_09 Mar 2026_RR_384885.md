### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’ ratings on the bank facilities of Pratham EPC Projects Ltd (PEPL; part of the Pratham group). 

The ratings continue to reflect the technical expertise and extensive experience of the promoters in the engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) industry, the healthy order book providing revenue visibility, and the robust financial risk profile and sound operating efficiencies of PEPL. These strengths are partially offset by the large working capital requirement and susceptibility to risks inherent in tender-based operations. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

To arrive at the ratings, Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of PEPL and its subsidiary, which includes Pratham International Contracting –L.L.C - O.P.C, UAE (PIC). 

_Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation._ 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Technical expertise and extensive experience of the promoters** : PEPL was founded in 2008, by mechanical engineers, Mr Pratikkumar Vekariya and Mr Nayankumar Pansuriya. Presence of around two decades in the EPC industry has enabled the promoters to gain a strong understanding of market dynamics and establish healthy relationships with suppliers and customers. 

**Healthy order book providing revenue visibility:** As of December 2025, PEPL held unexecuted orders worth Rs 722 crore, which provide revenue visibility over the medium term. 

**Robust financial risk profile** : Low reliance on external debt has kept the capital structure healthy, as reflected in negligible gearing of 0.16 time and low total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOLANW) ratio of 0.42 time, as on March 31, 2025. Debt protection metrics were strong, aided by low leverage and healthy profitability. Interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratios stood at 16.74 times and 1.28 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025, and are likely to remain at similar levels over the medium term. 

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Susceptibility to risks inherent in tender-based operations** : Revenue and profitability entirely depend on the ability to win tenders. Exposure to intense competition requires entities to bid aggressively to get contracts, and thus, restricts the operating margin. Amidst the cyclicality inherent in the construction industry, ability to maintain profitability via operating efficiency becomes critical. 

**Large working capital requirement** : Gross current assets (GCAs) stood at 248 days as on March 31, 2025, driven by large receivables of 94 days and inventory of 133 days. GCAs have ranged between 115 and 330 days over the three fiscals ended March 31, 2025. Given the nature of the EPC business, PEPL needs to extend long credit periods to its customers and hold large inventory. 

**<u>Liquidity</u>** Adequate 

Bank limit utilisation averaged 76% for the 12 months through October 2025. Expected cash accrual of Rs 15-18 crore should more than suffice to cover the negligible term debt obligation of Rs 1.06 crore per fiscal over the medium term. The current ratio was healthy at 3.53 times as on March 31, 2025. The promoters are likely to extend equity and unsecured loans to cover the debt obligations and working capital requirement. Low gearing and moderate networth offer financial flexibility to withstand adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes PEPL and its subsidiaries will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of the promoters in the EPC industry and their established relationships with clients. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Steady growth in revenue by 20% and sustenance of operating margin, leading to higher cash accrual Improvement in the working capital cycle, with GCAs at 180 days 

# **Downward factors** 

Decline in revenue or operating margin, leading to net cash accrual below Rs 10 crore Any large, debt-funded capital expenditure, weakening the capital structure Substantial increase in working capital requirement, weakening the liquidity and the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

PEPL was formed as a partnership between Mr Vekariya and Mr Pansuriya in 2008. Subsequently, the firm was reconstituted as a private limited company in 2014, and as a public limited company in 2023. The company provides EPC services, mainly for oil and gas pipelines, irrigation, power, industrial projects, water and environment solutions. 

It is listed on the SME (small and medium enterprises) platform of the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd (NSE Emerge). 

PIC was incorporated in June 2024, so as to facilitate cross-country orders within UAE, Saudi Arabia and the MENA (Middle East and North Africa) region. 

PIIPL and PIC are subsidiaries of PEPL. The latter holds 52% stake in PIIPL and 100% stake in PIC.
