### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Due to inadequate information, Crisil Ratings, in line with SEBI guidelines, had migrated the rating on the long-term bank facilities of Suraksha Diagnostic Limited (SDL, Formerly known as Suraksha Diagnostic Private Limited) to 'Crisil B/Stable Issuer Not Cooperating'. However, the management has subsequently started sharing requisite information, necessary for carrying out comprehensive review of the rating. Consequently, Crisil Ratings is migrating the rating on the long-term bank facilities of SDL to **‘Crisil A-/Stable’** from 'Crisil B/Stable Issuer Not Cooperating' and has assigned its **‘Crisil A2+’** rating on the short-term bank facilities of the company. 

The rating reflects company’s established market position, healthy operating margin and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by geographical concentration in revenue and exposure to intense competition. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of SDL and its subsidiaries to arrive at the ratings of SDL.

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

**Established market position:** The promoters have over three decades of experience in the diagnostic services business. SDL is one of the large players in Eastern India with 58 centres and 9 labs as on June 30, 2025. It offers 1,000+ pathology tests and 450+ radiology tests. The company has tied up with more than 10,000 doctors and generates around 90% revenue from walk-in customers. This has led to a strong brand presence and established market position of the company in Eastern India, especially West Bengal. Revenue for fiscal 2025 was over Rs 250 crore, while that for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 was over Rs 73 crore. Revenue is expected to grow at a healthy rate driven by growth in number of centres. 

**Healthy operating margin:** SDL follows a ‘Hub-and-Spoke’ business models where a small number of labs (hubs) can cater to several centres (spokes). This business model allows for reduced overheads leading to healthy operating margin of around 33.5%, maintained over the previous two fiscals ended fiscal 2025. Sustenance of operating margin over the medium term will remain a key rating monitorable. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Working capital cycle is efficient due to the nature of business where majority sales are on a cash basis and inventory holding is minimal, reflected in GCAs of around 60 days as on March 31, 2025. The same has resulted in limited reliance on external debt. Networth was sizeable at over Rs 208 crore and gearing strong at 0.03 time as on March 31, 2025. Further, Total Outside Liabilities to Tangible Networth (TOL/TNW) was healthy at 0.59 time as on the same date. Debt protection metrics were comfortable, reflected in adjusted interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios of 9.34 times and 11.38 times, respectively, in fiscal 2025. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

**Exposure to intense competition and geographical concentration in revenue:** As operations are restricted to West Bengal and neighbouring states, geographical concentration risk persists. Also, the industry is characterised by intense competition from some large players having pan-India presence and several smaller players having strong regional presence. The same may impact operating margin and scalability. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 75 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 2.5-3 crore over the medium term. In addition, it will be act as cushion to the liquidity of the company. Current ratio was moderate at 1.10 times on March 31, 2025. High cash and cash equivalents of around Rs 55-56 crore as on March 31, 2025, should further aid liquidity. Low gearing and moderate net worth support its financial flexibility and provides the financial cushion available in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes SDL will continue to benefit from its established market position. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Substantial improvement in scale driven by geographical diversification in operations and sustenance of operating margin leading to Net Cash Accrual of over Rs 90 crore Sustenance of financial risk profile. 

# **Downward factors** 

Significant decline in scale and operating margin less than 25% leading to significantly lower than expected Net Cash Accruals 

Debt funded capex and/or inorganic strategy leading to weakening of the financial risk profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Incorporated in 2005, SDL provides integrated and comprehensive diagnostic services in pathology and radiology. It also provides medical consultation and genomics testing services. It is one of the largest diagnostic chains in Eastern India. The company follows a ‘Hub-and-Spoke’ business model. SDL is listed on BSE and NSE.
