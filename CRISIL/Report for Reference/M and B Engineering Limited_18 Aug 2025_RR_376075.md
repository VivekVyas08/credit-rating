### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of M and B Engineering Ltd (MBEL) to **‘Crisil A/Stable/Crisil A1’** from ‘Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+’. 

The upgrade reflects the significant improvement in the financial risk profile of MBEL driven by its initial public offering (IPO) comprising fresh issue of Rs 275 crore. The funds will be used towards capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 130.58 crore, software upgrade of Rs 5.2 crore, repayment of term loan of Rs 58.75 crore and for general corporate purposes, including IPO expenses. The networth increased sharply to more than Rs 579 crore as on August, 2025, from Rs 304.15 crore as on March 31, 2025, driven by the IPO, reducing dependence on external debt. As a result, the gearing is expected to remain comfortable, below 0.5 time, over the medium term despite planned capex to increase capacity by 40,000 tonne per annum. The liquidity profile of the company has also improved because of the IPO as the proceeds will help the company prepay its entire term debt. Expected healthy cash accrual will enhance the liquidity profile over the medium term. The planned capex will improve the company’s business risk profile over the medium term because of ramp-up of operations. 

The ratings continue to reflect the established track record of MBEL in the engineering and construction industry and its healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the company’s large working capital requirement and exposure to intense competition. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of MBEL and its wholly owned subsidiaries, Phenix Construction Technologies Inc and Phenix Building Solutions Pvt Ltd.

### **<u>Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description</u>** 

### **Strengths:** 

**Established track record of operations:** MBEL has been engaged in the business of Pre Engineered Buildings, Steel Structural and Self Supported Roofings for over two decades. It has a strong track record of executing projects for industrial and government buildings. It was set up by the late Mr Manibhai Patel and is now managed by Mr Hasmukh Patel and his family members. Over the years, the promoters have developed strong technical and project management capabilities. Turnover stood at Rs 989.09 crore in fiscal 2025 and is likely to grow over 30% in fiscal 2026, led by orders worth Rs 800 crore in the pipeline as of April 2025. The strong channel network of the promoters will support the business risk profile over the medium term. 

**Healthy financial risk profile:** Networth and gearing were comfortable at Rs 304.15 crore and 0.60 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. The fresh issue of Rs 275 crore in the IPO in August 2025 has resulted in a sharp improvement in the company’s capital structure, backed by increase in the networth to Rs 579 crore. Debt protection metrics were robust, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratio improving to 6.43 times and 0.48 time, respectively, in fiscal 2025 from 3.5 times and 0.29 time, respectively, in fiscal 2024. The financial risk profile is expected to remain healthy in the absence of any major debt-funded capex plan over the medium term. 

**Weaknesses:** 

**Large working capital requirement:** The company had gross current assets (GCAs) of around 222 days (based on revenue) as on March 31, 2025, driven by receivables and inventory of 71 days (based on revenue) and 137 days (based on cost of sales), respectively (64 days and 100 days, respectively, as on March 31, 2024). The company maintains large inventory to hedge against volatility in metal prices, given the long transit time for imports and manufacturing, and to shield the operating margin. 

**Exposure to intense competition:** Pre-engineered buildings (PEBs) are used as a substitute for civil construction, majorly in the industrial segment, and the company faces competition from several unorganised players amid low entry barriers. The operating margin may remain above 12% over the medium term. Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor the revenue growth and market position of MBEL. 

# **<u>Liquidity: Strong</u>** 

Fund-based bank limit utilisation was negligible at 0.82% on average in the 12 months through May 2025, though utilisation of the non-fund-based limit was high at 85.39%. Expected annual cash accrual of Rs 90-100 crore will aid the liquidity in the absence of term debt obligation over the medium term. The current ratio was moderate at 1.38 times, while cash and bank balance stood at Rs 29.64 crore as on March 31, 2025. Strong gearing and moderate networth support financial flexibility and provide cushion against any adverse condition or downturn in the business. The funds raised through the IPO for general corporate purposes, amounting to Rs 66 crore, will further cushion the liquidity profile. 

# **<u>Outlook: Stable</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes MBEL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters in the PEB segment and their funding support. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upwards factors:** 

Sustainable growth in revenue and healthy profitability resulting in higher net cash accrual Sustenance of healthy capital structure and improvement in the working capital cycle with GCA below 190 days 

# **Downward factors:** 

Pressure on topline and operating margin falling below 8% resulting in lower cash accrual Further stretch in the working capital cycle or large debt-funded capex weakening the liquidity profile 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Based in Ahmedabad, MBEL was incorporated as Manibhai & Brothers Construction Pvt Ltd and reconstituted as a public limited company with the current name in fiscal 2011. It is promoted and managed by Mr Hasmukhbhai Patel, Mr Chirag Patel, Mr Girish Patel, Mr Malav Patel and their family members. The company manufactures and installs PEBs and selfsupported steel roofing systems. It has expanded its footprint in south India by setting up a new plant for PEBs in Chennai. The company came out with an IPO of Rs 650 crore, comprising fresh issue of Rs 275 crore and offer for sale (OFS) of Rs 375 crore, in August 2025.
