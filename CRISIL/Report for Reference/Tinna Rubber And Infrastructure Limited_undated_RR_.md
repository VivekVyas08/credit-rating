CRISIL has suspended its ratings on the bank facilities Tinna Overseas Limited (TOL). The suspension of ratings is on account of non-cooperation by TOL with CRISIL's efforts to undertake a review of the ratings outstanding. Despite repeated requests by CRISIL, TOL is yet to provide adequate information to enable CRISIL to assess TOL's ability to service its debt. The suspension reflects CRISIL's inability to maintain a valid rating in the absence of adequate information. CRISIL considers information availability risk as a key credit factor in its rating process and non-sharing of information as a first signal of possible credit distress, as outlined in its criteria 'Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings'. 



### **_About the Company_** 

TOL was incorporated as a private limited company in 1987, and was reconstituted as a public limited company in March 1992. Initially, the company manufactured footwear and rubber products for shoe soles. In 2001, it started manufacturing bitumen modifiers. Until 2008-09, TOL's operations were managed by Mr. Bhupendar Kumar Sekhri, his two brothers, and their sons. The promoter group underwent a major restructuring in 200809, wherein the directors decided to separate, and Mr. Kapil Sekhri, Mr. Gaurav Sekhri, and Mr. Madan Kukreja were admitted as directors, while Mr. Vijay Sekhri, Mr. Anil Sekhri, and Mr. J M Pushkarna retired; Mr. Bhupendra Sekhri is the company's current chairman.
