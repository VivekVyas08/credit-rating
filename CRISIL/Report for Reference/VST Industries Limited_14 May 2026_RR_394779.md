### **<u>Detailed Rationale</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its 'Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+' ratings on the non-convertible debentures (NCDs), fixed deposit programme and short term bank facility of VST Industries Limited (VST). 

The ratings continue to reflect the strong financial risk profile and established market position of VST. These strengths are partially offset by its small market share, regional concentration in revenue, moderation in operating profitability and susceptibility to regulatory changes in the tobacco industry. 

VST reported a healthy operating performance in FY26, with revenue growth of 5% to Rs 1465 crores (net of excise). The growth was driven by a 25% increase in net cigarette sales, (which accounted for 85 ~~%~~ of revenues), supported by better price realization and strong volume growth. However, the unmanufactured tobacco segment experienced a 35% moderation owing to decline in prices amid better global production and supply chain disruption due to ongoing conflict in middle east region. 

Going forward in the medium term, Crisil ratings expect revenue growth to remain at 1-2%, with cigarette revenues expected to remain flat in FY27 despite higher taxes supported by price hikes. The unmanufactured tobacco segment is expected to grow at a steady rate of 5%-10% over the medium term. Operating margins, which improved to 30.7% in FY26 mainly due to one-time gain on account of tax transition in Q4, and the same is expected to normalize to 24-25% in the medium term. 

### **<u>Analytical Approach</u>** 

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of VST. 

### **-** **<u>Key Rating Drivers Strengths</u>** 

### **Strong financial risk profile** 

VST has a strong financial risk profile, characterized by debt-free status since 2011, healthy accruals, and robust liquidity. The company plans to maintain modest capex levels of Rs 50-60 crore per annum, focused on maintenance, which will be funded through internal accruals. With estimated cash balances of Rs 593 crores as of March 31, 2026, and annual accruals of Rs 100-110 crores, VST's liquidity is healthy and sufficient to meet its capex plans. Additionally, the company's strong ESG profile further supports its already robust credit risk profile. 

### **Established market position with reputed brands** 

VST is a well-established cigarette manufacturer with operational track record of over eight decades. It holds a significant position in the domestic market, ranking third, with a strong presence in key regions such as West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, and Uttar Pradesh. The company boasts a diverse portfolio of popular brands, including Charminar, Charms, and Total, across various segments. Its recent foray into the King size in demi-slim format segment with Editions and Trio further solidifies its market position. With strong brand loyalty, VST's business risk profile is expected to remain stable in the medium term. 

# **<u>Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses</u>** 

# **Small market share and regional concentration in revenue** 

VST, the third-largest player in the domestic cigarette market by volume, holds a market share of around 7%. While its operations have historically been concentrated in south and east India, the company has made efforts to expand its reach into new markets, including Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Maharashtra, and Karnataka. Additionally, as a cigarette manufacturer, VST's business is inherently sensitive to changes in regulations, taxation, and consumer behavior. 

# **Susceptibility to regulatory changes** 

The cigarette industry remains highly vulnerable to changes in government policies and regulations. An elevated tax structure on one hand, and curbs on promotion, consumption and packaging of cigarettes on the other hand, constrain growth. 

The government introduced significant tax changes on February 1, 2026, including a GST hike to 40% on RSP and additional excise duty, prompting VST to take significant price hikes in Q4 FY26. However, due to sticky demand, volumes are anticipated to recover gradually in the medium term. Despite this, VST's margins are expected to remain healthy in the range of 24-25%, supported by the price hikes. 

# **<u>Liquidity</u>** Strong 

The liquidity profile of VST remains strong supported by healthy accruals and cash and equivalents of Rs 593 crore as of March 31, 2026. The company's net cash accruals improved to Rs 224 crore in FY26, driven by higher operating margins of 30%. With no debt, modest capex of Rs 60-70 crores and a strong cash position, VST's liquidity is expected to healthy over the medium term. 

# **<u>ESG Profile</u>** 

Crisil Ratings believes the environment, social and governance (ESG) profile of VST supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

The tobacco sector has a high environmental impact because of its raw material sourcing strategies and waste-intensive processes. It also has a high social impact owing to health hazards associated with the use of tobacco, including smoking addiction and underage consumption, as well as risk of government intervention by way of restriction on sales, regulation of marketing practices and higher tax. 

The company’s strong focus on addressing some of these ESG risks supports its already strong credit risk profile. 

# **<u>Key ESG highlights:</u>** 

VST aims to 50% renewable energy in overall energy mix by fiscal 2030. 

- The company aims to operate 100% of its fleet (owned and third-party) on electric vehicles by 2030, as part of its Roadmap 2030 targets. 

- The Company continues to provide inclusiveness training to management staff to promote a work environment free from discrimination, sexual harassment and undue biases in compensation, training, and employee benefits. Its governance structure is characterised by half of its Board comprising independent directors, split in chairman and CEO positions and extensive disclosures. 

There is growing importance of ESG among investors and lenders. Crisil Ratings believe that VST’s commitment to ESG will play a key role in enhancing stakeholder confidence, given high shareholding by foreign portfolio investors. 

# **<u>Outlook</u>** Stable 

Crisil Ratings believes VST will sustain its credit risk profile driven by strong financial risk profile and stable business risk profile. 

# **<u>Rating sensitivity factors</u>** 

# **Upward factors** 

Sustained and material improvement in market share to over 25%. 

Significant improvement in networth, along with stable operating margin and sustenance of strong financial risk profile 

# **Downward factors** 

- Decline in operating performance driven by de-growth in sales or moderation in operating profitability to 18-20% on a sustained basis. 

- Weakening of the financial risk profile with considerable decline in cash and liquid investments due to debt-funded capex or major acquisition. 

# **<u>About the Company</u>** 

Based in Hyderabad, VST manufactures and markets cigarettes and trades in unmanufactured tobacco. The company is an associate of British American Tobacco Plc (rated 'BBB+/Negative/A-2' by S&P Global Ratings), which holds 32.2% stake.
