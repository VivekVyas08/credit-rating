# Oil India Limited — Sensitivity Model (Step 12, scaled per case.json)

Scaling decision recorded in `case.json` (`step12_scaling_decision`): full 5-year 3-statement
modelling replaced with a focused sensitivity on the 2–3 rating-driving variables, because the
forward view here turns on one identifiable, well-disclosed project rather than a diffuse set of
business drivers, and no sector-assumptions document was supplied to source a fuller base case.
All figures Rs crore, consolidated basis. Base case = FY2027/FY2028; FY2026 actual is the anchor.

## The three variables that actually move this rating

### 1. NRL refinery-expansion debt drawdown (the dominant variable)
Sanctioned consortium facility: Rs 21,678 cr + Rs 4,113 cr standby = **Rs 25,791 cr total facility**
(per AR note 25.2). Drawn to date embedded in FY26 consolidated non-current borrowings of
Rs 35,067 cr (which also includes the parent's own USD bonds/ECBs of ~Rs 17,541 cr and the
Rs 2,498.37 cr new NCD) — the NRL-specific drawn balance is not separately disclosed, so the
projection below models the FACILITY HEADROOM, not a precise drawn balance.

| | FY2026A | FY2027E | FY2028E |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consolidated non-current borrowings | 35,067 | ~41,000 | ~46,000 |
| Implied incremental draw | — | ~6,000 | ~5,000 |
| Facility headroom remaining (of Rs 25,791 cr sanctioned) | — | narrowing | largely drawn |
| Repayment moratorium | — | continues | continues (starts 30-Sep-2029) |

Source for the draw-pace estimate: FY26 CWIP grew Rs 8,117 cr y-o-y (Rs 29,527 cr → Rs 37,644
cr) — a reasonable proxy for the annual capex pace while the project remains in construction.
**Sensitivity: if the project's scope is enhanced a fourth time (as happened once already,
Sep-2025) or slips its 44-instalment repayment start of 30-Sep-2029, consolidated gearing
would move further from the 0.65x FY26 print — this is the single largest swing factor in the
entire credit profile and is named as the primary monitorable.**

### 2. Crude oil realisation
FY26 standalone crude-oil segment PBT was Rs 4,301.44 cr on production of 3.450 MMT. Q1 FY27
segment PBT alone (Rs 2,998.64 cr) already exceeds two-thirds of the whole of FY26, driven by a
price spike the AR's own MD&A links to a Strait of Hormuz disruption — treated as **not
sustainable at that run-rate** (IEA/EIA/OPEC forecasts cited in the same MD&A diverge on 2026
and converge only for a 2027 demand recovery).

| Scenario | Crude price assumption | Consolidated PBDIT impact vs FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Reverts toward the FY24-FY26 average realisation | Broadly flat to FY26 |
| Upside (Q1 FY27 run-rate sustained) | Elevated, geopolitically-driven | +15-20% |
| Downside | Reverts below the 3-year average (global demand contraction per IEA/EIA, cited in the AR) | -10-15%, interest coverage falls toward high single digits from 10.28x |

No formal price deck was supplied (sector-assumptions document gap, Step 1); this is a
qualitative range bracketed by the company's own MD&A commentary, not a modelled cash-flow
forecast — stated as a judgement, not a management figure.

### 3. Reserve Replacement Ratio trajectory
RRR improved 0.94 (FY25) → 1.02 (FY26). A drilling programme of ~78 wells is guided for
FY2026-27 (stated in the AR MD&A) against 74 wells actually drilled in FY26 — a modest
step-up. **Base case: RRR sustained at or modestly above 1.0x**, consistent with the company's
own stated target ("targeted to be sustained at or above unity"). A sustained sub-1.0x reading
across two consecutive years would be the clearest deterioration signal for the standalone
E&P business, independent of the NRL financing story.

## What this does NOT replace
This is not a substitute for the full 5-year 3-statement model the SOP names as the default —
it is the analyst's judgement that, for THIS case, the three variables above capture materially
more of the rating's actual sensitivity than a fully built revenue-waterfall model would, given
the document gaps stated above. If a sector-assumptions document or management's own multi-year
capex/production guidance becomes available, this should be upgraded to the full workflow.
