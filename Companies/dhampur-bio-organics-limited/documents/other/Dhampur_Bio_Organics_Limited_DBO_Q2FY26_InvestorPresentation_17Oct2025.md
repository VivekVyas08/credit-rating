### **Business Overview** 



## **₹ 2,714 Crores**

Dhampur Bio Organics Limited (DBO) is a leading cane processing company with experience and pedigree extending across 9 decades. Utilising its advanced and integrated manufacturing setups, DBO capitalises on the favourable trends in the sugar and broader agricultural economy. The company is focussed on repositioning itself as a value-added sugar manufacturer on one hand and a bio-energy company on the other. The three manufacturing facilities – in Asmoli, Mansurpur and Meerganj; are strategically located in close proximity to each other enabling better utilisation of resources and increased synergies in production and management.
