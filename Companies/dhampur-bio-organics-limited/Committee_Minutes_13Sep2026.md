## 1. Why the Committee reconvened

The rating issued on 04-Sep-2026 was **withdrawn from delivery and re-decided**. The operator
supplied CareEdge's own published rationale for this issuer of the same date, which showed six
errors in the delivered package — the analytical basis, the rated amount, the liquidity figure,
the period attribution of the Meerganj sale, the outlook, and the omission of the sector's
defining operating metric.

The Committee records that the package it approved on 04-Sep had passed **all five delivery
gates, at 34/34 tie-outs and 100.0% mutation coverage**. It notes that this is a governance
finding about the gates, not only about the analyst: the checks then in place verified internal
consistency, and a report built off the wrong statement is internally consistent. The remedy
adopted is `tools/step14_diff.py`, now gate 6, which compares the package against the agency's
own action for the same issuer.

## 4. The basis, decided rather than asserted

The Committee determined the basis as **standalone**, on the reproduction test: the standalone
statements reproduce the agency's published cash (₹4.50 crore), inventories (₹994 crore),
tangible net worth (₹1,030 crore), TOI (₹2,082 crore) and gearing (1.14x in FY2025) — the
consolidated statements reproduce none of them. The subsidiaries carry no borrowings and move
tangible net worth by ₹3.04 crore.

It further records that the 04-Sep package asserted the basis was "methodology-determined, not
elective" **without the criteria having been opened**, and that this sentence was accepted by
the gates as the compliance it claimed. The basis is now declared at intake and cross-checked
against both the case record and the agency's own action.

## 5. Anchor divergence — expressly resolved (>±2 notches)

Stage 1 anchor **A+** (index 4) → Stage 2 overlay **−3** → Stage 3 **CARE BBB+; Positive**.

- **−1 business/scale:** crushing throughput fell 41.44 → 34.98 → **32.92 lakh tonne**, and the
  residual base after the June-2026 sale is two plants in one western/central UP command area —
  the catchment the red-rot episode hit.
- **−2 calibration:** the composite is carried by gearing 1.03x and a stable ₹1,029.68 crore
  tangible net worth, and under-weights what binds this credit — interest coverage 2.08x, total
  debt/GCA 12.67x, net debt/PBILDT 8.06x on a 6.29% margin and 3.46% RoCE. CareEdge sugar peers
  at this coverage and leverage sit in BBB, not A.
- **Support notches: nil.** Standalone listed issuer.
- **Hard caps left live.** A sugar miller's earnings are accrual-realised, so accrual coverage
  is the correct test; disapplication would be a judgement uplift dressed as a sector correction.

## 6. Why Positive, and why not an upgrade

The Committee accepted that the 04-Sep Stable rested on a **period error**. The Meerganj
undertaking transferred in **June 2026 — FY2027**; none of the ₹305.00 crore of proceeds is in
the 31-Mar-2026 balance sheet, which still carries the full ₹1,060.46 crore of debt against
₹289.66 crore of long-term debt. Correctly placed, the deleveraging is a **forward catalyst**,
which is what an outlook expresses. Placed in the past, as v1 did, it stopped being one.

An upgrade was considered and rejected: the margin has fallen for a second year to 6.29%,
coverage is 2.08x, and net leverage of 8.06x is far outside the 4.20x positive trigger. The
Committee requires the deleveraging to appear in the balance sheet alongside a sustained margin
recovery.

**The Committee records that the analyst had seen the agency's Positive outlook before
re-deriving it.** It weighed the conclusion accordingly and relied on the arithmetic, which is
independent of that knowledge: long-term debt more than halves on the proceeds alone.

## 7. Other matters weighed

**Income-tax proceeding.** The Section 132 search of 29-Oct to 04-Nov-2025 was verified in the
issuer's **own** note 36(c), not carried from the CRA text (R2). No demand is quantified; the
Company records that uncertainty exists. Named in the rationale, carried as a negative
sensitivity and a surveillance trigger.

**Liquidity.** Unencumbered cash of **₹4.50 crore** only. The ₹6.19 crore of other bank
balances is margin against guarantees and the earmarked Molasses Storage Fund and was excluded.
The Committee noted that the 04-Sep package counted it, overstating free liquidity ~8x.

## 8. Surveillance (R15)

Next scheduled review by **13 September 2027**. Event-driven triggers: any crystallised
Income-tax demand or adverse order; gross recovery below 10.5% in SS2026-27; overall gearing
above 1.30x; interest coverage below 1.50x; **failure to complete the announced debt prepayment
from the Meerganj proceeds during FY2027**; any change in administered ethanol pricing or
diversion policy. Default monitoring: 1-day/1-rupee → D.
