Rationale  
The rating assigned to the proposed non-convertible debenture programme of Balkrishna Industries Limited (BKT) draws 
strength from the company’s established position in the global off-highway tyre (OHT) industry, supported by its strong brand 
franchise, extensive product portfolio and diversified presence across end-user segments and geographies. The rating also 
factors in the promoters’ long track record in the tyre industry, as well as its cost-competitive manufacturing operations backed 
by backward integration, healthy operating profitability and robust financial flexibility. 
BKT is a leading tyre exporter, exporting its wide range of products spanning over 3,600 stock-keeping units (SKUs) to over 160 
countries. The company has a strong presence in the domestic market, which supported healthy revenue growth at a 
compounded annual growth rate (CAGR) of 7% over FY2022-FY2026. It has a sizeable scale of operations, reflected in an 
operating income of around Rs. 10,823 crore and a robust operating profit margin (OPM) of around 24% in FY2026. The 
company’s revenues and earnings profile are expected to grow steadily, supported by planned capacity additions in the OHT 
segment, stable replacement demand and increasing contribution from the carbon black business and on-road tyre segments, 
including truck and bus radial (TBR), passenger car radial (PCR) and two-wheeler (2W) tyres. The company commenced 
commercial sales in the TBR and 2W tyre segments in Q1 FY2027 and PCR tyres are scheduled to be launched by the end of Q3 
FY2027. While the rising share of on-road tyres, which typically carry relatively lower margins, could result in some moderation 
in blended profitability, the impact is expected to be largely offset by operating efficiencies. 
BKT is undertaking a sizeable debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) programme primarily towards expanding its tyre 
capacities (both on- and off-highway segments) which would drive growth over the medium term. The overall capex outlay is 
estimated at around Rs 6,800 crore till FY2029, of which about Rs 3,800 crore had been incurred by June 2026. The balance Rs 
3,000 crore is expected to be spent over FY2027-FY2029, with a major portion of Rs 1,500-2,000 crore planned for remaining 
part of FY2027. An improvement in operating profits, along with scheduled debt repayments, is likely to support an 
enhancement in debt metrics over the medium term. The company is expected to maintain a robust credit profile, aided by 
healthy cash flow generation, comfortable leverage and strong debt coverage indicators. 
The rating, however, remains constrained by the inherently competitive nature of the global tyre industry, exposure of 
earnings to volatility in raw material prices (natural rubber and crude derivatives such as synthetic rubber and carbon black 
feedstock) and foreign exchange (forex) rates as well as vulnerability to regulatory and trade policy changes in key export 
markets. Increased crude oil and adverse currency movement impacted the company’s margins in Q1 FY2027. However, 
calibrated price hikes are expected to support near-term margins. Further, demand for OHT products remains linked to 
economic activity, agricultural incomes and capex cycles across major overseas markets, exposing the company to cyclical 
downturns in end-user industries.

The Stable outlook reflects ICRA's expectation that BKT’s earnings growth would be supported by its strong competitive 
position in the global OHT market and benefit from its diversification into new business segments. The company is anticipated 
to maintain healthy profitability and a robust financial profile, despite the ongoing capex programme and near-term cost 
inflation pressures.   
Key rating drivers and their description  
Credit strengths  
Established position in off-highway tyre industry with diversified product portfolio in OHT segment – BKT is one of the 
leading global manufacturers of OHT tyres with a diversified presence across agriculture (59% of FY2026 OHT volumes) and 
off-the-road segments, which includes earthmoving, industrial and construction and mining, among others. The company 
derived around 70% of its OHT volumes from the replacement market which provides better realisations compared to OEM 
sales and reduces dependence on cyclical OEM demand. BKT has established a strong global distribution network and 
remains the largest exporter of OHT tyres from India, despite a moderation in export volumes in FY2026 amid subdued 
demand in Europe and tariff-related challenges in the USA. The slowdown was offset by healthy growth in the domestic 
replacement market, aided by favourable monsoons, infrastructure spending and goods and services tax (GST) rate 
rationalisation on tyres. The company's presence in the niche OHT segment, strong brand recall, premium pricing position 
and diversified presence across geographies, products and markets continue to support business stability and profitability. 
Backward integration into carbon black manufacturing supports margins – BKT's integrated carbon black operations, with a 
capacity of 3,60,000 MTPA (including 30,000 MTPA of specialty carbon black), support cost efficiencies and raw material supply 
reliability for its tyre business, while also generating third-party sales (around 60% of the volumes presently). Carbon black 
contributed around 9% of revenues in FY2026 and is expected to continue to account for around 10% of revenues over the 
near-to-medium term. While specialty carbon black is likely to support margin expansion as external sales scale up, its 
contribution remains limited at present.  
Strong financial risk profile – The company's financial risk profile remains strong, supported by healthy operating profitability, 
robust cash flow generation and a conservative capital structure. Despite undertaking sizeable growth capex over FY2025-
FY2026, BKT has maintained low leverage and robust debt coverage metrics, as marked by Total Debt/TNW of 0.4 times as on 
March 31, 2026 and DSCR of 4.8 times. While Total Debt/OPBITDA moderated to 1.6 times in FY2026 from 1.3 times in FY2025 
and is expected to remain higher in FY2027 due to ongoing capex, it is likely to improve thereafter supported by scheduled 
debt repayments and healthy earnings. The company's financial flexibility continues to be strong, reflected by its large net 
worth, sizeable investments and access to diverse funding sources. 
Credit challenges  
Susceptibility of margins to fluctuations in raw material prices and forex rates – The company's profitability remains exposed 
to volatility in key raw material prices, including natural rubber and other crude-linked derivatives, which constitute a 
significant proportion of operating costs. The volatility in crude oil, natural rubber and freight costs from geopolitical 
developments in West Asia caused a moderation in the operating margin in Q1 FY2027. Nevertheless, its earnings profile is 
expected to remain supported by operating efficiencies, backward integration and its ability to partially pass on cost increases 
through price hikes, although with some lag. Further, a significant reliance on imported raw materials (50-55% of requirement), 
and borrowings in euro, the company continues to be exposed to forex fluctuations. However, with most of company’s 
revenue generated from exports, it benefits from a considerable natural hedge. The remaining net receivable exposure is 
largely covered through forward contracts to mitigate the said risk. In FY2026, it reported forex loss of about Rs. 290 crore 
owing to adverse currency movements. ICRA further notes that the demand for OHT products remains exposed to cyclical 
trends in economic activity, farm incomes and capex across key end markets. 
Exposure to regulatory risks – BKT remains exposed to evolving environmental, sustainability and trade-related regulations 
across key markets, including the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), which is scheduled to come into effect

been taking measures to strengthen sourcing traceability and is expected to pass on the incremental compliance costs to the

Environmental and social risks 
Environmental considerations – The tyre industry is exposed to environmental risks arising from regulatory requirements 
related to emissions, waste management and end-of-life tyre disposal. The implementation of the EPR framework, w.e.f. July 
2022, for waste tyres has heightened compliance obligations for tyre manufacturers and importers. Additionally, the proposed 
EUDR is expected to further increase traceability requirements for natural rubber-based products exported to Europe. To 
mitigate these risks, BIL has implemented Sustainable Procurement Policy and Natural Rubber Procurement Policy, 
strengthened supply chain transparency through digital traceability systems and EUDR-aligned data management processes, 
compliance with applicable regulatory requirements, and increased the use of renewable energy, among others.  
Social considerations – The tyre manufacturing business has moderate exposure to social risks owing to its dependence on a 
skilled workforce and the need to maintain high standards of occupational health and safety. Any major labour disruptions, 
workplace safety incidents or regulatory non-compliance could affect operations and profitability. However, BKT continues to 
invest in employee capability development, leadership development programmes, technical training, and succession planning 
initiatives and did not report any fatalities during the last four years. Another social risk that BKT faces pertains to product 
safety and quality, wherein instances of product recalls and high-warranty costs may not only result in financial implications 
but could also harm the reputation and create a more long-lasting adverse impact. In this regard, BKT has established advanced 
in-house R&D and testing infrastructure, including the Mahabirprasad Poddar Knowledge Centre at Bhuj, dedicated tyre testing 
facilities and digital production traceability systems. 
Liquidity position: Adequate  
BKT’s liquidity remains Adequate, supported by expected retained cash flows of Rs. 1,250-1,500 crore in FY2027, healthy 
investments of around Rs. 3,100 crore as on March 31, 2026 and buffer in fund-based working capital of around Rs. 570 crore 
as on June 30, 2026, against term debt repayment of around Rs. 430-440 crore in FY2027 and Rs. 45 crore in FY2028 for the 
existing debt. The capex plans of around Rs. 2,500-3,000 crore in FY2027 would be funded through a mix of internal accruals 
and external debt.  
Rating sensitivities  
Positive factors – ICRA could upgrade BKT’s rating if it demonstrates a significant increase in the scale of operations and 
earnings, led by material ramp-up of on-highway tyre segments and continued volume growth in OHT, leading to an 
improvement in debt metrics, while maintaining healthy liquidity. 
 
Negative factors – Pressure on the rating could arise if there is any sustained decline in the earnings on account of raw material 
cost inflation or weak demand. Stretched working capital cycle or delays in ramp-up of new capacity, adversely impacting debt 
metrics or liquidity position, could also exert downward pressure on the rating. Specific credit metrics that could result in a 
downgrade include DSCR less than 3.2 times on a sustained basis, with a material depletion in cash reserves and liquid 
investments.

About the company  
Incorporated in 1961, Balkrishna Industries Limited (BKT) is a leading manufacturer and exporter of specialty off-highway tyres 
(OHTs) and caters to a diverse range of applications, including agriculture, mining, industrial, construction, earthmoving and 
all-terrain vehicle (ATV) segments. It is promoted by Mr. Arvind Poddar and Mr. Rajiv Poddar, and the promoters held 58.29% 
equity stake in the company as on June 30,2026.  
The company has a portfolio of over 3,600 SKUs and exports its products to more than 160 countries across the globe. BKT 
operates four tyre manufacturing facilities located at Bhuj (Gujarat), Waluj (Maharashtra), Bhiwadi (Rajasthan) and Chopanki 
(Rajasthan), with an aggregate tyre manufacturing capacity of 3,60,000 MTPA, which is planned to be expanded to 4,25,000 
MTPA by FY2027. It is also engaged in manufacturing and sale of carbon black with installed capacity of 3,60,000 MTPA. BKT 
ventured into the on-highway tyres segment with the commercial launch of TBR and 2W tyres in Q1 FY2027 and intends to 
launch PCR tyres by the end of Q3 FY2027.
