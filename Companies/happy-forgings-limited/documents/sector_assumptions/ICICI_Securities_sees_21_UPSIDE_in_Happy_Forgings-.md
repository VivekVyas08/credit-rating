## **Strong quarter; growth outlook improving** 

Happy Forgings (HFL)’s Q1FY27 operating performance was ahead of our estimates. HFL’s domestic CV/PV/farm segments continue to do well on the back of wallet share gains from existing customers. Exports have also started to accelerate. HFL’s healthy order book (OB) (INR 9.5bn executable over 2–3 years) across segments is expected to drive growth, enabling it to continue outpacing peers/industry. Extending capabilities into heavier and complex components could spur profitability further. In our view, HFL has an extensive long-term growth runway given its current scale and large addressable global opportunity, owing to an ongoing global supply chain shift. Retain **BUY** ; TP revised to INR 2,200 (vs. INR 1,750), based on 40x Sep’28E EPS (vs. 35x earlier) on expanding capabilities and end-segments. 

## **Q1FY27: Ahead of estimate** 

HFL reported revenue of INR 4.5bn (+27% YoY/+6% QoQ), 3% ahead of our estimate. EBITDA came in at INR 1.41bn (+29% YoY/+6% QoQ). EBITDAM stood at 31.3% (+280bps YoY/-20bps QoQ), 80bps ahead of our estimate. PAT stood at INR 915mn (+39% YoY/+9% QoQ), 10% above our estimate. 

## **Operational update** 

HFL’s tonnage growth during Q1 was up 23.1% YoY (+3% QoQ) at 17,793MT. Realisation increased 3% YoY to INR 253/kg (+3% QoQ), led by richer product mix. **<u>Segment-wise:</u>** CVs/PVs/farm/OHV/industrial grew 7%/69%/27%/40%/ 56% YoY. Domestic + deemed exports revenue grew 27% YoY. Direct exports revenue also grew ~27% YoY. Share of machining revenue improved to 90% (vs. 89% in FY26). In case of CVs – the domestic business grew 18% YoY, while exports declined 12% YoY owing to geopolitical challenges. 

## **Demand outlook** 

HFL’s domestic CV business could see strong momentum led by new order execution (market share gain). The farm segment continues to do well; management notes that a rise in wallet share with existing customers would drive growth. For both US and EU, CV and farm segments are witnessing a gradual recovery as the underlying industry has stabilised. Execution of new programs in industrial and PV segments (alongside wallet share gain) could propel growth further. Overall, we expect HFL’s growth trajectory to improve meaningfully in FY27–29E backed by new order visibility (INR 9.5bn OB).

# **Margin outlook** 

HFL has a pass-through arrangement for changes in RM prices with a 1–3-month lag. The company is negotiating with its customers for pass-through of other inflationary costs (freight cost, etc.) and is aiming to sustain its margin around current level. This could be led by a gradual improvement in capacity utilisation (currently: ~59%), favourable product mix (new orders are at higher realisation), increase in scrap realisation and higher operating leverage. 

# **Other highlights** 

- Total new business OB stands at INR 9.5bn – executable over the next 2–3 years. OB mix by segments – Industrials: 35–40%; PVs: 25–30%; CV: 25–30%. ~60% of these orders are for exports. 

- FY27/28 capex guidance stands at ~INR 8bn for machining capacity, heavy-duty components and solar power project (INR 250–300mn p.a. cost saving from Q4FY27). 

- Regarding capex for heavy forge components, management indicated that realisation/kg and gross margins are significantly higher than the current business owing to higher complexity/weight and limited competition.
