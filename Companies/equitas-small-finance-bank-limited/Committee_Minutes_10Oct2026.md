## 4. Anchor divergence — resolved (+1 notch)

The Stage 1 anchor is A+ (composite 2.877). The Committee records **+1, "Peer benchmarking /
calibration"** (`Inputs!C63`, justified at `D63`); `Rating!C34` = 1.0. The reasons:

- **The anchor reads a trough as structural.** Its profitability input is the FY2026 trough
  ROTA of 0.18%.
- **Capital buffer.** A FY2027 credit cost of 3.99% of average assets, twice FY2026, is needed
  to take CRAR to 15%.
- **Recovery already reported.** Q4 FY2026 PAT was ₹212.68 crore and Q1 FY2027 PBT ₹244.07 crore.
- **The book is de-risked.** Microfinance is about 10% of advances, 71% of it CGFMU-covered.
- **Peer calibration.** CareEdge's AA- SFB peer (Ujjivan) has similar capital and CASA but a
  riskier mix (52% micro-banking).

The Committee **rejected AA** because of two years of ROTA below 0.5%, CASA at 26%, depositor
concentration of 20.6% and negative ALM gaps.

## 5. What the Committee weighed

**Anchor exposure.** The exposure to CareEdge's 06-Oct-2025 grade (pre-flight, AR) is declared in
workpaper §0. The view (§13) was written before Step 14. The agreement with CareEdge was noted
and not counted as evidence.

**Stable vs Negative.** The Committee considered a Negative outlook, because ROTA has been below
1% for two years — the agency's own trigger language in its October 2025 action. It chose Stable
because the H2 FY2026 recovery that action anticipated has occurred: Q3 PAT ₹90.03 crore, Q4
₹212.68 crore, Q1 FY2027 PBT ₹244.07 crore. The FY2027 test is quantified as a negative
sensitivity: ROTA below 0.8%.

**Capital trajectory.** Even the base case takes CRAR to about 16.5% by FY2031 at 15-18%
growth. The Committee sets the CAR trigger at 17%, with a capital plan expected.

**Information gap.** State-wise concentration is not disclosed in the documents (recorded search,
workpaper §14), while the agency treats it as a key weakness. The Committee records it as a
material monitorable and an information request.
