## Qualitative fact register (all traced to SBI documents)

- GoI stake **55.03%** (AR FY2026 shareholding table) — above 51% floor, headroom for dilution.
- **Rs. 25,000-crore QIP in July 2025** — largest ever in Indian capital markets; >64% foreign participation (AR FY2026). Drove CET-1 +148 bps YoY along with retained earnings (year-end profit inclusion explains Q4 jump 10.99→12.29).
- D-SIB buffer **0.80%** (Pillar 3 row 67) — SBI is India's most systemically important bank (highest D-SIB bucket).
- RWA Rs. 42.51 lakh crore; RWA/total assets ≈ 55.8% (Pillar 3).
- Customers 53 crore+; ~27% of UPI ecosystem volume; 10 crore+ YONO users; 245 overseas offices, 29 countries (AR FY2026).
- Dividend Rs. 17.35/share FY2026; EPS Rs. 87.59.
- Strong subsidiaries (SBI Life, SBI Funds — AUM leadership, SBI Cards, SBI General, SBI Capital) — support cushion, minor capital-call risk.

## Independent rating view (formed BEFORE peer/anchor comparison)

- **Standalone profile:** strongest deposit franchise and systemic position in India; asset quality at decade-best levels (all 929 asset-quality metrics in "strong" band); capital comfortable post-QIP; earnings healthy though NIM-cyclical. Weakest links (CASA drift, CD ratio rise, op-profit/ATA) are franchise-manageable and typical of the cycle.
- **Support:** GoI 55.03% + most systemically important bank → highest support expectation; PONV probability remote (GoI's demonstrated pre-emptive capitalisation stance per 929).
- **Senior/issuer level: [ICRA]AAA (Stable).**
- **Tier 2 notching decision (929 + 998):** notching from senior applies where PONV invocation risk is meaningful (weak capital/asset quality, lower rating categories). SBI: cushions >3% on all tiers, strong solvency, sovereign backstop → **no notching. Tier 2 Series 2: [ICRA]AAA (Stable).**
