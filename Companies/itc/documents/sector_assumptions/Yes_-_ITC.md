### Staggered price hikes & geopolitical impact to hurt near-term margins 

4QFY26 performance: ITC Ltd. (ITC) 4QFY26 performance was most likely to have been benefitted by cigarette pipeline filing in anticipation of a price hike (Net of Excise Duty/NCCD on Sales, cig. revenue would have been up by 10-11% YoY). While deferred sales in Agri-business amidst West Asia conflict had an impact on the quarter. 

Cig. EBIT growth improves further: Cig. EBIT growth stood at 7.2%. For FY26, consumption of high-cost leaf tobacco inventory weighed on margin, which was partially eased through product mix improvement and strategic cost management. Going forward, starting from 1QFY27 cigarette volumes will be under pressure due to steep price hike led by unprecedented tax hike. Also, staggered price action will benefit ITC within the legal cigarette industry but will put pressure on near-term margins. 

FMCG-Others saw another quarter of strong performance: FMCG-Others business saw a robust quarter with 14.7% revenue growth and ~170bps YoY improvement in EBITDA margins. Paperboards, Paper & Packaging (PPP) continue to see improvement in its operating performance with EBIT margin up 200bps QoQ (now up 180bps YoY). Agri Business revenue was down sharply YoY due to deferral of sales amidst West Asia conflict which we believe will largely reverse as and when situation comes to normalcy. 

Valuation & View: Resilient rural markets, early signs of urban recovery, progressive scale-up of export shipments of Nicotine & Nicotine derivative products, low base (especially FMCG-Others and PPP) and recent acquisitions should aid growth in FY27 but sharp increase in cigarette taxes is a big dampener. Dividend yield remains the best in the industry at 4.7%. Based on target multiple of ~20x (discount on its 5yr avg fwd. multiple of ~23x) now on March’28E EPS, we get a revised target price (TP) of Rs340 (Rs380 earlier). Maintain ADD. 

##### Result Highlights (Standalone) 

- **Headline performance:** ITC’s 4QFY26 standalone revenue (adjusted for excise duty) was down 6.9% YoY to Rs160.5bn (vs est. of Rs175.2bn). EBITDA was up by 7.3% YoY to Rs64.3bn (vs est. Rs59.6bn). APAT (Adjusted profit from continued operations) was up 4.9%YoY to Rs51.1bn (vs est. Rs49.2bn). 

- **Cigarette** revenue grew by 31.7% YoY to Rs110.7bn. Net of Excise Duty/NCCD on Sales revenue was up 10-11%. Cig. business EBIT grew by 7.2% YoY. 

- **Other businesses:** FMCG-Others revenue grew by 14.7% YoY to Rs63bn. Segment EBITDA margin stood at 10% (+170bps YoY); EBIT margins up 200bps YoY to 8.3%. Agri business was down 15.7% YoY to Rs30.7bn with EBIT margin down 120bps YoY to 5.8% as EBIT was down 29.6% YoY. Paperboards, Paper & Packaging (PPP) revenue was up just 1.8% YoY to Rs22.3bn with segment EBIT margin up 180bps YoY to 11%. 

- **Margin:** Gross margin improved to 64% (+930bps YoY and +910bps QoQ). While EBITDA margin was up 530bps YoY to 40% (vs est. 34%). 

- **FY26** : Revenue, EBITDA and APAT grew by 3.6%, 4.9% and 4.7% YoY, respectively. Gross/EBITDA margin are up 100bps/40bps YoY to 55.2%/35.1%.

## ANALYST VIEW & INVESTMENT THESIS 

### 1-Year & 3 -Year View 

There is -17% downward revision in our FY27E EPS as we bake in the impact on cigarette volumes and margins due to unprecedented hike in cigarette taxes and staggered price action by ITC. Going forward with rural markets staying resilient and urban showing early signs of recovery, growth rates across other key businesses should improve over the next few quarters except Agribusiness which will be dependent on improvement in geo-political scenario. 

We now build just 1.4% EPS CAGR over FY26-FY28E due to subdued revenue CAGR and 50bps EBITDA margin contraction on a base already impacted by sharp escalation in leaf tobacco and subdued demand. Lower tobacco leaf procurement cost should be of some support for operating performance in coming quarters. 

PPP business has been facing pressure from multiple issues impacting both topline and profitability of the business. There are signs of improved operating performance in last 2 quarters but it doesn’t move a needle for the overall business. 

Resilient rural markets, early signs of urban recovery, progressive scale-up of export shipments of Nicotine & Nicotine derivative products, low base (especially FMCG-Others and PPP) and recent acquisitions should aid growth in FY27 but sharp increase in cigarette taxes is a big dampener. Dividend yield remains the best in the industry at 4.7%. ITC is currently trading at ~20x/18x FY27E/FY28E EPS. Based on target multiple of ~20x (discount on its 5yr avg fwd. multiple of ~23x) now on March’28E EPS, we get a revised target price (TP) of Rs340 (Rs380 earlier). Maintain ADD. 

Over the medium-term, we don’t see any major change in EBIT mix of the business which currently continues to be dominated by cigarette business. Hence, cigarette EBIT growth leading to earnings growth will continue to be a key driver of stock price movement over the mediumterm. Stability in cigarette tax regime and recovery in FMCG-Other as well as PPP business will be key to growth.
