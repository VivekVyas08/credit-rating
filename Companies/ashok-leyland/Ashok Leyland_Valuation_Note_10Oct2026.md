## Market cushion

Market price Rs 150.48 on 01-Oct-2026 (third-party aggregator,
[Incredmoney](https://www.incredmoney.com/stocks/ashok-leyland-ltd); not verified against
NSE/BSE; 52-week range Rs 134.21-215.42) × 587.39 crore shares = **market capitalisation

The DCF is 0.68 times the market price. The market prices in more growth, or a lower discount
rate, than this deliberately conservative base case, which assumes a cyclical dip in FY2030.

## Credit reading

Equity is not a source of liquidity a lender can count on (ICRA_CONVENTIONS §8 counts only
committed funding). But a market capitalisation of ~74 times debt, AL's own listing (NSE/BSE),
and a demonstrated track record of raising debt (rated CP programme of Rs 2,000 crore,
undrawn bank lines of Rs 2,854.34 crore) together support a **strong financial-flexibility**
assessment. A share-price fall of 40% — about the FY2019-21 drawdown in revenue — would still
leave the cushion above 40 times debt.
