## 4. Anchor divergence — expressly resolved (+1 notch, at AA+)

The Stage 1 anchor is AA (composite 2.016). The Committee records **+1 notch, "Peer benchmarking /
calibration"** in `Inputs!C63`, justified at `D63`; Stage 3 computes [ICRA]AA+ (Stable)/[ICRA]A1+
and `Rating!C34` shows a 1-notch divergence. The reasons:
- AL's financial-risk score (1.54) belongs to the AA+/AAA cohort: standalone Total debt/OPBDIT
  0.21x, interest coverage 33.77x and net cash of Rs 5,755.76 crore.
- Liquidity survives a repeat of FY2019-21 without borrowing.
- The 18-Jul-2026 back-test documents that the composite-to-grade map under-rates AA+/AAA
  issuers.

The Committee **rejected +2 (AAA)**, for three reasons:
- Single-industry CV cyclicality: volumes −49% FY2019-21, and a loss in FY2021.
- A second-place M&HCV position that is eroding: 31.8% → 30.3% since FY2023.
- Recurring cash calls from the group, which run from AL outward: promoter-group ICDs up to
  Rs 670 crore, Rs 2,670.36 crore of equity into subsidiaries over FY2024-26, guarantees of
  Rs 785.71 crore, and a captive NBFC whose credit costs nearly doubled.

## 5. What the Committee weighed

**Anchor exposure.** The Committee noted the exposure to ICRA's 02-Jun-2026 grade (pre-flight,
the AR's §U, and the Step 7b over-read), declared in workpaper §0. It satisfied itself that the
view rests on the Compliance Map: every rated-entity metric is in the strongest band, the
automotive metrics are in the strong band, and the constraints are business and governance, not
leverage. The two analyses agree; the Committee did not treat that agreement as evidence in
itself.

**Liquidity — Superior, not ICRA's Strong.** Accepted. ICRA's descriptor rests on December-2025
cash of about Rs 3,800 crore. The audited March-2026 free liquidity is Rs 6,950.80 crore, and
FY2027 operating cash flow alone covers every call on cash (workpaper §11).

**Governance.** The ICDs to promoter-group companies were nil at each year-end, and no default,
qualification or covenant breach exists. The Committee therefore treats them as a constraint on
the rating's ceiling and a named sensitivity, not as a notch off the standalone profile. A
recurrence at a larger scale, or any ICD outstanding at a period-end, would be an event for
review.

**Why Stable.** The net-cash balance sheet gives wide headroom, while the group constraint is not
expected to resolve within 12-18 months.

## 6. Surveillance (R15)

Next scheduled review: by June 2027, after FY2027 results (and no later than 15 months for the
bank facilities, per RBI). Event-driven triggers:
- any ICD to promoter-group companies outstanding at a quarter-end, or a single-year peak above
  Rs 1,000 crore;
- equity support to subsidiaries or HLF above Rs 1,000 crore in a year;
- automotive Total debt/OPBDIT above 1.5x;
- M&HCV share below 28%;
- NCLT outcome on the HLF-NDL merger;
- notification of state Labour Code rules with a material charge.

Default monitoring by facility type: under ICRA's practice for listed issuers, the NCD and
term loans are monitored on their scheduled dates; the commercial paper on maturity; cash credit
on continuous overdrawal. Data feed: quarterly results and SEBI LODR disclosures (ICRA's own
default-recognition policy to be applied as published; CRISIL's facility-type table is not
carried over — FM55).
