## 1. What the company is

Ashok Leyland Limited (AL) is the Indian commercial-vehicle (CV) maker of the Hinduja Group.
Hinduja Automotive Limited, UK, holds 50.85% and is the holding company (FY2026 AR). AL makes
medium and heavy commercial vehicles (M&HCV: trucks and buses), light commercial vehicles (LCV),
engines and gensets ("Power Solutions"), ferrous castings and defence vehicles, and sells spare
parts and services.

**FY2026 at a glance (standalone, audited):** revenue from operations Rs 44,007.03 crore (+13.6%);
OPBDITA Rs 5,732.25 crore (13.03%); PAT Rs 3,565.53 crore; total vehicles 2,20,437 (a record,
+13.0% on 1,95,097); exports 18,082 units; standalone net cash Rs 5,755.76 crore after excluding
earmarked balances (engine). Revenue mix (note 3.7.1): commercial vehicles Rs 36,842.91 crore
(82% of gross product + service + other operating revenue of Rs 44,731.26 crore), spare parts and
others Rs 4,450.37 crore, engines and gensets Rs 1,414.29 crore, services Rs 1,257.11 crore,
castings Rs 602.41 crore. Outside India: Rs 3,090.13 crore, 7.0% of contract revenue.

nos. in FY 2018-19", FY2026 AR MD&A). The upcycle is now five years old.

## 5. Industry

India's domestic CV market sold 10,79,871 units in FY2026 (+12.6%): M&HCV 4,22,988 (+12.9%),
LCV 6,56,873 (+12.5%); CV exports 94,793 (+17.4%) (SIAM, quoted in the FY2026 AR MD&A). The
company's outlook is measured: "FY27 is expected to mark a year of disciplined,
consolidation-led growth for the Indian commercial vehicle industry" (MD&A). The drivers it names
are fleet replacement, vehicle scrappage, infrastructure spending and the shift to
higher-tonnage trucks; the threats include West Asia-related fuel risk (MD&A).

Industry structure **[web]**: Tata Motors leads domestic M&HCV, with an Emkay-reported Q3 FY2026
domestic M&HCV share of 47.9% ([AutocarPro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tata-motors-cv-unit-targets-40-percent-market-share-by-fy28-banks-on-iveco-deal-and-digital-platforms-133189));
AL is second ([Trucks & Buses, Q4 FY2026](https://www.trucksbuses.com/news/tata-motors-leads-mhcv-goods-carrier-sales-across-india-in-q4-fy26-ashok-leyland-holds-strong-second-position));
VE Commercial Vehicles (Eicher-Volvo) sold 1,03,404 units in FY2026 and is strongest in light and
medium-duty trucks; Daimler India (BharatBenz) truck sales fell 5% in CY2025 (Autobei). The
sources use different bases, so the figures are not comparable with each other or with AL's.

## 6. The group around the rated entity

AL standalone carries the rated instruments. Around it (FY2026 AR):

- **Hinduja Leyland Finance (HLF, 61.12%)** — the captive vehicle financier. Gross loans
  Rs 49,156.66 crore (+30%), ECL Rs 1,279.86 crore (Rs 888.22 crore), FY2026 credit-cost charge
  ("impairment relating to financing activities") Rs 1,234.24 crore against Rs 651.95 crore. Its
  merger into the listed NDL Ventures Limited (25 NDL shares for 10 HLF shares) has RBI
  (08-Aug-2025), CCI (17-Feb-2026) and BSE (18-May-2026) clearances and awaits NCLT, Mumbai.
  HLF holds 92.6% of reported group borrowings (engine).
- **EV and technology subsidiaries** — Optare Plc (93.28%) owns Switch Mobility UK, which stopped
  manufacturing at Sherburn; Switch Mobility India "achieved net profitability". AL has put
  Rs 2,069 crore into Optare since FY2024 (Rs 1,200 + 498.76 + 370.58 crore) and Rs 300 crore into
  OHM Global Mobility; Optare group's net assets are Rs 130.78 crore against AL's cost of
  Rs 3,000.23 crore, and the carrying value is a Key Audit Matter.
- **Other loss-makers (FY2026, Schedule III):** Hinduja Tech (−199.60), Optare group (−148.26),
  OHM (−35.69), Gro Digital (−17.00), Albonair GmbH (−10.61), Ashley Aviation (−2.22) — Rs crore.
- **Guarantees** for subsidiaries Rs 785.71 crore (Switch Mobility UK 633.12, Albonair GmbH
  152.59), plus open-ended support undertakings (notes 3.9.2, 3.10).

## 7. Risks (credit lens)
