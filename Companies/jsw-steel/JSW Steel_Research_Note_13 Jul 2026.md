## 1. TL;DR

- **FY2026 was transformed by one transaction:** JSW transferred Bhushan Power & Steel (BPSL) into a 50:50 JV with JFE Steel (slump sale to JSW–JFE entity for ₹29,475 cr, completed 27-Mar-2026), deconsolidating BPSL and booking an **₹18,051 cr non-cash exceptional gain** (AR FY26 Note 48). Reported PAT of ₹22,316 cr therefore massively overstates run-rate earnings — the company's own normalised FY26 net profit is **₹8,698 cr** (Q4 FY26 results deck).
- **Deleveraging is real and large:** net debt fell from ~₹80,300 cr (Dec-25) to **₹53,870 cr** (Mar-26, company definition, ex-leases); net debt/EBITDA **2.91x → 1.81x** in one quarter (Q3/Q4 decks), with a further ~₹7,880 cr expected by end-Jun-26 on JFE's second tranche (Centrum, 15-May-26).
- **Operations recovered:** FY26 operating income ₹185,470 cr (+9.9% YoY), OPBDITA ₹29,821 cr at a 16.1% margin — back to FY24's margin from the FY25 trough of 13.6% — helped by the 12% safeguard duty (domestic prices +~₹3,500/t by Jan-26, ICICI Direct).
- **The counterweight is capex:** ~**₹1.26 lakh cr approved for FY27–31** (Nuvama), taking India capacity 31.9 → ~48.8 mtpa by FY30 (company roadmap; ICICI Direct maps ~55.5 mtpa by FY31 including BPSL JV expansion), plus the POSCO 6 mtpa JV (Apr-26). Whether leverage stays inside management's self-tightened 3.0x net-debt/EBITDA ceiling through this programme is the central credit and equity question.
- **Street is split on exactly that:** two BUYs (Motilal Oswal ₹1,350; Anand Rathi ₹1,340; plus Mirae Asset Sharekhan ₹1,500), one HOLD (ICICI Direct ₹1,300), one NEUTRAL (Centrum ₹1,294, cut from BUY), one REDUCE (Nuvama ₹1,129). The divergence hinges on the multiple paid for debt-funded growth, not on the operating outlook.

## 2. Company and segments

India's largest steelmaker: ~30.1 mt FY26 sales volume across flats, longs and value-added products, ~10% exports (AR FY26; workpaper compliance map). Five reporting segments per the AR segment note; steel dominates the profit pool. Group structure is complex — **46 subsidiaries, 19 JVs, 5 associates** (AR FY26), with financial guarantees extended to overseas subsidiaries (JSW Netherlands, Periama, Acero Junction) and loan-loss allowances on overseas exposures.

**Strategic events in/after FY26** (all traced to company decks/AR unless noted): Supreme Court **upheld** JSW's BPSL resolution plan on 26-Sep-2025 (Q2 deck) — the JFE JV that followed is a value-unlock, not a distress response; BPSL slump-sale/deconsolidation 27-Mar-26; JFE paying ₹15,750 cr for 50% (tranche 1 ₹7,875 cr received Mar-26); Illawarra coal stake to 30% (Q2 deck); MdR Mozambique coal acquisition completed and BMM Ispat approved (Q4 deck); POSCO 6 mtpa Odisha greenfield JV (Apr-26); Piombino merger to lift promoter holding to 46.74% (Anand Rathi). Raw-material integration targets: ~50 MT captive iron ore and 25% captive coking coal by FY31 (ICICI Direct, from company guidance).

## 7. What to watch (dated where known)

1. **JFE Tranche-II** (~₹7,880 cr) — expected by end-Jun-2026 (Centrum); Anand Rathi notes slump-sale/Tranche-II processes conclude by Sep-2026. Confirms the final deleveraging quantum (~₹37,000 cr total).
2. **Net debt/EBITDA vs the 3.0x management ceiling** each quarter as the ₹1.26 lakh cr programme accelerates (Utkal Odisha ₹31,600 cr 5 mtpa Ph-1 to FY30; Dolvi +5 mtpa by Sep-27; Salem +5 mtpa by FY30).
3. **Safeguard-duty step-down** (11.5% FY27, 11% FY28) against coking-coal costs — EBITDA/t trajectory (ICICI Direct models ₹8.8k → ₹10.8k FY26–28E).
4. **Rating-watch resolutions** at ICRA and India Ratings (both Positive watch triggered by the JV announcement, per the AR rating note and the ICRA Dec-25 rationale) now that the transaction closed ahead of the agencies' 2.1–2.2x leverage expectation.
5. **Piombino merger** completion (promoter stake → 46.74%, Anand Rathi) and any movement in the ~11.8% promoter pledge.
6. **BMM Ispat and POSCO JV** funding structure — whether JV-level debt stays non-recourse.
