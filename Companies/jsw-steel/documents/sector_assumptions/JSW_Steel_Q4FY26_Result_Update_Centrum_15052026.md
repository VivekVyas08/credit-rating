

# **JSW Steel** 

## **Scale-up story intact, valuations now fair** 

**JSW Steel’s (JSTL IN) Q4FY26 consolidated net sales increased ~12% YoY and ~10% QoQ to ~Rs 498bn, broadly in line with our and Consensus estimates of ~Rs 486bn and ~Rs 499bn, respectively. EBITDA excluding one-off incentive income of ~Rs 4.6bn grew ~28% YoY and ~26% QoQ to ~Rs 82bn, largely in line with our and Consensus estimates of ~Rs 81bn and ~Rs 85bn, respectively. Adjusted for exceptional gain of ~Rs 180bn on Bhushan Power and Steel (BPSL) JV deal, PAT stood at ~Rs 35.2bn vs ~Rs 15.3bn in Q4FY25 and ~Rs 10.7bn in Q3FY26. The BPSL transaction resulted in deleveraging of ~Rs 295bn, reducing net debt to ~Rs 539bn as of March 2026 vs ~Rs 803bn as of December 2025, with further deleveraging of ~Rs 78.8bn expected by end-June'26 upon JFE Steel’s (JFE) second tranche investment. Going ahead, while near-term cost pressure may weigh on margin, we expect this to be offset by higher steel prices. Further, multiple ongoing expansion projects provide growth visibility, while management’s lower leverage ceiling targets indicate balance sheet discipline despite elevated capex. We maintain our TP of Rs 1,294, based on 8.5x (unchanged) March 2028E EV/EBITDA with 50% weight to CWIP. With the stock trading near our TP, we revise JSTL to NEUTRAL from BUY.** 

#### **EBITDA/tonne rises ~21% YoY and QoQ each on higher prices** 

Consolidated sales volume grew ~6% YoY and ~4% QoQ to 7.92mn tonnes. Operating cost rose ~5% YoY and ~4% QoQ to Rs 53,720/tonne. However, realization grew ~6% YoY and QoQ each to Rs 62,876/tonne. Consequently, EBITDA/tonne surged ~21% YoY and QoQ each to Rs 10,318 vs our estimate of Rs 10,666. 

#### **Capacity expansion roadmap remains aggressive…** 

JSTL is pursuing a robust long-term expansion plan, aiming to grow standalone capacity from current 31.9mn tonnes to 48.8mn tonnes by FY30. Including the 4.5mn tonnes capacity at the JSW-JFE JV, total India capacity is likely to reach 53.3mn tonnes by FY30. By FY32, standalone capacity is targeted at 62mn tonnes and JV capacity at 16mn tonnes, taking total India capacity to ~78mn tonnes. As part of this strategy, the Board has approved a 5mn tonnes brownfield expansion at Vijayanagar (Karnataka) with a capex of ~Rs 260bn, taking Vijayanagar’s capacity to ~25mn tonnes by FY30.

**…with JVs and inorganic initiatives complementing growth strategy** 

Further, JSTL has partnered with POSCO in a 50:50 JV to build a 6mn tonnes integrated steel plant at Dhenkanal (Odisha) and also aiming to synergize with POSCO's 1.8mn tonnes downstream unit in Maharashtra. Also, JSTL has approved the amalgamation of BMM Ispat (BMMIL) at an enterprise value of ~Rs 64bn, strengthening its long products portfolio and aim to raise capacity from 0.9mn tonnes to 1.8mn tonnes. 

#### **Valuation and outlook** 

Following the deconsolidation of BPSL from end-FY26 onwards, our FY27 estimates now exclude BPSL numbers vs earlier assumptions of nine months consolidation. Thus, our FY27 EBITDA estimate is cut by ~6%, while raised by ~3% for FY28.  We maintain our TP of Rs 1,294, based on 8.5x (unchanged) March 2028E EV/EBITDA with 50% weight to CWIP. With the stock trading near our TP, we revise JSTL to **NEUTRAL** from BUY.
