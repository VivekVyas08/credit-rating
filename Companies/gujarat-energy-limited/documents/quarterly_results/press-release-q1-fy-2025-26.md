About the Company
Gujarat Gas Limited is the largest City Gas Distribution Company in India. The company has a
successful track record of providing uninterrupted services to households for over 3 decades
through a network of 43,300+ kms of gas pipeline, distributing approx. 8.88 mmscmd of natural
gas in Q1 FY 26. The company operates 830 CNG stations and provides natural gas to more than
23.02 lakh households in six states and one union territory.
