## 4. Independent rating view (Step 13 — formed BEFORE opening the prior action)

Strong capitalisation (CET1 16.2%) + large established franchise support a high-AA profile. The FY26 earnings collapse (RoA 0.18%) + the FY25 governance/accounting episode are the binding constraints; the microfinance-led asset-quality deterioration is cyclical and stabilising. Q1FY27 recovery (RoA 0.78%) + the approved capital raise are mitigants.
**Initial independent landing: Crisil AA/Negative (senior), A1+ (short-term).**

## 5. Liquidity (Step 11)
LCR averaged **118%** through FY26 (well above the 100% minimum); the bank held surplus liquidity (₹39,600 cr excess in FY25; ₹53,131 cr in Q1FY25 per prior disclosures). Deposits are granular (retail 47.9%); ₹52,502 cr of deposits carry a lien (encumbered — not freely available). **Descriptor: Strong** (conclusion of the arithmetic, not an adjective) — a large, granular, retail-anchored funding base with comfortable regulatory buffers, tempered by the deposit-franchise pressure through the stress period.
