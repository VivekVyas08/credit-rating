## 4. Anchor divergence — resolved (+1 notch)

The Stage 1 anchor is Crisil AA+ (composite 1.732). The Committee records **+1, "Business /
franchise strength beyond the financial composite"** (`Inputs!C61`, justified at `D61`);
`Rating!C34` = 1.0. The reasons:

- **Diversity the composite cannot see.** No segment above 39.5% of FY2026 segment EBITDA; the
  consumer share rose from 46.7% to 53.4% (FY2024-26); in FY2025 an O2C fall of Rs 7,401 crore
  was more than offset by Digital and Retail (+Rs 10,312 crore).
- **Liquidity and flexibility beyond the template's bands.** Cash and marketable securities of Rs
  2,49,704 crore cover debt due within a year plus CP 4.85 times; market capitalisation is about
  3.2 times adjusted debt.
- **Direction of leverage.** Base case adjusted debt/PBDIT 2.30x in FY2027 falling to 1.60x by
  FY2031 with no new borrowing.
- **Known template bias.** The back-test (18-Jul-2026) shows the anchor under-rates AA+/AAA.

The Committee **did not treat** the AR's statement of CRISIL AAA (Stable) as evidence (anchor
exposure declared, workpaper §0).

## 5. What the Committee weighed

**AAA vs AA+.** The stress case (O2C trough, slower consumer growth, capex +15%, a Rs 1 lakh crore
working-capital unwind) peaks at adjusted debt/PBDIT 3.79x and coverage 3.43x — weaker than AAA
metrics. The Committee held AAA because the stress combines four adverse moves at once, the
cash floor of Rs 2 lakh crore holds throughout, and the base case improves every year.

**Stable vs Negative.** Considered because of the Rs 10 lakh crore AI data-centre plan, which has
no disclosed phasing or funding. Stable was chosen because the plan is not in any filed capex
guidance; it is instead written as a quantified downward sensitivity (each Rs 1 lakh crore of
debt-funded spend adds ~0.46x to FY2027 adjusted debt/PBDIT; trigger 3.5x).

**Wide debt measure.** Including supplier finance (Rs 76,377 crore), adjusted debt/PBDIT is 2.91x;
the Committee is satisfied the rating holds on either measure and asks for the reason behind the
Rs 1,09,196 crore rise in contract liabilities.

## 6. Surveillance (R15)

- **Next review:** after H1 FY2027 results (October-November 2026), and annually thereafter; bank
  facilities reviewed at least once in 15 months (RBI).
- **Event triggers:**
  - adjusted debt/PBDIT above 3.5x, or net adjusted debt/PBDIT above 2.0x;
  - announced funding structure for the AI data-centre programme, or capex materially above
    Rs 1.5 lakh crore a year;
  - consolidated PBDIT below about Rs 1.75 lakh crore;
  - an adverse Supreme Court outcome on the KG D6 demand or the SEBI RPL matter;
  - the Jio Platforms IPO (change in RIL's stake and cash).
- **Default monitoring:** per CRISIL basics of ratings, Annexure I to Section III, by facility type:
  debentures, CP, term loan and ECB — one day / one rupee; working-capital and buyer's credit
  limits — continuously overdrawn more than 30 days; letters of credit — 30 days from
  devolvement; bank guarantees — 30 days from invocation.
