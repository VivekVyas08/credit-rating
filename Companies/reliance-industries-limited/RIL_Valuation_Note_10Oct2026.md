## 2. DCF of the consolidated business (cross-check only)

Unlevered free cash flow = PBDIT excluding treasury income, less cash tax on EBIT (23% statutory
x 50% cash share, matching the model), capex and the change in working capital (base case).
ANALYST ASSUMPTIONS: WACC 11.0%, terminal growth 5.0%. Net adjusted debt (5,02,514 - 2,49,704 =
2,52,810) and non-controlling interest at book (1,81,836) are deducted.

| Output | Value |
|---|---|
| Implied equity value | Rs 11,67,560 crore |
| Per share (1,353.25 crore shares) | Rs 863 |
| Market price | Rs 1,170 |

The DCF sits below the market price because the base case carries no value for the AI and
new-energy programmes beyond their capex, deducts minorities at book (not market), and uses a
conservative cash-tax treatment; the sensitivity grid (WACC 10-12%, g 4-6%) is in the Valuation
tab and chart 6. None of this changes the credit reading in §1.
