## 1. What the company is

Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) reported FY2026
value of sales and services Rs 11,75,919 crore and revenue from operations (net of GST) Rs
10,75,675 crore (FY2025-26 AR, consolidated P&L). It runs five reported segments (audited segment
information, Q4 FY2026 release):

The promoter group holds 49.11% (FY2025-26 AR, distribution of shareholding), through LLPs

India (63.16%), Reliance New Energy (100%) (Form AOC-1).

## 2. The shift in the earnings mix (the trend is the finding)

Consumer businesses (Retail + Digital) rose from 46.7% of segment EBITDA in FY2024 to 51.5% in
FY2025 and 53.4% in FY2026; energy (O2C + Oil and Gas) fell from 48.3% to 41.0%. The FY2025 O2C
downturn shows why that matters: O2C EBITDA fell from 62,389 to 54,988 (-7,401) while Digital
(+8,326) and Retail (+1,986) more than replaced it, so total segment EBITDA still rose (1,70,829 to
1,74,797). In FY2026 O2C recovered to 60,546 and Digital grew 17.8% to 76,560.

## 4. Capex and the next investment cycle

Company-reported capex (excluding spectrum) was 1,31,769 / 1,31,107 / 1,44,271 in FY2024-26 and
38,682 in Q1 FY2027. Capital commitments doubled to 66,517 (note 34). The FY2025-26 AR states a
plan "to invest Rs 10 lakh crore for the development of multi-gigawatt (GW) scale AI-ready data
centres" and a Rs 75,000 crore clean-energy commitment (100 GW renewable capacity by 2030; battery
giga-factory of 40 GWh commissioning from 2026; 3 MMTPA green hydrogen by 2032). No phasing or
funding plan for the AI programme is disclosed — the central forward question for the credit.

## 6. Management, governance and events

- Auditors Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP and Chaturvedi & Shah LLP — unmodified opinions; KAMs on
  litigation, a Level-3 valuation (Rs 77,852 crore JDFPL preference shares, standalone), Jio
  revenue recognition and IT controls.
- Open matters: KG D6 gas-migration demand USD 2.81 bn (RIL share USD 1.87 bn), SLP filed; SEBI
  RPL disgorgement Rs 447.27 crore plus 12% interest, Supreme Court judgment reserved
  30-Jan-2026; Hathway and DEN license-fee demands (3,202 / 2,157) contested.
- Q1 FY2027: Jio Platforms filed its DRHP with SEBI; Moody's upgraded RIL to Baa1; S&P upgraded
  RIL to A- in Dec-2025 (AR).
