## What this means for the credit
1. **Financial flexibility is strong for the rating level.** Market cap covers total debt ~8.4x; the promoters hold 66.69% with no pledge disclosed, so a meaningful equity-raise or monetisation lever exists without control loss. Listed-equity access + five-bank lender diversification (HDFC, Citi, ICICI, Bajaj Finance, Axis Finance) support refinancing capacity.
2. **The flexibility is sentiment-sensitive.** The multiple rests on the same unproven margin path the rating flags; a failed recovery would compress both the equity cushion and the credit headroom together (960: financial flexibility can erode with mcap decline). This is why flexibility supports but does not lift the rating.
3. **No DCF performed** — a full intrinsic valuation adds nothing to the credit question at this rating level; the market-multiple context above is sufficient for the flexibility assessment. (FCF actuals: FY26 ₹98.1 cr; base-case NCA FY27 ~₹108 cr — from the model.)
