> ### ⚠ CRISIL VARIANT — the section is titled **"ESG Profile"** and follows a fixed 4-part shape
> Every CRISIL rationale carries it (verified in the reference library: Craftsman Automation
> 29-Jun-2026 L88-102; Hindustan Zinc 07-Aug-2026). **Omitting it is a section-completeness
> failure — it was omitted from four CRISIL rationales in this project on 11-13 Aug 2026 even
> though a peer report carrying it had been read the same session (CLAUDE.md failure mode 19).**
> Reproduce this shape:
>
> 1. **One-line verdict:** *"The ESG profile of {{Co}} supports/does not constrain its {{strong}} credit risk profile."*
> 2. **Sector-level impact paragraph:** environmental impact of the SECTOR (emissions, water, waste, energy/resource intensity) then social impact of the SECTOR (workforce size, community, health & safety) — then one line that the entity is mitigating.
> 3. **"ESG highlights:"** bullets, each with a COMPANY-DISCLOSED NUMBER, covering: emissions/climate targets (net-zero year, SBTi, Scope 1/2/3 reduction %) · renewable/energy · water & biodiversity · social (gender diversity %, training, LTIFR) · **governance** (board size, % independent/women directors, board-level ESG committee, disclosure quality) — plus any auditor EOM/qualification, which belongs HERE as well as in the drivers.
> 4. **Closing paragraph:** growing ESG importance to investors/lenders, tied to the issuer's funding access (market borrowing share, capital-market access).
>
> **Sourcing rule:** every ESG figure comes from the COMPANY's own integrated/annual report or BRSR
> (Indian listed issuers publish BRSR — grep `BRSR|Business Responsibility|Scope 1|Scope 2|LTIFR|
> water positive|net zero|renewable|women director|gender diversity|CSR`). Never assert an ESG claim
> without its number and source. If the documents carry no ESG disclosure, say so explicitly —
> "ESG disclosures were not available in the documents reviewed" — rather than dropping the section.
