## The rules that govern every file here (full text: `UNIFIED_RATING_GUIDE.md` §0 R1–R11)

1. **Company documents are the ONLY data source.** A third-party database (screener.in) or web research may only **verify** an AR-extracted figure or give **labelled context** — never supply a report figure. **AR wins on disagreement.**
2. **All three agencies' reference rationales AND published rating actions are craft/context only** — never a source of any figure, not even attributed.
3. **Undisclosed amounts** (typically CP/bank-facility limits) = **"to be confirmed by issuer"**; never reconstructed. *A total that ties while lines are wrong is still wrong.*
4. **Rated LIMITS ≠ drawn BORROWINGS** — state both; reconcile at component level (LT/ST/leases).
5. **Never mix computation bases in one ratio row** — show each, labelled, with a "Basis of preparation" note.
6. **Every number computed in code; every claim traced.** No "~" figures.
7. **Your view forms before the prior report is opened** (Step 13 before Step 14).
8. **Every step has a stop-and-ask gate** — ask, then wait.

## Scope note
Covers **non-financial corporates** and **financial-sector issuers** (banks/SFBs, NBFCs, HFCs) fully; **insurers** are routed in `02` but the `Rating_Scorecard.xlsx` anchor does **not** cover them (solvency-margin framework differs) — rate them outside the scorecard.
