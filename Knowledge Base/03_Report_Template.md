## House style — the three, compared

| | ICRA | CRISIL | CareEdge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rating written | `[ICRA]AA (Stable)` / `[ICRA]A1+` | `Crisil AA/Stable` / `Crisil A1+` | `CARE AA; Stable` / `CARE A1+` (semicolon) |
| Currency | "Rs. <n> crore" | "Rs <n> crore"; "1 crore = 10 million" | **₹** <n> crore |
| Typical mandate | often instrument-specific (e.g. CP-only -> **no outlook**) | **bank facilities** LT+ST for large corporates | bank facilities and/or instruments; CP = "Commercial Paper (Standalone)" |
| Signature requirement | methodology **name + vintage** in Analytical approach | "Links to related criteria" names exact files | **"Applicable criteria"** names every exact paper |
| Filename | `<Co>_<date>_Rating Rationale.md` | `<Co>_<date>_CRISIL_Rating Rationale.md` | `<Co>_<date>_CARE_Rating Rationale.md` |
| Running furniture | — | "None of the Directors on Crisil Ratings Limited's Board are members of rating committee…" | footer "CARE Ratings Ltd." + "Press Release" |

## Instrument-table data rule (binding, all three agencies)

Only company-document-sourced amounts appear in the rating-action table and instrument annexure (AR: NCD ISINs/coupons/amounts/maturities, CP-outstanding statement, drawn borrowings). Programme/facility **LIMITS the company does not disclose are written "to be confirmed by issuer"** — never reconstructed, **never imported from any CRA's published action** (CRA material is craft/context only). **Rating history** comes from the company's **AR ratings note**, not from a CRA record. State drawn balance-sheet borrowings separately from rated limits (different measures), and **never mix computation bases in one ratio row** — show each basis as its own labelled row, defined once in a "Basis of preparation" note.

# PART 1 — ICRA template detail

*Distilled from the 649 reference reports (exemplars: Titan Company Limited 31-Mar-2026 [corporate]; Union Bank of India 26-Mar-2026 [bank]; Deepak Builders 16-Sep-2025 [issuer not cooperating]).*

## Document header

```
<Date, e.g. March 31, 2026>

**<Company Name>: <Rating action headline>**
```
Headline patterns (verified across the 649 reports): "Ratings reaffirmed"; "Ratings reaffirmed; rated amount enhanced"; "Ratings reaffirmed and assigned for enhanced amount"; "Ratings reaffirmed; outlook revised to Positive/Negative from Stable"; "[ICRA]AA- (Stable)/[ICRA]A1+; assigned"; "Long-term rating upgraded to [ICRA]AA (Stable) from [ICRA]AA- (Positive); outlook revised to Stable from Positive; short-term rating reaffirmed"; "Ratings upgraded to [ICRA]BBB (Stable)/[ICRA]A3+"; "Ratings downgraded…"; "Continues to remain under issuer Non-Cooperating category"; "Ratings withdrawn"; "Update on Material Event". Upgrade/downgrade headlines state both new AND previous rating with outlooks.

## Section order (mandatory, in this exact sequence)

1. **Summary of rating action** — table: Instrument* | Previous rated amount (Rs. crore) | Current rated amount (Rs. crore) | Rating action. Total row. Footnote: `*Instrument details are provided in Annexure I`. Rating action cell format: `[ICRA]AAA (Stable); reaffirmed`, `[ICRA]AA+ (Stable); assigned`, `[ICRA]A1+; reaffirmed/assigned for enhanced amount`.
2. **Rationale** — 3–6 paragraphs, the analytical heart (see writing rules below). Ends with a dedicated paragraph explaining the outlook: "The Stable outlook on the long-term rating reflects ICRA's opinion that …".
3. **Key rating drivers and their description**
   - **Credit strengths** — each driver: **bold headline phrase** – followed by a dense supporting paragraph with specific numbers (market share, growth %, margins, coverage ratios, store counts etc.).
   - **Credit challenges** — same format.
4. **Environmental and social risks** — two short paragraphs: **Environmental considerations** –…, **Social considerations** –…, each stating whether exposure is low/moderate/high for entities in that industry and why. (Governance is discussed in Rationale/drivers if material.)
5. **Liquidity position: <Superior|Strong|Adequate|Stretched|Poor>** — one paragraph quantifying sources (cash & liquid investments with date, undrawn lines, expected CFO) against claims (scheduled repayments by year, capex, dividends). Must justify the descriptor.
6. **Rating sensitivities** — **Positive factors** – … / **Negative factors** – … Concrete and where possible quantified triggers (e.g. "if net TOL/TNW exceeds X times on a sustained basis"). At AAA, Positive factors = "Not applicable".
7. **Analytical approach** — 3-row table:
   | Analytical approach | Comments |
   |---|---|
   | Applicable rating methodologies | full published name AND vintage of every methodology actually applied, matching the Methodology folder titles — e.g. "Corporate Credit Rating Methodology (July 2025)" + "Rating Methodology – Jewellery (Retail) (March 2026)"; or "ICRA's Rating Methodology for Banks and Financial Institutions (October 2024)". Cite only what was read and applied; check scope statements. |
   | Parent/Group support | "Not applicable" or explanation of support factored in |
   | Consolidation/Standalone | which financials used; reference to Annexure II if consolidated |
8. **About the company** — 1–2 factual paragraphs: incorporation, ownership/promoters with % stakes and as-on date, business segments, market position, scale.
9. **Key financial indicators (audited)** — table, last 2 audited FYs + interim periods (marked *Unaudited). Rows differ by entity type:
   - *Corporate*: Operating income, PAT, OPBDIT/OI, PAT/OI, Total outside liabilities/Tangible net worth (times), Total debt/OPBDIT (times), Interest coverage (times). Source line: `Source: Company, ICRA Research; All ratios as per ICRA's calculations; Amount in Rs. crore; PAT: …; OPBDIT: …`
   - *Bank*: Total income, Profit after tax, Total assets, CET I, CRAR, PAT/ATA, Gross NPAs, Net NPAs.
   - *NBFC*: Total income, PAT, Assets under management, Gross NPA/Stage 3, CRAR, (gearing, RoMA as relevant).
10. **Status of non-cooperation with previous CRA:** Not applicable / details.
11. **Any other information:** None / details.
12. **Rating history for past three years** — table: current ratings (type, amount rated, amount outstanding where relevant, rating) + chronology of rating history for past 3 FYs with dates.
13. **Complexity level of the rated instruments** — table Instrument | Complexity indicator (Simple/Moderately Complex/Highly Complex) + standard explanatory paragraph ending "Details on the complexity levels of the instruments are available on ICRA's website: Click here".
14. **Annexure I: Instrument details** — table: ISIN | Instrument name | Date of issuance | Coupon rate | Maturity | Amount rated (Rs. crore) | Current rating and outlook. Footnote for proposed/untied amounts.
    - **Key features of the rated instruments** (additional section, used for Basel III Tier-II/AT-I bonds and similar): describes loss-absorption triggers, call options, coupon discretion.
15. **Annexure II: List of entities considered for consolidated analysis** — table: Company name | Ownership | Consolidation approach (Full consolidation/Equity method) — or "Not Applicable".
16. **ANALYST CONTACTS** (3–4 names, phone, email) → **RELATIONSHIP CONTACT** → **MEDIA AND PUBLIC RELATIONS CONTACT** → **HELPLINE FOR BUSINESS QUERIES** → **ABOUT ICRA LIMITED** → registered office/branches → copyright + standard disclaimer paragraph.

## Writing rules for the Rationale (house style)

- Third person, present/present-perfect tense; the agency speaks as "ICRA": "ICRA notes…", "ICRA expects…", "ICRA draws comfort from…", "…remains monitorable".
- Para 1: what the rating action considers — lead with the strongest structural credit factors (market position, parentage, financial profile), then recent operating performance with specific numbers (revenue growth %, margin bps movement, coverage times, period-on-period).
- Middle paras: developments and their credit impact — capex/acquisitions, leverage trajectory (name the metric: net TOL/TNW, Total debt/OPBDIT), regulatory/industry developments, forward-looking view ("likely to improve gradually, supported by…").
- Balance: strengths always tempered with "The ratings, however, remain constrained by / continue to factor in…".
- Final para: outlook justification.
- Company short name defined at first use: "Titan Company Limited's (Titan)".
- Financial-year notation: FY2026, 9M FY2026, Q3 FY2026; amounts "Rs. X crore"; ratios "X times"; margins with bps movements; dates "as on March 31, 2025".
- First-person plural never used; no hedging like "we believe".
- Footnotes for technical definitions (e.g. SMA-1/SMA-2 in bank reports).

## Key rating driver rules

- 2–4 strengths, 2–3 challenges typically.
- Headline pattern: bolded noun-phrase capturing the driver + qualifier, e.g. "**Market leadership position in the branded jewellery and wristwatch industries with a large scale of operations**", "**Exposed to regulatory risks and seasonality in demand**".
- Each body paragraph must contain verifiable specifics: segment revenue shares, CAGRs, store/branch counts, ratio levels and dates, peer context.

## Variants

- **Bank/NBFC**: section 4 heading is "Environmental and social risks"; drivers cover deposit franchise/AUM growth, asset quality (GNPA/NNPA, slippages, restructured book), capitalisation (CET-I/CRAR vs regulatory minimum), earnings (NIM, PAT/ATA), solvency; Parent/Group support row often cites sovereign ownership or promoter support with capital-infusion track record.
- **Issuer Not Cooperating**: headline "…Continues to remain under issuer Non-Cooperating category"; rating denoted `[ICRA]B+(Stable); ISSUER NOT COOPERATING`; short standardised Rationale (repeated info-seeking attempts, best-available-information basis, link to previous detailed rationale); no drivers/liquidity/sensitivities sections; abbreviated Analytical approach.
- **Withdrawal**: cite 140~Policy on Withdrawal; state reason (redemption, closure of facilities, at issuer's request with NOC).
- **Provisional ratings**: prefix "Provisional" per policy 107; list pending steps/documents.
- **Securitisation/PTC** (verified vs AU Small Finance Bank 27-May-2026): different section order — Summary of rating actions → Rationale → **Pool performance summary** → **Transaction Structure** → Key rating drivers → **Key rating assumptions** → **Details of Key counterparties** → Liquidity position → Rating sensitivities → Analytical approach → **About the originator** (replaces About the company) → rating history → SEBI-circular disclosure annexure → Complexity → Instrument details.
- **Rating Watch**: rating carries "&" suffix (e.g. [ICRA]AA+&) and words "Rating Watch with Positive/Negative/Developing Implications"; no outlook while on Watch; Rationale must explain the event driving the Watch and expected resolution path.
- **(CE) ratings**: suffix (CE) with the credit-enhancement mechanism (guarantee/DSRA/structure) described; disclose the unsupported rating alongside.
- **Provisional ratings**: "Provisional" prefix; list documents/steps pending per policy 107.
- **Structured/EL-scale**: instrument-level detail, waterfall, credit enhancement; EL ratings use [ICRA]…(EL) scale per 1021.
- Heading spellings vary across vintage (e.g. "Annexure-1", "Key financial indicators (standalone)", "Environment and social risks") — for NEW reports use the current-format headings as in the numbered list above (Titan Mar-2026 exemplar).

## MANDATORY verification gate — BEFORE drafting, not after (added after HAL error, Jul 2026)

No figure enters a draft until it passes a programmatic maker-checker pass. Non-negotiable sequence:

1. **Extract** every number from the audited statements into a script (amounts in original units — lakh/crore stated explicitly to avoid conversion slips).
2. **Recompute** the FULL ratio suite per 927 in code — not just the report-table indicators: profitability (OPM, NPM, RoCE), leverage (gearing, TOL/TNW, debt/OPBDIT, NCA/debt), coverage (interest cover, DSCR), working capital (debtor/inventory/payable days, WC cycle, current ratio), cash flows (FFO, CFO, RCF, FCF). Save the output as a `<Company>_Ratio_Workpaper_<date>.md` file alongside the report. Never carry a ratio from memory, a prior draft, or the anchor ICRA report.
3. **Reconcile** against the anchor ICRA report where one exists; every difference must be explained (e.g., standalone vs consolidated PAT) and the chosen basis disclosed to the user.
4. **Trace non-financial claims** (shareholding %, order book, LD %, contingent liabilities, capex plans) to a specific location in the source document — a claim inherited from the anchor report must be re-verified in the company's own documents.
5. Only then draft. The draft copies numbers FROM the verified computation output, never retyped from notes.

## Quality checklist before delivering any report

1. Verification gate above completed and clean.
2. Rating action grammar matches the Summary table and headline.
3. Methodologies cited exist in the Methodology folder and match the sector.
4. Liquidity descriptor consistent with the liquidity paragraph's arithmetic.
5. Sensitivities consistent with drivers (a named challenge should map to a negative trigger).
6. LT/ST rating combination consistent with mapping.md.
7. All 16 sections present in order; tables formatted as above.
8. Re-run the verification script against the FINAL draft text (numbers can drift during writing).

# PART 2 — CRISIL template detail

*Section order and house style extracted from CRISIL's published rationales in `CRISIL/Report for Reference/` (e.g. ITC 24-Apr-2026). Fill from the company's own documents; reference reports teach FORMAT only, never facts.*

## Header block
```
## Rating Rationale
<Month DD, YYYY> | <City>
# <Company Legal Name>
<italic one-line action, e.g. "Ratings reaffirmed at 'Crisil AAA/Stable/Crisil A1+'">
```

## Rating Action table
A boxed table: **Total <facility type> Rated** (with "Enhanced from…" if changed) | **Long Term Rating** Crisil AAA/Stable (Reaffirmed) | **Short Term Rating** Crisil A1+ (Reaffirmed). Add the standard note ("None of the Directors on Crisil Ratings Limited's Board are members of rating committee… 1 crore = 10 million. Refer to Annexure for Details of Instruments & Bank Facilities.").

## Body sections (in this order, using CRISIL's underlined headings)
1. **Detailed Rationale** — opening "Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed/assigned…", then 2–4 paragraphs: latest financials (operating income, EBITDA margin trend), financial-risk summary, the dominant credit development, and the sentence pattern "The ratings continue to reflect [business strengths]… These strengths are partially offset by [weaknesses]."
2. **Analytical Approach** — consolidation statement ("Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of [company] and its subsidiaries… citing significant business and financial linkages"), pointer to the List of Entities Consolidated annexure.
3. **Key Rating Drivers - Strengths** — 3–4 bold-underlined drivers written on CRISIL's factors (market position via distribution/brand equity; operating efficiency via integration/access to resources; strong financial risk profile via cash-flow adequacy and financial flexibility). Each = heading + one paragraph.
4. **Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses** — 1–2 drivers (regulatory/industry risk; input-output price-linkage / working-capital exposure), each with mitigants.
5. **Liquidity: <Superior/Strong/Adequate/Stretched/Poor>** — cash + liquid investments, cash accrual, **bank limit utilisation vs drawing power**, coverage of capex/dividend/WC.
6. **ESG Profile** — paragraph + "Key ESG highlights:" bullets (climate targets, social, governance).
7. **Outlook: <Stable/Positive/Negative>** — one line ("Crisil Ratings believes [company] will maintain…").
8. **Rating sensitivity factors** — **Upward factors** / **Downward factors** as bullets (e.g. "gearing exceeding 0.5 time", adverse regulation, decline in cash).
9. **About the Company** — one paragraph.
10. **Key Financial Indicators (Consolidated)** — table: Operating income (Rs crore), PAT, PAT margin (%), Adjusted debt/Adjusted networth (Times), Interest coverage (Times); 2 latest years.
11. **Any other information** / **Note on complexity levels**.
12. **Annexures**: Details of Instrument(s); List of entities consolidated (extent + rationale); Rating History for last 3 Years; Details of Bank Lenders & Facilities; list of instruments & regulators.
13. **Criteria Details** → "Links to related criteria" (name the exact files: Basics of Ratings; Criteria for consolidation; the sector master).
14. Media/analytical contacts, About CRISIL, Disclaimer.

## House-style notes
- Ratings written as `Crisil AAA/Stable/Crisil A1+` (prefix "Crisil", not "CRISIL"/"CARE"/"[ICRA]").
- Amounts "Rs <n> crore"; "1 crore = 10 million".
- CRISIL rates **bank facilities** for large corporates (not just CP) — Rating Action usually shows LT + ST bank loan facilities.
- Deliverable filename: `<Company>_<date>_CRISIL_Rating Rationale.md`.

# PART 3 — CareEdge template detail

*Section order and house style extracted from CareEdge's published press releases in `CareEdge/Report for Reference/` (e.g. Emami 17-Dec-2025). Fill from the company's own documents; reference reports teach FORMAT only, never facts.*

## Header block
```
Press Release
# <Company Legal Name>
<Month DD, YYYY>
```

## Facilities/Instruments table
Columns: **Facilities/Instruments | Amount (₹ crore) | Rating¹ | Rating Action**. Ratings as `CARE AAA; Stable / CARE A1+`, action = Assigned / Reaffirmed / Withdrawn. Footnote "Details of instruments/facilities in Annexure-1."

## Body sections (CareEdge order)
1. **Rationale and key rating drivers** — 3–5 paragraphs: strengths first ("continue to derive strength from…"), then financial performance (TOI, PBILDT margin, gearing), then the key development, then a constraints sentence. Use CARE ratio names (PBILDT, overall gearing, TD/GCA).
2. **Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions** — **Positive factors** / **Negative factors** as bullets, with explicit thresholds (e.g. "Overall gearing and TD/PBILDT over 0.50x on a sustained basis", "ROCE below 20%").
3. **Analytical approach: <Consolidated/Standalone/Combined>** — one paragraph naming the basis and pointing to Annexure-6 for the entity list.
4. **Outlook: <Stable/Positive/Negative/Not applicable>** — one paragraph (for CP-only, "Not applicable").
5. **Detailed description of key rating drivers:** →
   - **Key strengths** — headings mapped to methodology blocks A–D: economy/industry position; diversification; earnings capacity & cost structure/operating efficiency; financial risk (ratios, cash flow, flexibility); management evaluation. Each = `## heading` + paragraph.
   - **Liquidity: <Superior/Strong/Adequate/Stretched/Poor>** — its own heading (note CARE often uses "Strong"). Cover accruals, repayment obligations, cash/liquid balance, working-capital utilisation, operating cycle; for CP, the working-capital carve-out / back-up liquidity point.
   - **Key weaknesses** — industry/regulatory risk; input-price volatility & competition; then **Environment, social, and governance (ESG) risks** as a driver.
6. **Applicable criteria** — list the exact CareEdge papers used (Definition of Default; Rating of Short Term Instruments; the base master e.g. Manufacturing Companies; Financial Ratios – Non financial Sector; Rating Outlook and Rating Watch; Consolidation; Liquidity Analysis of Non-financial sector entities; + the sector paper).
7. **About the company and industry** — **Industry classification** table (Macroeconomic indicator | Sector | Industry | Basic industry), company paragraph, then **Brief Financials - Consolidated (₹ crore)** table: TOI, PBILDT*, PAT, Overall gearing (times), Interest coverage (times); 2–3 periods incl. latest (H1 if available). Footnote "*PBILDT: Profit before interest, lease rentals, depreciation and tax".
8. **Status of non-cooperation with previous CRA** / **Any other information** / **Rating history for last three years: Annexure-2**.
9. **Annexures 1–6**: 1 Details of instruments/facilities; 2 Rating history (3 years); 3 Covenants; 4 Complexity level; 5 Lender details; 6 List of entities consolidated (Full/Moderate + rationale).
10. Contact us; About us; Disclaimer; "© <year>, CARE Ratings Limited."

## House-style notes
- Ratings written `CARE AAA; Stable / CARE A1+` (semicolon before outlook; prefix "CARE").
- Currency symbol **₹**; "CareEdge Ratings" and "CARE Ratings Limited" used interchangeably in prose; running footer "CARE Ratings Ltd." + "Press Release".
- CareEdge rates bank facilities and/or instruments; CP shown as "Commercial Paper (Standalone)".
- Deliverable filename: `<Company>_<date>_CARE_Rating Rationale.md`.
