## **About the Company** 

Incorporated in November 1973, EIGL is engaged in the manufacturing, trading and supply of industrial and medical gases viz. Oxygen, Nitrogen, Argon, Carbon Dioxide, Helium, Acytelene (DA) Nitrous Oxide and specialty gas mixtures. EIGL was set up by Mr. Shanti Prasad Agarwala and his family. In October 2013, Air Water Incorporation (AWI), a leading Japan-based company, acquired 51% stake in EIGL, to enter the Indian market. EIGL’s plants are located at Uluberia (36,500 MTPA), Vishakhapatnam (40,150 MTPA) and Hyderabad (40,150 MTPA).
