## **About the company and industry** 

## **Industry classification** 

MMTC is a public sector undertaking, and was incorporated on September 26, 1963, to facilitate foreign trade in India and canalise the export and import of essential minerals and metals. It is under the administrative control of the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, and Government of India (GOI) held 89.93% stake in the company as on March 31, 2025. MMTC deals in multiple products and markets. The company’s business operations span across six major divisions including minerals, metals, precious metals, agro products, fertilisers & chemicals and coal & hydrocarbons.
