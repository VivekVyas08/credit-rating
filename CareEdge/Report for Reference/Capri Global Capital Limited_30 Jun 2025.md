Capri Global Capital Ltd (CGCL), a listed systemically important NBFC promoted by Mr. Rajesh Sharma, has Capri Global Holdings Pvt Ltd holding a 41.12% stake as of June 2025. The company raised ₹1,440 crore via rights issue in March 2023 and ₹2,000 crore through QIP in June 2025, reducing promoters’ stake to 59.95%. Its 100% subsidiary, Capri Global Housing Finance Limited, operates in housing finance, while a newly incorporated subsidiary handles car loan distribution. Together, they form the “Capri Group,” focusing on gold loans, MSME, wholesale construction finance, housing finance, and indirect lending. As of March 2025, CGCL reported a tangible net worth of ₹4,173 crore, consolidated AUM of ₹22,860 crore, and a distribution network of 1,111 branches across 19 states and UTs.
