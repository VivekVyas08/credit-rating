# **CARE SUSPENDS THE RATINGS ASSIGNED TO BANK FACILITIES OF NANDAN EXIM LTD.** 

CARE has suspended, with immediate effect, the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Nandan Exim Ltd. The ratings have been suspended as the company has not furnished the information required by CARE for monitoring of the ratings.
