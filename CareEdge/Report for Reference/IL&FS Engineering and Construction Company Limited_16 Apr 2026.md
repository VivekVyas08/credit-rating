## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) had placed the rating(s) of IL&FS Engineering and Construction Company Limited (IECCL) under the ‘issuer noncooperating’ category vide its press release dated January 21, 2025, as IECCL failed to provide information for monitoring of ratings. 

IECCL continues to be non-cooperative despite repeated requests for submission of information through phone calls and e-mails dated December 07, 2025; December 17, 2025; and December 27, 2025. Considering the extant Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) guidelines, CareEdge Ratings has reviewed the rating based on the best-available information, which in CareEdge Ratings’ opinion is not sufficient to arrive at a fair rating. Ratings for IECCL’s bank facilities are denoted as CARE D; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING. 

### **Users of this rating (including investors, lenders and public at large) are hence requested to exercise caution while using above rating(s).**

### **Default in debt servicing obligations** 

There are continued delays and defaults on IECCL’s principal and interest payments per annual report of FY25. IECCL has been classified as an non-performing assets (NPA) since 2019.
