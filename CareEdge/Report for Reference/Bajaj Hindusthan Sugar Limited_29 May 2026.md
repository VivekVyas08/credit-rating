## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has withdrawn the outstanding rating of ‘CARE D’ assigned to the instruments of Bajaj Hindusthan Sugar Limited (BHSL) with immediate effect following confirmation from the Debenture Trustee regarding full repayment/extinguishment of the existing OCDs. 

The last rated amount of the OCDs was ₹3,483.25 crore. Subsequently, one instalment was repaid, reducing the outstanding amount to ₹3,215 crore (ISIN: INE306A08EV6). The repayment/extinguishment was carried out pursuant to the implementation of the Resolution Plan under RBI’s June 7, 2019 Prudential Framework. The outstanding instruments have now been replaced through issuance of new OCDs under new ISIN: INE306A07135 with revised terms and conditions as approved under the Resolution Plan.
