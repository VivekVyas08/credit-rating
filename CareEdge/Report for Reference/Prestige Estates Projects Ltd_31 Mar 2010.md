# **CARE ASSIGNS ‘IPO GRADE 3’ TO THE PROPOSED IPO OF PRESTIGE ESTATES PROJECTS LTD.** 

CARE has assigned a **‘CARE IPO Grade 3’** to the proposed Initial Public Offering (IPO) of Prestige Estates Projects Ltd. (PEPL). ‘CARE IPO Grade 3’ indicates **average fundamentals** . CARE assigns IPO grades on a scale of Grade 5 to Grade 1, with Grade 5 indicating strong fundamentals and Grade 1 indicating poor fundamentals. CARE’s IPO grading is an opinion on the fundamentals of the issuer. The grade assigned to any individual issue represents a relative assessment of the ‘fundamentals’ of the issuer. 

PEPL proposes an IPO of equity shares of face value of Rs.10 each aggregating Rs.1,200 crore, for which the price band is yet to be decided. 

|**Instrument**|**Grading**|
|---|---|
|IPO Grade|‘CARE IPO Grade 3’|



# **Grading Rationale** 

The grading derives strength from experienced promoters and management, long track record of operations, PEPL’s diversified portfolio of real estate projects, strong brand image of the group, its huge land bank, high booking in case of residential projects and a strong client base in case of commercial projects. 

The grading is however constrained by geographic concentration risk as most projects are in Bangalore, decline in revenue in FY09 and the company’s high exposure to subsidiary and associate companies as well as the auditor’s qualifications in FY09 annual report. The grading also factors in the moderate outlook of the real estate industry, which faced severe downturn recently and is yet to see complete revival. 

PEPL’s ability to diversify geographically will be critical to sustain its growth in the business. In addition to this, PEPL’s ability to maintain high bookings in case of residential projects and improve the bookings in case of commercial projects, improve margins, expand land reserves and complete the various ongoing projects on time will be the key determinants of the company’s long term fundamentals. 

# **Company Profile** 

PEPL was founded by Mr. Late S. Razack, Mr. Irfan Razack, Mr. Rezwan Razack and Mr. Noaman Razack in 1986. It has a diversified portfolio of real estate projects with focus on projects in the residential, commercial, hospitality and retail segments of the real estate industry. Most of PEPL’s projects are in Bangalore. It also has a small presence in other regions of South India like Chennai, Hyderabad, Cochin, Mysore and Mangalore. 

As on September 30, 2009, PEPL has completed construction of 27.09 million sq. ft. of developable area across the residential, commercial, hospitality and retail segments in Bangalore. PEPL has the reputation of developing some of the most identifiable landmarks in Bangalore.
