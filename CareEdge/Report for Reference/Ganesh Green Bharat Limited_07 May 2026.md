## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

<mark>Ratings assigned to bank facilities of Ganesh Green Bharat Limited (GGBL) derive strength from experienced promotors, comfortable financial risk profile, and growth in consolidated total operating income (TOI) post commencement of operations of 1100 MW of solar photovoltaic (PV) module manufacturing capacity, in FY26 (FY refers to April 1 to March 31). Ratings also factor company’s moderate profitability, its satisfactory debt coverage indicators, and an adequate liquidity profile.</mark> 

<mark>These rating strengths are partially offset by stabilisation risk associated with further augmentation of the manufacturing capacity, and GGBL’s presence in an intensely competitive industry. Ratings also factor susceptibility of profit margins to volatility in raw material (RM) price and technology obsolescence risk.</mark> 

## **Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions** 

### **Positive factors** 

- Increase in capacity utilisation leading to growth in consolidated TOI above ₹1,400 crore while maintaining profit before interest, lease rental, depreciation and taxes (PBILDT) margin above 11% on sustained basis and continued comfortable financial risk profile. 

### **Negative factors** 

- Significant moderation in TOI than envisaged and/ or moderation in PBILDT margin to below 8% on a sustained basis. 

- Any debt-funded capital expenditure or increase in working capital intensity, resulting in moderation in TD/PBILDT above 2.50x on a sustained basis. 

- Moderation in PBILDT interest coverage ratio below 3x, on a sustained basis. 

## **Analytical approach:** Consolidated 

To arrive at ratings of GGBL, CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has taken consolidated view of GGBL and its subsidiary, Souraj Energy Private Limited (SEPL), considering managerial, financial, and operational linkages. Details of entities consolidated are listed under Annexure-6. 

## **Outlook:** Stable 

The Stable outlook reflects CareEdge Ratings’ expectations that the company shall sustain its financial risk profile on back of growing scale of operations post augmentation in installed capacity and promotor’s experience in solar module manufacturing and allied services. 

## **Detailed description of key rating drivers:** 

## **Key strengths** 

### **Growth in TOI with commencement of 1100-MW solar module manufacturing capacity with moderate profitability; despite stabilisation risk pertaining to further expansion persists** 

GGBL is engaged in solar module manufacturing, which contributed ~70% of its revenue in FY25. This apart, the company also undertakes installations of solar pumping system, which contributed another ~25% to the TOI, with the balance being contributed by Electrical Contracting Service and water supply projects.

For solar module manufacturing, GGBL had an installed capacity of 236 MW in 2019, which was gradually augmented to 750 MW in December 2024 and to 1100 MW in December 2025. Consequently, GGBL’s consolidated total operating income grew from ₹90.23 crore in FY23 to ₹319.00 crore in FY25. For H1FY26, GGBL reported TOI of ~₹341.10 crore and for full year FY26, growth in TOI is expected to remain healthy considering commissioning of an additional capacity of 350 MW in December 2025. 

The growth trajectory is expected to continue in FY27 as well, with the company expanding its capacity further by 800 MW, though optimal utilisation of the augmented capacity remains to be seen. 

As on April 25, 2026, the company had an outstanding order book of ~₹705 crore, comprising ~₹473 crore of solar module supply and ₹232 crore of the balance services, indicating moderate revenue visibility. 

Company’s profitability remained moderate, with PBILDT margin in the range of 14-15% in FY25 and H1FY26 and is expected to sustain at above 13% in the near-to-medium term with growth in TOI. 

## **Comfortable financial risk profile and satisfactory debt coverage indicators** 

Financial risk profile of GGBL, on a consolidated basis, is comfortable marked by overall gearing of 0.24x (PY: 0.94x) and total outside liabilities to total net worth (TOL/TNW) 0.64x at FY25 end (PY: 1.59x). 

Networth base of the company stood at ~₹205 crore at FY25 end, augmented with receipt of initial public offer (IPO) funds amounting to ₹125 crore. 

Debt coverage ratios, on a consolidated basis, are also satisfactory marked by PBILDT interest coverage of 10.08x (PY: 6.78x) and TD/PBILDT of 1.01x (PY: 1.49x), in FY25. Going forward, same are envisaged to improve with scaling up of operations and sustained profitability. 

## **Experienced promotors** 

The company’s operations are led by directors, Ketanbhai Narsinhbhai Patel, Rajendrakumar Narsinhbhai Patel, and Niravkumar Sureshbhai Patel, who have an experience of ~20 years in the solar energy industry. 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **Presence in intensely competitive industry** 

The solar PV module industry is highly competitive, with significant competition from Chinese and other domestic PV module manufacturers. Government initiatives to boost domestic manufacturing have led to substantial capacity expansions by leading manufacturers. These factors cumulatively have led to an excess supply in the domestic market and has intensified competitive intensity, which can potentially impact profit margins. 

To mitigate this, GGBL might foray into setting up of solar cell manufacturing line as backward integration in line with peers in this industry. However, the same has not been finalised yet. 

## **Exposure to RM price fluctuation risk and risk related to evolution in technology** 

GGBL’s profitability is vulnerable to fluctuation in key raw material prices, particularly solar cells, which constitute ~50-60% of the total RM cost. However, this risk is mitigated to an extent with ~80-85% of GGBL’s RM procurement being order backed. 

Solar PV modules and solar cells are subject to technical advancements, exposing the products manufactured by the company to the risk of obsoletion in terms of technology. The company currently has manufacturing line facilitating the latest TOPCon technology, and it can also switch the production within lines for manufacturing modules with other technologies as well, per enduser demand. 

# **Liquidity** : Adequate 

The liquidity of GGBL is expected to remain adequate marked by minimal term debt repayment obligation in FY26-28, which are expected to be met out of its gross cash accruals. 

GGBL’s cash flow from operations stood negative at ~₹38 crore in FY25 since the company had inventory stocked up for meeting requirement of fulfilling orders for upcoming ~3-4 months, however, the company received funding of ₹125 crore through equity
