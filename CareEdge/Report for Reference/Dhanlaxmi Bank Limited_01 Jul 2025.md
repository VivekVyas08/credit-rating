Established in 1927, DBL is a Kerala-based small-sized private sector bank headquartered at Thrissur. As on March 31, 2025, the bank has presence across 15 states and one Union Territory through 261 branches, 282 ATMs, and 17 BCs with an employee base of 1,756. The bank’s shares are listed in BSE and NSE and are widely held. DBL has no identifiable promoter, and the share holding pattern is well diversified with major holding by resident individuals at 42.78% as on March 31, 2025.
