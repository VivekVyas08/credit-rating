## **Rating Rationale** 

The revision in the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of RattanIndia Power Limited (RPL) takes into consideration the 

delays in servicing of its debt obligations by the company. 

Further, CARE has withdrawn the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of RPL, with immediate effect, at the request of the company and receipt of no objection certificates from the financial institutions and banks for withdrawal of ratings assigned by CARE.
