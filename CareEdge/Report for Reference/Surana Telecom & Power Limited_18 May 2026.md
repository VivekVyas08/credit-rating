## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

CARE Ratings Ltd (CareEdge Ratings) has reaffirmed and simultaneously withdrawn outstanding ratings of ‘CARE BB-; Stable; Issuer Not Cooperating / CARE A4; Issuer Not Cooperating’ (Double B Minus; Outlook: Stable; Issuer Not Cooperating/ A Four; Issuer Not Cooperating) assigned to the bank facilities of Surana Telecom and Power Limited (STPL) with immediate effect. The above action has been taken at the request of STPL along with No Objection Certificate from HDFC Bank and No Due Certificate from Aditya Birla Finance Limited which have extended the facilities rated by CARE. 

The ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Surana Telecom and Power Limited (STPL) remain constrained by its significant exposure to associate entities, relatively small scale of operations, and pending grid access approval for its 3 MW capacity. The ratings are further impacted by the inherent exposure of its projects to climatic conditions and technological risks. 

However, the ratings derive comfort from the experienced promoters and the established track record of the Hyderabad-based Surana Group. The presence of long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) with various DISCOMs provides revenue visibility. Additionally, the company maintains comfortable leverage and coverage metrics, which support its overall credit profile. 

## **Analytical approach:** Consolidated

(BGEL) and Aryavaan Renewable Energy Pvt Ltd while arriving at the ratings of Surana Telecom and Power Limited. (list of entities consolidated in Annexure 6)
