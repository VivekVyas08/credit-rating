# **CARE REAFFIRMS GRADINGS OF VARIOUS MARITIME COURSES OFFERED BY SHIPPING CORPORATION OF INDIA – MARITIME TRAINING INSTITUTE** 

CARE reaffirms its highest grading of ‘CARE Grade 1’ (One) to various maritime courses offered by Shipping Corporation of India – Maritime Training Institute (SCI-MTI). These gradings indicate ‘Outstanding’ ability of the institute to meet the requirements of those courses. 

The gradings derive strength from outstanding infrastructure facilities of the institute, excellent training facilities, long experience of the management as well as faculty, broad based guidance from the governing council, very good quality systems, proactive management policies, committed support from Shipping Corporation of India (SCI) as a training department of the company, financial strength of SCI, sustainability of operations, reputation amongst students as well as shipping companies for various courses offered by the institute. 

Shipping Corporation of India (SCI) is a Government of India (GoI) shipping company owning, operating and managing about 79 diversified vessels of about 4.76 mn DWT. SCI-MTI was established in 1988 on the bank of Powai Lake in the suburbs of Mumbai, by SCI to train their employees various skills of the shipping industry. 

SCI-MTI is located in 45 acre campus beside the Powai Lake, in the suburbs of Mumbai. Public transport is available right at the main door of the institute. All the courses except for Fire Fighting and Advanced Fire Fighting (which are conducted in a separate Fire fighting complex) are conducted in Sagar Gyan (the main academic building). FireFighting complex has classroom facilities as well as a ship mock-up for practical work. All the classrooms are adequately spaced as per requirements and are equipped with an elevated podium, blackboards, projectors, separate seating for individual student etc. Electric, carpentry, plumbing, machine shop and hot work shops are conducted in a well equipped workshop located inside the campus itself. SCI-MTI provides its students with in-house hostel and catering facilities if desired by them. There are four hostels with total capacity of 230 students. The rooms are either separate or on a twin sharing basis. 

Governing Council, under the principal of the institute, guides the administration of SCIMTI. The other members of governing council include the management representative (looking after the quality) and three deans of the institute. 

Most of the faculty had been working with SCI on its ships and almost all of them have been with SCI-MTI for more than 5 years. 

SCI-MTI was certified as a Maritime Training Center conforming to the Quality System Standard ISO 9001:2000 by Det Norske Veritas (DNV). The MTI is a branch of the World Maritime University and also a Training Center for UNCTAD and renders its services to other developing countries in Asia and Pacific Region along with the domestic demand. MTI was conferred with "Golden Peacock National Award" for excellence in training for the year 1997. The MTI has a policy of collecting organized feedback from its students. The institute has an efficient MIS system providing information on enrolment, fees collected, details of students trained in each course and their performance record.

SCI-MTI does not have a formal marketing set-up. However, over the years, it has been established as a renowned institute in maritime training (both amongst the students as well as shipping companies). It has also been able to attract shipping companies (other than SCI) and other marine related companies to sponsor candidates for various courses. As for the finances, the institute does not face any problem because of the backing of SCI. Cash flow budgets and planning is done on an annual basis.
