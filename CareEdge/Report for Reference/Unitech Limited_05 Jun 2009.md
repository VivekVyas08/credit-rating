# **CARE WITHDRAWS RATING ON RS. 50 CR NON CONVERTIBLE DEBENTURES OF UNITECH LTD.** 

CARE has withdrawn the **‘CARE BB’** rating assigned to Rs. 50 cr NCDs of Unitech Ltd. The company has confirmed that there is no outstanding amount under the rated instrument.
