# **CARE WITHDRAWS THE RATING ASSIGNED TO BANK FACILITIES  OF GUJARAT PIPAVAV PORT LTD.** 

CARE has withdrawn the rating assigned to the Gujarat Pipavav Port Ltd.(GPPL)’s bank guarantee, backed by letter of comfort from IDFC Ltd., of upto Rs.47 crore with immediate effect, as GPPL has fully repaid the said facilities and there is no amount outstanding against the said facilities.
