## **About the Company** 

Container Corporation of India Ltd (CONCOR) was incorporated in March 1988 as a Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways by taking over network of seven Inland Container Depots (ICDs) of Indian Railways (IR). The company was formed in order to have a separate organization for promoting and managing the growth of containerization in India as well as developing multi-modal (surface, rail, water and air) transport logistics and infrastructure to support India’s growing inland as well as international trade. CONCOR's core business is characterized by three distinct activities, that of a carrier, a terminal operator, and a warehouse operator. CONCOR, at present, is the sole container freight subsidiary of IR. It is a Central Government enterprise with 54.80% shareholding held by Government of India (GoI) as on September 30, 2020.
