Other largest shareholders are NYL and New York Life International Holdings Limited, who acts as a strategic partner to MEL and will continue evaluating co-investment opportunities. The company has delivered ~12.93 lsf of office space development and is undertaking ~65 lsf of residential projects in Delhi NCR region.
