## **About the Company** 

Rajratan Global Wire Limited (RGWL) along with its subsidiary Rajratan Thai Wire Limited (RTWL), is engaged in manufacturing of Tyre Bead Wire (TBW) and High Carbon Steel Wire (HCSW; also known as “black wire”). TBW is a high carbon bronze coated steel wire largely used in the inner circumference of automobile tyres and is the link through which the vehicle load is transferred from rim to the tyre. RGWL’s manufacturing facility is located in Pithampur (Madhya Pradesh) and that of RTWL’s is located in Ratchaburi (Thailand). The group had combined installed capacity of 62,400 Metric Tons per Annum (MTPA) as on Sep. 30, 2017. RGWL (the domestic company) is the second largest TBW manufacturer having a market share of 39% in the domestic market.
