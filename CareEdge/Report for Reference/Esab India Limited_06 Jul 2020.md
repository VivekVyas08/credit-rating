## **About the Company** 

ESAB India started its operations in 1987 by acquiring the welding business of Peico Electronics & Electricals Limited (now Philips India Limited). The Company continued its expansion in the Indian market with the purchase of Indian Oxygen Limited's welding business in 1991 and Flotech Welding & Cutting Systems Limited in 1992, followed by the merger of Maharashtra Weldaids Limited in 1994. ESAB India is part of the ESAB Group (holding 73.72% stake). The ultimate holding company of ESAB is Colfax Corporation USA listed in New York Stock Exchange.
