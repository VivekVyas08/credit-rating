# **Arvind Limited** 

May 15, 2026 

## **Credit update** 

On May 06, 2026, vide disclosures to the stock exchanges, Arvind Limited (Arvind), announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, Arvind Advanced Materials Limited (AAML) acquired majority stake of ~61% in Dalco-GFT (Dalco) LLC, a US-based manufacturer of specialised needle-punched non-woven fabrics, for a consideration of US$85.40 million, marks AAML’s strategic entry into the US technical textiles market. The transaction is valued at an enterprise value of ~US$136 million. Existing investors will continue to hold the remaining significant minority stake of ~39%, which AAML intends to acquire in a phased and structured manner in the next four years. 

Acquisition consideration of US$85.40 million, along with working capital-related debt of US$25.00 million, is funded through total debt of US$110.00 million. This comprises US$60.00 million of debt raised at AAML and debt of US$50.00 million at the Dalco level, a portion of which has been utilised to refinance Dalco’s existing debt of ~US$15.00 million. 

Dalco has an operational track record of nearly four decades and it operates two manufacturing facilities in North Carolina and South Carolina, with an aggregate installed capacity of ~75 million pounds per annum. The company serves diversified end-use sectors including mobility, geotextiles, industrial applications, and flooring & furniture. In CY25 (CY refers to January 01 to December 31), Dalco reported revenues of ~US$100 million, with profit before interest, lease rentals, depreciation and taxation (PBILDT) margins exceeding 17% and a return on capital employed of over 40%. Dalco’s customer profile is well-diversified, with contract tenures for its mobility segment typically ranging from 5-12 years, providing adequate revenue visibility. 

With acquisition related debt of US$110 million, Arvind’s consolidated debt is likely to increase to ~₹3,000 crore compared to ₹2,065 crore as on March 31, 2025. Despite the short-term impact of increased net debt levels, given Arvind’s steady margin profile and expected strong internal accrual generation, CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) does not expect any material deterioration in the company’s credit profile on a sustained basis. Arvind’s debt to PBILDT on a consolidated basis is expected to remain below 3x. The management has indicated that the debt availed by AAML is expected to be deleveraged ahead of its threeyear repayment tenure while debt at Dalco-GFT is self-liquidating over five years with its steady operating cash flow. 

From a strategic standpoint, the acquisition is expected to strengthen AAML’s technical textiles capabilities and expand its addressable market within the advanced materials segment. Dalco’s established expertise in needle-punched non-woven fabrics is expected to facilitate technology and process knowledge transfer, enhancing AAML’s product development and manufacturing capabilities. The transaction is also expected to improve Arvind’s overall business risk profile, supported by Dalco’s presence in a relatively high-margin segment with an established market position. The transaction is also expected to support procurement and supply-chain optimisation, thereby contributing to improved margins and better operating efficiencies in the medium term.
