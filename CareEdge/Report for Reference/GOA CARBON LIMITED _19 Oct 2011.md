# CARE SUSPENDS THE RATINGS ASSIGNED TO THE BANK FACILITIES OF 

# GOA CARBON LIMITED 

CARE has suspended, with immediate effect, the ‘CARE BBB’ [Triple B] rating assigned to the longterm bank facilities of Goa Carbon Limited (GCL) aggregating Rs.51.00 crore. CARE has also suspended, with immediate effect, the ‘CARE A3+’ [A Three Plus] rating assigned to the short-term bank facilities of GCL aggregating Rs.245.00 crore. The ratings have been suspended as the company has not furnished the adequate information required by CARE for monitoring the rating.
