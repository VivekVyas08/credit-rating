## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

<mark>The revision in the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Parth Electricals & Engineering Limited (PEEL) takes into account significant growth in scale of operations, sustained profitability and moderate capital structure.</mark> 

<mark>The ratings continue to derive strength from experienced promotors, with established track record of operations and reputed as well as diversified clientele. The ratings, also take cognisance of growth in PEEL’s scale of operations in FY25 (refers to period from April 01 to Mar 31), satisfactory debt coverage indicators and adequate liquidity position.</mark> 

<mark>The ratings however remain constrained on account of moderate financial risk profile, low net worth base and modest scale of operations. The ratings also factor susceptibility of its profitability margins to volatility in raw material prices and presence of PEEL in competitive and fragmented electric equipment manufacturing and installation industry.</mark> 

## **Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions** 

### **Positive factors** 

- <mark>Sustained growth in total operating income (TOI) above Rs.300 crore while maintaining PBILDT margin at prevailing levels on a sustained basis</mark> 

- <mark>Augmentation of net worth base alongwith satisfactory completion of capex within envisaged cost and time</mark> 

### **Negative factors** 

- <mark>Decline in TOI below Rs.150 crore and moderation in PBILDT margin below 7% on sustained basis</mark> 

- <mark>Availment of higher than envisaged borrowings leading to moderation in overall gearing beyond 1.0x.</mark>

## **Moderate Profit margins; albeit susceptibility of profitability to volatility in raw material prices** 

PEEL’s PBILDT margin stood moderate at 9.65% in FY25 (10.66% in FY24) with PAT margin of 5.56% (PY:5.29%). Key raw materials i.e. Steel sheets, aluminium, copper, cables, switchgears, and electrical accessories constitute ~90%-95% of the total cost, and exhibit high volatility in its prices impacting profit margins. However, PEEL is majorly engaged in execution of short to medium term projects along with order backed procurement from suppliers which mitigates price fluctuation risk to certain extent. 

## **Diversified and reputed customer profile** 

The company has established diverse clientele in past 19 years, which includes reputed corporates namely IED Infrastructure Pvt Ltd, M/S Shivranjani Construction Co, Waaree Energies Limited (Rated; CARE A+; Stable/A1+), Om Power Transmission Pvt Ltd., Schneider Electric Infrastructure Ltd, Monte Carlo Limited Tata Powers etc. With assurance for supply of quality products and services, PEEL is able to secure repeated orders from these clients. 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **Moderate capital structure** 

The entity's capital structure improved but stood moderate, as marked by an overall gearing of 0.84x as on March, 31, 2025 (1.29x as on March, 31, 2024) with high reliance on external debt. The total outside liabilities to net worth stood moderate at 1.39x as on March, 31, 2025, improved from 3.65x as on March, 31, 2024). The improvement in capital structure was on account of accretion of profit to reserves, prepayment of term loans, and infusion of equity of Rs.15 cr. through private placement in FY25. PEEL has moderate networth base of Rs.39.84 cr. as at FY25 end. Going forward, the capital structure is expected to improve on account of private placement of Rs.9.18 cr. and with expected infusion of equity through IPO in current year. Debt coverage indicators remain satisfactory marked by Total Debt/Gross Cash Accruals (TD/GCA), and interest coverage ratio of 2.88x (FY24: 3.01x) and 4.67x (FY24: 5.25x) respectively as on March 31, 2025. 

## **Moderate; albeit growing scale of operations** 

The scale of PEEL although grew significantly in FY25 and stood moderate at Rs.174.64 cr. (PY. Rs.86.78 cr. The increase in TOI was mainly on account of timely execution of orders with major focus on manufacturing division and incremental revenue from technology sharing agreement with Schneider Electric Infrastructure Ltd.  Further, the company has booked revenue of ~Rs.37 crore in current year till June 18, 2025 and is expected to achieve TOI of ~Rs.220 in FY26. PEEL has order book of Rs.98.95 cr. as on April 30, 2025 and continuously receives order from reputed clientele. 

## **Highly fragmented and competitive industry** 

PEEL operates in a highly competitive and fragmented industry and thus witnesses intense competition from largely unorganized players as the projects especially in the tender-based where the revenues are dependent on the company’s ability to bid successfully. This fragmented and highly competitive industry results into price competition thereby affecting the profitability margins of the companies operating in the industry. 

# **Liquidity** : Adequate 

The liquidity position of PEEL is adequate marked by sufficient GCA to service its term debt obligations and cushion in form of unutilised portion of fund based working capital limits (FBWC). PEEL is expected to generate GCA in the range of ₹10-15 crore against term debt repayment obligations of ₹0.50-₹1.50 crore for projected FY26-FY28. As per lender interaction, the average utilization of fund based working capital limits (FBWC) was around 70% for the last 12 months ended May 2025. PEEL has moderate gross working capital cycle on account of collection period ranging from 50-120 days and inventory holding period in range of 30-45 days in past 3 years ending FY25. However, PEEL credit period from suppliers in range of 35-140 days in past 3 years trailing FY25.
