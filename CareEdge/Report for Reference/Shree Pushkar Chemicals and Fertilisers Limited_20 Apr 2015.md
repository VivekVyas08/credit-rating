# **Grading Rationale** 

_The grading assigned to IPO of SPCFL derives strength from long standing track record of promoters in dye and dye intermediate industry, integrated production facilities, moderate financial performance and comfortable leverage/ coverage ratios._ 

_The grading strengths are however constrained by customer and revenue concentration risk, moderate scale of operations, substantial capex in relation to current networth with project execution & stabilization risk, highly fragmented & commoditized dyes and dye intermediates industry, and regulatory & environment risk associated with chemicals business._ 

# **Background** 

Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited (SPCFL) promoted by first generation entrepreneur Mr. Punit Makharia and Mr. Gautam Makharia, commenced business in the 1993 with a trading business under the name “Shree Pushkar Petro Products Ltd”. The name was subsequently changed to “Shree Pushkar Chemicals and Fertilisers Ltd” during March 2012. Over the years the company has started manufacturing wide range of dye intermediates in India with zero wastage and integrated facilities, with currently two manufacturing facilities located at Lotte Parshuram, Maharashtra. 

SPCFL is an ISO 9001:2008 company, engaged in manufacture of products in 4 major segment viz., Dye Intermediates, Acid Complex (comprising sulphuric and its derivative acids), Cattle Feed Supplement and Fertilizers (Single Super Phosphate & Soil Conditioner). The company is also a recognized Government Export House. 

SPCFL reported a PAT of Rs. 10.39 cr. on total operating income of Rs. 210.37 cr. during FY 14 (refers to the period April 01 to March 31).
