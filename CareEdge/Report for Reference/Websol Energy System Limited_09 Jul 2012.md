# **Rationale** 

CARE has revised the ratings assigned to the long term bank facilities due to continued deterioration in the financial risk profile of the company & subdued outlook of the solar PV manufacturing industry. 

Further, as the company has not furnished the information required by CARE for monitoring of the rating, CARE has suspended the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of WESL. 

# **Company Profile** 

Incorporated in 1990, WESL is engaged in the manufacturing of Solar Photovoltaic Cells (SPCs) and modules with an installed capacity of 90 MW. The company is a 100% Export Oriented Unit and mainly caters to the export market. 

During the twelve months ended March 31, 2012 the company reported a loss of Rs.166.7 crore on a turnover of Rs.130.2 crore.
