## **About the Company** 

UEL was promoted by Mr. Shyam Sundar Mundra under the name of _‘M and B Switchgears’_ in 1979 as a proprietorship concern to manufacture transformers and switchgears. It was subsequently converted in to a partnership firm in 1995 and then incorporated in 1999. Over the past few years, UEL has diversified its operations to solar power segment and hence, its name was also changed from _‘M and B Switchgears Limited’_ to its present one in August 2013. 

UEL is currently engaged in mainly two businesses segments, viz., establishing solar power plants on Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) basis (which contributed ~92.5% of its revenue in FY17) and power generation through solar energy (which contributed ~ 6.5% of its revenue in FY17). As on March 31, 2017, the company owns & operates 14 MW of solar power generation capacity.
