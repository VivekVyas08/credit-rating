# **CARE WITHDRAWS THE RATING ASSIGNED TO BANK FACILITIES OF AJMERA REALTY & INFRA INDIA LTD** 

CARE has withdrawn the rating assigned to the Ajmera Realty & Infra India Ltd’s (ARIIL) long term and short-term bank facilities aggregating Rs.1,389.37 crore with immediate effect, as ARIIL has repaid the said facilities
