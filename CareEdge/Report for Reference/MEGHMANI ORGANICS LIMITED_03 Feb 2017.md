## **Detailed Rationale** 

**_CARE has withdrawn the ratings assigned to the NCD issue & CP issue of Meghmani Organics Ltd. with immediate effect, as the company has fully repaid the amounts under the NCD issue and there is no amount outstanding under the NCD issue as on date. Further, MOL has confirmed that there is no amount outstanding against the CP issue as on date._** 

## **Applicable Criteria** **<u>Policy on Withdrawal of ratings</u>** 

## **About the Company** 

Established in 1995, MOL is engaged in manufacturing and marketing of a range of Pigments and Agro Chemicals, with a strong focus on the global markets. MOL has seven manufacturing plants in and around Ahmedabad and Bharuch in India and a strong domestic and export marketing network to support its global as well as India focused initiatives. MOL has also established its presence in the chlor-alkali business via. its subsidiary Meghmani Finechem Ltd. (MFL)
