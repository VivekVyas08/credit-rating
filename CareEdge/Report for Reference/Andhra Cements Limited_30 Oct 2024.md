CARE Ratings Ltd. has withdrawn the ratings assigned to the Bank facilities of Andhra Cements Limited (ACL) with immediate effect, as the company has surrendered the aforementioned bank facilities rated by us and there is no amount outstanding under the facility as on date.

## **About the company** 

Andhra Cements Limited (ACL) (ISIN: INE666E01020) has cement manufacturing facilities at Dachepalli, Guntur District (Durga Cement Works) with a split grinding unit at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh (Visakha Cement Works). Post the admission of the company for corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP), the resolution applicant, Sagar Cements Limited upon successful implementation of the Resolution Plan took over the control of the management and ownership of the Company and by virtue of which the company has become subsidiary of Sagar Cements Limited with effect from March 23, 2023.
