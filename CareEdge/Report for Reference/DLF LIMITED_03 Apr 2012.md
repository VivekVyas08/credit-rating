# **CARE WITHDRAWS THE RATING ASSIGNED TO COMMERCIAL PAPER (CP) ISSUE OF DLF LIMITED** 

At the request of the company, CARE has withdrawn, with immediate effect, the rating assigned to the Commercial Paper (CP) issue of DLF Limited, as there is no outstanding amount under the rated instrument.
