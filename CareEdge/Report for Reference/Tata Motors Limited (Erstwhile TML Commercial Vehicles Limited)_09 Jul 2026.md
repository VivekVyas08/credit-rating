## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

<mark>Reaffirmation of ratings for bank facilities and instruments of Tata Motors Limited (TML; formerly TML Commercial Vehicles Limited) continues to derive strength from the strong business and financial profile of the commercial vehicles (CV) business, its leadership position in the Indian CV industry, broad product portfolio across trucks, buses, passenger carriers, small commercial vehicles and pickups, and strong parentage of the Tata Group. The company continues to hold a leadership position in the domestic CV industry, with FY26 VAHAN market share of 35.7%, including 55.0% in heavy commercial vehicle (HCV), 39.5% in intermediate, light, and medium commercial vehicle (ILMCV), 26.8% in small commercial vehicle (SCV), and 36.4% in passenger carriers. The completion of the demerger/restructuring was successfully concluded effective October 01, 2025.</mark> 

<mark>Ratings also derive comfort from the company’s strong liquidity, negative working-capital cycle, significant free cash generation, wide distribution/service network, continued focus on profitable growth and the strategic roadmap. The proposed acquisition of Iveco Group N.V. for Euro 3.8 billion is expected to significantly expand global scale and diversify the product/geographic footprint; however, refinancing, integration, synergy realisation and exposure to European/Latin American CV cycles remain key monitorable. The acquisition was earlier expected to close by April 2026, however the same is now expected to get concluded by Q2FY27 given pending approvals.</mark> 

<mark>Ratings are constrained by the inherent cyclicality of the domestic CV industry, susceptibility of margins to commodity and inputcost volatility, competitive pressures across product segments, regulatory/technology transition risks, diesel-price sensitivity affecting total cost of ownership, requirements to support new launches, safety/emission compliance, electrification, alternate fuels and digital solutions.</mark> 

## **Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions** 

### **Positive factors** 

- Sustained improvement in operating performance, market share and free cash generation, leading to maintenance of net cash position and stronger-than-expected debt protection metrics even after factoring acquisition-related debt and integration requirements. 

### **Negative factors** 

- <mark>Deterioration in leverage profile, resulting in net debt/PBILDT above 0.75x.</mark> 

- <mark>Weakening operational performance leading to significant loss of market share and decline in earnings before interest, taxation, depreciation, and amortisation (EBITDA) margin on a sustained basis.</mark>

# **Key strengths** 

## **<mark>Strong linkages and support from Tata group</mark>** 

<mark>TML is part of the Tata Group, one of India’s largest and most diversified business groups, with presence across information technology, steel, automotive, consumer and retail, infrastructure, financial services, aerospace and defence, tourism and travel, telecom and media, and trading and investment. TML remains strategically important to the group given its leadership in CVs, sizeable revenue contribution and role in mobility/logistics. As part of the Tata Group, the company benefits from strong brand recall, governance standards and significant financial flexibility.</mark> 

## **Strong market position and well-diversified product portfolio in the Indian CV business** 

TML is the market leader in the domestic CV segment with FY26 VAHAN market share of 35.7%, including 55.0% in HCV, 39.5% in ILMCV, 26.8% in SCV and 36.4% in passenger carriers. The company offers a broad portfolio spanning HCV trucks, ILMCV trucks, buses, vans, passenger carriers, SCV cargo and pickups, electric vehicles (EVs) and alternate-fuel platforms. FY26 total CV volumes grew 13% y-o-y to 435,227 units, with HCV trucks up by 13%, ILMCV trucks up by 19%, passenger carriers up by 9%, SCV cargo and pickups up by 8% and exports up by 54%. The company continues to operate a wide sales and service network and has launched/expanded products such as Ace Pro, Winger 9S and Azura range, while upgrading truck range to meet European safety norms. 

## **Healthy financial risk profile and strong free cash generation; Iveco acquisition remains key monitorable** 

FY26 performance reflects significant strengthening in profitability and cash generation, with revenue of ₹77,399 crore, EBITDA of ~₹10,200 crore, EBITDA margin of 13.2%, earnings before interest and taxation (EBIT) margin of 11.0%, profit before tax (PBT) before exceptional items of ₹8,682 crore, free cash flow of ₹9,186 crore and domestic-business net cash of ~₹7,500 crore. The proposed Iveco acquisition is expected to be transformational, with potential for TML to enhance its global scale, broader market access, strengthened product capabilities and potential technology synergies. Further, acquisition funding/refinancing, integration challenges, timely synergy realisation and exposure to Iveco Capital and the cyclical European CV market will remain key rating monitorable. Leverage levels are expected to increase in near term in FY27 following the acquisition. However, the company's strong operating cash flow generation, planned refinancing initiatives, and targeted debt reduction measures are expected to support a gradual deleveraging trajectory in FY28. 

# **Liquidity** : Strong 

<mark>TML’s liquidity profile is strong, supported by healthy gross cash accruals in FY26, business cash and liquid investment stood ~₹10,332 crore as on March 31, 2026. Company’s net debt position also remains negative implying strong liquidity buffer available. The company also benefits from strong banking relationships and Tata Group’s financial flexibility. Liquidity is expected to remain adequate for capex, working capital and debt servicing; however, funding/refinancing and cash deployment related to the Iveco acquisition is expected to moderate key debt metrics in short term.</mark> 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **<mark>Exposure to inherent cyclical automobile industry and competitive pressures</mark>** 

<mark>TML remains exposed to the cyclical nature of the commercial vehicle industry, which is closely linked to macroeconomic activity, infrastructure, mining and construction momentum, freight availability, replacement demand, financing costs, and fleet-operator profitability. FY26 witnessed a subdued first half, followed by a strong recovery in H2 after the GST 2.0 rollout and festive demand, highlighting the inherent cyclicality in volumes. However, sales in Q1FY27 have picked up as compared with Q1FY26, indicating an improvement in demand momentum. Nevertheless, diesel prices, geopolitical developments, competitive intensity, and sectorspecific freight activity remain important demand monitorables.</mark> 

## **<mark>Susceptibility of margins to raw material price volatility</mark>** 

<mark>Key raw materials for CV original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) include steel, iron, aluminium, copper, rubber, aggregates and other components, whose prices have been volatile in recent years. While TML has demonstrated improved realisations, pricing discipline and cost efficiencies, the ability to pass through input-cost increases fully remains constrained by competition and demand conditions. Hence, margins remain susceptible to commodity and input-cost volatility.</mark>

## **Moderate capital intensity of its businesses** 

The automotive industry remains capital intensive due to continuous product development, technology upgrades, safety and emission compliance, electrification, alternate fuel platforms, software/digital solutions and new product launches. TML has guided for investment spends of 2-4% of revenue and FY27-FY28 capital intensity will also depend on Iveco integration and growth investments. The company’s ability to generate healthy free cash flow through industry cycles while funding capex, new technologies and acquisition-related commitments remains a key monitorable. 

# **Environment, social, and governance (ESG) risks** 

## **Environmental:** 

- TML has adopted a holistic approach towards net-zero, involving its business and operational impact and strategy. 

- TML decarbonisation journey is driven by its products, where it focuses on clean mobility solutions aspiring to achieve netzero in its CV business by 2045 and through its operations by focusing on maximising renewable electricity share to 100% by 2030. 

- TML is embedding sustainability in its business by focusing on the three interconnected pillars of driving decarbonisation of businesses and value chain, applying a systemic circular economy approach to reduce resource consumption and waste, and preserving and restoring the natural environment. 

- TML sustainability goals are aligned with global climate change mitigation targets, and the company follows universally accepted guidelines and commitments such as the Science Based Targets Initiative (SBTi) and RE100 to reach these goals. 

- TML operations are reflective of its pioneering objective of enabling more individuals to access and utilise clean mobility solutions. 

- TML has made considerable investments in renewable energy sources, innovations in emissions reduction technologies, sustainable materials, and circular processes. 

## **Social:** 

TML also actively promotes education in the country by supporting underprivileged children and developing community health and well-being. 

## **Governance:** 

TML has been practicing principles of fair, ethical, and transparent governance over the years and lays strong emphasis on transparency, accountability, honesty, integrity, and ethical behaviour.
