## **About the Company** 

Eveready Industries India Ltd. (EIIL), a major player in the domestic FMCG sector engaged in manufacturing & marketing of dry cell batteries (carbon zinc, rechargeable and alkaline), flash lights and trading of packet tea & lighting products, was incorporated as Ever-Ready Company (India) Ltd. in June, 1934 with registered office at Kolkata. 

EIIL reported a PAT of Rs.69 crore and Rs.83 crore on total operating income of Rs.1,394 crore and Rs.1,101 crore during FY16 & 9MFY17 respectively.
