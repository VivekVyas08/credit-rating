## **About the Company** 

Manoj Vaibhav Gems ‘N’ Jewellers Private Limited incorporated on March 13, 1989, was promoted by Late Mr Grandhi Narendra and family as ‘Hotel Anant Private Limited’ (HAPL). Later HAPL was acquired by Mr Manoj Kumar Grandhi and family in 2003 and was renamed as ‘Vaibhav Empire Private Limited’. Furthermore, the company was renamed as ‘Manoj Vaibhav Gems ‘N’ Jewellers Private Limited on July 4, 2016. The company is engaged in retailing of gold jewellery, diamond jewellery, studded precious / semi-precious stones jewellery, silver jewellery, articles & antique show pieces. MVGJPL has a flagship jewellery show room spread over 30000 sq. ft. area & five floors with dedicated floors for each type of jewellery in Visakhapatnam city area, Andhra Pradesh. During FY15, MVGJPL has added two new showrooms each at Gajuwaka and Kakinada. During FY16, MVGJPL has opened 3 jewellery retail stores at Parvathipuram (1150 sq.ft.), Bobbili (2000 sq.ft.) and Rajahmundry (500 sq.ft.) in state of Andhra Pradesh. At present the company has six stores totaling a retail area of 45,056 sq.ft, across Andhra Pradesh. The company is accredited with Bureau of Indian Standard (BIS) hallmark for gold jewellery and is certified by International Gemological Institute Antwerp (IGI) for diamond jewellery. During FY18, the company has entered Telangana jewellery market with opening of Dilshuknagar showroom on April 09, 2017.
