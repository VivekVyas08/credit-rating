# **About the Company** 

Happy Forgings Limited (HFL) earlier constituted as a private limited company started its operations as a foundry unit in 1979, for forging of cycle parts. The company was later reconstituted as a closely held public limited company. The company is into manufacturing of around 800 types of forged and machined auto, engine components etc. mainly for four wheeler segments.The company has an 8000 Ton forging press, and 6 Hammers (ranging from 1T to 6T). The company restructured its debt in FY13 on account of heavy debt repayment obligations of the company due to debt funded capex undertaken.
