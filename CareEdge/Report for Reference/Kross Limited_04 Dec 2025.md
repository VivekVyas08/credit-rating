## **About the company** 

Incorporated in May, 1991, as private limited company named Kross Manufacturers (I) Pvt. Ltd. (KMIPL) which has changed its name to Kross Limited since January 17, 2017 and got listed on BSE and NSE platform (ISIN: INE0O6601022) during September 2024 and was promoted by Mr. Sudhir Rai and his wife Mrs. Anita Rai. The company commenced operations in 1993 and is engaged in manufacturing of automobile parts (axle shafts, coupling flanges, tractor parts, etc.) for commercial vehicles as well as tractors. It has three manufacturing plants including a forging unit in Jamshedpur.
