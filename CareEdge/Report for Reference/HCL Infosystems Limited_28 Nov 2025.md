### **<mark>Unsupported rating Reaffirmed at CARE BBB+/A2 and Withdrawn</mark>** 

Note: Unsupported rating does not factor in the explicit credit enhancement. 

## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has reaffirmed and withdrawn the outstanding (i) credit-enhanced ratings of ‘CARE AA(CE); Stable/ CARE A1+ (CE), and (ii) standalone ratings of CARE BBB+; Stable/ CARE A2, assigned to bank facilities of HCL Infosystems Limited (HCLI) with immediate effect. This action has been taken at the request of HCLI and ‘No Objection Certificate’ received from the bank(s) that have extended the facilities rated by CareEdge Ratings. 

Reaffirmation of credit-enhanced ratings assigned to bank facilities of HCLI factor in credit enhancement (CE) in the form of unconditional and irrevocable corporate guarantee (CG) from HCL Corporation Private Limited (HCLC). The credit enhanced ratings are based on CareEdge Ratings’ view on the CE provider’s credit profile. Ratings consider key credit risk assessment parameters of the CE provider and standalone ratings. 

## **Rationale and key rating drivers of HCLC** 

Reaffirmation of the CE ratings continues to reflect robust financial flexibility of HCLC, emanating from the significant mark-tomarket valuation of its equity investments in group companies, HCL Technologies Limited (HCLT) and other liquid investments, which have led to adequate coverage in terms of the mark-to-market value of investments to outstanding debt, both on-balance and off-balance sheet of HCLC. These investments generate steady dividend income. The holding company also derives significant benefits from experienced promoters and their resourcefulness. HCLC’s strength is amplified further by it holding the ‘HCL’ brand. 

However, these rating strengths are constrained by the investment’s exposures to fluctuations in capital market and absence of a significant stake of a strong underlying entity among its key investments. 

## **Rationale and key rating drivers of HCLI** 

Standalone ratings of HCLI continue to factor in its strong linkages with the resourceful HCL group and strong track record of financial support from its promoter group through HCLC in the form of interest-free unsecured loans and CG towards its outstanding working capital facilities. 

However, these strengths are tempered by the operating losses incurred over the years, the resultant scaling-down of operations and weak financial risk profile at a standalone level.

## **Analytical approach of CE provider - HCLC:** Standalone 

# **Outlook:** Stable 

CareEdge Ratings believes that HCLC is expected to maintain its healthy market value of investments-to-debt ratio in the medium term and remain a key holding company in the HCL group, considering it houses the ‘HCL’ brand. 

HCLI is also expected to continue to receive support from the HCL group to service its operational and financial obligations. 

# **Detailed description of key rating drivers:** 

## **(I) HCLI** 

# **Key strengths** 

## **Strong and resourceful promoter group with a long track record of operations** 

HCLC is one of the holding companies of the HCL group, which was founded in 1980, and is promoted by Shiv Nadar and his family. HCLI is also an HCL group company. Shiv Nadar is the Founder and Chairman of the HCL group and the Shiv Nadar Foundation. He founded HCL in 1976 and has wide entrepreneurship experience (~50 years) in the Information Technology (IT) sector. In 2008, he was awarded the Padma Bhushan for his efforts in the development of the IT industry in India. In 2007, Madras University awarded him an honorary Doctorate degree. He was also recognised as the E&Y Entrepreneur of the Year 2007 and has received several awards for his association with the IT sector. HCLC’s and HCLI’s day-to-day operations are managed by a team of qualified and experienced management, spearheaded by promoters, having vast experience in the IT business. The group has established a reputable image and brand name over the years. Promoters house their investments in IT, healthcare, and talent management solutions through investment companies, VAMA and HCLC. The group’s flagship company HCLT (market capitalisation of ₹4.29 lakh crore as on November 14, 2025) has 60.81% promoter shareholding, which includes 44.17% by VAMA. 

## **Track record of financial support by HCLC to HCLI** 

In light of losses incurred by HCLI, the support from the HCL group, particularly HCLC, gives significant comfort. HCLC has infused interest-free unsecured loans of ₹355 crore (outstanding as on March 31, 2025) in HCLI. HCLC provides unconditional and irrevocable CG to HCLI’s working capital facilities, which is ~₹396 crore as on March 31, 2025. The total support extended by HCLC to HCLI is ₹751 crore as on March 31, 2025. Considering continuous losses, HCLC’s financial support is key to the company being a going concern entity. 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **Continued PBILDT losses, leading to HCLI’s weak financial risk profile** 

HCLI has been continuously making profit before interest, lease rentals, depreciation and taxation (PBILDT) losses considering historical low-margin contracts and delayed receivables, a significant portion of which has been written-off, and high legal costs for large litigations being undertaken to recover these receivables. The company has negative net worth of ~₹291 crore as on March 31, 2025. Gradually, the company has shut down all its segments, except the system integration (SI) business. The management completed projects of the SI business and only annual maintenance contracts are pending, which need to be honoured. No new business is expected to be received. The continued losses have fully eroded the net worth, and the company is able to sustain itself, considering the significant financial support received from HCLC. 

## **(II) HCLC** 

# **Key strengths** 

## **Healthy financial risk profile with a strong net worth base and healthy asset profile of HCLC** 

HCLC has a strong capital structure with a healthy net worth base of ₹4,932 crore (standalone) as on March 31, 2025. It has an almost unleveraged balance sheet, with only major debt obligations being the guaranteed debt for its associates. HCLC has marketable investments of ~₹2,837 crore as on March 31, 2025. The off-balance sheet debt, i.e. CG towards HCLI bank guarantees, stood at ₹396 crore as on the same date, leading to an investment value by debt of 7.17x. The company is expected to maintain at least a market value investment-to-debt cover of 6x. HCLC houses the brand ‘HCL’ in its book, which holds significant value to the HCL group.

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **Exposure to fluctuations in capital markets** 

Financial flexibility in terms of the value of marketable investments to debt of HCLC is exposed to prevailing market sentiments and share prices. Increase in market-related risks, leading to a sharp fall in the share prices of investments, will be a key rating sensitivity factor. 

# **Liquidity** : 

## **HCLI:** Adequate 

The company is operating at cash losses, but there is no term loan or fund-based working capital loan outstanding as on date. Currently, only bank guarantee obligations are outstanding against projects undertaken by HCLI. Amid these cash losses, liquidity support is largely derived from the committed funding support from HCLC amounting to ₹1,500 crore in the form of CG/Unsecured loans. The company has ₹167 crore of cash and cash equivalents as on September 30, 2025. However, the comfort of cash & cash equivalents and liquid investments is negated considering ongoing arbitration with Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Limited (MTNL), where it has provided ~₹120 crore as cash deposit until the arbitration is completed. 

## **HCLC:** Strong 

HCLC has healthy investment portfolio amounting ₹2,837 crore as on March 31, 2025. Against this, the company has guaranteed debt of ₹396 crore as on March 31, 2025, for its group companies, mainly HCLI. The company has steady flow of interest and dividend income from its investment.
