# **CARE WITHDRAWS RATING ASSIGNED TO THE FD PROGRAMME OF APOLLO HOSPITALS & ENTERPRISES LTD** 

The rating assigned to the Fixed Deposit programme of Apollo Hospitals & Enterprises Ltd has been withdrawn as the company has not been using CARE’s rating for raising fixed deposits.
