## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

CARE Ratings Ltd. had, vide its press release dated November 23, 2023, placed the rating(s) of Simplex Infrastructures Limited (SIL) under the ‘issuer non-cooperating’ category as SIL had failed to provide information for monitoring of the rating and had not paid the surveillance fees for the rating exercise as agreed to in its Rating Agreement. SIL continues to be non-cooperative despite repeated requests for submission of information through e-mails, phone calls and a letter/email dated October 05, 2025; October 15, 2025 and October 25, 2025. In line with the extant SEBI guidelines, CARE Ratings Ltd. has reviewed the rating on the basis of the best available information which however, in CARE Ratings Ltd.’s opinion is not sufficient to arrive at a fair rating. 

### **Users of this rating (including investors, lenders and the public at large) are hence requested to exercise caution while using the above rating(s).** 

The ratings take into account delays in servicing of debt obligations by the company as mentioned in the audit report (FY25).
