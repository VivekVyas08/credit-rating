# **October 20, 2014** 

# **CARE SUSPENDS REAL ESTATE PROJECT STAR RATING ASSIGNED TO** 

# **PARSVNATH PRIVILEGE BY PARSVNATH DEVELOPERS LTD** 

CARE has suspended, with immediate effect, the Real Estate Project Star rating assigned to the project ‘Parsvnath Privilege’ being developed by Parsvnath Developers Limited. The rating has been suspended as the company has not furnished the information required by CARE for monitoring of the rating.
