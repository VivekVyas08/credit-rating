# **CARE WITHDRAWS ‘CARE A1 (SO)’ RATING ASSIGNED TO CENTRUM CAPITAL LTD.** 

CARE has withdrawn, with immediate effect, the **‘CARE A1 (SO)’** rating assigned to Commercial Paper issue of Centrum Capital Ltd. amounting to Rs.25 crore. Since the CP limit has not been utilized, the rating for the same stands withdrawn.
