## **About the Company** 

Incorporated in 1976, Thirumalai Chemicals Ltd. (TCL) is engaged in the manufacturing of industrial chemicals viz. Phthalic Anhydride (PAN), Phthalate Esters (PE; diethyl phthalate and phthalimide) and food acids (maleic acid, fumaric acid and malic acid) which find applications in plastics, paints & resins industry and as additives for the food industry. TCL has a plant in Ranipet, Tamil Nadu with manufacturing capacity for PAN of 140,000 metric tonnes per annum (mtpa), food acids of 17,000 mtpa and PE of 7,800 mtpa. Additionally, the company also manufactures Maleic Anhydride (MAN; 30,000 mtpa) at its Malaysia based wholly owned step-down subsidiary - Optimistic Organic SDN BHD (OOSB). 

During FY17 (refer to period April 01 to March 31), on a standalone basis, TCL achieved total operating income of Rs.869.24 crore and PAT of Rs.75.47 crore as compared to total operating income of Rs.792.11 crore and PAT of Rs.42.73 crore during FY15. On consolidated basis, TCL reported total income of Rs.1,036.90 crore (Rs.949.36 crore in FY16) and PAT of Rs.70.54 crore (Rs.32.15 crore in FY16).
