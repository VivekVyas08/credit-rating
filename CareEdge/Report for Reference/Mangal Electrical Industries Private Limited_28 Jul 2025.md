## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has reaffirmed and withdrawn the outstanding ratings of “CARE B+; Stable; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING / CARE A4; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING” (Single B Plus; Outlook: Stable; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING/ A Four; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING) assigned to the bank facilities of Mangal Electrical Industries Private Limited (MEIPL) with immediate effect. The rating withdrawal is at the request of MEIPL and submissions including  company’s declaration for nonavailment of rated proposed fund based and non-fund based limits, ‘No dues certificate’ for certain bank facilities and ‘No Objection Certificate’ for balance bank facilities rated by CareEdge Ratings. 

The ratings assigned to the bank facilities of MEIPL continue to remain constrained on account of a moderate capital structure and debt coverage indicators as well as the company’s presence in a highly fragmented transformer manufacturing industry with susceptibility of margins to volatile raw material prices and foreign exchange rate fluctuation. However, the ratings derive strength from experienced management with established track record of operations and integrated operations for transformer manufacturing along with moderate scale of operations and profit margins.

## **Key Weaknesses** 

### **Moderate capital structure and debt coverage indicators** 

MEIPL’s capital structure continued to remain moderate owing to an accretion of profits to reserves despite an increase in overall debt. Thus, overall gearing stood at 0.92x as on March 31, 2025, compared to as 0.80x as on March 31, 2024. Further, as a result of higher profitability and stable interest costs interest coverage improved and stood at 5.49x at the end of FY25 compared to 3.37x during the previous year. Additionally, TD/GCA also remained moderate at 2.82 years as on March 31, 2025 (P.Y.: 3.79 years) as a result of higher cash profit (GCA) despite the increase in overall debt. 

### **Presence in a highly fragmented transformer manufacturing industry with susceptibility of margins to volatile raw material prices and foreign exchange rate fluctuation** 

Due to the low entry barriers owing to low capital requirement and readily available technology, there are large numbers of organized and unorganized players present in the manufacture of lower voltage transformer segment. The high degree of fragmentation restricts the pricing flexibility and bargaining power of players which is further intensified by the tender driven

# **Key Strengths** 

## **Experienced management with established track record of operations and integrated operations for transformer manufacturing** 

Mr Ashish Mangal & Mr Rahul Mangal are the key promoters of MEIPL. Mr Rahul Mangal has more than 20 years of experience in electrical equipment industry and looks after purchase, operations and finance functions. Mr Ashish Mangal has more than 15 years of experience in managing business and looks after administration function of the company. MEIPL has appointed qualified & experienced second tier management to look after day-to-day operations. Initially, MEIPL was engaged in manufacturing of CRGO steel lamination, a key input used for manufacturing transformers. Later, during 2007, the company integrated forward into manufacturing of transformers and started supplying its products to power sector utilities. The company has also commenced manufacturing PCC poles from 2012 onwards. Additionally, MEIPL from FY15 onwards has also commenced undertaking Engineering Procurement and Construction (EPC) contracts in the power sector under Rajeev Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana (RGGVY)/Deen Dayal Upadhaya Gram Jyoti Yojna (DDUGJY) schemes of Central Government. 

## **Moderate scale of operations and profit margins** 

During FY25, TOI of the company grew by around 22.08% to Rs. 550.48 crore compared to Rs. 450.89 crore at the end of FY24 but remained at a moderate level. The PBILDT margin improved to 15.14% in FY25, compared to 9.78% in FY24, supported by the increase in revenue. Further, with stable interest and depreciation costs, PAT margin also improved and stood at 8.61% at the end of FY25 compared to 4.66% at the end FY24.

# **About the company** 

MEIPL was initially formed as a partnership firm in 1990 under the name ‘Mangal Electrical Industries’ and was subsequently converted into a private limited company in April 2008 and the name was changed to its present form. The company has been promoted by Mr Rahul Mangal along with his brother Mr Ashish Mangal, who have also promoted Dynamic Cables Pvt. Ltd. MEIPL is involved in the manufacturing of transformers, electrical laminations for transformers from cold rolled grain oriented (CRGO) Steel and prestressed cement concrete (PCC) poles. The company also undertakes Engineering Procurement and Construction (EPC) contracts in the power sector under Rajeev Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana (RGGVY)/Deen Dayal Upadhaya Gram Jyoti Yojna (DDUGJY) schemes of Central Government.
