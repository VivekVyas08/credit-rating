# **Detailed Rationale & Key Rating Drivers** 

CARE has been seeking information from Sumit Woods Limited to monitor the rating vide email communications dated January 04<sup>th</sup> 2019, January 11<sup>th</sup> 2019, January 25<sup>th</sup> 2019, January 29<sup>th</sup> 2019, February 08<sup>th</sup> 2019, February12<sup>th</sup> 2019, February 19<sup>th</sup> 2019 and March 07<sup>th</sup> 2019; and numerous phone calls. However, despite our repeated requests, the company has not provided the requisite information for monitoring the rating. In line with the extant SEBI guidelines, CARE has reviewed the rating on the basis of the best available information which however, in CARE’s opinion is not sufficient to arrive at fair rating. The rating on the company’s bank facilities will now be denoted as CARE B+; Stable ISSUER NOT COOPERATING. 

Subsequently, CARE has withdrawn the revised rating of CARE B+; Stable ISSUER NOT COOPERATING (Single B Plus; Outlook: Stable) assigned to the bank facilities of Sumit Woods Limited with immediate effect. The above action has been taken at the request of the company and ‘No Objection Certificate’ received from the bank that has extended the facilities rated by CARE.
