## **Detailed Rationale and Key Rating Drivers** 

CARE has withdrawn the outstanding ratings of “CARE BB+ (CE); Stable; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING” assigned to the bank facilities of VMS TMT Private Limited (VTPL) with immediate effect, as the company has surrendered the aforementioned bank facilities rated by us and there is no amount outstanding under the facility as on date. 

**Analytical approach:** Not Applicable. Earlier approach was assessment of Guarantor i.e. VMS Industries Limited (VIL). The bank facilities of VTPL were backed by unconditional and irrevocable corporate guarantee extended by VIL. However, the said corporate guarantee stands withdrawn now as VTPL has surrendered the facilities sanctioned by SBI and same are being taken over by new lender wherein corporate guarantee was not required.
