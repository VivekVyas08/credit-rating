## **About the Company** 

Incorporated in 1998, IRB Infrastructure Developers Limited (IRBIDL) is an established integrated surface transportation infrastructure company with expertise in development of BOT Toll Road Projects. The company’s business segments are toll roads, construction, airport development and real estate. The company operates largely as a holding company, while the construction activities are carried through its EPC arm, Modern Road Makers P. Ltd. (MRMPL).
