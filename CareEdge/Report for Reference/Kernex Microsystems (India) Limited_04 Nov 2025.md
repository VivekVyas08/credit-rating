## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

Ratings assigned to bank facilities of Kernex Microsystems (India) Limited (KMIL) derive strength from its strong order book position reflecting substantial revenue growth in coming years, significant turnaround in operational performance FY25, comfortable financial risk profile and qualified management team. Being one of the only three industry players having patented technology for KAVACH puts KMIL in a strong market position. The recent Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO) approval (October 2025) for Kavach Version 4.0 will result in an accelerated revenue growth in near future. 

However, rating strengths are partially offset by working capital intensive operations necessitating higher reliance on bank borrowings, exposure to execution and technology risk and tender-based nature of business. 

## **Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions** 

### **Positive factors** 

- Total operating income (TOI) increasing to over ₹500 crore while maintaining existing profit before interest, lease rentals, depreciation, and taxation (PBILDT) margin on a sustained basis. 

- Significant improvement in working capital cycle. 

### **Negative factors** 

- Elongation in working capital cycle beyond 150 days. 

- Significant decline in revenue or profitability by over 30% from existing levels.

CareEdge Ratings believes the entity will continue to benefit from the patented technology for KAVACH in bagging more orders, ensuring revenue growth. 

## **Detailed description of key rating drivers:** 

## **Key strengths** 

### **Experienced directors and qualified team** 

The company has appointed qualified professionals with over two decades of experience in project planning, execution, and management. The Board of Directors comprises experienced individuals with diverse professional backgrounds who oversee the company’s overall functioning. The Executive Directors, Sitarama Raju Manthena and Manthena Badari Narayana Raju, possess over two and a half decades of experience and are actively involved in the company’s strategic planning and overall operational management. 

### **Patented technology with only three players in the segment** 

Train Collision Avoidance System (TCAS), popularly known as Kavach, has been developed by the RDSO in collaboration with KMIL, Medha Servo Drives Private Limited, and HBL Engineering Limited, and is jointly patented by all three original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). The system is aligned with the European Train Control System (ETCS) and adheres to Safety Integrity Level (SIL) 4 standards, representing the highest level of safety integrity for such systems. for the latest version, KAVACH 4.0, by RDSO in October 2025, which will ensure swifter execution of projects and growth in revenue. Being one of the only three OEMs authorised to design, develop, manufacture, and deploy both hardware and software for KAVACH, KMIL enjoys a strategic market position. The entry barriers in this segment are high due to the complex technical requirements, stringent safety standards, and long product validation cycles. Moreover, since the company caters to the Indian Railways - one of the world’s largest railway networks, it benefits from large-scale, recurring demand for such safety systems. 

## **Strong order book, long-term revenue visibility** 

The company has an outstanding order book of ₹2,563 crore as on September 30, 2025, translating to an order book-to-TOI ratio of 13.5x, indicating healthy revenue visibility over the medium-to-long term. However, the order book is concentrated, with one major order worth ₹1,730 crore towards supply and installation of 2,500 Loco TCAS (Version 4.0) units to Chittaranjan Locomotive Works (CLW). 

KMIL is in the process of bidding for new projects for which tenders have been rolled out by the Indian Railways. 

## **Weak operational performance in the past despite improved performance in FY25** 

The company was earlier engaged in manufacturing and supplying anti-collision devices (ACD), which was discontinued in 2017 following Indian Railways’ transition to the TCAS. Since then, KMIL has been working in close collaboration with RDSO on the design and development of TCAS. However, due to the stringent technical and quality requirements prescribed by RDSO, the product approval process was prolonged, leading to muted revenue and losses considering high fixed overheads until FY22. The company began receiving TCAS orders from FY23 onwards, but initial execution was constrained by limited working capital availability. Subsequently, the company infused an equity of ~₹100 crore in FY23 and FY24 to fund operational losses and for working capital. Consequently, in FY25, the company successfully executed TCAS supply and installation projects, resulting in a significant turnaround in performance with revenue increasing to ₹189.88 crore along with operational profits and profit margin of 21.55%. 

## **Comfortable financial risk profile** 

The company’s financial risk profile stood comfortable marked by an overall gearing of 0.26x as on March 31, 2025. The debt coverage indicators also remained healthy, reflected in a total debt/PBILDT of 0.99x and an interest coverage ratio of 5.91x in FY25. Going forward, the company is expected to increase its reliance on working capital bank borrowing. However, the leverage and coverage indicators are likely to remain comfortable backed by healthy net worth base and improvement in cash accruals. 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **Working capital intensive operations** 

The company operates in a highly working capital-intensive business indicated by an operating cycle of 171 days for FY25. Operations demand long turnaround time for assembly, installation, and quality checks. A significant lead time is involved in procuring material from RDSO-approved vendors where limited credit period is available widening the working capital gap. Though collections from Indian Railways are timely, bills can be raised only post-installation. Given the large order book and higher lead time, the company is required to maintain elevated inventory levels. Hence, reliance on bank borrowings is expected to increase going forward and prudent working capital management remains a key monitorable. 

## **<mark>Execution and technology risk from RDSO-linked approvals</mark>** 

<mark>Execution timelines are exposed to delays arising from technological complexities and dependency on RDSO approvals. Any upgradation in system specifications—such as migration from TCAS version 3.2 to version 4.0-requires fresh testing and certification by RDSO, which can temporarily stall project execution and billing. Such procedural delays in obtaining technical clearances may impact the company’s ability to achieve planned revenue milestones.</mark> 

## **Exposure to tender-based operations and dependence on Indian Railways** 

KMIL secures contracts by participating in tenders floated by Indian Railways for the installation of the KAVACH (TCAS), where awards are made to the lowest bidder (L1). This tender-based procurement mechanism limits the company’s pricing flexibility and exposes it to competitive undercutting, often resulting in modest operating margins. However, the risk is partly mitigated due to presence of only three OEMs, including KMIL. However, since KMIL’s entire revenue is derived from Indian Railways, the company remains exposed to high customer concentration and dependence on a single end-user segment. 

# **Liquidity** : Adequate 

The company’s liquidity position remains adequate, marked by healthy gross cash accruals of ₹35.33 crore in FY25. In absence of term debt, the profits generated by the company are sufficient to meet its interest obligations. Reliance on working capital borrowings remained moderate with an utilisation of ~80% in the past 12 months period ended August 2025. Liquidity is further supported by free cash and cash equivalents of ₹15.02 crore and current ratio of 2.16x as on March 31, 2025.

KMIL offers end-to-end electronic manufacturing services encompassing design, development, assembly, testing, and system integration related to railway safety systems. Its service portfolio includes design and engineering, embedded software and hardware development, wire and cable assemblies, and full turnkey solutions including mechanical assembly and packaging.
