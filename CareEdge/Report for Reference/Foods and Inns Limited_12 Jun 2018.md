## **Detailed Rationale & Key Rating Drivers** 

The revision of rating assigned to bank facilities of Foods and Inns Ltd (FIL) takes into account the deterioration in liquidity profile as a result of cash-flow mismatch owing to elongation of receivable cycle. 

CARE has withdrawn the outstanding ratings of ‘CARE B; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING Based on best available information [CARE Single B; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING Based on best available information]/CARE A4; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING Based on best available information’ [CARE A Four; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING Based on best available information] assigned to the bank facilities of Foods and Inns Ltd with immediate effect. The above action has been taken at the request of Foods and Inns Ltd and ‘No Objection Certificate’ received from the banks that have extended the facilities rated by CARE. 

## **Detailed description of the key rating drivers Key Rating Weaknesses** 

## **_Deterioration of liquidity position_** 

The liquidity position of FIL stands deteriorated as a result of cash flow mismatch owing to increased receivables thereby leading to higher utilisation of fund based limits.

# **About the Company** 

Incorporated in 1967, Foods and Inns Ltd (FIL) commenced its operations with canning facilities to supply canned foods to the Allied Armed Forces. Later, FIL went public in 1971 and subsequently, in 1983, the company diversified into manufacturing processing and exporting of tropical fruit pulp, of which, mango pulp (contributing around 80% of revenues) in aseptic and canned packaging, is the largest component while other fruits which are similarly processed include guava, papaya etc. FIL is a primarily engaged in the business of manufacturing processing and exporting of tropical fruit pulps, concentrates, <u>puree and spray dried fruit and vegetable powders both in domestic and International markets.</u>
