## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

The ratings assigned to bank facilities of New Delhi Television Limited (NDTV) factors its strong parentage of Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL rated, ‘CARE AA-; Stable/ CARE A1+’) long track record of NDTV in news broadcasting industry and established market position of its flagship channels ‘NDTV 24X7’ & ‘NDTV India’ with its presence in among top new channels in India. 

Ratings further derive comfort from support extended by AEL in form of unsecured loans and AEL’s articulation to support NDTV on need basis, both operationally and for additional funding requirements. AEL has also extended corporate guarantee to the entire bank facilities of NDTV. 

Ratings favourably factor in the launch of three new regional channels, ‘NDTV Rajasthan’, ‘NDTV MPCG’, ‘NDTV Marathi’, relaunch of its existing channel ‘NDTV profit’, capex incurred for such launch and equipment upgrade and significant increase in advertisement revenue following onboarding of BARC ratings from November 2024. 

CARE Ratings Limited (CARE Ratings) notes proposed scheme of merger by NDTV group, where all subsidiaries except NDTV convergence Limited i.e. ‘NDTV Networks Limited’, ‘NDTV Media Limited’, ‘NDTV Labs Limited’ and NDTV Worldwide Limited’ will be merged in NDTV for streamlining of operations. 

Rating strengths are constrained by ongoing litigations against company and its modest performance with continued operating losses during FY24 and FY25. Operating losses are expected to be continued in the medium term. Nevertheless, AEL’s demonstrated support, extension of corporate guarantee on entire debt and strategic importance of NDTV for Adani group mitigates the risk to an extent.

## **Long track record of operations coupled with experienced and qualified management team** 

NDTV, for more than three decades, has been a leading content provider and subsequently it also entered news broadcasting in 2003 with the launch of its first channel in India. This enabled the company to develop editorial skills, build a brand name and increase viewer-ship base over the years. NDTV Convergence Limited (subsidiary of NDTV) owns the digital media business of the group and controls all NDTV websites, including NDTV.com, one of the popular portals in India. 

NDTV is headed by a well-qualified management team with long experience in the media and entertainment industry. The board includes personalities in the Indian television news industry including Mr. Upendra Kumar Sinha, Mr. Sanjay Pugalia and Mr. Senthil Sinniah Chengalvarayan. Mr. Sinha, a prominent figure in the financial sector, served as the Chairman of SEBI from 2011 to 2017. Mr. Pugalia has an extensive experience in digital, television, and print media. He has served as the launch and head of CNBC Awaaz for 12 years. Mr. Chengalvarayan has a career spanning over 35 years. He has served as founding editor of CNBC TV18 and later was at the position of Editor-in-Chief at Network 18’s Business News. 

## **Established market position of its flagship news channel, launch of three regional channels and onboarding of** 

## **BARC ratings** 

The NDTV group’s flagship news channel ‘NDTV 24X7’ has established market position among the top English new channel in India with significant market share. In FY24 and FY25, NDTV invested in capex, upgrading old infrastructure and launched three regional channels, ‘NDTV Marathi’, ‘NDTV MPCG’ and ‘NDTV Rajasthan’ for expanding its reach and availability. It also relaunched its existing channel ‘NDTV profit’. NDTV has onboarded Broadcast Audience Research Council effective November 2024, which resulted in significant growth of revenue for Q3FY25 and Q4FY25. 

NDTV has availed external debt for reimbursement of capex incurred and for projected capex to be incurred in FY26. NDTV has incurred ~₹120 crore in FY24 and FY25 for launching channels, upgrading old infrastructure and broadcasting equipment. 

## **Proposed scheme of merger** 

NDTV Limited has proposed scheme of merger, where NDTV subsidiaries, except NDTV convergence i.e. ‘NDTV Networks Limited’, ‘NDTV Media Limited’, ‘NDTV Labs Limited’ and NDTV Worldwide Limited’ will be merged in NDTV Limited for streamlining operations. CARE Ratings does not expect such merger to have a significant adverse impact on the company’s operations. 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **Modest financial risk profile** 

The NDTV’s total operating income (TOI) grew significantly to ₹465 crore in FY25 from Rs 370 crore during FY24, owing to onboarding of BARC rating and launching of new channels. However, significant marketing and distribution expenses incurred during FY25 lead to operating losses of Rs. 173 Crore during FY25 as against losses of Rs. 28 Crore during FY24. Accumulation of past losses, led to erosion of net worth base and deterioration in its capital structure. 

As on March 31, 2025, unsecured loans from AEL and outstanding external term loan stands at ~₹168 crore and ₹77 crore respectively. As per terms of sanction letter for external debt, unsecured loans from promoters are subordinated to external debt and shall not be repaid till currency of the loan. Considering unsecured loans of promoter as net worth, Adjusted total outside liabilities divided by tangible net worth stood at 1.81 times as on March 31, 2025. 

## **Uncertainties on the outcome of litigations pending against the company** 

There are several ongoing litigations against the NDTV group, especially pertaining to tax demand with direct tax authorities involving significant amounts. These direct tax litigations are across stages, ranging from preliminary discussions to tax tribunal or court. Proceedings and resolutions of these matters can take extended time, and outcome of which will be crucial. The company has also received demand notice from Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) for alleged non-disclosure of tax demand, a notice from Directorate of enforcement (ED) for alleged contraventions under Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (“FEMA”). In addition to these, a few other litigations are also pending. However as per the management’s articulation, such liabilities are not expected to crystallise and in unforeseen circumstances, AEL will provide required support. Movement in such matters will be a key rating monitorable. 

# **Liquidity** : Stretched 

The company has cash and cash equivalents of ~₹10 crore as on March 31, 2025. The company also has sanctioned working capital facility of ₹15.00 crore, which stands fully utilised as on March 31, 2025.  Given the operational losses, the repayments shall be supported by AEL.  Timely and need based support from AEL shall remain crucial.

NDTV was promoted by Dr. Prannoy Roy and Radhika Roy and is into business of broadcasting news and other content on TV.
