# **CARE SUSPENDS THE RATINGS ASSIGNED TO BANK FACILITIES OF BEDMUTHA INDUSTRIES LIMITED** 

CARE has suspended, with immediate effect, the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Bedmutha Industries Limited. The ratings have been suspended as the company has not furnished the information required by CARE for monitoring of the rating(s).
