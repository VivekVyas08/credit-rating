# **CARE ASSIGNS ‘IPO GRADE 2’ TO ASTEC LIFESCIENCES LTD.** 

CARE has assigned a **‘CARE IPO Grade 2’** to the proposed Initial Public Offer (IPO) of Astec LifeSciences Limited (ALSL). ‘CARE IPO Grade 2’ indicates below-average fundamentals. CARE assigns IPO grades on a scale of Grade 5 to Grade 1, with Grade 5 indicating strong fundamentals and Grade 1 indicating poor fundamentals. CARE’s IPO grading is an opinion on the relative assessment of the fundamentals of that issuer. ALSL proposes an IPO of 75 lakh equity shares of face value of Rs.10 each, at a price which will be determined through the book-building process. 

The grading factors in promoters’ experience in the agro chemicals and pharmaceuticals industry, long track record of ALSL, consistent growth in terms of turnover and profitability levels in the last few years, moderate gearing and diversified geographical  presence. However, the grading is constrained by ALSL’s relatively small size of **_operations, pre-dominant control_** by a single family member, product-concentration risk, absence of long-term contracts with raw material suppliers and clients, dependence on IPO proceeds for the proposed project completion **_,_** various pending court cases against the company & the promoter and **_moderate corporate governance practices. The grading is further constrained by project risk involved in setting up of new manufacturing facility with deployment of proposed IPO funds._** 

ALSL is engaged in the manufacture and sale of intermediates, active ingredients and formulations in the off-patent–proprietary category. ALSL has its manufacturing activities in Maharashtra, at Dombivli and Mahad. The company is headed by the promoter, Mr. Ashok Hiremath, Chairman and Managing Director of the company who is assisted by a team of senior executives. 

The proceeds from the proposed issue of shares are intended to be deployed for expanding the manufacturing facilities at Mahad  which is proposed to be a 100% Export Oriented Unit, augmenting the existing Research and Development facility at Dombivli,  meeting registration expenses, long-term working-capital requirements, general corporate purposes and issue expenses. 

ALSL operates under two major segments viz agrochemicals and pharmaceuticals. Hexaconazole, Tebuconazole, Metalaxyl and Propiconazole are some of the key products in agrochemical segment which together contributed 80% of total sales in FY09 whereas Dicap, a pharmaceutical intermediate, accounted for 14% of total sales. Share of ALSL’s exports in total revenue increased from 25.32% in FY08 to 29.52% in FY09. ALSL exports its products to East Asia, Europe, Middle East and USA. 

Various legal cases have been filed against ALSL including a case filed by Nath Biogene (I) Limited, alleging supply of spurious material by ALSL and claiming Rs.75 crore as damages/compensation. The case was quashed by the Hon’ble High Court, Mumbai but is currently pending with the Supreme Court. Another case filed by the Government of Maharashtra accusing the company of illegal manufacturing, storage, selling and distribution of misbranded products. Another case filed by Manpada Police Station in relation to the outbreak of fire at the company’s Dombivli unit in 2004, alleging it to be on account of negligence of the company’s officials These cases are pending at various stages of hearing. 

Total income of ALSL has grown by 82.45% Y-o-Y in FY08 and 52.87% Y-o-Y in FY09 driven by increase in sales volume of the existing products. The agrochemical products have dominated the overall sales of the company with their share being 84.70% in FY09.
