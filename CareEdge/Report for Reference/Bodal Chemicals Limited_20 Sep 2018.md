## **Detailed Rationale & Key Rating Drivers** 

CARE has withdrawn the outstanding ratings of ‘CARE A; Positive/ CARE A1’ [Single A; Outlook: Positive/A One] assigned to the bank facilities of Bodal Chemicals Ltd. (BCL) with immediate effect. The above action has been taken at the request of Bodal Chemicals Ltd. and ‘No Objection Certificate’ received from the banks that have extended the facilities rated by CARE. 

## **Analytical approach:** Not Applicable 

**Applicable Criteria** <u>Policy on Withdrawal of ratings</u> 

## **About the Company** 

BCL commenced operations in 1989 as a partnership firm and was subsequently converted into a private limited company in 1993. BCL has integrated dye/dye-intermediate manufacturing facilities with presence in reactive, acid and direct dyes, dye intermediates and basic chemicals. BCL’s installed capacity for manufacturing basic chemicals, dye intermediates, dyestuff and Linear Alkyl Benzene Sulphonic Acid (LABSA) stood at 1,90,000 metric tonne per annum (MTPA), 33,000 MTPA [including installed capacity of S P S Processors Pvt. Ltd. (SPS)], 39,000 MTPA and 18,000 MTPA respectively as on June 30, 2018.
