## **Rationale and key rating drivers** 

<mark>The ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Utssav CZ Gold Jewels Limited (UGJL) factor in the promoters extensive experience in the gold jewellery industry, UGJL’s comfortable capital structure and debt coverage indicators, growing scale of operations with moderate profitability, and its adequate liquidity profile.</mark> 

<mark>The ratings, however, remain constrained by the working capital-intensive nature of operations, susceptibility of profitability to fluctuations in gold prices due to unhedged inventory, and presence of intense competition in the jewellery industry which limits pricing flexibility.</mark> 

## **Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions** 

### **Positive factors** 

- <mark>Volume driven growth in scale of operations marked by total operating income above Rs. 1000 crore while maintaining PBILDT margin above 7%.</mark> 

- <mark>Improvement in capital structure with overall gearing below 0.75x on sustained basis.</mark> 

- <mark>Improvement in working capital cycle with operating cycle days reducing below 60 days on sustained basis.</mark> 

### **Negative factors** 

- <mark>Volume driven decline in scale of operation marked by total operating income below Rs. 500 crore along with significant moderation in profitability.</mark> 

- <mark>Any large debt-funded capex leading to deterioration of debt coverage indicators, marked by Term debt/GCA above 0.75x on sustained basis.</mark> 

- <mark>Any further deterioration in working capital cycle leading to stretch of liquidity.</mark>

## **Outlook:** Stable 

The stable outlook reflects CARE Ratings’ expectations that UGJL shall continue maintaining adequate financial risk profile in the medium term, while benefiting from its established operational track record and experience of promoters. 

## **Detailed description of key rating drivers:** 

## **Key strengths** 

### **Extensive experience of promoters in gold jewellery industry** 

UGJL is promoted by Mr. Pankajkumar Jagawat and Mr. Shashank Jagawat, who together have over two decades of experience in the gold and CZ-studded jewellery manufacturing business. Since the company’s inception in 2007, both promoters have been actively involved in strategic and operational management, contributing to the company’s steady growth. 

The promoters also own Shanti Gold International Limited (SGIL), incorporated in 2003 and engaged in manufacturing of CZstudded gold jewellery. SGIL was recently listed on NSE and BSE in August 2025. The promoters’ established presence through SGIL underscores their industry expertise, market relationships, and access to formal sources of capital. 

### **Comfortable capital structure and debt coverage indicators** 

UGJL’s capital structure improved significantly, with overall gearing improved to 1.03x as on March 31, 2025 (PY: 2.14x), aided by sizeable equity infusion through private placement and IPO (₹75 Cr) alongwith profit accretion of ₹25 Cr. Net worth consequently strengthened to ₹126.16 Cr as on March 31, 2025. Total debt primarily comprises working capital borrowings, with

Debt coverage indicators of UGJL remained comfortable marked by PBILDT interest coverage of 6.42x, Total debt/ PBILDT of 3.28x and Total debt/ GCA of 4.98x in FY25. Total debt primarily comprises of working capital limits with negligible term debt o/s. 

## **Growing scale of operations and moderate profitability** 

Scale of operations of UGJL grew by CAGR of 65% in past 3 years and stood moderate at 646.48 crore in FY25 as against Rs. 340.39 crore in FY24. The significant growth is aided by rising gold prices along with volume growth of 42% y-o-y. Profitability of company remained moderate marked by PBILDT margin of 6.15% in FY25 as against 6.30% in FY24. Cost of metal comprises of 93% of the total sales and thus efficient inventory management remains the key to maintain profitability. Management expects the profitability to improve marginally going forward on the back of addition of diamond studded jewellery in the product mix which is a higher margin segment. 

# **Key weaknesses** 

## **<mark>Working capital intensive nature of business</mark>** 

UGJL’s operations are working capital intensive. The company procures gold primarily on a cash basis while offering its customers a credit period of 30–60 days. Additionally, to cater to varied customer preferences, UGJL maintains a range of jewellery designs in stock, translating to around 30–45 days of inventory. Consequently, the company’s working capital cycle stood at 76 days in FY25, compared with 71 days in FY24. As a result of this business model, cash flow from operations was negative at ₹83.05 crore in FY25. Also, utilization of working capital limits remains moderate at around 80-90%. 

## **Profitability Margins Susceptible to Gold Price Volatility and Competitive Pressure** 

UGJL’s profitability remains exposed to fluctuations in gold prices, as a significant portion of its inventory is unhedged. High inventory levels of raw materials amplify this risk. Additionally, the company operates in a highly competitive jewellery market, which is largely unorganised, constraining pricing flexibility and limiting the ability to pass on cost increases to customers. Furthermore, UGJL’s operations remain concentrated in CZ-studded jewellery with limited presence in plain gold, diamond, or watches, exposing the company to potential revenue impact from shifts in consumer preferences. 

# **Liquidity** : Adequate 

<mark>Liquidity of UGJL remains Adequate marked by GCA of ~Rs. 26 crores in FY25 as against negligible debt repayment. Also, UGJL had free cash and bank balances of Rs. ~30 crore at FY25 end. Also, current ratio remains moderate at 1.66x.</mark>
