# **Background** 

CARE Ratings Limited’s (CARE Ratings’) ‘Policy on Default Recognition’ is in line with the extant regulatory guidelines, wherein, in respect of ratings for instruments / facilities with a pre-defined repayment schedule, ‘one-day-one-rupee’ delay is considered as default, whereas for facilities with no pre-determined repayment schedule, a 30-day grace period is allowed. This apart, the regulators have stipulated default recognition criteria in case the rated instrument / facility is rescheduled, as also specific timelines when a rating can be upgraded from ‘Default Grade’. 

CARE Ratings’ ‘Policy on Default Recognition’ (available at <u>www.careratings.com) stipulates the curing period as</u> follows: 

Generally, 90 Days - for upgrade from Default to Non-Investment Grade **Curing Period** Generally, 365 Days - for upgrade from Default to Investment Grade 

The ‘default grade’ rating for an instrument / facility shall not be upgraded unless all instruments / facilities of similar seniority are ’cured’ of their defaults. For subordinate instruments / facilities and structured instruments, the curing period will apply at the instrument / facility level. 

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) in its Master Circular states that “After a default is cured and the payments regularized, a CRA shall generally upgrade the rating from default to non-investment grade after a period of 90 days based on the satisfactory performance by the company during this period. CRAs may deviate from the said period of 90 days on a case to case basis, subject to the CRAs framing a detailed policy in this regard.” 

# **Policy on Curing Period** 

Pursuant to the aforementioned circular, CARE Ratings’ policy on curing period is as follows: 

CARE Ratings shall generally apply the curing period as mentioned in CARE Ratings’ ‘Policy on Default Recognition’. However, on a case-to-case basis, CARE Ratings may upgrade to / assign a ‘Non-D’ rating before the expiration of curing period. The upgrade from the ‘default grade’ to ‘non-investment grade’ rating / assignment of a ‘Non-D’ rating before expiration of 90-day timeline and an upgrade from the ‘default grade’ to ‘investment grade’ rating / assignment of an ‘investment grade’ rating before expiration of 365-day timeline shall happen only if the defaulted payments are regularised. The rating so assigned shall be based on CARE Ratings’ analytical judgment on the likelihood of recurrence of default. CARE Ratings may continue with the ‘default grade’ rating even after completion of the curing period, if it is of the view that there is a possibility of default in the near term. 

Some situations where the ratings may be assigned / upgraded before the expiration of above-mentioned curing period, include: 

# **<u>Credit Reasons resulting into an improvement in the underlying credit profile of the entity:</u>** 

- Change in ownership and/or management control moving to another entity / group; or 

- Sizeable inflow of long-term funds; or
