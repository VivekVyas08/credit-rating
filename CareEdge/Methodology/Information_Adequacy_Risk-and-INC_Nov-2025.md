**Criteria on Information Adequacy Risk and Issuer Non-Cooperation** [Issued in August 2022] [Issued in November 2025] 

# **Background** 

The assessment of the credit quality of an issuer / entity encompasses detailed analysis of the business, management, financial and industry risk associated with the issuer. Hence, to assign and monitor the ratings, it is important to have access to adequate and reliable information about the issuers on a continuous basis. 

While assigning ratings, CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) relies on data / information furnished by the issuers as well as publicly available information that it considers to be reliable. The reliance on data / information provided by the issuers is even more pronounced for the unlisted issuers where there is limited information available in the public domain. As such, cooperation from the issuers in terms of providing adequate and timely information is extremely critical for assignment and continuous monitoring of the ratings. 

# **Assessing the risk of non-availability of information** 

CareEdge Ratings believes that the purpose of credit ratings is best served if there is a transparency in the information sharing and discussions between the rated issuers and CareEdge Ratings, so that all the non-public information can be suitably incorporated for conducting a proper credit risk assessment. This assumes importance as information sharing is not a one-time exercise but an ongoing and continuous process which is critical to the review of the ratings. On the contrary, for the issuers who do not share information or engage into discussions with CareEdge Ratings, the credit risk assessment is solely based on public information, and that too, only to the extent this information is accessed by CareEdge Ratings. Hence, typically, such ratings are based on the past performance analysed from old or outdated datapoints and hence lack a holistic assessment, which may not reflect the true robustness of ratings that enjoy the benefit of full cooperation from the issuers. It is a continuous endeavour of CareEdge Ratings to elicit cooperation from its rated issuers for the rating review process to the best possible extent. The list of ’Minimum / Indicative Information requirement’ in terms of various sectors or types of ratings is enclosed as an Annexure at the end of this criteria paper. 

In accordance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) circular no. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD4/CIR/P/2016/119 dated November 1, 2016, in case the issuer does not provide the information as sought for ratings or does not pay the surveillance fees, then the issuer can be categorised as ‘noncooperating’ by attaching a suffix ’ISSUER NOT COOPERATING*’ (INC) with the credit rating symbol. The suffix indicates ‘Issuer did not cooperate; Based on best available information’. Users such as lenders, investors etc. are advised to take appropriate caution while using the credit ratings with the suffix ‘INC’. 

# **What constitutes non-cooperation and timelines for the same** 

Once a rating is accepted, it is subject to continuous monitoring during the life of the security / instrument / facility till the withdrawal of the rating by CareEdge Ratings (as per the withdrawal policy of CareEdge Ratings). As mentioned earlier, CareEdge Ratings relies on data / information furnished by the issuer as well as publicly available information for assigning or reviewing a rating. Any non-cooperation or inadequate information provided by the issuer may not yield meaningful assessment of the credit profile of the issuer by CareEdge Ratings.

# **Review process for non-cooperating issuers** 

In case of non-cooperating issuers, CareEdge Ratings reviews the ratings of securities / instruments / facilities on the basis of the ‘best available information’. This includes any information shared by the issuers with CareEdge Ratings, any publicly available information, including the report published by the Debenture Trustees (DT) from time to time, feedback from lenders / auditors / DTs, etc.

The cases which are in the INC category are reviewed on the basis of best available information till the time the ratings are withdrawn. CareEdge Ratings’ Rating Committee takes on record that the issuer is not cooperating and takes an appropriate rating action. 

Whenever CareEdge Ratings becomes aware of any missed payment on a rated security / instrument / facility for the INC cases, the rating of that security / instrument / facility is brought down to ‘CARE D’ in line with CareEdge Ratings’ policy on default recognition. 

CareEdge Ratings’ process of INC review has been updated in line with the SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/CRADT/CIR/P/2020/2, dated January 3, 2020. Accordingly, with effect from July 1, 2020, CareEdge Ratings has put in place a process to review and downgrade any investment grade INC rating to non-investment grade with INC status, if the issuer has all the outstanding ratings as non-cooperative for more than 6 months. 

Other aspects with respect to the issuers not cooperating with other credit rating agencies (CRAs): 

In line with the aforesaid SEBI circular dated January 3, 2020, and subsequent clarifications provided by SEBI, CareEdge Ratings shall examine whether an issuer’s rating(s) from all other CRA(s) have persistently remained in the INC category for 12 months or more before taking-up an initial rating assignment. This is applicable from January 1, 2021. 

- If 12 months have passed, CareEdge Ratings shall not take-up the assignment, until the issuer resumes cooperation with any of the previous CRA(s), or its rating(s) with the INC status are withdrawn by all the previous CRA(s). In case of securities, CareEdge Ratings shall not carry out the rating assignment until the issuer resumes cooperation with any of the previous CRA(s), or there is evidence that the issuer has made a request to all the previous CRA(s) to withdraw its rating(s) (duly acknowledged by all the previous CRA(s)). The latter would be subject to the rating for the issuer’s securities being eligible to be withdrawn by the previous CRA(s) (in line with the policy on withdrawal of the ratings). 

- If 12 months have not passed, CareEdge Ratings may take-up the initial rating assignment provided, in such cases, CareEdge Ratings will have to issue the PR before the expiry of 12 months from the INC PR of the previous CRA.

# **Annexure** 

# **Minimum information requirement, in the absence of which the case may be tagged as INC** 

# **Standard minimum information requirement for corporate sector cases:** 

1. No default statement(s) 

2. Latest annual report / audited financial statements 

3. Latest quarterly / half-yearly / annual financial results or performance results 

4. Latest liquidity position 

5. Details of debt obligations and repayment schedule 

6. Current and past quantitative / operational details (as applicable / if deemed necessary) 

7. Details about capex / investment plans 

8. Updated details of major lenders / auditors / debenture trustees / issuing and paying agents (IPAs), etc. 

9. Management interaction 

# **Standard minimum information requirement for Infrastructure sector cases** 

1. No default statement(s) 

2. Latest annual report / audited financial statements 

3. Latest quarterly / half-yearly / annual financial results or performance results 

4. Latest liquidity position 

5. Details of debt obligations and repayment schedule 

6. Business plan / cash flow projections for at least next 2 years 

7. Current and past operating performance (e.g., toll collections / actual plant load factor (PLF) / annuities / cargo volumes handled / tariff revision, etc.) 

8. Status of any major project(s) under implementation 

9. Details about capex / investment plans (for holding companies) 

10. Updated details of lenders / auditors / debenture trustees / IPAs, etc. 

11. Management interaction 

# **Standard minimum information requirement for banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) sector cases** 

1. No default statement(s) 

2. Latest annual report / audited financial statements 

3. Latest quarterly / half-yearly / annual financial results or performance results 

4. Details of debt obligations and repayment schedule 

5. Details of top individual / group exposures (in case of wholesale lending non-banking finance companies (NBFCs) / banks etc.) 

6. Updated details of lenders / auditors / debenture trustees / IPAs, etc. 

7. Capital adequacy ratio (CAR), asset liability maturity (ALM) statement as at the end of latest quarter / half-year / year 

8. Current and past operational data, including portfolio cuts, product profile, product-wise assets under management (AUM), product-wise delinquencies / gross non-performing asset (GNPA), collection efficiency, etc. (as applicable / if deemed necessary) 

9. Latest liquidity position 

10. Management interaction
