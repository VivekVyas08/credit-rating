# **Industry Overview** 

Power transmission is the key link in the overall power sector value chain. It facilitates evacuation of power from power-generating units spread across the country and supply to distribution entities, which supply power to endconsumers. The power transmission system typically comprises transmission lines, sub-stations, switching stations, transformers and distribution lines. 

Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL) is a Government of India (GoI) undertaking, responsible for interregional and inter-state transmission network and such inter-state transmission projects are governed per regulations of Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC). State transmission utilities manage intra-state network, and such projects are governed by regulations of State Electricity Regulatory Commissions (SERCs). 

The Government of India has been promoting private sector participation in power transmission space with a view to rapidly enhance the power transmission capacity. Most of the projects are awarded through tariff-based competitive bidding (TBCB) route, with the exception only made for implementing certain strategic and high technology projects awarded to PGCIL on nomination basis. This criteria document outlines the rating methodology for rating inter-state and intra state power transmission projects, which are also awarded to central and stateowned transmission utilities. 

# **Rating methodology** 

While analysing power transmission companies, CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) focuses on evaluating following broad parameters: 

- Project risk 

- Industry and regulatory risk 

- Management risk 

- Operational risk 

- Financial risk 

- Environment, Social, Governance (ESG) risk 

# **Project risk** 

Credit assessment of under-implementation transmission projects critically factors the promoter group and the following project risks. However, once it becomes operational, weightage ascribed to the promoter group is relatively reduced, as an operational infrastructure project is typically financed without recourse to the promoter group. Accordingly, for an operational project, higher weightage is given to the business profile of the asset as enumerated below. 

- **Pre-commissioning risk** 

In the pre-commissioning stage, evaluation of transmission projects largely involves permitting risks (availability of statutory approvals and clearances) and funding risk. The clearances for the project include environmental and forest clearances and crossings related to railway, rivers, roads, etc., from government authorities. The projects also involve acquisition of Right of Way (RoW) from parties for the development

of transmission infrastructure. Since securing of necessary approvals and RoW requires dealing with government and private parties, it makes under-implementation projects susceptible to delays and progress/status of these parameters is an important rating determinant for greenfield projects. 

- **Funding risk** 

For timely completion of the project, CareEdge Ratings positively considers financial closure, and further evaluates the status of promoter’s funds infusion, debt tie-up and key terms of the sanctioned debt. A strong track record of the promoter in executing similar-sized projects and its financial strength are important risk mitigants. Given the associated permitting and construction risk in the projects, the cost and time overruns are common, and hence, financial resourcefulness to fund cost-overruns/meet contingencies is also evaluated while arriving at the rating. 

- **Construction risk** 

The construction risk mainly involves analysis of the project location, type of topography, track record and credentials of engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) contractor and experience of the promoter in executing similar kind of projects. Projects in hilly areas with difficult terrain are generally more prone to construction risk against projects in the plains. Financial strength of EPC contractors and their track record of execution of similar kinds of projects in the past is also a key factor while evaluating the construction risk. The strong track record of promoters in executing such projects and its financial strength are important risk mitigants, in terms of timely execution and arrangement of finances in cases of increase in project cost due to contingencies. 

However, as transmission projects span long distances, cost overruns and delays are common. Hence, ratings may be lower in the project-implementation stage. ‘Fixed time-fixed price’ EPC contracts with presence of Liquidated damage (LD) clause in case of delay or levy of penalties can mitigate risk to some extent. For evaluation of under-construction projects, CareEdge Ratings evaluates the actual physical progress against planned progress. 

# **Industry and regulatory risk** 

CERC determines/approves tariff for inter-state transmission system (ISTS) projects, while SERC determines tariff for intra-state projects. For Central or State projects, where in tariff is determined on cost plus basis, CareEdge Ratings analyses the timeliness and adequacy of tariff determination/revision applicable in regulated tariff mechanism (RTM) projects. Delays in such determination/ revision could be a credit concern and may have been caused because of delay in the petition filing by the developer or delays in the issuance of an order by the regulator. Largely projects under Central and State-owned transmission utilities are on cost-plus basis, while majority projects awarded to private players are under tariff-based competitive bidding (TBCB) mechanism. Tariff regulations issued by CERC for all cost-plus projects are for a block period of five years while tariff regulations for SERC can vary based on states. 

Under the TBCB mechanism, the bidder specifies a fixed amount as the fee to be paid by the beneficiary for utilising the transmission line. CareEdge Ratings considers credit profile of cost-plus projects with strong counterparty better than projects under TBCB route.

# **Management risk** 

Evaluation of management quality is an essential part of all rating assessments. The promoter’s ability to support the project in times of financial stress or exigencies, track record in implementing and operating large transmission projects and availability of technical manpower, among others, are factored in while evaluating the management risk. Transmission projects, which have economic/strategic significance such as inter-state and intra-state projects being executed with the support of central and state government, respectively, are analysed for the linkages in the project special purpose vehicle (SPV) and central government or respective state government. 

# **Operational risk** 

While evaluating operational transmission project, CareEdge Ratings focuses on following broad parameters: 

- **Demand and supply risk** 

   - The transmission projects are protected from the demand risk as the arrangement between the project developer and the beneficiary is regulated by a long-term agreement, ‘Transmission Service Arrangement’ (TSA). The annual fixed charge is billed to the beneficiary as a fee for the usage of transmission line per TSA, given that the line availability is maintained by the project developer and transmission charges are paid monthly. A TSA is usually for the long term and provides revenue visibility of the project subject to maintenance of operational parameters. 

- **Operation and maintenance risk** 

Transmission projects are required to be maintained by the project developer from general wear and tear over the project life. Revenues of transmission projects are linked to annual availability of transmission line and regulators have prescribed normative annual transmission line availability of 98.00%. Projects shall be eligible for incentive, if line availability remains above 98.50% and no incentive will be payable for line availability beyond 99.75%. Operations and maintenance (O&M) costs of a transmission project generally do not constitute a substantial portion of cash outflow. However, repeated breakdowns in the transmission line can increase O&M costs of a project and impact recovery of annual fixed charge (including penalty, if applicable) if annual availability remains below normative availability. CareEdge Ratings evaluates nature of the O&M contract for maintaining transmission networks. Projects with superior ability to absorb escalation in O&M expenses without adverse impact on their debt-servicing ability are viewed favourably. 

- **Risk related to natural calamities and contingency plan** 

Transmission assets have a long life of 30-35 years, which usually exceeds loan tenure. While breakdowns are rare, assets in the hilly terrain, flood and cyclone-affected areas are prone to natural calamities, which can temporarily impact project cash flows. Projects are analysed for backup plans to restore connectivity, insurance cover to protect returns, financial health of O&M contractor, track record of execution of O&M for transmission projects, liquidity cushion available with the SPV (mainly debt service reserve account [DSRA] balance) and financial health of the parent company. 

- **Counterparty credit risk** 

CareEdge Ratings while evaluating the credit profile of a transmission project, analyses the periodical payment track record and credit profile of the beneficiary. CareEdge Ratings also evaluates collection efficiency of projects. Transmission projects, for analysis of counterparty credit risk, can be broadly divided as projects covered under point of connection (PoC) mechanism, and those that are standalone intra-state transmission lines.

# **Projects covered under PoC mechanism** 

Under the PoC mechanism, all ISTS licensees have to share monthly collections arising from ISTS charges. Central Transmission Utility of India Limited (CTUIL), a 100% subsidiary of PGCIL has been entrusted with the responsibility of billing all ISTS customers on behalf of all ISTS licensees and disburse collections among all ISTS licensees according to the executed TSA and Revenue Sharing Agreement (RSA) with ISTS licensees and ISTS customers, respectively. In this way, ISTS projects are not directly exposed to counterparty risk as the collection role is being played by CTUIL. Risk of non-payment of dues from a single project is borne by all ISTS licensees in the proportion to their share in the revenue pool, diversifying counterparty credit risk. ISTS projects with the PoC mechanism are placed better compared to state transmission projects with high collection efficiency of over 98% for the pool and low receivable days of ~60 days. 

# **Standalone intra-state transmission lines** 

The intra-state transmission projects are exposed to the counterparty credit risk given the beneficiaries of the projects are state distribution companies (DISCOMs). The state projects are analysed for the financial position of the state DISCOM, track record of state DISCOM’s payment to the SPV, timely filing of tariff petition and issuance of tariff order from SERC, regulatory environment of the state and timely payment of subsidy by state government to DISCOM. As a payment security, the receivables are backed by revolving letter of credit opened by Long Term Transmission Customer (LTTC) in favour of transmission licensee equivalent to one month of billing, which mitigates the counter party risk to some extent in intra state transmission projects. per the provisions, the transmission licensee shall have the right to regulate the power in case of non-payment of dues by the beneficiary. 

# **Financial risk** 

The main parameters of financial risk analysis of transmission projects: 

- Financial structure and leverage 

- Recovery of annual fixed cost (AFC) and cash flow adequacy 

- O&M cost and interest rate risk 

- Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) 

- Trust & retention account (TRA) and DSRA 

- Tail period 

# **Financial structure and leverage** 

Under financial structure and leverage, CareEdge Ratings analyses financial structure of the transmission project, debt and equity funding of the transaction and overall leverage of the project. The transmission projects are capital intensive and generally funded in debt:equity of 70:30 leading to high leverage. Conventionally, lower overall gearing is seen positively but the transmission projects with stable and predictable cash flow with longer repayment periods tend to mitigate the high gearing to an extent. Foreign exchange variation risk is also assessed for projects with un-hedged exposure to project debt raised in foreign currency. CareEdge Ratings also evaluates quantum of capital creditors pending for settlement for a long time, large contingent liabilities and disputes and financial impact in case of unfavourable event triggering payments.

# **Recovery of annual fixed cost and cash flow adequacy** 

The recovery of annual fixed cost (AFC) of transmission project, which consists of return on equity, depreciation, interest on loan capital, interest on working capital and O&M expense, is linked to the line availability and not subject to usage of transmission system. For instance, if the licensee is able to maintain line availability of above 98.00%, full AFC is recoverable. Licensee is also entitled to earn incentives, if line availability remains above 98.50%. CareEdge Ratings analyses the track record of the licensee in maintaining line availability. In a transmission project, the cash flow adequacy is certain given the long-term TSA is signed between the licensee and beneficiary and line availability is maintained above the normative level. Licensees incur nominal expense on O&M of the transmission system. While evaluating a transmission project’s financial profile, adequacy of project cash flows is estimated against repayment obligations. 

# **Debt service coverage ratio** 

Transmission projects can sustain on lower debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) given the certainty attached to project cash flows. Revenue stream is fixed based on TSA signed between the beneficiary and licensee and is not subject to usage of the transmission system. Transmission projects with the lower DSCR can also fetch a better credit profile unlike manufacturing set ups or other projects, where cushion in DSCR needs to be plentiful. 

# **Project life coverage ratio** 

For projects exposed to refinancing risk, CareEdge Ratings also analyses project life coverage ratio (PLCR), which provides a sense of refinancing ability of the transmission asset. PLCR is calculated as net present value (NPV) of cash flow available for debt servicing over the remaining life of project divided by the current debt levels. 

# **DSRA and TRA/escrow mechanism** 

CareEdge Ratings while evaluating the financial risk of a transmission project derives comfort from the presence of DSRA for servicing interest and principal obligations. DSRA can aid the project in exigencies, such as lower line availability due to issue in transmission system, damage to the transmission system due to natural calamities, delay in the receipt of payment from beneficiary, among others. The presence of DSRA lends comfort and enhances the liquidity profile of the transmission project. CareEdge Ratings also derives comfort from the TRA mechanism under which the cash flows arising from the transmission project are routed through TRA account and applied per the waterfall mechanism. Excess cash flows after payment of statutory dues, O&M expenses and interest & principal repayments are invested in permitted investments per provision of TRA. The presence of TRA/escrow mechanism prevents the diversion of funds outside the project and is considered as credit positive. 

# **O&M cost and interest rate risk** 

Projects are analysed for exposure to variability in O&M cost and interest rate. O&M cost of a transmission project are nominal though variability in the cost in absence of a fixed price contract can impact profitability going forward. Projects with fixed-price O&M contracts are placed better. With interest cost being the major outflow component, uptick in the interest rates can impact a project’s overall debt service ability. Adverse movement in the interest cost of project against fixed revenue streams (under TBCB route) can substantially lower project returns.

# **Tail period** 

Tail period is defined as the period left after completion of repayment of project debt till the maturity of TSA. Transmission projects generally have longer tail period, which provides cushion for contingencies. Longer tail period helps transmission licensee in easy refinancing project debt at competitive rates with elongated repayment schedules. Longer tail period leads to healthy PLCR and mitigates refinancing risk related to bullet payments. 

# **Environment, Social, Governance (ESG) risks** 

Transmission projects have moderate Environmental and Social (E&S) risks, especially prominent during construction. 

Environmental risk: In the past, overhead transmission lines have been known to cause harm to wildlife species through bird strike casualties and disturbances to animal habitats from electro-magnetic radiation. Hence, environmental risk at certain geographies may be significant. 

Social risk: Effective management of social risk through the smooth settlement of discord with locals during land acquisition is factored in the analysis. 

Governance risk: CareEdge Ratings also assesses composition of the Board of Directors of the transmission project developer, level of compliance with policies, quality of financial reporting and adequacy in disclosure from perspective of corporate governance. 

# **Conclusion** 

The rating outcome is ultimately an assessment of fundamentals and probabilities of change in fundamentals. CareEdge Ratings analyses each of these factors and their linkages to arrive at the overall assessment of credit quality of the issuer. In under-implementation projects, project risk assessment is given higher importance, whereas in operational projects, risk assessment is primarily focused on availability of cash flows against debt service obligations and counterparty credit risk. While the methodology encompasses comprehensive technical, financial, commercial, economic and management analysis, credit rating is an overall assessment of all aspects of the issuer.
