## **Background** 

The ratings assigned by CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) are not a one-time exercise, but are kept under surveillance till the time the obligations under the rated securities / instruments / facilities are fully extinguished or the ratings are withdrawn, in the situations explained in detail in this document. 

CareEdge Ratings withdraws a credit rating under the following situations: 

- **Full redemption:** On full redemption of the rated security / instrument / facility, i.e., on confirmation by the rated entity and the lender / trustee / issuing and paying agent (IPA) that the full maturity value of the rated security / instrument / facility has been paid-off and there is no amount outstanding against the same. CareEdge Ratings also withdraws the rating if it has adequate supporting documents to establish that the rated security / instrument / facility has been fully extinguished / closed. 

- **Proposed debt rated but not placed:** On receipt of confirmation from the rated entity by CareEdge Ratings that the rating awarded has not been used for mobilising funds, and as such, no amount is outstanding against the rated security / instrument / facility. 

- **Merger / amalgamation / liquidation:** On merger / amalgamation / liquidation of the rated entity, wherein it may no longer be useful or necessary for CareEdge Ratings to maintain a rating on the rated entity’s obligations. 

- **Transfer of the rated security / instrument / facility from one entity to the other:** In case of transfer of the rated security / instrument / facility from one entity to the other, based on a written confirmation from the lender / trustee / IPA stating that, as per their records, there is no outstanding amount against the rated security / instrument / facility in the name of the rated entity. 

- **Withdrawal in case of provisional ratings:** Please refer to Policy on Assignment of Provisional Ratings available on www.careratings.com. 

- **Withdrawal in case of ratings based on credit enhancement:** 

For the ratings based on credit enhancement, if during the tenure of the rated security / instrument / facility, the credit enhancement ceases to exist or there is a change in the terms (in concurrence with the lender / investor), CareEdge Ratings would review the rating for withdrawal of the same and simultaneously assign a new rating based on the revised terms. Accordingly, if an entity’s ‘credit enhanced (CE) rating’ is being withdrawn, the unsupported rating shall also be withdrawn simultaneously without any specific documentation for withdrawal of unsupported rating. However, the practice of withdrawal and simultaneous reassignment of a new rating would not be followed if the credit enhancement fails to work as anticipated. In such cases, CareEdge Ratings would take an appropriate rating action. 

For example, if CareEdge Ratings has relied on a corporate guarantee and assigned a ‘CE rating’ to an issue and the corporate guarantee ceases to exist, the rating would be reviewed for withdrawal and a new standalone rating would be assigned simultaneously. On the other hand, if CareEdge Ratings has relied on a corporate guarantee and assigned a ‘CE rating’ to an issue and there is a missed payment without the guarantee being

Apart from the above, CareEdge Ratings would withdraw the ratings of specific securities / instruments / facilities as mentioned below: 

- **Capital market securities / instruments (i.e., bonds, non-convertible debentures (NCDs), or other capital market securities / instruments):** 

   - a) Withdrawal in case of a single rating outstanding on the rated security / instrument: CareEdge Ratings can withdraw the rating, subject to: 

      - CareEdge Ratings has rated the security / instrument continuously for 5 years OR 50% of the tenure of the security / instrument, whichever is longer; **AND** 

      - CareEdge Ratings has received an undertaking from the issuer that a rating is available on that security / instrument from another credit rating agency (CRA) registered with Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI); **AND** 

      - CareEdge Ratings has received an undertaking from the other CRA that a new rating has been assigned to such security / instrument. 

   - b) Withdrawal in case of multiple ratings outstanding on the rated security / instrument: In case of multiple ratings on a rated security / instrument (where there is no regulatory mandate for multiple ratings), CareEdge Ratings can withdraw the rating earlier than stipulated provided: 

      - CareEdge Ratings has rated the security / instrument continuously for 3 years OR 50% of the tenure of the security / instrument, whichever is longer; **AND** 

      - CareEdge Ratings has received no-objection certificates (NOCs) from 75% of the bondholders by value of the outstanding debt for withdrawal of the rating; **AND** 

      - CareEdge Ratings has received an undertaking from the issuer that a rating is available on that security / instrument from another CRA accredited by SEBI; **AND** 

      - CareEdge Ratings has received an undertaking from the other CRA(s) that a rating is available on such security / instrument. 

- **Bank facilities:** The ratings on all types of bank facilities can be withdrawn at the request of the rated entity, subject to receipt of NOCs from all the lenders specifically mentioning that the NOCs are being issued for such withdrawal of the rating of the facility availed from the lender. 

- **Perpetual debt securities / instruments that are listed or proposed to be listed on a recognised stock exchange:** The ratings of perpetual debt securities / instruments like AT1 bonds, that are listed or proposed to be listed on a recognised stock exchange, can be withdrawn subject to the following conditions: 

      - CareEdge Ratings has rated such securities / instruments continuously for 5 years; **AND** 

      - CareEdge Ratings has received an undertaking from the issuer that a rating is available on such securities / instruments; **AND**

- **Open-ended mutual fund Schemes:** The ratings of open-ended mutual fund schemes being perpetual in nature and having no specified maturity can be withdrawn upon receipt of the request for withdrawal from the asset management company (AMC). The ratings would be placed on ‘Notice of Withdrawal’ for 30 days before being withdrawn at the end of such notice period for withdrawal. 

- **Issuer ratings:** Issuer ratings can be withdrawn upon receipt of the request for withdrawal from the rated entity. 

- **Commercial papers and certificate of deposits:** CareEdge Ratings withdraws outstanding ratings for money market instruments, such as commercial papers, certificates of deposit, etc., upon receipt of the request for withdrawal from the rated entity, if the instrument is not placed or there are no obligations outstanding against the rated instruments. In this regard, CareEdge Ratings relies on confirmation from the rated entities and also takes confirmation from the IPA or auditors or other sources to satisfy itself that the rated obligations have been repaid fully and there are no outstandings against the same. 

- **Security receipts (SRs):** The ratings of SRs can be withdrawn at the request of the securitisation companies (SCs) / asset reconstruction companies (ARCs), subject to receipt of NOCs from all the investors and an undertaking from the trustee that a rating is available on that SR from another CRA(s). The NOCs from the investors should specifically mentioning that the NOCs are being issued for such withdrawal of the ratings of the SRs. The ratings of SRs also can be withdrawn after expiry of the resolution period upon receipt of request for withdrawal of its recovery ratings from the SC / ARC.

Withdrawal of the ratings under the different situations mentioned above is subject to clearance of fees payable by the clients to CareEdge Ratings.
