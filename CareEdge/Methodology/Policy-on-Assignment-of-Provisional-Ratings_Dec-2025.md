When a rating is assigned pending execution of certain critical documents or steps to be taken, such rating is a ‘Provisional’ rating indicated by the prefix ‘Provisional’ before the rating symbol. A rating is considered as provisional, and not final, when it is contingent upon occurrence of certain steps or execution of critical documents Upon execution of critical documents / completion of steps to the satisfaction of CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings), the final rating is assigned, provided the documents executed are consistent with the draft documents available at the time of assigning the provisional rating. However, in case of rating of an Infrastructure Investment Trust (InvIT) / Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT), the rating is provisional subject to fulfilment of certain conditions based on which the rating is assigned. 

This document explains in detail the process followed by CareEdge Ratings while assigning provisional ratings, further review, and finalisation / confirmation of the same. This policy is in line with the extant guidelines of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and subsequent clarifications received from SEBI on the same. 

# **When are Provisional ratings assigned?** 

Provisional ratings are assigned when the ratings are contingent upon completion of the following steps / documentation, as applicable: 

- a. Execution of letter of comfort, corporate guarantee, or other forms of explicit third-party support. 

- b. Execution of documents, such as debenture trust deed / debenture trustee agreement, legal agreements / opinions, representations and warranties, and final term sheet. 

- c. Assignment of loan pools or finalisation of cash flow escrow arrangements. 

- d. Setting-up of a debt service reserve account. 

- e. Opening of an escrow account. 

- f. For a proposed REIT or InvIT, pending formation of the trust, only after receipt of SEBI registration subject to a declaration from the sponsor to this effect being submitted to the credit rating agency. 

CARE Ratings assigns a provisional rating when the execution of the documents that are ‘central’ / ‘critical’ to the rating are pending, e.g., letter of comfort, corporate guarantee, or such other forms of explicit third-party support or cash flow cover pool mechanisms like in securitisation transactions, covered bonds or in case of REITs / InvITs. 

Further, CareEdge Ratings does not assign provisional ratings for an issuer evaluating strategic decisions, such as funding mix for a project, acquisition, debt restructuring, scenario-analysis in loan refinancing, etc.

Upon review of the case by the end of the stipulated period, the following aspects are considered to decide about providing extension of the time period and maintaining the rating as ‘provisional’ or withdrawing the rating: 

- If funds are not raised within one year from the assignment of the provisional rating and there are no fundraising plans in the next six months, then CareEdge Ratings withdraws the rating on receipt of confirmation from the rated entity that the provisional rating awarded has not been used for mobilising funds, and as such, no amount is outstanding against the rated instrument / facility. 

- If funds are not raised, then the status of the fund-raising plan concerning the rated instrument / facility and the likelihood of funds being raised in the next six months is evaluated. In case funds are not raised in such extended period of six months (i.e., funds are not raised in total period of 18 months from the assignment of the provisional rating), then CareEdge Ratings reviews the rating for withdrawal on receipt of confirmation from the rated entity that the provisional rating awarded has not been used for mobilising funds, and as such, no amount is outstanding against the rated instrument / facility. # **In case of material changes in the terms of the transaction** 

- If funds have been raised and there are material changes in the terms of the transaction after the assignment of the initial provisional rating, then the initial provisional rating is withdrawn and simultaneously a fresh rating is assigned to the transaction considering the revised terms of the transaction. Further, both the withdrawal and the fresh rating are communicated by a single press release. 

- If funds have not been raised and there are material changes in the terms of the transaction after the assignment of the initial provisional rating, then the initial provisional rating is withdrawn. Also, at the request of the client, a fresh provisional rating can be assigned based on the revised terms of the transaction. 

In case the issuer has raised only part of the rated amount, or the pending steps / documentation is complete for only part of the rated amount, the rating for such part amount is converted to final and is subject to the validity period for the placed instruments as mentioned above. 

# **Validity period in case of Issuer ratings for REITs / InvITs / AIFs** 

Provisional Issuer ratings can be assigned to REITs / InvITs which are not linked to the placement of a specific debt instrument. In such cases, the validity period is computed from completion of fund raising and issuance of units by the REITs / InvITs. The validity period comprises 90 days, along with an extension of up to 90 days granted on a case-to-case basis, similar to cases involving issuance of debt instruments. Similarly, in case of Alternate Investment Funds (AIFs), the validity period of the provisional ratings is reckoned from the issuance of units by the AIFs. 

# **Disclosures in the press releases** 

The following disclosures are included in the press release while assigning provisional ratings: 

- i. The pending steps / documentation considered while assigning the provisional rating. 

- ii. Risks associated with the provisional nature of the credit rating, including risk factors that are present in the absence of the completed documentation / steps. 

- iii. The rating that would have been assigned in absence of the pending steps / documentation considered while assigning the provisional rating. In cases where the absence of the said steps / documentation would not result in any rating being assigned (for instance, in case of a provisional rating for REITs / InvITs - pending formation of the trust), the same is specified in the press release. 

- iv. While assigning a provisional rating to an unplaced instrument, the press release specifies that in case the instrument is subsequently issued, the provisional rating shall be converted into the final rating as per the validity period mentioned above. 

- v. While assigning a provisional rating to a placed instrument, the press release specifies the rating and the timeline implications as per the validity period mentioned above.

In case of provisional ratings for proposed REITs or InvITs, the following disclosures are also made in the press release, wherever applicable: 

- i. The broad details of the assets that are proposed to be held by the REITs / InvITs, the proposed capital structure, etc. 

- ii. The press release discloses that CareEdge Ratings has taken an undertaking from the sponsor stating that the key assumptions (relating to the assets, capital structure, etc.) align with the details filed by the sponsor with SEBI. 

- iii. In case of change in the provisional rating due to change in the aforesaid key assumptions, the press release states that the rating by CareEdge Ratings is based on a declaration from the issuer that similar changes have been made in the filing with SEBI. 

The press release made consequent to confirmation of all provisional ratings, i.e., conversion to the final rating, mentions details of execution of appropriate documents based on which the rating has been confirmed. 

# **Unaccepted Provisional ratings** 

In case provisional ratings assigned are not accepted by the issuer (or sponsor, in case of REITs / InvITs), then in the ‘unaccepted ratings’ list, the following supplementary disclosures are provided on the website of CareEdge Ratings: 

- a) Steps / documentation pending which the provisional rating was to be disclosed. For instance, in case of REITs / InvITs, such disclosure contains the broad details of the assets to be held under the Trust, the proposed capital structure, etc. 

- b) Rating that would be assigned in absence of the said steps / documentation, wherever applicable.
