Driven by technological advancements, evolving competitive landscape, and key policy initiatives, the Indian telecom sector has undergone a complete transformation in the last decade. The sector is gradually transitioning to non-telecom business such as enterprise solutions, cloud services and Wifi-Lan services, which have applications across fields. This shift is improving overall revenue and reducing retail dependency of mobility service providers. Successful pan-India rollout of 5G by major players has further augmented this transition. 

CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) assesses credit worthiness of Mobile Service Providers based on analysis of quantitative financial and operational parameters and qualitative factor assessments. Qualitative factors include analysis of the operating environment/industry and company-specific parameters, which include an analysis of its operating efficiency and its competitiveness compared to other players in the industry. While rating mobile service providers, CareEdge Ratings makes a critical assessment of following parameters: - 

# **Operating Environment** 

CareEdge Ratings analyses features of the industry to understand risk factors affecting all service providers within the industry and then focuses on explicit factors affecting the operator, which helps ascertaining relative position of the operator in the given industry. 

Key factors that CareEdge Ratings considers while evaluating the industry are as follows: 

- **Structure of the industry** 

Evaluation of the industry structure includes an analysis of the number of players present within the industry, its stability, entry barriers and level of competition. Key characteristics of the Indian mobile service providers sector include high growth rates of data consumption, a large, plateaued subscriber base, low Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) and Average Revenue Per Minute (ARPM) and high level of competition. The sector has consolidated with three large players and government-owned entities remaining in the sector. 

However, the sector continues to require large investments in network deployment and rollout of services and these projects typically have a long gestation period. CareEdge Ratings analyses ability of operators to invest in new technologies and complete their investments and capex plan in a timely manner, their capacity to absorb the significant customer acquisition cost and financial flexibility to fund initial losses and unanticipated delays. 

- **Regulatory Framework** 

Regulatory actions determine area, type, nature of service and levies on operators. Although regulatory uncertainties have reduced in the last few years with emergence of a more stable and predictable regime, regulatory risk continues to be of paramount importance. New regulation can bring about a major change in the competitive environment, resulting in major uncertainty with respect to the company’s operations. The presence of an independent regulator in the form of Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) and periodic introduction of several regulatory measures have ensured that the sector continues to remain tightly regulated.

# **Company-specific/Business Parameters** 

- **Market Position** 

CareEdge Ratings considers market position a crucial operating factor in analysis of mobile service providers. The relative position and strength of a service provider within its segment are integral to sustainability of its operations and its ability to withstand competitive pressures from other providers. Market position is assessed based on subscriber base, revenue share, performance track record and net subscriber additions  including subscriber churn. 

Given the capital-intensive nature of the industry and significant marketing costs involved in acquiring subscribers, ability to transition existing customers to adopt higher technologies, maintain a widespread presence and achieve a large scale of operations is important. Scale and size of operations determine an operator’s ability to distribute fixed costs over a larger base, ensuring better asset utilisation and scale advantages in marketing and distribution costs. 

CareEdge Ratings evaluates an operator’s relative position within its operating circles through trend analysis and peer group analysis of subscriber base, high-value subscribers, ARPUs and subscriber churn rates. CareEdge Ratings examines the growth potential of the service areas in which the provider operates and strategic initiatives undertaken to enhance its customer value proposition. 

- **Technology Risk** 

Telecom sector has experienced rapid technological advancements, including the roll-out of 5G and integration of artificial intelligence (AI), necessitating significant investment in network upgrades. Ineffective management of these technological evolutions can result in operational inefficiencies. The telecom industry relies heavily on the global supply chain for equipment and technology platforms, which are vulnerable to disruption caused by geopolitical tensions and other factors. The industry also faces challenges in integrating new technologies and ensuring seamless compatibility of equipment from different vendors with existing infrastructure, potentially leading to increased operating costs. 

Technology disruptions such as satellite broadband services through low earth orbit satellites, may impact terrestrial mobile service providers. Therefore, operators’ readiness to continuously invest in technology upgrades, offering innovative and broader services while maintaining superior network coverage and quality of service will be a key differentiator. Mobile service providers that can successfully migrate users to adopt higher versions of deployed technology will have a competitive advantage. 

Given the significant financial investment required for technological upgrades, CareEdge Ratings evaluates an operator’s financial capacity to make regular technology investments and assess their capability to enhance revenue and expand operating margins. 

- **Geographical Diversification/Consumer Mix** 

An operator with a diversified reach and strong presence across numerous service areas holds a significant advantage over those with a limited geographical footprint. Diversification ensures that the operator is not overly reliant on a few service areas or a specific customer segment for its revenues. 

The customer mix, particularly ratio of postpaid to prepaid customers, is a critical factor in evaluating in operator’s performance, as it directly influences ARPU. Prepaid ARPUs tend to be lower compared to

postpaid ARPUs due to service differentiation. A higher proportion of prepaid subscribers often leads to increased churn due to price sensitivity, making a substantial share of postpaid subscribers advantageous. The technology mix of the subscriber base also plays a crucial role in determining ARPUs. Revenue generation is influenced by the quality of the subscribers, which is determined by the technology they use – 2G, 4G or 5G and their service usage patterns. Mobile service providers frequently bundle attractive offers in collaboration with over-the-top (OTT) platforms to retain and attract subscribers. 

Consequently, CareEdge Ratings assesses an operator’s ability to increase the ARPU by transitioning customers to post-paid plans and implementing higher tariffs, all while maintaining market share through extensive coverage and service quality. 

- **Business diversification** 

While wireless mobile services have traditionally been the cornerstone of revenue for providers, they have increasingly diversified their income streams by expanding into non-mobile segments such as enterprise solutions, home broadband, and direct-to-home television. This strategic diversification has significantly boosted the share of non-mobile revenue. 

Amidst this transition in the industry, providers are placing greater emphasis on digital services, including media streaming, cloud-based solutions, and personalized business offerings. These additional services not only diversify revenue streams but also restrict subscriber churn. 

CareEdge Ratings evaluates business diversification by examining the revenue mix – from mobile and nonmobile services. 

- **Brand Image/Network Coverage & Quality** 

The sector is intensely competitive, with service providers vying to capture market share through expansive network coverage and superior service quality. The sufficiency of spectrum holdings across spectrum band (low -band 800 MHz and 900 MHz to high band 2300 MHz, 3300 MHz) with distant license expiry profile is crucial, as it indicates (i) number of subscribers that can be served, (ii) volume of data that can be handled (iii) distance that can be covered and (iv) technological services that can be supported. Consequently, service providers with spectrum across diverse frequency bands within a circle play a vital role in determining network coverage and service quality. 

A key factor contributing to an operator’s brand image is its network coverage and quality. Operators offering  seamless coverage, minimal call drops, high data speeds through low latency, superior voice quality are preferred by consumers, enhancing their market positioning and competitive edge. CareEdge Ratings also refers to findings of performance monitoring report on quality of service released by TRAI, which provide insights to performance of players. 

# **Financial Profile** 

CareEdge Ratings’ analysis of the financial profile of an operator considers both historical and projected financial performance. Trends over a 3-5-year period are analysed and compared to the industry average. CareEdge Ratings’ financial analysis is focused on the stability and sustainability of earnings and cash flow, which underpins the operator’s ability to timely repay their debt obligations. CareEdge Ratings also analyses the company’s future cash flows under adverse situations to analyse its financial flexibility across scenarios.

The sector is capital intensive as networks constantly require upgrading to meet technological advancements. A proportion of capital expenditure is considered non-discretionary, which can rise or fall depending on technological shifts, which occur periodically, which necessitates an increase in the capex to be incurred by the operator. The operators’ ability to generate sufficient internal cash flow to meet these capital requirements is a key factor that influences the operator’s credit quality. 

CareEdge Ratings also assesses an operator’s financial performance in terms of its profitability at the earnings before interest, taxation, depreciation, and amortisation (EBITDA) level. The operator’s EBITDA margins are influenced by mobile tariff plan mix and operating costs such as network operating expenses, license fee, spectrum usage charges contribute majorly to the cost structure. Customer acquisition costs remain high and longer gestation period of new technologies leading to lower returns on investments, diversification to new service areas and offerings remains crucial for providing higher revenue generation capabilities. 

- Leverage/Debt Profile - 

   - Leverage is a key factor in analysing an operator’s long-term creditworthiness and solvency given the high capital intensity of the sector. CareEdge Ratings analyses the capital structure including equity and debt funding. The extent of deferred payment liabilities arising out of spectrum purchase and dues pertaining to adjusted gross revenue (AGR) is also assessed. 

CareEdge Ratings generally excludes intangible assets while computing net worth, however, in case of mobile service providers, their spectrum holding, which is an intangible asset is inherent to their ability to offer their services, and hence, is not excluded while calculating the net worth. Companies in the mobile service providers sector are required to make huge initial outlays in initial rollout and in modernisation projects, which have a long gestation period. As a result, the operator’s ability to arrange for long-term debt with suitable maturity assumes paramount importance in determining viability of its operations.

# **Promoter/Management Evaluation** 

Mobile Service Providers Sector is a capex intensive sector and needs huge investments to fund capex plans (modernisation and replacement) arising from demand with change in the technology used and business environment. The sector is highly leveraged and with the service providers making all efforts for customer acquisition and retention through price competitive offers, the demand for huge investments to upgrade their infrastructure and to fund acquisition cost remains intact. Thus, financial flexibility and willingness of promoters in terms of ability to infuse additional equity as and when required is a critical parameter, while analysing the operator’s risk profile. Promoters’ financial flexibility and the way in which they propose to fund initial losses and the contingencies arising from unanticipated delays or longer-than-anticipated gestation period is critically examined by CareEdge Ratings. The company’s management of the company is assessed for its role in developing and implementing long-term strategies that provide the foundation for a sustainable long-term competitive advantage. CareEdge Ratings believes that the strength of the management team will play a crucial role in determining an operator’s medium-to-long-term competitiveness.

# **Environmental Social and Governance (ESG) risk:** 

CareEdge Ratings assesses environmental social and governance risks associated with mobility service providers and extent of their compliance in relation to the legal framework in India. 

**Environmental risk** in telecom sector assesses the extent of initiatives taken by the entity in achieving carbon neutrality. With the implementation of 5G services leading to increased data consumption, resulting in higher energy consumption, which will lead to more carbon emission impacting the environment. Hence, mobility service providers must implement energy efficient next-gen equipment and green energy power solutions across its networks to achieve low power consumption and high performance. 

Other initiatives include achieving net-zero life cycle of equipment, by standardisation of designs, reusage/repurposing of existing network equipment and or usage of network components, which are recyclable (creation of circular supply chain model) at the end of its useful life, minimising land pollution due to dumping wastage of electronic components. 

**Social risk** assessment includes stronger push for better connectivity to rural and remote areas improving access to remote learning or healthcare via telemetry will have greater impact in overall social development. Other measures comprise impact investing (social bonds, ESG bonds, blended finance models), means investing in social cause. 

**Governance risk** in a corporate is determined by a host of factors, which includes and is not limited to the following, , financial reporting, corporate practices, adherence to code of conduct, board composition, whistleblower policies, and investor relations, among others. CareEdge Ratings tries to form a broad opinion from the available information regarding the governance standards of the entity being evaluated. CareEdge Ratings also assesses composition of the company’s Board of Directors, level of compliance with policies, quality of financial reporting and the adequacy in disclosure from the perspective of corporate governance. 

# **Conclusion** 

The rating outcome is ultimately an assessment of fundamentals and probabilities of change in fundamentals. CareEdge Ratings assesses credit quality of a mobile service provider based on several qualitative and quantitative parameters, which include the operator’s positioning on technology, strategies to maintain and expand market reach through prudent financial planning, capability and plans to offer higher-margin services and to explore new revenue sources for better generation of returns from their investments.
