## **AUTHOR CONTACTS** 

This document updates and supersedes ICRA’s earlier policy on assigning provisional ratings, published in July 2021<sup>**1**</sup> . The revised policy incorporates select additional scenarios in which a provisional rating could be assigned and how ICRA’s rating rationales would articulate the related disclosures. 

# **Assignment of Provisional Ratings** 

- **(A) General applicability**<sup>**2**</sup> : ICRA assigns a ‘Provisional’ rating to debt instruments/ borrowing facilities to denote that the rating is contingent upon the occurrence of certain actions or the execution of certain documents. These pending actions/ documents are among the following: 

   1. Execution of letter of comfort, corporate guarantee, or other forms of explicit third-party support 

   2. Execution of documents such as debenture trust deed, debenture trustee agreement, legal agreements/ opinions, representations and warranties, final term sheet 

   3. Assignment of loan pools or finalisation of cash flow escrow arrangements 

   4. Setting-up of debt service reserve account (DSRA) 

   5. Opening of escrow account 

The Provisional rating indicates the final rating that is likely to be assigned after the pending actions/ documents are completed, _ceteris paribus_ . In case the pending actions/ documents are not completed, the rating could either be different or would not have been assigned _ab initio_ .

- **(C) Applicability specific to mutual fund schemes and capital protection-oriented schemes:** While rating the mutual fund schemes that are yet to be launched or are yet to invest in the underlying securities, ICRA discusses with the scheme’s fund manager the proposed investment mix in terms of the credit quality and the tenor of the portfolio that is intended to be maintained. Such schemes are generally assigned a provisional rating initially based on the expected credit quality and the tenor of the securities in the portfolio. Later, on the opening of the schemes for regular investments and investment in the underlying securities, the provisional rating is converted into a final rating. For more details, please refer to ICRA’s methodology for rating mutual fund schemes available on www.icra.in. 

Likewise, while rating the capital protection-oriented schemes that are yet to firm up their investment portfolio, ICRA generally assigns a provisional rating to such schemes based on the proposed mix of the share of investment in the debt instruments and in equity. The provisional rating is finalised on the receipt of regulatory clearance for the scheme and after the investments are made by the scheme. For more details, please refer to ICRA’s methodology for rating capital protection-oriented funds available on www.icra.in. 

- **(D) Other scenarios:** There could be situations where an entity already has a rating outstanding but intends to seek a credit rating for certain proposed debt as a part of the restructuring process relating to the existing debt. In other situations, there could be a company formed, say for the acquisition of another company, which might need a credit rating in connection with the proposed acquisition. A provisional rating could be assigned in either of the above cases (or other such similar circumstances) where the restructuring process/ acquisition process (or other functionally similar milestone) is at an advanced stage of completion, but certain documents like the final term sheet for borrowings are pending to be finalised. 

- **(E) Non-applicability** : ICRA does not assign any rating, including a provisional rating, to an entity that is evaluating strategic decisions such as an acquisition, the funding mix for a project, debt restructuring, scenario-analysis in loan refinancing etc. This restriction applies only to assigning scenario-based ratings/ advance ratings to entities that are evaluating strategic decisions. While ICRA does not assign a provisional rating in the aforesaid situations, this, however, does not preclude the possibility of assigning a final rating in some of the above situations when in ICRA’s assessment the consummation of the above events is near certain and is less a matter of supposition, and the rating is not being sought by the entity for evaluating strategic options. 

# **Symbolic Representation and Disclosure of Provisional Ratings** 

Provisional ratings assigned by ICRA, on both the long-term as well as the short-term scale, are prefixed with the word ‘Provisional’, signifying that the rating assigned to the instrument concerned is subject to execution of the pending actions/ documents. ICRA discloses the provisional nature of the rating in all its communication pertaining to that instrument, including the rating communication letter and the rating rationale. 

# **Monitoring and Review of Provisional Ratings** 

- (A) Post the assignment of the provisional rating, and until the completion of the pending actions/ documents, there may be changes in the fundamental credit profile of the entity, or changes in the terms of the transaction structure. It is also possible that the issuance is fully/ partially completed, or the borrowing facility is fully/ partially availed, even prior to the completion of the pending actions/ documents. The table in **Annexure** lists down the rating actions that ICRA will take in such scenarios. 

- (B) ICRA converts a provisional rating into a final rating upon a receipt of confirmation about the completion of the pending actions, and/ or execution of the pending documents. In case the pending actions/ documents are completed for only a

# **Validity Period/ Withdrawal of Provisional Ratings** 

(A) On an ongoing basis, ICRA would engage with the rated entity to understand the timelines within which the said actions/ documents are intended to be completed. 

**(i) In case the required actions/ documents/ issuance remain pending** : If neither the pending actions/ documents nor the issuance is completed, ICRA would withdraw the provisional rating after one year of assignment of the provisional rating. However, the rating may continue to remain provisional even after the completion of one year, in cases where the rated entity expressly indicates its intention to complete the pending actions/ documents over another six months. In the absence of the above or upon the completion of another six months (as the case may be), the provisional rating would be withdrawn by ICRA. Exceptions to the policy in terms of considering a further extension in timeline for converting a provisional rating into a final rating may be made, on a case-to-case basis, in certain circumstances ( _such as when the pendency in actions/ documents is because of reasons that are beyond the control of the entity_ ). 

**(ii) In case the required actions/ documents are pending but the issuance is completed** : Upon the completion of issuance, the rated entity would be expected to complete the pending actions/ execute the pending documents within 90 days (‘Validity Period’) from the date of issuance of the debt instrument/ date of availing the borrowing facilities. Accordingly, ICRA would convert the provisional rating into a final rating within 90 days of the completion of the issuance. 

The Validity Period may be extended by up to a further 90 days (‘Extended Validity Period’), on a case-to-case basis, in circumstances where the rated entity expressly indicates its intention to complete the pending actions/ documents over the near term. 

However, in case of continued pendency at the end of 90/ 180 days, the provisional rating would be converted into a final rating upon a review of the required actions/ documents to the extent these are completed by the end of the Validity Period/ Extended Validity Period. This implies that the provisional rating may even be revised at the end of the above period, while being converted into a final rating, to a level commensurate with the fundamental credit drivers of the entity, while ignoring the potential favourable impact of the pending actions/ documents on the rating. 

In no circumstance shall the validity of the provisional rating be extended beyond 180 days from the date of issuance. 

**(iii) In case of Provisional Issuer Ratings assigned to REITs/ InvITs** : Since the Issuer Ratings for such vehicles are not linked to the placement of a specific debt instrument, the Validity Period shall be computed from the completion date of the fund raising and the issuance of units by the REIT / InvIT. The Validity Period shall be of 90 days, along with the possibility to extend it by up to a further 90 days on a case-to-case basis, similar to the approach outlined above for the debt instruments. 

**(iv) In case of Provisional Ratings assigned to securitisation transactions** : Unlike other debt instruments where a final rating could be determined (after the passage of the Validity Period) without considering the pending actions/ documents, in the case of securitisation transaction ratings, doing so is not meaningful. Therefore, if the pending actions/ documents are not completed within one year of assignment of the provisional rating to securitisation transactions, ICRA would withdraw the provisional rating, even if the instrument has been issued.

# **Disclosures in Rating Rationales** 

Apart from the disclosures already made, ICRA shall make the following additional disclosures in the rating rationales while assigning provisional ratings: 

- (A) Pending actions/ documents considered while assigning the provisional rating. 

- (B) The risks associated with the provisional nature of the credit rating, including stating the broad impact on the rating in the absence of the completed actions/ documents. 

- (C) The rating that would have been assigned in the absence of the pending actions/ documents. In cases where the absence of the said actions/ documents would not have resulted in any rating being assigned (as it would not be meaningful), the same would be stated in the rating rationale. ( _Examples_ : In case of provisional rating for securitisation transactions, or in case of provisional rating for InvITs/ REITs, or in case of provisional rating assigned to the bank facilities/ debt instruments proposed to be issued by a special purpose acquisition company that intends to acquire another company). 

- (D) The timeline and the rating implications (as applicable) for the debt instrument/ borrowing facility, upon the passage of the validity period post the issuance or after availing the borrowing facilities. 

- (E) In case of provisional ratings assigned to InvITs and REITs: 

   - The broad details of the assets that are proposed to be held by the InvIT/ REIT, the proposed capital structure, etc. 

   - That an undertaking has been taken from the sponsor stating that the key assumptions (relating to the assets, capital structure, etc.) are in consonance with the details filed by the sponsor with SEBI. 

   - In case of a change in the provisional rating because of a change in the aforesaid key assumptions, stating that the revised provisional rating is based on a declaration from the sponsor that similar changes have been made in the filing with SEBI as well. 

# **Unaccepted Provisional Ratings** 

In case the provisional rating assigned is not accepted by the entity (or sponsor, in case of InvITs/ REITs), then in the "nonaccepted ratings” list published by ICRA on its website www.icra.in, the following supplementary disclosures shall be made: 

- (A) The details of the actions/ documents pending to be taken for converting the provisional rating into a final rating. ( _Example: In case of InvITs/ REITs, such disclosure shall include the broad details of the assets to be housed under the Trust, the proposed capital structure, etc_ ). 

- (B) The rating that would have been assigned in the absence of the said actions/ documents.
