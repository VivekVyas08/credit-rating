ICRA's rating process

Here's the full process captured in this flowchart — it's ICRA's rating process, from mandate through disclosure and ongoing surveillance.
Stage 1: Initiation & Mandate

Start
Rating agreement executed – MPRA (Master Pooling/Rating Agreement) and/or SOW (Statement of Work) is signed between the entity and ICRA, specifying the amount, instrument to be rated, and fees.
Case allocation – After conflict check and information request to the rated entity, the case is allocated to an Analyst and a Quality Analyst.

Stage 2: Analysis

Information processing – Analysts process the information, undertake plant/site visits (as applicable), and hold discussions with management, bankers, auditors, or other external entities as required.
Rating report preparation – A rating report/note is prepared based on the entity's data plus ICRA's own research on the company and industry; it's presented to the Rating Committee (RC).

Stage 3: Rating Assignment

Rating assigned by RC – The Rating Committee assigns the rating; meeting minutes are approved by the RC Chairperson.
Communication to entity – The assigned rating, along with its rationale, is communicated to the rated entity.

Stage 4: Branch by Rating Type

Decision: Fresh or Surveillance rating exercise?

Path A — Fresh Rating

Rating accepted by rated entity?
No → Entity has 5 working days to request a review from the RC date.
Then a further branch: entity may request review within 5 working days of the communication.
If not accepted after review window → Ratings disclosed as "unaccepted" on ICRA's website on the 6th working day (fresh) / 31st calendar day (via the surveillance-linked path) from the date of communication.
Yes → Rating + outlook + rationale disseminated and uploaded to ICRA's website within 2 working days of receipt of acceptance → Rating kept under continuous monitoring until withdrawn → End

Path B — Surveillance Rating

Entity requests review within 5 working days from RC date?
Yes → Review completed within 5 working days from RC date; rating with outlook disseminated/uploaded on ICRA's website.
No → Rating with outlook disclosed via Press Release (PR) on ICRA's website.
Parallel/related fresh-rating-review track:
Review process for fresh ratings completed within 30 calendar days from the date of the email/letter communicating the rating.
Rating accepted by rated entity?
Yes → Rating + outlook + rationale disseminated/uploaded within 2 working days of acceptance → feeds into Rating kept under continuous monitoring until withdrawn → End
No → Disclosed as unaccepted on the 31st calendar day from the date of the communication → still feeds into continuous monitoring → End

Governing Principle
Regardless of the branch (fresh/surveillance, accepted/unaccepted), every path converges on: "Rating kept under continuous monitoring until withdrawn." This reflects ICRA's practice that once assigned, a rating remains live and subject to ongoing surveillance — it doesn't lapse just because it was initially contested.
