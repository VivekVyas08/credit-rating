

# **Indicative Information Required by ICRA** 

## **[Credit Rating of Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs)]** 

### Relating to assets under management (AUM) 

- Movement in the AUM of security receipts (SRs) 

- Portfolio break-up basis industry, mode of acquisition, borrowers, and lenders 

- Recovery rating-wise distribution and ageing of SRs 

- Details of recoveries against SRs 

- Movement in loans, asset quality and provisions relating to the loans extended by the ARC 

### Relating to Borrowings and Liquidity 

- Borrowing profile including the volume of funds raised across periods, instrument, lenders, tenor, and interest rate 

- Asset-liability maturity profile 

- Trend in capital adequacy ratio including details of capital raising plans, if any 

### Risk Management Policy 

- Board approved policy for acquiring assets 

- Internal policies for provisioning against loans and valuation of SRs, if any 

### Other Information 

- Copies of the latest sanctioned letters from banks; term sheets relating to other borrowings 

- Annual reports for the past years and quarterly/ half-yearly financial statements, as applicable 

- Financial projections drawn by the ARC 

- Discussion with the bankers, statutory auditor, IPA, debenture trustee, audit committee (wherever applicable) 

- Bank statements [for at least six months for the initial rating, and the entire period from the last date of the rating rationale in the case of surveillance] 

- No-default statement (NDS)—sought at a monthly frequency 

Please note that the above information list is indicative in nature. ICRA may seek other/ additional information beyond what is covered in the above list. For arriving at a rating decision, the relative importance of the above information may vary from case-to-case.
