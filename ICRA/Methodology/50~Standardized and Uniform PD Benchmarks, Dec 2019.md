# **Standardized and Uniform Probability of Default Benchmarks [As prescribed by SEBI for the Credit Rating Agencies]** 

In accordance with the SEBI circular SEBI/ HO/ MIRSD/ DOS3/ CIR/ P/ 2019/ 70 dated June 13, 2019, given below are the standardized and uniform probability of default (PD) benchmarks for the long-term rating categories, for one-year, two-year and three-year cumulative default rates, both for short-run and longrun. 

- » The **long-run benchmarks** are computed based on a confidence interval of 95% over the weighted average of 1-year, 2-year and 3-year default rates pertaining to the 10-year period till FY2019. 

- » The **short-run benchmarks** are computed based on a confidence interval of 99.7% over the weighted average of 1-year, 2-year and 3-year default rates pertaining to the 10-year period till FY2019. 

The PD benchmarks have been computed based on the Marginal Default Rate (MDR) approach using monthly cohorts. For more details on the computation methodology, please refer to the SEBI circular available at this link.

**Note** : The PD benchmarks do not reflect ICRA’s actual or likely default rates for the various rating categories. These PD benchmarks are standardized by SEBI for all the Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs) in India and are intended to be used by SEBI to evaluate the performance of CRAs for the rating actions taken by them from April 1, 2020 onwards.
