# **Guidelines – Disclosure on Managing Conflict of Interest** 

ICRA’s long-established internal policies to mitigate conflicts of interest are essential for its credibility in the market and the independence of its Employees. A conflict of interest exists when an Employee’s personal interest interferes in any way with the interests of the Company. An Employee’s obligation to conduct the Company’s business in an honest and ethical manner includes the ethical handling of actual and potential conflict of interest between personal and business relationships. This includes full disclosure of any actual or potential conflict of interest. 

In addition to these guidelines, the Company has also adopted specific policy and procedures on outside business interest that apply to its Employees, Senior Management and directors. 

### **Interests in Outside Companies** 

Decisions to do business with individuals or companies must be made solely on the basis of the best interests of the Company. Employees should not participate in the selection of vendors, business partners or contractors, or make any decisions as part of their job (including participating in the rating process) for any entity, if such Employee or a Relative has a significant business interest in such entity. Employees should not acquire a significant interest in any customer or other entity that may create an actual or potential conflict with their duties on behalf of ICRA, unless an Employee obtains approval first from the manager or supervisor and then from Compliance function. 

Any interest in another company that would influence an Employee to make a decision based on that company’s or Employee’s own interests rather than ICRA’s interests is considered “significant.” An interest can be financial, such as owning stock, or personal, such as a family or other relationship with an owner of a company. If uncertain whether an interest is significant, an Employee should disclose such interest to the manager, who can decide whether such Employee should be assigned to duties involving the company in question and whether any significant interest may require further reporting to Compliance function. 



### **Positions with Outside Companies** 

An Employee or director serving as a director of an outside company may be regarded as a representative of ICRA and might find his or her duties with that company to be in conflict with ICRA’s interests. Employees should accept such a position only upon receiving approval first from their manager or supervisor and then from the Compliance function. In general, requests by Employees to serve on the board of directors of any issuer rated by ICRA will not be approved. Requests by directors to serve on the boards of other companies must be made in accordance with applicable policies of ICRA. An Employee should not take a parttime or second job. For any other position with an outside entity, including not-for-profit entities that may create a conflict of interest with the duties that the Employee performs for the Company, before accepting such position, whether paid or unpaid, at an outside entity, an Employee should discuss first with the manager or supervisor and then with the Compliance function whether such a position would present a conflict of interest. 

### **Recent Employment of Employees** 

Employees, who are new hires, are required to report to compliance function and/or their manager or supervisor any employment, or other relevant engagement as a director, officer, consultant or contractor, in the 12 months prior to joining ICRA. 

### **Employees as Consultants** 

A manager shall not hire an Employee to work as a consultant or as an independent contractor or contract worker for the Company where payment is made outside normal payroll routines.

This applies, regardless of whether or not the work is related to the duties of the Employee’s position, where payment is made outside normal payroll routines. There may be instances where special Company projects may warrant exceptions to these guidelines where payment is made within the normal payroll routines. Such exceptions must be approved in advance by Human Resources and Compliance function. 

Further, Human Resources and Compliance function must approve any situation in which a former Employee wishes to become an independent contractor or contract worker for ICRA. In addition, the Human Resources function should be consulted in situations in which an individual who has worked as an independent contractor for ICRA wishes to become an Employee. 

# **Business Interests of Employee’s Relative** 

Actual or potential conflicts of interest may arise from the business interests of an Employee’s Relative. This can include employment by, or consulting, contracting or advising an outside entity by a Relative, as well as a significant business interest of Employee’s Relative. Employees are required to disclose to the Compliance function and function head of any business interests of the Relative of which they are aware that could cause an actual or potential conflict of interest with the Employee’s professional activities. 

# **Other Relationships** 

Actual or potential conflicts of interest may also arise from the business interests of those with whom the Employee has a close personal or other relationship (e.g., the employment of a close personal friend). Employees are required to disclose to their manager or supervisor or Compliance function any such actual or potential conflicts of interest of which they are aware for an assessment. 

# **Purchases** 

The Company will purchase all of its services and supplies on the basis of quality, price and service. The fact that a vendor is also a customer of the Company, shall not be the basis for making purchasing decisions. 

# **Improper Personal Benefits from the Company** 

Conflict of interest arises when an Employee or director, or a member of his or her family, receives improper personal benefits as a result of his or her position in the Company. They shall not accept any benefits from the Company that have not been duly authorized and approved pursuant to Company policy and procedure, including any Company loans or guarantees of your personal obligations. The Company will not make any personal loans to nor guarantee the personal obligations of directors and Senior Management unless otherwise allowed under applicable laws or ICRA policies. 

# **Separation between Rating & Commercial Activities** 

ICRA maintains separation between its rating and commercial activities (includes sales & marketing activities) to prevent commercial considerations from compromising the integrity or independence of its credit ratings as per the applicable policies. ICRA also maintains integrity of its businesses by ensuring objectivity, confidentiality and proper use of client information. 

If an Employee determines that an actual or potential conflict exists that would make such Employee ineligible to participate in the process for determining a credit rating, the Employee must discuss first with the manager or supervisor and then with the Compliance function. 

# **Conflicts of Interest Related to the Ratings Process** 

ICRA’s Analyst may not approve, participate in, or otherwise influence the determination of a Credit Rating of any particular Issuer, Rated Entity or obligation if any of the following circumstances exist: 

- **a.** the Analyst, or the Analyst’s Immediate Relative, owns securities issued, guaranteed, or otherwise supported by the Rated Entity; 

- **b.** the Analyst or the Analyst’s Relatives has had a recent employment or other significant business relationship (i.e., within the preceding twelve months) with the Rated Entity that creates the impression of a conflict of interest that ICRA deems to be unacceptable or that constitutes a conflict of interest;

- **c.** the Analyst has, or had, any other relationship with the Rated Entity that creates the impression of a conflict of interest that ICRA deems to be unacceptable or that constitutes a conflict of interest;

If an Analyst becomes aware that an actual or potential conflict exists that would make the Analyst ineligible to participate in the process for determining a Credit Rating as described above, the Analyst must cease participation in that process. Employees must use reasonable efforts to monitor any actual or potential conflicts of interest as described above. 

If there are any questions, Employees should contact the Compliance function.

# **Defined Terms** 

# Analyst 

Any full/part time employee, including the chief rating officer or a member or participant in a Rating Committee who is involved in the process of arriving at a Credit Rating of issuers/instruments using the Covered Rating Information and / or presenting a report to provide facts and information that will enable the Rating Committee to take a rating decision or who participates at the Rating Committee. This excludes any member of the internal control team and any employee of the Rating Business who: (1) is not involved in the rating process or development of rating procedures or rating methodologies or (2) supports the rating process solely through administrative tasks, such as entering information into internal systems. 

# Credit Rating 

Credit Rating refers to an opinion regarding securities or any other instruments, issuers, entities, expressed in the form of standard symbols or in any other standardized manner assigned by ICRA. 

# Employee 

The term “Employee” means any full-time or part-time employee of ICRA Limited, wherever located. 

# ICRA or Company ICRA Limited 

Under these guidelines shall be referred to as “ICRA” or “Company” as that term is used throughout these guidelines. 

# Immediate Relatives 

“Immediate Relative” means (I) a spouse of an Analyst and (ii) the parent, sibling, and/or child of such Analyst or of the spouse, if they are either dependent financially on such Analyst, or consult such Analyst in taking decisions relating to trading in securities 

# Rated Entity 

A Rated Entity means any entity rated by ICRA or any entity that issues securities/instruments which are rated by ICRA or any entity that is seeking a Credit Rating from ICRA. 

# Relative 

A Relative with reference to any person means anyone who is related to another, if— (i) they are members of a Hindu Undivided Family; (ii) they are husband and wife; or (iii) one person shall be deemed to be the relative of another, if he or she is related to another in the following manners: spouse, father including step-father, , mother including step-mother, , son including step-son, son’s wife, daughter including step-daughter, daughter’s husband, brother including step-brother, sister including step-sister. 

# Senior Management 

Personnel of the Company, who are members of its core management team excluding Board of Directors and comprises all function heads and all members of management one level below the Managing Director and Group CEO and functional heads.
