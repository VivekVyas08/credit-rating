

# **Indicative Information Required by ICRA** 

## **[Credit Rating of Insurance Companies]** 

### Relating to Business 

- Segment-wise, product-wise, and channel-wise premium 

- Sub-segment-wise loss ratio and combined ratio for general insurers 

- Segment-wise, channel-wise persistency for life insurers 

- Segment wise value-of-new-business (VNB) and margins, besides the movement in embedded value (EV) of life insurers 

### Relating to Reinsurance 

- Details relating to reinsurance treaties, including information on the risk retained in each segment, reinsurance cover in each segment, and the event risk cover 

### Relating to Investments 

- List of top debt (non-SLR) and equity investments 

- List of non-performing investments with provisions held 

### Relating to Capitalisation 

- Solvency calculations 

- 

- Information about the capital raising plans, if any 

### Other Information 

- Annual reports for the past years and quarterly/ half-yearly financial statements, as applicable 

- Business and financial projections drawn by the insurer 

- Discussion with the statutory auditor, debenture trustee, audit committee (wherever applicable) 

- No-default statement (NDS)—sought at a monthly frequency 

Please note that the above information list is indicative in nature. ICRA may seek other/ additional information beyond what is covered in the above list. For arriving at a rating decision, the relative importance of the above information may vary from case-to-case.
