

# **Indicative Information Required by ICRA** 

## **[Credit Rating of Broking Entities]** 

### Relating to broking operations 

- Trend in operational metrics such as number of franchises, branches, active clients, and client additions 

- Details of broking turnover and income with breakup across various segments 

- Trend in revenue concentration, provisions, and ageing of debtors 

### Relating to asset management, wealth management & distribution of financial products 

- Assets under management across different schemes and performance of the funds/ schemes across time periods 

- Distribution channel mix along with Pricing arrangements and Trail income trajectory 

- Operational metrics such as number of families serviced, and relationship managers deployed etc. 

### Relating to margin trading facility (MTF) 

- Trend in loan book movement and its concentration across top clients and collaterals 

- Lending and square-off policies including policy related to acceptance of client collaterals 

### Relating to proprietary trading 

- Trading strategies deployed along with trend in number of jobbers and income sharing arrangements 

- Risk management policy 

Relating to professional clearing services 

- Trend in clearing volumes (including top clients), clearing fee income, and float (including top clients) income 

- • Investment policy pertaining to client floats 

Relating to funding and liquidity Profile 

- Details of fund based and non-fund-based borrowing arrangements, margin placement and utilisation trends 

- Details of on-balance sheet liquidity including un-encumbered liquid assets 

- Details of capital raising plans, if any 

### Relating to risk management and infrastructure 

- Risk management policies and regulatory inspection outcomes 

- Instances of disruption of services, and loss due to error trades 

### Other Information 

- Copies of the latest sanctioned letters from banks; term sheets relating to other borrowings 

- Annual reports for the past years and quarterly/ half-yearly financial statements or investor presentations, as applicable 

- • Financial projections drawn by the company 

- Discussion with the bankers, statutory auditor, IPA, debenture trustee, audit committee (wherever applicable) 

- Bank statements [for at least six months for the initial rating, and the entire period from the last date of the rating rationale in the case of surveillance] 

- No-default statement (NDS)—sought at a monthly frequency 

Please note that the above information list is indicative in nature. ICRA may seek other/ additional information 

beyond what is covered in the above list. For arriving at a rating decision, the relative importance of the above information may vary from case-to-case.
