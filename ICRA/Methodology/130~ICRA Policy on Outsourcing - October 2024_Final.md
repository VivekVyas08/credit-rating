# **ICRA Policy on Outsourcing** 

### **Statement of Purpose** 

ICRA is committed to comply at all times with the Master Circular for Credit Rating Agencies issued by Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”) dated May 16, 2024, as may be amended from time to time. 

This policy on Outsourcing of activities by the Company (“ **Policy** ”) will provide guidance for conducting an assessment on the requirement of Outsourcing activities of ICRA which are non-core in nature and can be appropriately Outsourced to Third Parties. ICRA while Outsourcing such activities shall ensure that it does not Outsource its core business activities and compliance function to any Third Party. For further details please refer to the attached Annexure I. 

ICRA shall ensure that the Outsourcing arrangements neither diminish the ability to fulfil its obligations towards clients and regulators nor impede effective supervision by the regulators. 

### **Selection of Third Party** 

It is imperative that ICRA exercises due care, skill and diligence in the selection of any Third Party to ensure that the Third Party has the ability and capacity to undertake the provision of the service effectively. 



ICRA shall review the financial and operational capabilities of the Third Party in order to assess its ability to continue to meet its Outsourcing obligations. 

ICRA due diligence should include an assessment of: 

- a. Third Party’s resources and capabilities, including financial soundness, to perform the Outsourcing work within the timelines fixed; 

- b. Compatibility of the practices and systems of the Third Party with ICRA’s requirements and objectives; 

- c. Market feedback of the prospective Third Party’s business reputation and track record of its services rendered in the past; 

- d. Level of concentration of the Outsourced arrangements with a single Third Party; and 

- e. The environment of the foreign country where the Third Party is located. 

ICRA shall enter into a clearly defined and legally binding written contract with each Third Party before Outsourcing an activity, in accordance with the applicable laws. Care shall be taken to ensure that the Outsourcing contract: 

- a. clearly defines what activities are going to be Outsourced, including appropriate service and performance levels; 

- b. provides for rights, obligations and responsibilities of the Third Parties, including indemnity by the parties; 

- c. provides for the liability of the Third Parties for unsatisfactory performance/other breach of the contract 

- d. provides for the continuous monitoring and assessment by ICRA of the Third Parties so that any necessary corrective measures can be taken up immediately, i.e., the contract shall enable ICRA to retain an appropriate level of control over the Outsourcing and the right to intervene with appropriate measures to meet legal and regulatory obligations; 

- e. includes, where necessary, conditions of sub-contracting by the Third-Party, i.e. the contract shall enable ICRA to maintain a similar control over the risks when a Third Party Outsources to further Third Parties as in the original direct Outsourcing; 

- f. has unambiguous confidentiality clauses to ensure protection of proprietary and customer data during the tenure of the contract and also after the expiry of the contract; 

- g. specifies the responsibilities of the Third Party with respect to the IT security and contingency plans, insurance cover, business continuity and disaster recovery plans, force majeure clause, etc.; 

- h. provides for preservation of the documents and data by Third Party; 

- i. provides for the mechanisms to resolve disputes arising from implementation of the Outsourcing contract; j. provides for termination of the contract, termination rights, transfer of information and exit strategies;

- k. addresses additional issues arising from country risks and potential obstacles in exercising oversight and management of the arrangements when ICRA Outsources its activities to foreign Third Parties. For example, the contract shall include choice-of-law provisions and agreement covenants and jurisdictional covenants that provide for adjudication of disputes between the parties under the laws of a specific jurisdiction; 

- l. neither prevents nor impedes ICRA from meeting its respective regulatory obligations, nor the regulator from exercising its regulatory powers; and 

- m. provides for ICRA and /or the regulator or the persons authorized by it to have the ability to inspect, access all books, records and information relevant to the Outsourced activity with the Third Parties. 

ICRA shall take appropriate steps to ensure that the Third Parties protect proprietary and confidential information of both ICRA and its clients from intentional or inadvertent disclosure to any unauthorised persons and ensure that such information is not misused or misappropriated. ICRA shall ensure that the employees of the Third Parties have limited access to the data handled and only on a ‘need to know’ basis and the Third Parties shall have adequate checks and balances to ensure the same. 

Additionally, ICRA shall not Outsource any activity if it would impair the supervisory authority’s right to assess or its ability to supervise the core business of ICRA. Further, ICRA shall not enter into an Outsourcing arrangement which may impair the ability of SEBI/SRO or auditors to exercise their regulatory responsibilities such as supervision/inspection of the ICRA. No Outsourcing arrangements will be entered with any Third Party which shall affect the rights of an investor or client against the Company in any manner. 

# **Approving Authority** 

The respective function head under whom the proposed outsourced activity is/was being performed or would have been performed shall be the Approving Authority of that outsourced activity. 

It will be responsibility of the Approving Authority to ensure that the requirements of this Policy are duly complied with. 

# **Risk Management Programme** 

ICRA maintains a comprehensive risk management programme which addresses the risk associated with the Outsourced activities and the relationship with the Third Party. Some of the key elements of the ICRA risk management programme include: 

- a) The impact of failure of a Third Party to adequately perform the activity on the financial, reputational and operational performance of ICRA and on the investors / clients; 

- b) Ability of ICRA to cope up with the work, in case of non performance or failure by a Third Party by having suitable back-up arrangements; 

- c) Regulatory status of the Third Party, including its fitness and probity status; 

- d) Situations involving conflict of interest between ICRA and the Third Party and the measures put in place by ICRA to address such potential conflicts, etc. 

ICRA group companies are not prohibited to act as a Third Party for the purpose of Outsourcing of an activity by ICRA, however, for avoidance of potential conflict of interest, any transaction between ICRA and its group companies shall be carried on maintaining an arm’s length distance between ICRA and such Third Party in terms of infrastructure, manpower, decision-making, record keeping, etc. ICRA shall make all applicable disclosures under the law in respect of the contractual agreement with such Third Party. 

ICRA shall and shall ensure that each Third Party shall establish and maintain specific contingency plans for each Outsourcing arrangement with the Third Party, including a plan for disaster recovery, in case a Third Party services become unavailable. ICRA shall also conduct periodic testing of critical security procedures and systems and review of backup facilities to confirm the adequacy of the Third Party’s systems. 

# **Records Maintenance** 

ICRA shall preserve the records relating to all activities Outsourced by it centrally so that it can conduct appropriate due diligence in selecting the Third Party. Such records should be regularly updated and remain readily accessible for review by ICRA’s Board and/ or senior management, as and when needed. 

# **Policy Review** 

ICRA’s Board shall mandate a regular review of this Policy in the wake of changing business environment and shall have overall responsibility for ensuring that all ongoing Outsourcing decisions taken by ICRA and the activities undertaken by the Third Parties are in compliance with this Policy. 

ICRA’s Board may mandate reviews of risk management system and requirements as stipulated by SEBI through internal or external auditors, wherever felt necessary.

## **Defined Terms** 

## **ICRA or Company** 

ICRA Limited under this policy shall be referred to as “ICRA” or “Company” as that term is used throughout this Policy. 

## **Third Party(ies)** 

A Third Party is a provider of Outsourced services. 

## **Outsource(ing/ed)** 

Outsource(ing/ed) means use of one or more than one Third Party either within or outside group by ICRA to perform any activities associated with its non-core business activities. 

# **Annexure I** 

1. The activities of ICRA which will be considered as “core business activities” and hence cannot be Outsourced as per SEBI Guidelines on Outsourcing of Activities are as follows. 

   - a) Activities primarily in the domain of “analytical” activity, namely: 

      - i. Assignment of credit ratings 

      - ii. Surveillance of assigned credit ratings 

      - iii. Development of rating policy and rating framework 

   - b) Research activities incidental to rating. 

2. ICRA will also not Outsource its compliance function in line with the above-mentioned SEBI guidelines. 

3. The activities relating to other corporate functions besides Compliance, such as Legal, Finance, Information Technology, Administration, Human Resources, etc., would not be within the purview of this Policy as they are not involved in delivering services offered by ICRA to its clients and are not specifically addressed by the SEBI guidelines 

4. The arrangements with a Third Party, for the purposes of providing manpower, to perform an activity under the supervision of ICRA employees, will also not be within the purview of this Policy. 

5. Other scenarios wherein services of a Third Party are proposed to be engaged on a one-time basis, should be referred to Compliance for assessment on the applicability of this Policy.
