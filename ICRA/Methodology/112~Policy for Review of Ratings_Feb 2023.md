ICRA’s Policy for Review of Ratings incorporates the process for review of rating in accordance with the circulars issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) from time to time. 

Upon a credit rating action undertaken by ICRA, if the rated entity is in disagreement with the credit rating, it can appeal for review. The rated entity appealing for a review, it should promptly submit additional information, data or clarifications, if any, on the aforesaid credit rating action to ICRA, to enable ICRA to review the credit rating. Such information should be material and relevant to the appeal for review. It will be the sole discretion of ICRA to ascertain whether the request for review should be accepted and if yes, the review would be carried out by a rating committee that shall consist of majority of members that are different from those in the rating committee of ICRA that assigned the earlier rating, and at least one-third of independent members _1_ . 

The said rating committee would review the credit rating and decide whether to retain the credit rating or change it, depending on the facts of the case. The decision of the rating committee in this regard, shall be final and binding on the rated entity. The review process will be completed within regulatory timelines and as per ICRA’s Operations Manual. 

ICRA will reserve the right to publish the credit rating within a reasonable period of time even before the expiry of regulatory timelines or as per ICRA’s Operations Manual, or in any circumstances that ICRA may deem fit. 

> _1 "independent" would mean a person not having any pecuniary relationship with the credit rating agency or any of its employees_

# **Defined Terms** 

**Credit Rating** 

A Credit Rating is an opinion on the relative credit risk of the issuer/ borrower/ instrument / borrowing limit expressed in the form of standard symbols or in any other standardized manner, assigned by ICRA and used by the issuer of instrument/ debt obligation, to comply with the requirements specified under regulations. 

## **Credit Rating Action** 

A Credit Rating Action is any one of the items below: 

1. the assignment of a Credit Rating to a Rated Entity or obligation, including subsequent Credit Ratings; 

2. a change in a Credit Rating (i.e., upgrade or downgrade); 

3. placing a Credit Rating on watch or changing the direction of an existing watch; 

4. the assignment or change in an outlook associated with a Rated Entity or one or more Credit Ratings; 

5. labeling a rating as “Issuer Not Cooperating” in circumstances where the rated entity does not provide the requisite information to ICRA or fails to adhere to the commercial terms; 

6. conversion of a provisional rating into final; 

7. an affirmation of a Credit Rating; and 

8. a withdrawal of a Credit Rating. 

## **ICRA** 

ICRA Limited under this policy shall be referred to as “ICRA” as that term is used throughout this policy. 

## **Rated Entity (ies)** 

A Rated Entity means any entity rated by ICRA or any entity that issues instrument/ raises debt obligations rated by ICRA or any entity that is seeking a Credit Rating from ICRA.

# **About ICRA Limited:** 

ICRA Limited was set up in 1991 by leading financial/investment institutions, commercial banks and financial services companies as an independent and professional investment Information and Credit Rating Agency. 

Today, ICRA and its subsidiaries together form the ICRA Group of Companies (Group ICRA). ICRA is a Public Limited Company, with its shares listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange. The international Credit Rating Agency Moody’s Investors Service is ICRA’s largest shareholder.
