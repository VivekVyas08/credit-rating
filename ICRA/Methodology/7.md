

# **Indicative Information Required by ICRA** 

## **[Credit Rating of Entities in the Public Finance Sector]** 

### Business Information 

- Details relating to the institutional, governance and administrative framework 

- Key statistics related to the entity’s jurisdiction – population, area, number of staff, etc. 

- Details related to various services being provided by the entity – water supply, sewerage, solid waste management, roads and drains, streetlights, building permission, passenger transport, schools, hospitals, etc., as applicable 

- Details of major projects being undertaken (ongoing/ proposed) 

### Financial Information 

- Income-expenditure, receipt-payment, balance sheet, annual budgets, as applicable 

- Financial projections drawn by the entity 

- Collection efficiency of various taxes and user charges 

- Details of borrowings, including the repayment schedule, interest rates, tenor etc. 

- Funding mix for the proposed capital expenditure/ projects and investment plans 

- Details of funds received from the state and central governments 

- Details of previous/ current /proposed rates for taxes, fee, tariff, and user charges 

### Other Information 

- Copies of the latest sanctioned letters from banks; term sheets relating to other borrowings 

- Guarantee deed or other explicit support forms—where applicable 

- Discussion with the bankers, statutory auditor, IPA, debenture trustee, audit committee (wherever applicable) 

- Bank statements [for at least six months for the initial rating, and the entire period from the last date of the rating rationale in the case of surveillance] 

- No-default statement (NDS)—sought at a monthly frequency 

Please note that the above information list is indicative in nature. ICRA may seek other/ additional information beyond what is covered in the above list. For arriving at a rating decision, the relative importance of the above information may vary from case-to-case.
