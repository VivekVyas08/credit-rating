

# **Indicative Information Required by ICRA** 

## **[Securitisation Transaction Ratings]** 

### Relating to Company’s Portfolio 

- Disbursement data for each asset class 

- Delinquency and write-off data for each asset class 

- Prepayment data for each asset class 

- Loan portfolio cuts based on various parameters such as ticket size, IRR, tenure, bureau score, geography, borrower category etc 

- Vintage-wise delinquency data (static analysis) 

### Relating to the Structure 

- Details of the structure being proposed 

- Cashflows of the pool and the PTCs 

- Cashflow waterfall for the tranche being rated 

- Other transaction documents, if any 

- No-default statement (NDS) from the Trustee—sought at a monthly frequency 

Please note that the above information list is indicative in nature. ICRA may seek other/ additional information beyond what is covered in the above list. For arriving at a rating decision, the relative importance of the above information may vary from case-to-case.
