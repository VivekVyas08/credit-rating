

# **ICRA Gifts, Entertainment and Anti-Bribery Policy** 

## **Preface and Introduction** 

ICRA Limited, in consonance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“ **SEBI** ”) Master Circular for Credit Rating Agencies dated July 03, 2023, and ICRA Code of Business Conduct, (“ **Code** ”), has formulated this Gift, Entertainment and Anti-Bribery Policy (“ **Policy** ”) to bring about greater transparency in its business. 

## **Applicability** 

This policy is applicable to full-time and part-time employees including contractual employees (“ **Employees** ”) and directors of ICRA Limited (referred to as “ **ICRA** ” or “the **Company** ”). 

Each subsidiary will be responsible for adopting a similar policy with additional restrictions for specific jurisdictions or practices if required by law or regulation or court order. 

## **Overview** 



Exchanging Gifts and participating in shared Entertainment experiences can strengthen business relationships, both between ICRA’s Employees as well as with customers, vendors, and other business contacts. However, giving or accepting Gifts and Entertainment may also lead to actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest or accusations of bribery. Moreover, offering, promising, giving, accepting, or soliciting anything of value, including a financial or other advantage, such as Gifts, Entertainment, or other benefit directly or indirectly to or from Public Officials may violate anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws. Commercial bribery not involving Public Officials is also illegal in many countries. 

To protect Employees from these risks and uphold the highest standards of integrity, ICRA limits the types of Gifts and Entertainment that Employees may give or receive. 

The Policy is designed to help all Employees and directors understand how to apply these principles in daily business activities. It confirms the basic elements of honesty, integrity, good judgment and professionalism that all Employees and directors are expected to observe. This Policy outlines the circumstances under which it is and is not appropriate to give or receive Gifts or Entertainment or anything else of value and describes the applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws. All of the anti-bribery and anti-corruption prohibitions contained in this Policy apply irrespective of whether an employee uses ICRA’s funds or their personal funds to finance improper Gifts, Entertainment, payments or other benefits on behalf of or to benefit ICRA. 

It also provides rules and guidance to Employees on soliciting or accepting any Gift or Entertainment. Similarly, this policy provides rules and guidance to directors on soliciting or accepting any Gifts in connection with their role as a director of the Company.

# **Policy Requirements** 

## **A.  Giving Gifts or Entertainment to Third Parties** 

## **1.  Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Laws** 

ICRA is subject to a variety of anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws, including, but not limited to, the Prevention of Corruption Act (PCA) of 1988, U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (the FCPA), the U.K. Bribery Act, and any other applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws. 

ICRA must ensure it accurately reflects all transactions in its books and records. Employees are prohibited from offering, promising, or paying bribes in both the commercial and public sectors. A bribe is anything of value that is offered or paid with a corrupt purpose to influence the improper performance of an official or professional function. ICRA also requires third-party intermediaries, agents, consultants, and business partners who work on ICRA’s behalf and interact with Public Officials or with customers or prospective customers to comply with these same laws and practices, as described in the Covered Third-Party Anti-Corruption Due Diligence and Contracting Procedure. 

Global anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws cover a broad range of conduct. If a situation arises that is not addressed in this Policy or any code, or if a situation arises where the appropriate course of action is unclear, contact the Compliance function. 

Outreach to and engagement with Public Officials for the purpose of advancing ICRA’s legitimate business interests is not considered improper, provided that such outreach or engagement complies with this Policy. For purposes of the anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws, it is irrelevant whether a person is considered a Public Official by the government or not. 

Extending or promising favored treatment, such as a promise of ICRA’s employment, internship, or secondment for a family member or other related person of a Public Official or other business contact, may be seen as an improper Gift depending on the specific facts and circumstances. Consult with the human resource function on concerns related to hiring protocols. 

A violation of this Policy can occur even if the provision of an improper Gift, Entertainment, other benefit or payment fails to achieve the purpose for which it was intended. 

## **2.  Payments** 

### _a.   Payments to Public Officials; Facilitation Payments_ 

Offering, promising or making payments to or on behalf of Public Officials warrants close scrutiny and must undergo advance review and approval by Compliance. Facilitation payments, which are commonly defined as payments to governmental officials for routine governmental action to which the individual or company is legally entitled (e.g., processing papers, issuing visas, and providing phone service), are prohibited as a general matter. Such payments may be made only in exceptional circumstances, such as when, for example, an Employee is placed under duress and faces potential safety issues or personal harm. However, these exceptional payments must be reported to the Legal Department immediately and must be accurately described and recorded in ICRA’s books and records. 

### _b.   Payments Through Third-Party Intermediaries_ 

ICRA must not make payments through third parties that, if made by ICRA itself, would violate this Policy or any applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws. This prohibition includes “turning a blind eye” to the likelihood that an agent or other third party is or will be making an improper payment in connection with ICRA’s business.

# **3.  Giving Gifts and Entertainment to Public Officials** 

## _a.   General Prohibition_ 

Bribery is not limited to the offer, promise, or payment of cash with corrupt intent, but also may include the provision of Gifts or Entertainment for an improper purpose (i.e., providing a Gift or Entertainment to corruptly influence a person to provide favorable treatment in exchange). It is never permissible to offer, promise, or provide a Gift or Entertainment for a corrupt or improper purpose. In addition, no Gift or Entertainment may be given, directly or indirectly, to a Public Official except in cases that are pre-approved by Compliance or other than in the very limited circumstances described hereinbelow. 

## _b.   Compliance Pre-Approval Required_ 

The following must be approved by Compliance in advance: 

- →  Giving or providing a Gift or Entertainment, directly or indirectly, to a Public Official. Compliance will only approve a Gift or Entertainment if it meets all the following requirements: 

   - The cost must be reasonable<sup>1</sup> and justifiable under the circumstances, and it must comply with applicable laws; 

   - It must not reasonably be interpreted as an attempt to obtain or retain an improper business advantage and must not reflect negatively on the reputation of ICRA or the recipient; 

   - There must be a legitimate business purpose, such as the promotion, demonstration, or explanation of ICRA products and services or the execution of performance of a contractual obligation; 

   - There must be supporting receipts and proper documentation of payment made, if any, in accordance with applicable expense reimbursement and accounting procedures; 

   - It must be given or provided in an open and transparent manner and must not be to induce or reward the improper performance of an official function or activity; 

   - A Gift must not involve the transfer of cash or cash equivalent (such as gift cards or gift certificates); and 

   - A Gift must not be extravagant in value and must be infrequent (no more than two times per year to any single Public Official, including to any known family members or designee of the Public Official). 

- →  Offering a Public Official (or to a known family member or designee of a Public Official) a position on, or fee for participating in, advisory boards or committees of the Company; 

- →  Recruiting or commencing the employment process for a known family member or designee of a Public Official seeking employment at ICRA (including a secondment or internship); 

- →  Requests by Public Officials to attend or to receive complimentary admissions or discounted registration fees for ICRA sponsored conferences and/or events; 

- →  Paying for a Public Official's travel, meals, and lodging when the Public Official is invited as a speaker / participant at ICRA sponsored conference and/or event; 

- →  Paying for a delegation of Public Officials’ travel, meals, and lodging when the Public Officials are invited to visit ICRA’s offices; and 

- →  Providing Gifts or Entertainment to the family members of a Public Official. 

In general, requests to provide a Gift or Entertainment to a Public Official who is involved in a current or pending assignment with ICRA, or where ICRA has any other decision or non-routine business pending before the Public Official or their affiliated agency, and where the requesting employee is also involved with or has knowledge of the assignment or other business or decision, will not be approved by Compliance.

## _c.   Compliance Pre-Approval not Required_ 

Certain nominal Gifts or Entertainment as specified below may be given or provided to a Public Official, without Compliance pre-approval, only if the provision is directly related to a legitimate business purpose and it otherwise complies with all requirements in this Policy, including compliance with local laws: 

- →  Meals and refreshments provided to attendees, participants, and speakers at ICRA’s conferences and events, provided that the costs of such meals and refreshments are reasonable<sup>1</sup> given the venue; 

- →  Meals and refreshments provided, incidental to meetings with Public Officials, regardless of venue, provided that such meals and refreshments are of nominal value (less than or equal to Rs. 5000/- per person); 

- →  ICRA's-branded items that are provided at ICRA's events and conferences, provided that such items are of nominal value; and 

- → ICRA's research, provided that such research has already been published. 

# **4.  Giving Gifts and Entertainment to Business Contacts who are not Public Officials** 

Gifts or Entertainment may be given or provided to a business contact (who is not a Public Official) only if the following general requirements are met: 

- →  A Gift must not involve the transfer of cash or cash equivalent (such as gift cards or gift certificates); 

- →  The cost must be reasonable<sup>1</sup> and justifiable under the circumstances; 

- →  The giving or provision of a Gift or Entertainment must be infrequent and must comply with applicable laws; 

- →  It must not reasonably be interpreted as an attempt to obtain or retain an improper business advantage and must not reflect negatively on the reputation of ICRA or the recipient; 

- →  There must be a legitimate business purpose, such as the promotion, demonstration, or explanation of ICRA’s products and services or the execution of performance of a contractual obligation; and 

- →  There must be supporting receipts and proper documentation of expenses, if any, in accordance with applicable expense reimbursement and accounting procedures. 

No Gift or Entertainment may be given, directly or indirectly, to a business contact (who is not a Public Official) who is involved in a current or pending rating assignment with ICRA, where the employee giving the Gift or Entertainment is also involved with or has knowledge of the rating assignment. 

# **5.  Requests for Corporate Sponsorships from Business Contacts** 

ICRA’s business contacts may request that ICRA to sponsor an event, such as a golf tournament, that the business contact is sponsoring. Such sponsorships may not be provided to a business contact who is involved in a current or pending rating assignments with ICRA. 

Any sponsorship that involves a Public Official also requires the prior review and approval of Compliance. 

ICRA shall not make any donation to a charitable organization, other than as may be required under relevant laws (viz. corporate social responsibility). Requests by Public Officials for such charitable contributions or donations require prior review and approval of Compliance. Payments of or requests from Public Officials for donations to specific charities, non-profit organizations, or other recipients, even if well-known, may be considered bribes if the donation is made to improperly influence any act or decision of that Public Official.”

# **C.  Accepting Gifts or Entertainment from Business Contacts** 

# **Core Principles** 

- The Policy contains the **Two Core Principles** : 

- Any Gift accepted must be free of any intent or understanding, express or implied, that any business decision will be influenced thereby, and must be unlikely to create the appearance of any such intent or understanding. 

- Any Gift accepted must be reasonable<sup>2</sup> and in accordance with this Policy. 

- Employees should never solicit or encourage any business contact to offer them a Gift or Entertainment. 

# **1.  Gifts** 

All Employees, regardless of function or business segment, are prohibited from accepting any of the following: 

- anything in the form of cash or any cash equivalent (viz. gold/silver coin, articles, gift certificate or gift card); 

- anything, regardless of its value, where there is any reason to believe that it is being offered in an attempt to influence the Employee’s work at ICRA; 

- anything that is extravagant or lavish in nature, or which exceeds local social or business custom; 

- anything that is intended to be concealed or is not offered openly and transparently; and/or 

- anything from a government official directly or indirectly through a third party. 

- anything from a business contact that has been solicited or encouraged for them to offer. 

ICRA places strict restriction on Employees and directors from soliciting or accepting any Gifts or Entertainment from business contacts of the Company including potential or existing clients, service providers, etc., with only exception of accepting following Gifts subject to compliance with this Policy: 

- a. Business contacts milestone celebration/ occasions (e.g., 25th or 50th anniversary of the organisation, etc.) 

- b. Festive occasions where it is a social custom (like Diwali, Christmas, etc.) 

- c. Events where ICRA Employees are invited as trainers, speakers or participants. 

Provided that the above Gifts should be reasonable and customary. 

For reference, some indicative examples of acceptable and unacceptable Gifts are as follows: 

- Corporate branded gifts such as a promotional ballpoint pen, calendar, diaries, coffee-table book, or similar stationary items which are of nominal value  are  acceptable, but an expensive branded or gold plated pen or wristwatch would not be acceptable; 

- Tickets to an ordinary sporting event would be considered customary and reasonable, but tickets to the world cup, IPL, or other similar major sporting event would be considered excessive in value and should not be accepted;

# **2.  Entertainment** 

Employees may accept an invitation from a business contact to participate in Entertainment and/or reasonably priced work-related meals with a business contact that meets the following requirements: 

- →  The business contact extending the invitation will also be present; 

- →  It has a legitimate business purpose; 

- →  It is reasonable and appropriate in the context of the business, occasion, location and the parties’ respective positions; 

- →  It is not given as a bribe, payoff, or kickback, nor does it create the appearance (or an implied obligation) that the business contact is entitled to preferential treatment; and 

- →  The invitation is not extended exclusively to ICRA Employee and is generally extended by the invitee to its other stakeholders/market participants too. 

Employees, however, must be mindful that the frequency of such events for any Employee or business contact does not create an appearance of impropriety. 

For example, while infrequent reasonably priced work-related meals with a business contact likely would be appropriate. However, expensive meals/dinners at high end restaurants/hotels sponsored by the same business contact would be inappropriate. Similarly, even reasonably price work-related meals offered frequently would be inappropriate. 

For Employees who are invited to speak at an external event, reimbursement or direct payment of expenses on their behalf by the event organizer, who may or may not be a Rated Entity, of reasonable and customary event related expenses, including travel, lodging, and meals, are not considered Gifts/Entertainment and may be accepted subject to such reimbursement/payments do not create any conflict of interest and prior approval is obtained from the respective function head and Compliance. 

Employees can accept a wedding invite form a business contact, subject to specific approvals from the function head subject to the following conditions: 

- i. It is not a destination event; 

- ii. Acceptable to gift a bouquet of flowers within the limit of Rs.1000. The bouquet cost would be reimbursed by the Company; and 

- iii. Not to accept any Gift offered during the event, that is not in accordance with this Policy. 

# **Reporting** 

All Gifts which are unacceptable under this policy shall be returned to the donor of the Gift by the Employee or director receiving the Gift along with a letter / email thanking the donor for the Gift and informing the donor that the Gift is being returned as the same cannot be accepted as per the Policy. 

Details of all Gifts received and/or returned by Employees should be entered in the gift register.

# **Defined Terms** 

**Compliance Officer (CO)** means the Employee appointed as the Compliance Officer under the SEBI (Credit Rating Agencies) Regulations, 1999. 

**Compliance** refers to the Compliance Department and includes Employees who are part of the Compliance Department. 

**Employee** means any full-time or part-time employee of ICRA wherever located. 

**Entertainment** means any meal, drink, entertainment, sports, music, cultural, recreation or other event, either in person or virtual, that the recipient attends or participates in alongside the inviter that has monetary value and for which the recipient does not pay fair market value. The fair market value represents the price at which the event could reasonably be accessed or obtained in the open market (which may be different than its face value). 

**Gift** means any gift, gratuity, favor, benefit, discount, forbearance, or other tangible or intangible item provided from one party to another that has monetary value and for which the recipient does not pay fair market value, such as the following: 

- →  Cash or cash equivalents, including gift certificates or stock; 

- →  Loans or forgiveness or guarantees of loans or other obligations; 

- →  Tangible items or merchandise, such as gift baskets, wine, clothing, mugs, pens, collectibles, or hospitality bags; →  Tickets or passes to in-person or virtual entertainment, sports, music, cultural, recreation or other event (where the person providing the tickets or passes does not attend the event with the recipient); 

- →  Charitable donations (whether in cash or various forms of sponsorship such as dinners or golf tournaments); or 

- →  Use of any facility, property, or service. 

The fair market value represents the price at which the gift could reasonably be accessed or obtained in the open market (which may be different than its face value). A Gift may also include any personal or professional favors for a person or such person’s family members or related persons, such as an offer of employment, internship, or secondment. 

**Legal** refers to the Legal Department and includes Employees who are part of the Legal Department. 

**Public Official** is construed broadly and includes not only elected officials of a government, but also any officer or employee of a government or any department, agency, or instrumentality thereof (such as a government-controlled company or other commercial enterprise) or of a public international organization. The term public official also includes any person acting in an official capacity for or on behalf of any such government or department, agency, or instrumentality, or for or on behalf of any such public international organization. Contact Compliance if it is unclear whether a particular person is a public official or whether a particular entity is an instrumentality. Examples of Public Officials include the following: 

- →  Head of state 

- →  Royal family member 

- →  Ministry or agency official 

- →  Judge, magistrate or legislator 

- →  Officer or employee of a government-controlled company, including government-controlled financial, banking, healthcare and transportation institutions and utilities 

- →  Private person acting officially as an agent or intermediary on behalf of a government department, agency or instrumentality 

- →  Official of a public international organization (e.g., World Bank, IMF, UN) 

- →  Employee of any government agency 

- →  Political party, party official, or candidate for public office or other public official role 

- →  Employee of a government-sponsored pension or retirement plan 

**Rated Entity** means any entity rated by ICRA or any entity that issues securities/instruments which are rated by ICRA or any entity that is seeking a rating from ICRA.
