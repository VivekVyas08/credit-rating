## **Role of the Rating Committee(s)** 

The role of ICRA’s RCs involves taking rating decisions on the creditworthiness of rated entities based on credit issues examined by the agency’s analytical resources as well as through independent judgment. Such rating decisions are taken in a manner that is consistent with ICRA’s rating policies and practices and is in accordance with the agency’s established rating methodologies/criteria. 

## **Organization of Rating Committee** 

- a. Constitution of Rating Committee(s) 

The Chief Ratings Officer, in consultation with the Group Heads, constitutes Rating Committees comprising of leadership team from the analytical function of ICRA. Further, the Chief Ratings Officer may appoint/remove members from the Rating Committee(s) as may be required from time to time. 

- b. Composition, selection criteria and eligibility for a Rating Committee member 

ICRA’s RCs will comprise of not less than 5 members. The RCs will be chaired by the designated Chairperson. 

RC members are appointed based on their experience, sector focus and demonstrated analytical abilities. Criteria for being selected as a RC member includes, inter alia, demonstrated ability to take up challenging organizational responsibilities, analytical grasp of credit issues, decision making abilities, integrity and adequate knowledge and experience. ICRA also strives to maintain diversity in RC composition in terms of sector exposure of members. To become eligible to be considered as a potential RC member, a minimum of five years of analytical experience in ICRA or relevant experience other than ICRA is a must. 

- c. Rating Review Committee 

All cases of request for review of rating(s) provided to any instrument of an issuer shall be reviewed by a Rating Review Committee (“RRC”) which consists of majority of members who were not part of the Rating Committee that assigned the earlier rating, and at least one-third independent<sup>_1_</sup> members. 

> _1 "independent" would mean a person not having any pecuniary relationship with the credit rating agency or any of its employees. Appointment including reappointment/termination of appointment of independent member(s) of the RRC is approved by the Rating Sub Committee._

- d. Quorum requirement and system of voting 

For taking rating decisions, ICRA’s RC requires a minimum quorum of 3 members. In the case of a RRC the minimum quorum is defined as 3 members; wherein one-third of the members are independent. 

Every RC member, including the Chairperson, carry one vote, Number of votes cast in favour and against a rating action are recorded in the minutes of the meeting of the respective Rating Committee. 

In case of a tie, the Chairperson of the Rating Committee or the Rating Review Committee, as the case may be, will have a casting vote to decide the final rating outcome. 

# **Duties of Rating Committee members:** 

- Provide an unbiased perspective during RC discussions while being open to contrary views of other members 

- 

   - Engage in meaningful credit discussions 

- Stay updated with developments across economies, sectors, corporate entities and financial institutions; be adequately updated about issuers/ borrowers to be rated 

- 

   - Evaluate the quality of analytical reports and presentation and provide feedback to analysts as appropriate 

- Evaluate additional information or clarification, if any, given by issuer with respect to previously assigned rating in review cases 

- Maintain confidentiality of classified information related to all issuers/ borrowers 

- Not to participate in case discussions where the member has an actual or potential conflict of interest 

- Ensure compliance with various codes, policies as being made applicable and communicated to them by ICRA from time to time. 

# **Managing Conflict of Interest:** 

In case of any possible conflict of interest situation, the concerned member of the RC and RRC shall disclose such conflict of interest. Such member shall not participate in any manner in formulating or arriving at a rating.
