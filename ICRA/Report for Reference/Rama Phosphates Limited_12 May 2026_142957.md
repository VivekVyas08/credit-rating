## **About the company** 

RPL manufactures phosphatic fertilisers - single super phosphate (SSP), fortified fertilisers namely boronated SSP as well as sulphuric acid and its derivatives. It also has a soya oil division which is engaged in the extraction of soya oil. RPL markets its fertilisers under the Suryaphool and Girnar brands in Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh and Haryana. The company’s facilities at Indore, Pune, Udaipur and Nimbahera have a combined manufacturing capacity of 698,000 tonnes per annum (tpa) of SSP, 211,100 tpa of sulphuric acid and 120,000 tpa of soya oil.
