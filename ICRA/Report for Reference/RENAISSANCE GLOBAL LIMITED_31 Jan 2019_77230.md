## **About the company** 

Renaissance Jewellery Limited (RJL) was incorporated in 1989 as Mayur Gems & Jewellery Exports Private Limited (MG&J). In 1995, Mr. Niranjan Shah and his family members acquired the entire shareholding of MG&J. Upon conversion into Public Limited Company in 2005, the name was changed to Renaissance Jewellery Limited. RJL exports studded jewellery and conducts this business along with its overseas subsidiaries based in the US, the UK, U.A.E.  and Bangladesh.
