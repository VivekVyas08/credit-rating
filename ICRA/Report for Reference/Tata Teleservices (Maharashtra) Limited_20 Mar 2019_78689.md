## **About the company** 

Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited (TTML) was a telecommunication service provider licenced to provide telecommunication services in Mumbai Metro and Rest of Maharashtra (including Goa). It was an integrated player

For FY2018, company reported revenues of Rs. 1,871 crore and net loss of Rs. 9,842 crore as against revenues of Rs. 2,703 crore and net loss of Rs. 2,356 crore in FY2017.
