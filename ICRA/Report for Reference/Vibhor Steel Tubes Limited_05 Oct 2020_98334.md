ICRA is withdrawing the rating and that it does not have information to suggest that the credit risk has changed since the time the rating was last reviewed.

# **About the company** 

VSTPL was promoted by Mr. Vibhor Kaushik and Mr. Vijay Kaushik in 2003. The company is engaged in manufacturing of hollow section, GI and ERW (Electric Resistance Welded) black pipes, ranging from size 1/2” to 8” in diameter. It has two manufacturing facilities, one located at Raigarh, Maharashtra; with a production capacity of 1,25,000 MT per annum and the second located in Mehboob Nagar, Telangana; with a production capacity of 96,000 MT per annum
