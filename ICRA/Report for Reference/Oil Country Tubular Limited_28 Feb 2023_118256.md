# **Rationale** 

ICRA has withdrawn the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Oil country tubular limited at the request of the company and based on the No Due certificate (NDC) received from its banker. The Key Rating Drivers, Liquidity Position, Rating Sensitivities, Key financial indicators have not been captured as the rated instruments are being withdrawn. # **About the company** 

Incorporated in 1985, OCTL is primarily involved in the processing of oil country tubular goods used in the Oil and Gas Exploration and Production (E&P) industry. OCTL’s product range primarily comprises drill pipes, casing pipes, production tubing and couplings/premium connections/tool joints. It is a public-listed company and is promoted by the Hyderabad based Kamineni Group. The company’s manufacturing unit is located at Narketpally in Nalgonda district of Telangana.
