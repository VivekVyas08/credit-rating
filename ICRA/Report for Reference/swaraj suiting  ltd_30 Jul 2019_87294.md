# **About the company** 

Swaraj Suiting Private Limited (SSPL) was incorporated in 2003 by Mr. Mohammad Sabir along with his wife and was initially engaged in the manufacture of synthetic fabrics but later shifted to trading in synthetic fabrics in 2009 by selling off its Sulzer weaving looms to an associate concern, Swaraj Sulz Pvt. Ltd. The Company re-entered into manufacturing of synthetic fabrics in 2011 and commenced commercial operations in January 2013. The Company has an installed capacity of 123 Airjet looms translating into 225 Lakh Meters Per Annum (LMPA). The revenue mix of the company comprises of manufacturing and trading of grey fabrics and job work production of denim fabric for Arvind Limited.
