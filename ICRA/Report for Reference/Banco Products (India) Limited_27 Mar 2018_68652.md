## **About the company:** 

Incorporated in 1961 as Gaskets and Oilseals Limited, Banco Products (India) Limited (BPIL) is a leading manufacturer of radiators, intercoolers as well as oil-coolers. Radiators – particularly aluminium radiators – dominate the revenues of the company. While copper-brass radiators are targeted at the Indian railways and the other industrial original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), the aluminium radiators find application in commercial vehicle (CV) and tractor industries. Thus, the company supplies its products to both automotive and non-automotive industries.

BPIL also has an indirectly wholly owned subsidiary-Kilimanjaro Biochem Limited (KBL; 5% stake held by BPIL and balance 95% held by LMML), which manufactures and supplies alcohol in the African market through its extra neutral alcohol (ENA) manufacturing plant in Tanzania.
