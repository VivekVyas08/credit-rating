# **Rationale** 

ICRA is withdrawing the rating assigned to The Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd’s (JKB) Rs. 1,050-crore Basel III compliant Tier II bonds. ICRA does not have information to suggest that the credit risk has changed since the time the rating was last reviewed. There is no amount outstanding against the bonds as the rated bonds were not issued. The key rating drivers, liquidity position, and rating sensitivities have not been captured as the rated instrument is being withdrawn. # **About the company** 

The Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd (JKB) is a private sector scheduled commercial bank, incorporated on October 1, 1938, with its registered office in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir. JKB functions as a universal bank in the Union Territories (UTs) of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh and as a specialised bank in the rest of the country. It is also the only private sector bank designated as the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) agent for banking business and carries out the banking business of the UTs of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, besides collecting central taxes for the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT). As on December 31, 2020, the Government of Jammu & Kashmir (GoJ&K) was the majority shareholder in the bank, accounting for 68.18% of the equity shares. The Parliament passed the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Bill, which proposed the bifurcation of the state into the two UTs of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh. As a result, in January 2021, the bank approved the transfer of GoJ&K’s 8.23% shareholding in JKB to the UT of Ladakh, though the bank will continue to operate as a single entity. 

JKB had a market share of less than 1% in both advances and deposits as of September 2020 in the Indian banking sector with a total asset base of Rs. 1.11 lakh crore as on September 30, 2020. It has a strong presence in the UTs of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, with a modest presence in the rest of India. In H1 FY2021, the bank reported a net profit of Rs. 50.4 crore compared to a net loss of Rs. 894.5 crore in H1 FY2020.
