# **Rationale** 

ICRA has removed the ratings from the ”Issuer not co-operating” category and has withdrawn the ratings assigned to the bank facilities of Onward Technologies Limited (OTL), at the request of the company and based on the No objection certificate (NOC) received from the bankers, and in accordance with ICRA’s policy on withdrawal of ratings. ICRA does not have information to suggest that the credit risk has changed since the time the rating was last reviewed. 

The key rating drivers, liquidity position, key financial indicators and rating sensitivities have not been captured as the rated instruments are being withdrawn. The previous detailed rating rationale is available at the following link: Click here.

# **About the company** 

Incorporated in the year 1991, Onward Technologies Limited is a software outsourcing company engaged in the fields of digital and ER&D services catering to the Transportation, Mobility, Industrial Products, Heavy Machinery and Healthcare sectors. The company has its presence in the Indian market as well as overseas markets such as the US, the UK and Germany. Headquartered in Mumbai, the company has 14 offices across 6 countries with offshore capability centres in India.
