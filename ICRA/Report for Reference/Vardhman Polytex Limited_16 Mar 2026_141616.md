## **About the company** 

Incorporated in 1981, VPL primarily manufactures cotton and cotton polyester blended spun yarn. Its manufacturing facilities in Ludhiana, Bathinda (both in Punjab) and Nalagarh (Himachal Pradesh) together have installed capacity of 1.95 lakh spindles. VPL also has a yarn dyeing unit in Ludhiana, with an installed capacity of 15.0 tons per day (tpd). It has a small presence in
