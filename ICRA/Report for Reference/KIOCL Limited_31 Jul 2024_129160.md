## **About the company** 

KIOCL is a flagship company under the Ministry of Steel, GoI, with a Miniratna status. It was incorporated in April 1976. It is an export-oriented unit with expertise in iron ore mining, filtration technology and production of high-quality pellets. Its pellet unit, with an installed capacity of 3.5 mtpa, is in Mangaluru, Karnataka. Apart from the pellet unit, it also has a blast furnace (currently not in operations) to produce foundry-grade pig iron and a reclaimer to load pellets directly from the stockyard to the vessel. KIOCL has diversified into Operation & Maintenance services along with mineral exploration pertaining to its various core areas of expertise.
