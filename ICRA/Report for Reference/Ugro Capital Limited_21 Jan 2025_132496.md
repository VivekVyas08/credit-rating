## **Rationale** 

ICRA has withdrawn the rating for the pass-through certificates (PTCs) issued under MSME loan receivables securitisation transaction originated by Ugro Capital Limited (Ugro/ Originator). All the payouts to the investor in the above-mentioned instrument have been made and no further payments are due to the investors. The key rating drivers, liquidity position, rating sensitivities have not been captured as the rating assigned to the instrument has been withdrawn. ## **About the originator** 

Ugro Capital Limited (Ugro) is a specialised small business lending platform registered as a systemically important non-deposit taking non-banking financial company. It was incorporated in 1993 as Chokhani Securities Ltd. and was renamed Ugro Capital Limited pursuant to a change in the management in 2018. The company commenced disbursements in January 2019. It provides financing to the MSME sector and had assets under management (AUM) of more than Rs. 9,000 crores as of March 2024. Ugro has been listed on the BSE since 1995.
