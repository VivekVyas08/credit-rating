## **About the company** 

HTPL, incorporated in 1985 as M/s Ram Lal Harbans Lal Limited. The company started production of mild steel (MS) pipes from hot rolled coils in 1988. Later, it added more products to its portfolio such as CR coils and strips in 1996, GI Pipes/Coils in 2001 and GP Pipes in 2015. HTPL has four manufacturing units – two in Sikandrabad, Uttar Pradesh, one in Sanand, Gujarat and one in Hindupur, Andhra Pradesh. The total manufacturing capacity of the company is 420,000 TPA.
