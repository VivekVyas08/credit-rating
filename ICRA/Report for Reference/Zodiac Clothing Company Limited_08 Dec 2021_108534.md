# **About the company** 

Promoted by Mr. M.Y. Noorani, the House of Zodiac commenced operations as a partnership firm in 1954 for manufacturing neckties for men. Zodiac Clothing Company Limited (ZCCL) was incorporated in 1984 and came out with an initial public offer in 1994. It started exporting readymade garments, mainly men’s neckties and shirts, in 1960s. Most of its current exports are made to Europe and North America. ZCCL entered the branded shirts business in India in the late 1970s when it positioned itself in the premium men’s wear segment. ZCCL is present in men’s formal wear segment through its flagship brand, Zodiac, in the party/club wear segment through its brand, ZOD! and in the relaxed casual wear segment through its brand, Z3, launched in 2008. The three brands are under a perpetual licensing arrangement with Metropolitan Trading Company (MTC), a partnership firm 100% owned by the promoters of ZCCL. MTC charges a royalty of 1% over the annual turnover of ZCCL. In the exports segment, with focus on design-driven value addition, it is mainly present in men’s casual wear and semi-formal wear categories. The company operated its domestic branded business through exclusive brand outlets (EBOs) and multi-brand outlets (MBOs) and has manufacturing facilities in Karnataka and Gujarat.
