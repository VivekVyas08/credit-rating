## **Rationale** 

The rating action for Ascent Hotels Private Limited (AHPL) factors in the expectation of sustenance of the company’s financial performance and its strong linkages with its parent, SAMHI Hotels Limited (SHL; rated [ICRA]A+ (Stable)/[ICRA]A1). AHPL reported an operating income of Rs. 165.5 crore in FY2026, with YoY growth of 18.2%, aided by steady demand traction, a resultant improvement in ARR and occupancy rates. The revenue growth is expected to remain steady, stemming from the anticipated stable demand and the demand-supply gap. However, any impact due to the conflict in West Asia remains a key monitorable. The company’s operating margins remained healthy at 23.8% in FY2026, but dipped from 34.0% in FY2025, due to the conflict-induced temporary disruptions in Q4 FY2026, and certain one-off expenses. The margins are expected to remain healthy going forward on the back of better operating leverage benefits as revenues continue to expand. AHPL also has strong financial flexibility with SHL holding a 65% stake in it, while the remaining is held by GIC. Further, SHL is expected to provide timely and adequate financial support to AHPL, as and when required. 

Through the fund infusion from GIC in FY2026 (amounting to Rs. 224 crore), the related party debt provided by SHL to AHPL has been repaid, which was further utilised towards reducing net debt at the parent/group level. The total debt/OPBITDA (including related party loan) was 5.1 times and interest coverage was 2.4 times for AHPL in FY2026, with improvement expected over the near to medium term through better accruals and reduction in cost of debt. 

The Stable outlook on the long-term rating reflects ICRA’s expectations that the company will be able to sustain its credit profile, supported by its cash accruals, adequate liquidity position and moderate capex. 

## **Key rating drivers and their description** 

### **Credit strengths** 

**Strong parentage –** SHL has an established market position in the hospitality industry with 31 operating hotels and 4,899 operational rooms across 14 cities and 10 brands, as on March 31, 2026. SHL held a 100% stake in AHPL, till May 2025, when GIC invested Rs. 224 crore for a 35% stake, which reduced SHL’s stake to 65%. AHPL enjoys strong financial flexibility with SHL as its parent. Moreover, SHL is expected to provide timely and adequate financial support to AHPL, as and when required. 

**Favourable location; management tie-up with well-known international hospitality operator –** The company owns an upper upscale hotel in Nagar Road, Pune (Maharashtra), under the Hyatt Regency brand. The hotel has 222 operational rooms and 79 serviced apartments. The property is in a key commercial area, attracting corporate travellers and meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions (MICE) events while commanding a strong revenue per available room (RevPAR) in the micromarket. SHL benefits from the global branding, marketing, and advertising networks of the international operator.

**Revenue growth to remain steady, supported by demand-supply gap in hotel industry –** AHPL reported an operating income of Rs. 165.5 crore in FY2026, with YoY growth of 18.2%, aided by steady demand traction, and a resultant improvement in ARR and occupancy rates. The revenue growth is expected to remain steady, stemming from the anticipated stable demand and the demand-supply gap. However, any impact due to the West Asia conflict remains a key monitorable. 

### **Credit challenges** 

**High geographical and segment concentration –** The company has a single hotel property, which operates in the upper upscale segment, thus exposing it to any localised downturns/unforeseen events or region-specific risks. The same has also kept the company’s scale of operations relatively modest. 

**Moderate coverage metrics –** During FY2026, AHPL received Rs. 224 crore fund infusion from Reco Bellflower Private Limited, an affiliate of GIC, for a stake of 35%, which has been utilised towards repayment of related party debt of Rs. 226 crore provided by SHL, with further utilisation towards reducing net debt at the parent/group level. The company had total external debt of Rs. 195.6 crore as on March 31, 2025, which reduced to Rs. 170.6 crore as on March 31, 2026. The total debt/OPBITDA (including related party loan) was 5.1 times and interest coverage was 2.4 times for AHPL in FY2026, with improvement expected through better accruals and reduction in cost of debt. 

**Vulnerability of hotel revenues to inherent industry cyclicality, economic uncertainties, and exogenous events –** The operating performance of the property remains vulnerable to industry cyclicality/seasonality, macroeconomic cycles and exogenous factors (geopolitical crises, terrorist attacks, disease outbreaks, etc.). At the group level, the risk to revenues is partially mitigated by SHL’s geographically diversified portfolio, which allows it to withstand any demand vulnerability-related to a micromarket or specific town/city to an extent. 

## **Liquidity position: Adequate** 

AHPL’s liquidity position is Adequate with expected healthy cash flow from operations of Rs. 25-30 crore p.a. and consolidated free cash and bank balance of around Rs. 1.0 crore as on March 31, 2026 and undrawn working capital lines of over Rs. 20 crore and undrawn term loans of Rs. 100 crore as on May 31, 2026. Against this, AHPL has a scheduled principal repayment obligation of Rs. 1.4 crore in FY2027 and Rs. 2.7 crore in FY2028 for its existing loans, and minimal capex plans in FY2027. 

## **Rating sensitivities** 

**Positive factors –** Improvement in the credit profile of the parent entity, while sustaining AHPL’s operating and financial performance, could be a trigger for an upward revision in ratings. 

**Negative factors –** Weakening in SHL’s credit profile/AHPL’s linkages with SHL, or absence of timely and adequate financial support from SHL, when required, would be a negative rating trigger. The rating could also be downgraded if there is significant deterioration in earnings or significant weakening of debt metrices and liquidity position on a sustained basis.

## **About the company** 

Incorporated in 2006, AHPL owns an upscale hotel in Wakefield Estate, Nagar Road, Pune (Maharashtra), under the Hyatt Regency brand. Hyatt Regency Pune has an operational inventory of 222 rooms, and 79 serviced apartments. The property offers facilities such as a temperature-controlled indoor swimming pool, salon, fitness centre, spa, banquet hall, meeting rooms, exhibition area and four lounge-cum-restaurants. Originally promoted by the Jatia Group, AHPL became a whollyowned subsidiary of SHL in February 2016. In May 2025, GIC invested into AHPL, post which the shareholding by SHL in Ascent has reduced to 65%, with GIC holding a 35% stake.
