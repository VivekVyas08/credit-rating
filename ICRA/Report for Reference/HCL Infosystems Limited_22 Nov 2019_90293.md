# **Rationale** 

The rating assigned to the borrowing facilities of HCL Infosystems Limited (HCLI) has been withdrawn as there is no amount outstanding against the rated facility, and at the request of the company. ICRA does not have information to suggest that the credit risk has changed since the time the rating was last reviewed. The last detailed rationale is available at the following link.
