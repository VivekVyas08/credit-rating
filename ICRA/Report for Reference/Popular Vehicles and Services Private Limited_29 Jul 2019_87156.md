# **About the company:** 

Popular Vehicles and Services Private Limited, incorporated in the year 1983, is an authorised dealer of Maruti Suzuki India Limited in Kerala and Chennai. It has seven showrooms and thirty-six Service centres located across the Kerala and Chennai markets, making it the second largest distributor of MSIL vehicles in the country. It is the flagship company of the Kuttukaran group, which also has interests in automobile dealerships of other OEMs and distribution of spare parts. The group, which was started by Mr. Kuttukaran Porinchu Paul as a small-scale spare parts player in Kerala, has recorded healthy growth over the years having diversified its presence into dealership of passenger cars and trucks. Five of the six major entities in the Kuttakaran group are subsidiaries (viz. Popular Mega Motors India Limited, Popular Auto Dealers Private Limited, Popular Autoworks Private Limited, Prabal Motors Private Limited, and Vision Motors Private Limited of PVSL and other is an associate entity (Kuttukaran Trading Venture)
