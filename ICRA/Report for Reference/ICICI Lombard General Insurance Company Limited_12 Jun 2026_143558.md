## **Rationale** 

<mark>The rating factors in ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited’s (ICICI Lombard) strong market position. The company is the largest private general insurer with a market share of 8.9%</mark><sup>1</sup> <mark>in terms of gross direct premium income (GDPI) in FY2026. It has an established presence across the retail as well as corporate segments, supported by its diversified distribution mix,</mark> which has aided growth. 

<mark>ICICI Lombard’s capitalisation remains strong, with a solvency ratio of 2.67 times as on March 31, 2026 (versus regulatory requirement of 1.50 times), supported by healthy internal capital accretion of capital. The company’s profitability profile also remains strong, as indicated by the average combined ratio of 104.6% and an average RoE</mark><sup>2</sup> <mark>of 17.3% over the last five years. Its underwriting performance and profitability metrics compare favourably with the industry average. Given the robust solvency and the expectation that internal accruals will remain healthy, ICRA does not expect ICICI Lombard to require any</mark> capital infusion in the medium term. 

<mark>The rating also factors in the strong promoter profile with ICICI Bank Limited</mark><sup>3</sup> <mark>(ICICI Bank) holding an equity stake of 51.26% as on March 31, 2026. The rating considers the shared brand name, ICICI Lombard’s strategic importance to ICICI Bank and the bank’s representation on its board of directors, which strengthen ICRA’s expectation of adequate and timely capital support if</mark> required. 

<mark>ICRA notes the share of motor-third party (Motor-TP; 20.0% of GDPI in FY2026), which exposes ICICI Lombard to reserving risks as this segment is long tail in nature. However, the company’s loss-reserving triangle, which involves actuarial estimates, indicates that it has maintained adequate reserves in the past. This is evidenced by the favourable claims experience in the last few years in the Motor-TP segment as well as other lines of business.</mark> 

The Stable outlook factors in the expectation that the company will receive support from ICICI Bank, if required, and will maintain its solvency level above the negative rating trigger.

## **Credit challenges** 

**<mark>High share of long-tail business exposes the company to reserving risks</mark>** <mark>– A major risk faced by an insurance company is the underwriting of the business at adequate premium pricing in relation to the underwritten risk. The uncertainty regarding the extent of claims is relatively higher in the Motor-TP segment, which accounted for 20-24% of ICICI Lombard’s total GDPI in the last few years. The long-tail nature of the Motor-TP segment, given the legal process involved for claims settlement, could result in uncertainty regarding the level of future claims in relation to the past reserves made for this segment. The company’s loss-reserving triangle, which involves actuarial estimates, indicates that it has maintained adequate reserves in the past and witnessed favourable claims experiences in this segment vis-à-vis reserving during the last few years. However, the eventual outcome for the risk-in-force may be known with considerable lag, which could impact its future profitability and solvency. Further, the profitability of this segment could be impacted as the pricing of Motor-TP rates is regulated, with no price hikes in the last many years. The ability to grow the business while maintaining adequate pricing for risk underwritten will be key for long-term profitability.</mark> 

# **Environmental and social risks** 

The health, mortgage, and trade credit insurance businesses undertaken by ICICI Lombard face low environmental risks, unlike property or asset insurance that is exposed to risks emanating from natural or climate change-related calamities. Such events may pose material risk of elevated claims, higher than those modelled, which could possibly adversely impact the financial performance. # **Liquidity position: Strong** 

ICICI Lombard’s net written premium was Rs. 23,374 crore in FY2026 in relation to the maximum net claims paid of Rs. 13,541 crore in the last few years. The company’s operating cash flow was positive in FY2026, reflecting its ability to meet its expenses and claims through premium inflows. The investment portfolio remains adequately diversified, with Rs. 20,723 crore (35.5% of total investments as on March 31, 2026) deployed in central and state government securities, thereby providing liquidity support to meet any unexpected increase in policyholder claims. Shareholders’ investments of Rs. 14,929 crore remained strong with no outstanding sub-debt as on March 31, 2026.

# **About the company** 

ICICI Lombard is a publicly listed general insurance company. ICICI Bank is a promoter, holding 51.26% of the outstanding shares as on March 31, 2026. 

ICICI Lombard offers a comprehensive and well-diversified range of products, including motor, health, crop/weather, fire, personal accident, marine, engineering and liability insurance, through multiple distribution channels. It is the largest private general insurance company in India with a market share of 8.9% as on March 31, 2026.
