# **About the company** 

Hind Rectifiers Limited (HRL or the company) was incorporated in 1958 and is involved in designing and manufacturing power semiconductor devices, power electronic equipment and railway transportation equipment. The company is a Part-I supplier of conventional single phase and three-phase power equipment to the Indian Railways. It also holds prequalification status from various other reputed public sector and private sector undertakings. HRL has four manufacturing facilities – one each in Mumbai and Nashik (Maharashtra) and two in Dehradun (Uttarakhand).
