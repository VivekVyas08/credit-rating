# **About the company** 

Godrej Properties Limited (GPL) is the real estate venture of the Godrej Industries Group, which is involved in diverse business segments spanning FMCG, consumer products, chemicals, animal feed, real estate development and oil palm plantation through various Group companies. It was incorporated as Sea Breeze Constructions and Investments Private Limited on February 8, 1985 by Mr. Mohan Khubchand Thakur and Mrs. Desiree Mohan Thakur. In 1987, it became a part of the Godrej Industries Group and in 1989, it became a subsidiary of Godrej Industries Limited (erstwhile Godrej Soaps Limited), which holds 44.77% of the company’s equity share capital as on March 31, 2025. At present, GPL has presence in 11 cities in India and its business focuses mostly on residential real estate development. It delivered ~67 msf of real estate projects since FY2018 and has around 234 msf of total saleable area as on June 30, 2025.
