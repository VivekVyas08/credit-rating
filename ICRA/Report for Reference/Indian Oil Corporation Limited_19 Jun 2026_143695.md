# **About the company** 

IOC is one of the largest corporate entities in India, with the Government of India holding a 51.5% equity stake in the company. The company and its subsidiaries have a total refining capacity of ~80.8 MMTPA, which is around 31% of the total domestic refining capacity. The company accounted for ~40% of the motor spirit (MS) and high-speed diesel (HSD) sold within the country in FY2026. IOC also enjoys a dominant presence in the domestic crude and product transportation business, controlling a significant share in the country’s total downstream pipeline capacity. The company has interests across the gas value chain – from LNG import terminals to city gas distribution networks (CGD). IOC is also a large petrochemical producer with around 4.5-MMPTA capacity.
