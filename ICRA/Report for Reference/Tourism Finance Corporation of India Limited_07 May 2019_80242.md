## **About the company** 

Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited (TFCI) primarily provides financial assistance to tourism-related projects, such as hotels, resorts, amusement parks, etc. Since FY2012, TFCI has also been lending to other sectors such as infrastructure and solar power. From FY2018, TFCI started extending funds to non-banking finance companies. 

TFCI was incorporated in 1989 and has been notified as a public financial institution and is listed on the National Stock Exchange and the Bombay Stock Exchange. In FY2018, it reported a net profit of Rs. 75 crore on an asset base of Rs. 2,007 crore compared to a profit after tax of Rs. 70 crore on an asset base of Rs. 1,700 crore in FY2017. The company reported a net worth of Rs. 625 crore as on March 31, 2018.
