## **About the company** 

Piramal Pharma Limited (PPL) was incorporated in 2020 to streamline the Group’s structure and consolidate all pharmaceutical operations under one company.  It is one of the leading global pharmaceutical companies. The company’s pharmaceutical product portfolio can be categorised into three segments, namely, CDMO, complex hospital generics (critical care), and consumer healthcare (OTC). The company has a presence in more than 100 countries and has manufacturing plants in India, the UK, and North America.
