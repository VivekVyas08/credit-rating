## **Rating action** 

ICRA has withdrawn the [ICRA]A (Stable) (pronounced ICRA A with stable outlook) outstanding on the Rs. 140.00 crore term loan facilities and the Rs. 191.50 crore long-term fund-based facilities of Rane (Madras) Limited (RML/the company)1. ICRA has also withdrawn the [ICRA]A1 (pronounced ICRA A one) outstanding on the Rs.24.00 crore shortterm non-fund based facilities, the Rs. 125.00 crore short-term non-fund based facilities (sublimit), the Rs. 1.20 crore short term proposed facilities and the Rs. 40.00 crore commercial paper programme of the company. 

## **Rationale** 

The ratings for the bank lines have been withdrawn in accordance with ICRA's policy on withdrawal and suspension, at the request from the company, based on no objection certificate provided by its lenders. The rating for the commercial paper programme has been withdrawn basis confirmation from the company that there is no amount outstanding against the rated instrument and in accordance with ICRA's policy on withdrawal and suspension. This has also been done at the request from the company.

# **About the company:** 

Rane (Madras) Limited (RML / the company) is primarily a tier-I auto component supplier and has three broad product segments – steering gears, die-cast products and Rane Auto Parts (RAP). Steering gears and RAP primarily operate out of India, while die-cast products are done from India and in the USA, in Rane Precision Die Casting Inc (RPDC), USA. The steering gears segment manufactures a) steering gear products such as rack and pinions (R&P) and re-circulating balls (RCBs) for manual/mechanical gears, b) steering and suspension linkages such as tie-rods, drag links, inner ball joints (IBJ), outer ball joints (OBJ) and suspension ball joints and c) hydraulic cylinders and hydrostatic steering units for tractors. The die-cast division manufactures high pressure aluminium die castings used in engine housings and power steering applications. The company has four manufacturing units for the steering division in Kanchipuram district (Tamil Nadu), Pantnagar (Uttarakhand), Pondicherry and Mysore (Karnataka); two units for the die-cast division in India – both located in Hyderabad (Telangana); and one plant in Kentucky, USA for RPDC. 

RML belongs to the larger Rane Group, represented by key companies such as Rane Holdings Limited (RHL, rated [ICRA]AA- (Stable) / [ICRA]A1+), Rane Engine Valve Limited (rated [ICRA]BBB (Stable)/[ICRA]A2), Rane Brake Lining Limited (rated [ICRA]AA- (Stable) / [ICRA]A1+), Rane TRW Steering Systems Private Limited (rated [ICRA]AA- (Stable) / [ICRA]A1+) and Rane NSK Steering Systems Limited. RHL holds 61.6% stake in RML.
